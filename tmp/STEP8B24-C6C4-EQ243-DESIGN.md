
## Required construction, in dependency order

1. **Generated local operator.**  From the sealed C6c.2 prefix at source scale
   `j`, construct the literal local precision on `box` and its Dirichlet
   compression.  Its Green must be definitionally or theorem-wise the
   canonical `cmp99RegionalDirichletGreen`; `A`, `c` and `hAcoer` may not be
   independently supplied.
2. **CMP95/CMP96 dictionary.**  Prove that this generated inverse is the
   `G'(box)` occurring in (2.40)--(2.43), including the distinct `j` and
   `j+1` branches.  No diagonal identification of the two scales is accepted.
3. **Point-source kernels.**  Transport the common C6c.1/C6c.2 value and left
   derivative majorants to the local carrier, retaining a common positive
   rate and amplitude.  Compression/restriction must be explicit and the
   source-owner orientation must agree with the output-fixed localized-sup
   predicate.
4. **Arbitrary owner-supported input.**  Extend from point sources to every
   `FinitePiLpSupportedInOwner` input.  The only permitted scale losses are
   the stated `ell^2` value loss and `ell` derivative loss.  No fine-volume,
   owner-fibre cardinality, or second owner sum may appear.
5. **Uniform witness order.**  The source asserts constants uniform in the RG
   scale.  The producer must therefore have the logical order
   `exists B0 delta0, 0 < B0 and 0 < delta0 and forall depth/branch, ...`,
   not `forall depth, exists B0 delta0, ...`.  Choosing the constants after
   the depth would not feed the later walk sum or window 15.

## Post-P9 refinement: the admissible lifting mechanism

P0--P9 are now sealed, but P9 deliberately exposes only a per-depth
entrywise Combes--Thomas conclusion.  Summing that conclusion over a complete
source-owner fibre is not the C6c.4 proof: the triangle inequality would pay
the full fibre cardinality and would lose the printed `ell^2` value scale.

The admissible next lemma must instead remain one level earlier in the
Combes--Thomas argument.  For a field `f` supported in one source-owner fibre,
apply coercivity to the rooted *tilted inverse equation* before extracting a
coordinate.  The source tilt is then bounded in counting L2 by the already
sealed conversion

```text
  norm f <= ell^2 * finitePiLpSupNorm f
```

and the target coordinate is extracted only after the tilted operator-norm
estimate.  This spends the permitted `ell^2` once and introduces no fine-site
sum.  The owner-distance comparison must be stated explicitly, including the
bounded diameter of the source owner, so the fine-site tilt becomes a common
positive rate in the source-owner metric.

This refinement does not itself solve the physical uniformity problem.  The
per-depth coercivity floor and tilted rate still have to be bounded by one
`B0` and one `delta0` chosen before `depth`.  Those bounds must be derived from
the generated coefficient and the joint scalar regime; accepting them as an
arbitrary localized Green certificate would merely rename CMP96 (2.43).
