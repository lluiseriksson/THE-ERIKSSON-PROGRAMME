and proves addition at a common rate, but contains no theorem that weakens an
amplitude upward and a decay rate downward.  C6c.9 first needs the generic
lemma


accepted dictionary.

## Uniform quantifier order

The producer theorem must have the logical order

```text

  -> exists B0 > 0, exists delta0 > 0,
       forall depth, forall j-or-j+1 branch, forall admissible Omega,
         CMP99Eq342SourceLocalizedGreenCertificate ... B0 delta0.
```

fixing `Omega`, or a certificate family supplied by the caller fails the
uniformity claim of CMP99 (3.42).  The scalar definitions must not depend on
the finite carrier cardinality, the number of owners, or the RG depth.
