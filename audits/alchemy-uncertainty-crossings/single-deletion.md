# Single-player sensitivity of the S4 minus S1 crossing
The selection rule was fixed before refitting. Of 29,493 matched players, one uniquely maximised the squared contribution to the original contrast variance (44.03%). Removing this player from both datasets and holding the feature scaling fixed left 29,492 matched clusters. The fitted zero crossings were 3,658.51 and 3,715.33 unique combinations. The difference moved from +16.41 to +56.83, an increase of 40.41. Its sign persisted and the new point estimate lies within the original nominal paired interval of [-81.80, 114.62]. The first-order prediction was +49.66.
    
The nominal interval conditional on treating the reduced set as fixed was [-39.92, 153.57]. It does not account for choosing the most influential observation. This is one targeted sensitivity analysis. It does not bound arbitrary deletions or establish a causal effect. No additional players were removed. The frozen four-page manuscript and its assessment predate this supplement.
