# Which enrichment comparisons survive a family-wide correction?

Supplement to the PC-Bench rank audit (manuscript source b299fa669dca8758a76e39854ff85048074e0cb3). We treat the 94 configurations as one family, including both selection protocols. Our family choice is an explicit sensitivity analysis, not the depositors' prespecified family. All calculations use the published aggregate tables in [PC-Bench](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24405487).

Under uniform draws from all measured genes, Holm's step-down procedure at family-wise level 0.05 retains 25 configurations. Under uniform draws from the positive-importance support, **22 of those 25 are retained for every support-size vector within the rank bounds**. No configuration outside the original 25 can be retained for any such vector. The three between the inner and outer bounds are BRCA/LR/SITE/VAR5000, BRCA/XGB/SITE/VAR2000 and UCEC/XGB/SITE/ANOVA2000. Determining the true support sizes could settle their status. This does not assume that the extrema in all 94 rows arise simultaneously from the original fits.

For a probability vector p let H(p) denote the rejection set obtained by sorting the m entries and stopping at the first ordered value exceeding 0.05/(m-j+1), with j starting at one. Decreasing any component can only expand H: a previously accepted prefix of j tests still clears its first j ordered cutoffs, and a previously rejected test that moves later because others move ahead faces a weaker cutoff preceded by values at most its original value once past its original rank. Thus the vector of upper tail bounds gives an inner rejection set and the vector of lower bounds gives an outer one. The certificates use exact fractions, with a second check by the equivalent cumulative maximum of the scaled ordered probabilities. Holm's family-wise error interpretation requires valid marginal null probabilities; these are descriptive random-list references, not calibrated patient-level probabilities. [Holm (1979)](https://www.jstor.org/stable/4615733).

The supplement also gives the minimum positive-support size for crossing the *unadjusted* 0.05 cutoff in each of the 25 individually ambiguous rows. For BRCA/LR/MAD2000, under either validation protocol, a positive support of **4,901 or more** suffices; at 4,900 the tail is still at least 0.05. These thresholds apply to the observed hits and panel membership in the released rows. Per-row counts and both family rejection sets are in panel-family.json.
