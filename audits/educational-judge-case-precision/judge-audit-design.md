# Common-protocol audit of distinct baseline cases

Keep the observed adjudication of the post-calibration cases fixed. Use a common blinded reference protocol for new reviews. Assume the already confirmed baseline cases retain their status and the published case identities are stable. This is a proposed design. No additional case was reviewed.

Let B be the number of distinct baseline cases with at least one already confirmed flag. Sample n cases uniformly without replacement from the other N=2086−B baseline cases and adjudicate all original flags in each sampled case. Fix a post-calibration lower bound L for confirmed cases. If L>B, an absolute count tie requires at least K=L−B hidden positive baseline cases. A tie in yield requires at least K=ceil(2086L/72)−B. If K exceeds N, a tie is impossible. If K is nonpositive, an audit of baseline negatives alone cannot demonstrate the desired advantage at this post lower bound.

For 1≤K≤N, conditional on at least K hidden cases, the probability of zero discoveries is at most C(N−K,n)/C(N,n). The bound follows by counting samples disjoint from the hidden set and decreases monotonically in its size. It is attained with exactly K hidden cases. A zero-discovery audit rejects the tie-or-reversal null at one-sided level 0.05 whenever that ratio is at most 1/20. This is a sampling error guarantee for the prespecified randomized design. A finding of one or more positives requires the full hypergeometric tail or a separate decision rule.

At the feasible extremal B=24 and guaranteed L=27, N=2062. Absolute counts require excluding three hidden cases, whereas yield requires excluding 759. These thresholds describe entirely different demands on a human verification budget. For other B up to 24, known absences cannot be assumed independent. To implement, obtain the list of distinct baseline flagged cases, the baseline confirmations per case, all flags for each sampled case, and evidence that the post lower bound survives the same adjudication criterion. Without this information the design is prospective and supplies no empirical re-audit. A common adjudication yielding fewer than L post cases falsifies the conditional input.
