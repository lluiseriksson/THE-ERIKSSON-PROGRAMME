# One changed task on interior count strata at arbitrary fixed depth

There are \(m\) independent tasks of \(n\ge2\) independent Bernoulli attempts each. The full experiment counts all successes \(S\). The [first-success score](one-outlier.md) \(Z\) is zero unless all tasks hit and otherwise assigns the unrestricted-to-capped composition weight at the total wait. On a stratum \(S=k\ge m\), let \(P_k\) be the uniform law of the labelled outcomes and \(Q_k\) the same law weighted by \(Z\). Stratum calibration gives \(E_{P_k}Z=1\), so both are probability laws. Let \(R\) count the successes on a specified task. Their means for \(R\) are both \(k/m\), by exchangeability.

**Theorem.** Fix \(n\) and a compact subinterval \(I\) of \((1/n,1)\). For all sufficiently large \(m\) and every \(k\) with \(k/(mn)\in I\), \(R\) under \(P_k\) strictly dominates \(R\) under \(Q_k\) in convex order: each hinge \(f_h(j)=(j-h)_+\), \(1\le h<n\), has a strictly larger full expectation. In fact the minimum difference across these hinges and strata stays bounded away from zero. Every convex function on the integer support is the sum of an affine function and a nonnegative linear combination of these hinges.

Consequently, on every such stratum the full probability is at least the contribution of the stopped score when one task has any success probability and all the others have the same interior probability. Indeed the likelihood ratio from a homogeneous bank is a positive constant times \(t^R\) for interior replacements, where \(t\) is the ratio of the replacement and background odds. This sequence is strictly convex if \(t\ne1\). Endpoint comparisons follow by continuity. The hypotheses concern the success count of the stratum; the background success probability need not equal \(k/(mn)\). The proof gives no explicit minimum bank size. The separate lower-threshold estimate below extends the comparison to all strata bounded away from the all-success endpoint.

**Law of the total wait.** Write \(T=mn\) and \(L=m(n-1)\). In a full outcome where all tasks hit, specify their wait vector of total \(s\). The free suffixes have total length \(L-s\) and must accommodate \(k-m\) successes. The number of admissible suffixes is independent of the wait vector. Thus under \(Q_k\) the probability of \(s\) is
\[
 \binom{m+s-1}{s}\binom{L-s}{k-m}/\binom{T}{k}.
\]
This is exactly the law of the position of the \(m\)-th success minus \(m\) in a uniform length-\(T\) string with \(k\) successes. Along any sequence \(k/T\to a\in I\), \(s/m\to(1-a)/a\) in probability. To see this, count successes in prefixes of lengths \(\lfloor m(1/a\pm\epsilon)\rfloor\) for small fixed positive \(\epsilon\). Their hypergeometric means differ from \(m\) by order \(m\) on opposite sides, and their variances are \(O(m)\). Chebyshev's inequality brackets the position. Conditional on \(s\), the named wait \(U\) is a coordinate of a uniform capped composition of \(s\), and the suffix successes are uniformly placed.

**Limit of a named count.** Write \(q=1-a\), \(H(z)=\sum_{u=0}^{n-1}z^u\). There is a unique \(z>0\) with \(zH'(z)/H(z)=q/a\): the derivative of the mean with respect to \(\log z\) is the positive variance, and the range is \((0,n-1)\). Along the same sequence, the laws of \(R\) have the following limits.
* Under \(P_k\), \(R\) converges to \(B\sim\mathrm{Bin}(n,a)\) by sampling \(n\) slots without replacement.
* Under \(Q_k\), first draw \(U\) with probabilities \(z^u/H(z)\). Given \(U=u\), the limiting count is \(W=1+\mathrm{Bin}(n-1-u,a)\).

For the second statement, at fixed \(s\) choose \(z_s\) so that the tilted one-site mean equals \(s/m\). Conditioning \(m\) independent tilted sites on their sum \(s\) gives exactly the uniform capped composition. When \(s/m\to q/a\), \(z_s\to z\). The probability of a named value \(u\) therefore differs from \(z_s^u/H(z_s)\) by a factor tending to one: the factor is the ratio of the local probabilities for a sum of \(m-1\) sites evaluated at \(s-u\) and a sum of \(m\) sites at \(s\). For completeness, Fourier inversion for these bounded lattice variables gives each local probability as \((1+o(1))/\sqrt{2\pi l\,\sigma_s^2}\) for \(l=m-1,m\) at bounded displacement from its mean. Indeed, with \(z_s\) in a compact positive interval the variance is bounded away from zero, the centered characteristic function is \(1-\sigma_s^2 t^2/2+O(|t|^3)\) uniformly near zero, and off any neighborhood of zero in \([-\pi,\pi]\) its modulus is uniformly less than one since slots zero and one have positive mass. Scaling the inversion variable by \(\sqrt l\) gives the Gaussian integral by domination. Finally \((k-m)/(L-s)\to a\) and \(L-s\) grows linearly, so sampling a bounded number of suffix slots gives the asserted binomial limit. The preceding conditional arguments hold along every deterministic typical sequence and hence also after averaging over \(s\).

**Why all hinges have a strict sign.** In \(n\) independent Bernoulli-\(a\) trials the unconditional probability of a first hit after \(u<n\) misses is \(a q^u\). The remaining trials have exactly the same limiting suffix law as above. For \(1\le j\le n\) this gives the density ratio
\[
 \frac{\Pr(W=j)}{\Pr(B=j)}
 =\frac{1}{aH(z)}\,E\!\left[(z/q)^{U_B}\mid B=j\right],
\]
where \(U_B\) is the first-hit wait for the binomial string. We have \(z>q\) because truncating the geometric series with parameter \(q\) strictly decreases its mean from \(q/a\). Given \(B=j\), the hit sites are a uniform \(j\)-subset. Couple all positive subset sizes by taking the first \(j\) entries of a random permutation. The minimum hit position decreases stochastically and has positive probability of decreasing at each step. The displayed ratio is therefore strictly decreasing in \(j\).

Both limits have mean \(na\). Weighting the mass at \(j\) by \(j/(na)\) gives two probability laws on \(1,\ldots,n\). The full law divided by the stopped law has strictly increasing density ratio on this support. For \(1\le h<n\), \(g(j)=(j-h)_+/j\) is increasing and nonconstant. Its covariance with the full-to-stopped density ratio under the size-weighted stopped law is strictly positive, as is seen by taking the expectation of the product of the two differences over two independent draws. Multiplication by \(na\) proves \(E(B-h)_+>E(W-h)_+\). For uniformity, a sequence violating a positive lower bound would have a subsequence with constant \(h\) and \(k/T\) converging to a point of \(I\), contradicting this strict limiting inequality.

**Including the lower threshold.** The lower endpoint can in fact be included. Under the weighted law all tasks hit, so for every hinge its expectation is at most \(E_{Q_k}(R-1)=k/m-1\). Under the full law each hinge expectation is at least \(P_k(R=n)=(k)_n/(mn)_n\), where parentheses denote falling factorials and the numerator is zero for \(k<n\). Thus the minimum hinge gap is at least \((k)_n/(mn)_n-(k/m-1)\). When \(k/(mn)\to1/n\) with \(k\ge m\), the bound tends to \(n^{-n}>0\). The same compactness argument now proves uniform strict convex order for all \(m\le k\le (1-\epsilon)mn\) at sufficiently large \(m\), for any fixed \(0<\epsilon<1-1/n\). For fixed depth, any sequence of failing one-replacement strata with unbounded bank size must approach the all-success endpoint. This strengthens the interior theorem; the upper endpoint still needs a separate argument.

**Four-attempt check.** At \(n=4,\ a=2/5\), the tilt is \(z=1\), so the named wait under the limiting weighted law is uniform on four values. For the central hinge \(h=2\), the full expectation is \(128/625\). The weighted expectation is \(18/125\), by averaging \(E(\mathrm{Bin}(l,2/5)-1)_+\) for \(l=0,1,2,3\). The gap is \(38/625\). This is a stratum-conditional model value.

**Finite certificates.** The separate [producer](single-count-hinges.py) bounds each weight by rounding outwards with denominator \(2^{mn+40}\). For a named count \(j\ge1\) and named wait \(u\), the named suffix has \(\binom{n-1-u}{j-1}\) completions. For total wait \(v\) on the other tasks there are \(A_{m-1}(v)\) prefix choices, leaving \(\binom{(m-1)(n-1)-v}{k-m-j+1}\) other suffix completions (out-of-range coefficients are zero). Multiplication by \(w_{v+u}\) and summation brackets every weighted joint count. Full joint counts are \(\binom nj\binom{mn-n}{k-j}\). The interval test applied to each hinge uses the upper weighted bound for the lower deficit. With fewer than \(n\) failures the score equals one pathwise. Hinge expectations also agree when the hinge is affine on the common range. Both identities may be used to certify zeros exactly. Small designs with \(mn\le16\) were cross-checked by enumerating labelled binary strings.

For these bank sizes, summing the stratum inequalities proves the full tail comparison. The analytic theorem covers arbitrarily large banks on compact interior bands.
