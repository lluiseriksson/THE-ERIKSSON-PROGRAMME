Compare a uniform sample without replacement of min(k,n) recorded role positions against including the editor and min(k-1,n-1) uniformly sampled reviewer positions. Primary budget k=2; enumerate all attainable budgets as sensitivity. An article is detected if at least one selected position documents a comparison. Use the original dataset and prior inclusion audit. The averages are across 201 articles, not conditional on an event. Cross-check by enumerating subsets and by the exact hypergeometric formula for each article. Keep the original texts unchanged. Check sensitivity to errors by finding the minimum number of article histories whose coded positive positions would have to change to eliminate or reverse the primary contrast with roles and panel sizes fixed. Any replacement of the binary flags within a selected article is allowed in this sensitivity model. This exercise makes no claim about actual coding errors or about private reviewer behavior.
