# Simultaneous top-15 and top-20 screening in one ranking

Consider one pair of tie-free rankings of 55 tests, with Spearman correlation that rounds half-up to 0.73. If the selected top 15 misses every reference top-15 item, then **at least nine of the selected top 20 must be in the reference top 20**. Nine is attainable. This improves the unconditional guarantee of five at the second cutoff when the first cutoff has failed completely. Each condition in this statement concerns the same ranking. The figure from ContextSim does not disclose that this configuration was observed.

Proof and computation. For two cuts k < l, partition both the positions and the ranks at k and l. A permutation determines an integer 3-by-3 table of counts. Its row and column margins both equal (k,l−k,n−l). At each cutoff the number retained is the sum of counts in the corresponding northwest square. For each such table the minimum squared displacement can be computed without searching over permutations. If two positions in one row block receive inverted ranks, exchanging them keeps the table fixed and strictly decreases the squared cost. The same argument applied to the inverse permutation puts the preimages within a column block in increasing order. Thus at a minimum each cell comprises contiguous segments of positions and ranks, both matched in increasing order. The row and column prefix sums give the two offsets for that cell, and its contribution to twice the energy is its count times the squared offset difference. This sorted assignment exists for every integer table with the prescribed margins. Enumerating four free cells suffices; the rest follow from the margins. The returned certificate lists a minimizing permutation in every example.

For k=15, l=20 and required misses (15,12), table enumeration gives minimum half-squared displacement 3860. The mean-energy ceiling for this single ranking is 3811.5. Thus 12 or more misses at 20 are impossible. To see sharpness, start with (16,…,30,1,…,15,31,…,55), which has energy 3375. Interchange entries in positions 20 and 31 (ranks 5 and 31), gaining 286 units. The new order has 15 and 11 misses respectively and energy 3661. Finally swap adjacent positions 31 and 32, now carrying ranks 5 and 32. This adds exactly 27 units and leaves both selected sets unchanged. The resulting energy is 3688, in the required interval (3672.9, 3811.5]. Its retained counts are zero and nine.

Independent of the 55-item calculation, every pair of cuts and every feasible pair of lower demands was checked for n=3,…,8 against literal permutation enumeration, 490 cases.
