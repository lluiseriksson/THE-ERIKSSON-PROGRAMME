# Selective evidence corruption in FinVeriQA

The 60-item Mistral panel in Tables 10–11 of Rajpurohit et al. (arXiv:2611.25071v1) has 21 detections common to B and C under full value corruption, 25 additional detections by C alone, and 14 detected by neither. All corrupted answers on this panel were wrong. Thus choosing exactly k of the 60 to corrupt gives an incremental count of detections between max(0,k−35) and min(25,k), inclusive. Both endpoints can be reached simply by filling the 25 exclusive items last or first. At k=30, the extra detections range from zero to 25; the uniform-subset expectation is 12.5. At k=36 at least one extra detection is guaranteed. These statements compare corrupted questions, so they require no pairing of clean-context labels and make no assumption about the actual pattern of future errors.

For total silent errors under C, let h be the clean silent errors on the 60−k unaffected questions and y the corrupted misses. Table 10 reports 30 clean silent errors and 14 misses under complete corruption. With only the published margins, h ranges from max(0,30−k) to min(30,60−k). Given x extra detections, y ranges from max(0,k−x−21) to min(14,k−x). The clean marginal can be allocated freely across the chosen and unchosen questions when their individual states have not been reported. The attainable total under C is h+y. This describes the class of pairings allowed by published totals, not necessarily the realized panel's pairing. If the two checks share the same item-level clean outcomes, total silent errors under B equal h+y+x. Equal clean aggregate rates alone do not imply this item-level condition.

The largest C silent total over compatible pairings and subsets is min(30,60−k)+min(14,k). This falls strictly below the 30 clean silent errors for every compatible pairing only when k≥45. In particular, the uniform-mixture decrease in Table 10 is not a guarantee for selective corruption. The original authors explicitly describe their intermediate entries as mixtures. The gold-reference verifier is an upper-bound device, not a deployed live control.
