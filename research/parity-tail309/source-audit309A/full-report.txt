Decision and exact objection
The principal theorem in Sections1-6 is analytically sound under its stated strict hypotheses. The Walsh identity, tensor spectral comparison and strict moving-cutoff calculation all survive attack. However, exact source line87 additionally says, without an upper restriction on b: 'When b>K, every density is entirely above the cutoff, so the condition is a total moment cap; the old exact total-budget threshold2 applies.' The reduction to a total cap is correct; the unqualified threshold application is false at b=1, which satisfies b>K for every K<1.

Concrete counterexample to that extension: fix any admitted rho and any K in(2-2^(1/s),1), and prescribe b=1. A nonnegative mean-one density r>=1 is identically1 on every finite cube. Therefore E[r^s 1_{r>K}]=1 and gap(T_r)=1-rho in every dimension. Even if the allowed tail/total budget equals2 or exceeds2, the infimum gap in this fixed-floor class remains1-rho>0; the threshold2 collapse classification does not apply. For b>1 the mean-one class is empty. The invoked exact308 theorem explicitly restricts fixed floors to0<b<1 and its proof explicitly excludes b=1. The missing restriction is consequently mathematically material to this comparison, though it does not damage the main309 construction with b<K.

Required revision is limited to the scope of that sentence: the fixed-floor exact-threshold application is valid for K<b<1. b=1 must be excluded or treated as uniform, and b>1 as infeasible if discussed. The audit has not edited that sentence, the PR, the judges, or any dependency. This is not a new judge and does not invalidate the preregistration. J5 already requires a correct comparison of regions and explicit retained limits. I do not silently import b<1 into a paragraph that expressly changes the earlier b<K regime and only explicitly excludes b=K. An unqualified ACCEPT for the exact frozen source would cover the false extension; hence the overall verdict is FAIL/REVISE. The PR body itself does not repeat this extension and its stated principal construction is supported.

J1 and J4: quantified construction and nonempty strict region

F is continuous and strictly decreasing on the relevant interval. As max(b,alpha)<K and F(K)<tau, a sufficiently small fixed positive h gives u=K-h>max(b,alpha) and F(u)<tau. Thus the u described in the source exists for every advertised tuple; it is chosen once before L and is not a moving threshold parameter. Let v=2-u. Then0<u<K<1<v, and v<2^(1/s) is equivalent to u>alpha. Consequently F(u)<2 and the advertised sub-two range is nonempty.

On a uniform m-cube with m>=1, the full parity character has exactly half +1 and half -1 values. a_m=1+(u-1)chi_m consequently takes exactly u and v with probabilities1/2, has mean1 and minimumu>0. For the large-L construction set m=floor(L/2), n=L-m. Both are positive for L>=2 and both tend to infinity. On the independent product blocks, q=a_m+beta_n R_n has expectation1+beta_n m_n=Z_n. Therefore r=q/Z_n is strictly positive, mean-one, and r>=u/Z_n. Since Z_n->1 and u>b, the prescribed floor holds eventually, with positive slack. No claim of a uniform L0 across rho,K,b,tau is made.

After the tail limit is established below, its strict inequality F(u)<tau provides one finite L0 such that the tail constraint, floor and positive block dimensions all hold for every L>=L0. They also have mean1, floor1>=b and positive gap. Thus every dimension, including L=1, is covered without undefined zero-bit tilt formulas. All asymptotic statements survive finite replacement.

Equality tau=F(K) is not obtained by this argument: every fixed u<K has F(u)>F(K). Setting u=K would destroy the positive limiting low-sector cutoff K-u used below, and u_L varying with L would require new control. The source expressly disclaims both steps. J2: exact parity multiplier and full tensor spectrum
For any S subset[m], Q_m chi_S=rho^(|S|/2)chi_S and chi_m chi_S=chi_{[m] symmetric_difference S}=chi_{[m]\S}. Hence
 Q_m M_chi_m Q_m chi_S=rho^((|S|+m-|S|)/2)chi_{[m]\S}=rho^(m/2)chi_{[m]\S}.
The complement operation is an involution permuting the normalized orthonormal Walsh basis; it is unitary. The exact operator norm is rho^(m/2), not merely an upper estimate. Therefore Q_m M_a_m Q_m-P_m=(u-1)Q_m M_chi_m Q_m has norm(1-u)rho^(m/2), because u<1.

On the full product space, Q_L=Q_m tensor Q_n and multiplication by a_m+beta R_n equals M_a_m tensor I+beta I tensor M_R_n. Multiplying on both sides gives exactly the source's two summands. If B_L=P_m tensor S_w_n, their difference is

Since||P_n||=1, the norm is in fact exactly(1-u)rho^(m/2)/Z_n and tends to0.

Both factors of B_L are positive definite. Their orthonormal eigenbases tensor to an orthonormal eigenbasis, with eigenvalues the positive products. J3: mixed powers and strict moving cutoff
Let theta=min(1,s/2). Then theta and s-theta are both strictly between0 and s, including for noninteger1<s<2. For y>=0 the excess D(y)=(1+y)^s-1-y^s is nonnegative: its derivative is s[(1+y)^(s-1)-y^(s-1)]>0 and D(0)=0. For0<=y<=1, the mean value theorem gives D(y)<=s2^(s-1)y<=s2^(s-1)y^theta. For y>=1, D(y)=y^s D(1/y)<=s2^(s-1)y^(s-theta). These bounds imply D(y)<=s2^(s-1)(y^theta+y^(s-theta)). Scaling y=z/c proves exactly(M) for every fixed c>0 and z>=0. No polynomial/binomial expansion for an integer s is being assumed.

Use z=beta_n R_n. The coefficients beta_n are positive and bounded for large n, and both strict-interior moments vanish. The expectation of the nonnegative excess in(M) tends to0, while E z^s=beta_n^s->1. Thus E(c+z)^s->c^s+1 for c=u and c=v. Since parity sectors have equal probability and independence from the n-block, division by Z_n^s->1 gives
 E r_L^s ->1+(u^s+v^s)/2.
Strict convexity of x^s and u!=v with(u+v)/2=1 imply(u^s+v^s)/2>1. Therefore the total moment limit is strictly above2; no earlier exact total-cap2 constraint is met or claimed.

In the high sector v>K, v/Z_n>K eventually, so the strict event includes that entire sector. Its conditional tail moment tends to v^s+1.

In the low sector the strict event is exactly R_n>c_n=(KZ_n-u)/beta_n. Its limiting cutoff is K-u>0. Choose fixed positive c_- and finite c_+ bracketing c_n for all sufficiently large n. For any fixed t in(0,s),
 P(R_n>c_n)<=c_-^(-t)E R_n^t->0,
 E[R_n^s 1_{R_n<=c_n}]<=c_+^(s-t)E R_n^t->0.
Since the whole s-moment is exactly1, the complementary strict-event s-moment tends to1, with atoms at c_n correctly assigned to the weak complement. Restrict(M) to that strict event: the constant contribution is u^s times a vanishing probability, the z^s contribution tends to1, and the mixed excess is bounded by the already vanishing unrestricted mixed terms. Therefore the conditional low-sector tail moment, after division by Z_n^s, tends to1. Averaging gives the advertised strict-tail limit1+v^s/2. This proof explicitly handles the moving cutoff; convergence in probability alone would not suffice.

For every J>=K, the event{r>J} is contained in{r>K}, so the same family has the same all-L upper bound tau at all such cutoffs. The fixed-K limiting formula is not asserted at arbitrary J, where a different sector boundary could matter.
