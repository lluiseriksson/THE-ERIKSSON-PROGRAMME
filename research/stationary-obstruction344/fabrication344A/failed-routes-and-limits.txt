344A FAILED ROUTES AND LIMITS -- preserved fabrication record
The one question is the original stationary critical-moment uniform-gap question. These are three bounded analytical attempts within that question, not three separate papers or three audits. No mathematical program, CAS, numerical experiment or runtime was used.

A. Critical tail transfer from341.
Accepted341 yields TV(r,pi)->0 and Hellinger convergence, N->1, and small finite-degree compression. It does not supply uniform integrability of r^s. Splitting into g>=1-delta and g<1-delta only gives r<=pi/[N(1-delta)^2] on the good region. The bad region may have small stationary mass without its weighted operator contribution being small. For Q=P_sqrt(rho), u=h/g, the genuine PSD/Perron inequality Q M_u Q<=I supplies a norm bound of 1, but not a strict contraction of the bad contribution or a bound on cross blocks. No whole-operator spectral estimate follows from the estimates obtained in this attempt. Generic abstract density pairs would not settle the question because they need not arise from hPh; no such pair is presented here as an admissible counterexample.

B. Floor-assisted negative denominator control.
The actual floor pi=hg>=b gives h>=b/g. Positivity of P and Jensen's inequality for x->1/x imply
 g=Ph>=b P(1/g)>=b/(Pg), so g Pg>=b and 1/g<=Pg/b.
Therefore u=pi/g^2<=pi(Pg)^2/b^2. A possible quadratic-form bound would start from
 E u(Qf)^2<=b^(-2) E pi(Pg)^2(Qf)^2.
Holder with pi in L^s leaves ||(Pg)Qf||_(2s')^2, where s'=s/(s-1) and 2s'=(1+rho)/rho=1+1/rho. This is precisely the endpoint exponent of Q:L^2->L^(1+1/rho). The direct product Holder step has no spare exponent for a nonconstant Pg factor. Extra finite moments of Pg or small finite-exponent deviations do not alone justify a supremum multiplier bound. No inequality closing this step was proved. This is a failure of this particular direct endpoint argument, not a theorem that every weighted proof is impossible.

C. Uniform denominator convergence.
Proposed bridge: exact stationary matching plus the floor and critical cap might improve341's finite-exponent Ph convergence to ||Ph-1||_infinity->0. If true, the exact identity r/pi=1/[N(Ph)^2] would make uniform relative transfer immediate. Sections2-5 of source344A rigorously falsify this proposed implication by a family satisfying the original hPh relation and all stationary constraints, even with pi bounded above and below independently of dimension. The explicit stationary density is a small perturbation by a product of block majorities. A positive potential realizing it is provided by a fully proved strictly convex finite scaling problem. It is not a formal density gadget.

Abandonment and remaining obstruction.
After these three bounded mechanisms, stop attempting uniform convergence of Ph to 1: it is false. The precise remaining obstruction is weighted control of rare denominator excursions/cross blocks or an admissible test function producing actual gap collapse.344A does not compute or bound the second eigenvalue of its family. It does not refute a fixed nonvanishing comparison constant, prove divergence of E r^s, prove failure of a general tail theorem, or refute the uniform-gap conjecture. A future unit should make a new quantitative use of hPh rather than repeat the failed implication. No new question has been opened.
