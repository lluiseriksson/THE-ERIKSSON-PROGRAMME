2. AN EXPLICIT PAIR SELECTION THAT NEVER USES A DIAGONAL FIBER

Fix a plane frequency p=(p1,p2), p2 notin{0,N}. Choose a set J_p of two
opposite third-coordinate frequencies as follows:
 J_p={q,-q}, unless p1=p2=q or p1=p2=-q;
 J_(q,q)={u,u+N};
 J_(-q,-q)={-u,-u-N}.
Each set contains two distinct residues, separated by N, neither0 norN.
The exceptional sets avoid their corresponding diagonal value p1=p2.
In all other cases either p1!=p2 or their common value is notq,-q.
Thus no (p1,p2,r), r in J_p, is diagonal; all are nonzero.
The sets also satisfy J_(-p)=-J_p. The two exceptional plane frequencies
are negatives of each other. On the rest, {q,-q} is self-conjugate as a
set. No self-conjugate plane frequency occurs in H_plane, because p2 is
neither0 norN. These observations include n=8 and every wraparound index.

3. TWO ACTUAL COEXACT PLANES SPAN ALL VECTOR TRACE DATA

For each r in J_p let g_r=g(p1,p2,r), and let
       P_r=I-g_r g_r*/||g_r||^2
be the Hermitian orthogonal projector onto the actual coexact fiber.
Define S_p=sum_(r in J_p) P_r. We prove
                   S_p >= [2/(9n^2)] I_3.             (FLOOR)

Write a=(g_1(p),g_2(p)), and the two normals as (a,b),(a,b'), where
b=exp(2pi i r/n)-1 and b'=-exp(2pi i r/n)-1. Then |b-b'|=2 and
the exact Gram determinant is
 ||(a,b)||^2||(a,b')||^2-|<(a,b),(a,b')>|^2=4||a||^2.
This is obtained by expanding both sides; it is valid for complex a,b.
Both normal norms squared are at most12. If rho is the absolute inner
product of their unit normals, this gives
         1-rho^2 >=||a||^2/36.
The normals are nonparallel, since a is nonzero (p2!=0) and b!=b'.
The sum of their rank-one unit-normal projectors has eigenvalues1+rho,
1-rho,0. Therefore S_p, which is2I minus that sum, has eigenvalues
1-rho,1+rho,2. In particular
 min spec S_p=1-rho >=(1-rho^2)/2 >=||a||^2/72.
Finally |g_2(p)|=2|sin(pi p2/n)|>=2sin(pi/n)>=4/n. The last inequality
is the concavity chord sin x>=2x/pi on[0,pi/2]. Thus ||a||^2>=16/n^2,
proving FLOOR. This floor is for actual fiber projections, not an assumed
Gram matrix; S_p is invertible at every allowed plane frequency.

4. EXACT TRACE, REALITY AND ACTUAL CURL-ADJOINT REALIZATION

For h in H_plane define the three-dimensional Fourier coefficients
  hat(Eh)(p1,p2,r)=sqrt(n) P_r S_p^-1 hat h(p1,p2), r in J_p,
and set every other coefficient to zero. Here hat h is the unitary2D
transform and hat(Eh) the unitary3D transform. A trace has Fourier rule
  hat(Tr w)(p1,p2)=n^-1/2 sum_r hat w(p1,p2,r).
This follows by substituting the3D inverse and performing the two plane
sums with unitary2D normalization. Thus the constructed trace is exactly
n^-1/2 sqrt(n) S_p S_p^-1 hat h=hat h, with no scale mismatch.

All occupied frequencies have p2,r outside{0,N}, so Eh lies in range Xi.
They are non-diagonal by section2, so Eh is orthogonal to N_d. Each
coefficient lies in the actual g-perpendicular plane. To verify actual
image membership construct, at every occupied k, the skew coefficients
 W_(j,l)(k)=[g_j(k) hat(Eh)_l(k)-g_l(k) hat(Eh)_j(k)]/||g(k)||^2.
Then sum_j conjugate(g_j)W_(j,l)=hat(Eh)_l. Set all other W coefficients
to zero. Hence Eh=d1*W, not just a field satisfying a substituted divergence.
Since J_(-p)=-J_p, g(-k)=conjugate(g(k)), and h is real, the projectors,
S_p inverses, constructed field and skew potential all obey conjugate
pairing. They therefore define real physical fields and a real linear E.

Parseval and P_r^2=P_r give, frequency by frequency,
 sum_(r in J_p)||hat(Eh)(p,r)||^2
   =n <hat h,S_p^-1(sum P_r)S_p^-1 hat h>
   =n <hat h,S_p^-1 hat h>
   <=(9/2)n^3||hat h||^2.
Summing proves EXTENSION. The construction has no harmonic/zero-frequency
component, and no projection that might change observations is applied
after its plane trace is set.

5. ALL ORIGINAL CELLS AND THE SMALLER-DOMAIN RANGE

Original cells are {i} times I times J, with I=[0,tau_i-1],{tau_i},
or[tau_i+1,n-1], J={0} or[1,n-1]; empty cells omitted. Each vector
component row is sum_cell w_r/sqrt(n^3 |cell|). The proof works for every
choice of tau_i; no permutation property is invoked.
Take any w in Xi U, and h=Tr w. Its plane transform has no p2=0,N modes,
so h belongs to H_plane. Since w has no k3=0 modes,
sum_(k=0)^(n-1) w(i,j,k)=0 at every i,j. The same holds for Eh.
They have identical plane traces; therefore their sums over k=1,...,n-1
are also identical, each being minus that same plane value. Summing over
any I proves equality for EVERY original plane and long-third cell, with
the original normalization and each spatial component: C(E Tr w)=Cw.
This includes singleton and remaining volume-one cells and handles empty
intervals by omission. No equality between differently normalized rows is
assumed; equality is between the same row evaluated on two fields.
Thus C(Xi U) is contained in C(Xi O). The reverse inclusion is immediate.
This proves RANGE by an actual explicit linear lift, not a dimension count.
