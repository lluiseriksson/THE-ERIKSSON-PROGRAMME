1. ORIGINAL MODEL AND THE CLAIM THAT IS ACTUALLY PAID

Let n>=4 be even and retain279A's original counting-norm U=(im d1*)^2,
forward curl d1, checkerboard SOURCE weights H=cn^2,a=c/48,c>0 and full
restricted-form operator A_U, so Q(u)=<u,A_U u>. All spaces here are real;
unitary complex Fourier coordinates are used only to prove real identities.
At each x1=i take any tau_i in{0,...,n-1}, nonempty x2 intervals
[0,tau_i-1],{tau_i},[tau_i+1,n-1], and x3 cells{0},[1,n-1]. C is the FULL
original channel/spatial-component map with each cell sum normalized by
(n^3 |cell|)^(-1/2). No permutation, even-cut or motion assumption is needed
until Section6. These rows partition each original component's vertices.

Let N be the actual coexact fields supported only at diagonal frequencies
k=(t,t,t),t!=0. They are precisely vector profiles u(x)=v(x1+x2+x3)
with componentwise mean zero and spatial sum v1+v2+v3=0, separately in
each channel, by279's explicit curl preimage. Thus dim_R N=4(n-1).
Put O=U orthogonal-complement N. In279's notation L is the ENTIRE spectral
space below c, dim L=2(n-2), L subset N, and F=U orthogonal-complement L.
The true full-form high bound is A_F>=16c/3, and O subset F. Write C_N,
C_O,C_L,C_F for actual restrictions, never for changed observation rows.

The new geometric conclusions to be proved are
              range C_O = range(C|U) = range C_F,           (RANGE)
and the explicit dual estimate, for every real row vector alpha,
 ||C_N*alpha||^2 <= K_n ||C_O*alpha||^2,
              K_n = n^5/[16(n-3)].                         (ANGLE)
As a consequence there is a linear map R:N->O with
         C_O R=-C_N,             ||R||^2<=K_n.             (LIFT)
In particular EVERY prescribed low spectral part l has an exactly invisible
actual extension l+R l. This proves feasibility using the real cell geometry;
it does not assume surjectivity/full row rank or transfer it into a structure.
The lifting bound is polynomial and far too large to prove below-c energy.

2. THE LONG THIRD-COORDINATE CELL FORCES THE ANNIHILATOR

Let C_amb denote the same map on all ambient vector fields, P_U the counting
orthogonal projection onto U, and g=C_amb*alpha. Because the actual third-
coordinate partition is{0},[1,n-1], g(i,j,k) is independent of k for
1<=k<=n-1 at each fixed i,j. Normalization only changes its constant value.
All three components and both channels have this property, regardless of
how their x2 cut intervals change with i. Since U=im d1*, elementary
adjoint orthogonality gives U^perp=ker d1. Therefore
                     d1 g=d1 P_U g.                       (CURL)

Each component of d1 g is independent of k=x3 on1<=k<=n-2:
Delta1 and Delta2 differentiate two values from the same long x3 interval;
Delta3 g vanishes there. Consequently
           Delta3 d1 g=0 on the slab 1<=x3<=n-3.          (SLAB)
This is why n>=4 is required. It is a fact about the original adjoint rows,
not an assertion that P_U preserves cell means or long-cell constancy.

First suppose C_O*alpha=0. Then v=P_U g lies in N. Each component of
d1 v is a function of s=x1+x2+x3 alone. Independence of x3 at1 and2 in
(CURL) says its values at i+j+1 and i+j+2 are equal for every i,j. All
residues occur as i+j+1, so that component is constant. Its total sum is0
because it is a curl of periodic differences. Hence d1 v=0. But v lies
in U and ker d1=U^perp, so v=0. Thus ker C_O*=ker(C|U)*. Orthogonal
complements in the finite-dimensional row space prove the first equality
in(RANGE); O subset F subset U proves the second. This includes dependent
rows and any globally zero row combinations. No inverse has been assumed.

3. PAYING A QUANTITATIVE LIFT, RATHER THAN JUST RANGE NOTATION

For general alpha decompose v=P_U g=d+o in N direct-sum O. On the slab
of(SLAB), Delta3 d1 d=-Delta3 d1 o. The left side depends only on s, so
its squared counting norm is equally distributed among the n x3 slices.
It follows exactly that
 ||Delta3 d1 d||^2
   =n/(n-3)||Delta3 d1 d||^2_slab
   <=n/(n-3)||Delta3 d1 o||^2
   <=48n/(n-3)||o||^2.                                   (UPPER)
Indeed |g3|^2<=4 and the forward-curl squared symbol norm is at most
D=sum_j|gj|^2<=12 (unordered j<l counting forms, no factor two).

For d at a nonzero diagonal frequency put z=exp(2pi i t/n), gj=z-1.
Since d is actual coexact, its coefficient is perpendicular to(1,1,1).
The curl identity gives ||d1 dhat||^2=3|z-1|^2||dhat||^2; the extra
Delta3 factor gives3|z-1|^4||dhat||^2. For all t!=0,
 |z-1|^2>=4 sin^2(pi/n)>=16/n^2.
Parseval therefore yields
              ||Delta3 d1 d||^2>=768/n^4 ||d||^2.         (LOWER)
Combining(UPPER),(LOWER) gives(ANGLE) with the printed K_n, since
d=C_N*alpha,o=C_O*alpha. The Nyquist diagonal t=n/2 is included.

For completeness, the standard finite factorization step is explicit.
Define T on range C_O* by T(C_O*alpha)=C_N*alpha. Inequality(ANGLE)
makes T well-defined and bounds its norm by sqrt(K_n). Extend it by zero
on the orthogonal complement in O. Taking adjoints gives C_O T*=C_N.
Thus R=-T* proves(LIFT). This is the classical Douglas factorization
argument; the original-cell estimate(ANGLE), not that argument, is the
model-specific content. No actual observation or coexact domain is changed.

In particular for l in L, h=R l belongs to O subset F, cancels every
original mean, and has ||h||^2<=K_n||l||^2. Since b(x)<=H and the counting
gradient symbol satisfies D<=12, A_U<=12H I. Thus
 <h,(A_F-cI)h><=(12H-c)K_n||l||^2.                        (COSTUP)
This is an explicit finite upper bound on cancellation cost. It grows
like c n^6 and supplies NO subtarget witness. It is retained as a paid
but quantitatively inadequate bound, not advertised as a solution.

4. EXACT SHIFTED COST WITH ALL ROW DEPENDENCIES RETAINED

Let S=range(C|U) in the full row space, B=A_F-cI on F, and D_L=cI-A_L
on L. By279, B>=13c/3 I and 7c/8 I<=D_L<cI. Define
 M=C_F B^(-1) C_F* on the ORIGINAL full row space.
For any alpha, <alpha,M alpha>=||B^(-1/2)C_F*alpha||^2. Hence
ker M=ker C_F*=S^perp by(RANGE). Thus M is strictly positive on S,
has range S, and its Moore-Penrose inverse Mdagger is the ordinary inverse
on S and zero on S^perp. This proves precisely the allowed inverse domain;
no claim that all raw rows are independent is made.

For any l in L set y=C_L l, automatically in S, and
              f_*=-B^(-1)C_F* Mdagger y.                 (MINIMIZER)
Then C_F f_*=-M Mdagger y=-y. For another feasible f=f_*+h, C_F h=0,
and <h,B f_*>=-<C_F h,Mdagger y>=0. Thus the attained minimum is
 min_(f in F:C_F f=-C_L l) <f,B f> = <y,Mdagger y>.        (EXACTCOST)
This follows by direct completion of the square; it is not a supplied
Gram hypothesis. In particular it is bounded above by(COSTUP).

The original target on the whole invisible subspace is now equivalent to

Use(EXACTCOST). Conversely a negative direction of(CRITERION), combined

5. AN IMMEDIATE RANK CONSEQUENCE, WITHOUT A NEW RANK ASSUMPTION

(RANGE) implies P_L(ker(C|U))=L by the explicit extension l+R l.
So ker(C|U) contains a linear subspace of dimension2(n-2) whose low
projection is bijective onto L: the graph of R restricted to L. Its
vectors are generally not small-energy, by(COSTUP). One cannot discard
an entire low direction on the ground that its original means are nonzero.

can spend positive, or very large, strong energy. It does not reopen the
stopped projection/trace ansatz; no prescribed projected input is repaired.
