2. Improve the full-field upper without changing the observation map.

semidefinite and Q_A(y)<=M||y||^2 with M=4DH.
For any positive semidefinite finite form Q, polarization Cauchy–Schwarz

after taking the symmetric positive square root. Since
2sqrt(pq)<=p/2+2q for nonnegative p,q,
Q(x+y)>=Q(x)-2sqrt(Q(x)Q(y))+Q(y)>=Q(x)/2-Q(y).          (237.1)
For z=x+y,x=(I-R)z,y=Rz, this yields

K >= [3(D-1)/(8C_D)] c n^2.                            (237.6)
Together237.2 and237.6 show that the minimum normalized restoring penalty
