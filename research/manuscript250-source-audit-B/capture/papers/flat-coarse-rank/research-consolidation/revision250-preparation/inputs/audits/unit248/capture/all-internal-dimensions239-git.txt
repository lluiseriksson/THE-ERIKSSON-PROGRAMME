239 — Local rectangular rank for every internal dimension
Author-role analytical proof candidate. No execution, grade or priority claim.
This removes238's threshold g>=n^2; it does not change the coefficient mechanism.

1. Precise theorem and normalization.
Let D>=3, integer n>=2, ANY integer g>=1,c>0,V=n^D,h=Dg.
Keep the positive constant profile with v_alpha=1/sqrt(g),a=c/(16D),
H=cn^2,B=H I_g-(H-a)vv^T, and directional matrix
A_(j,k,alpha),(j',k',beta)=delta_jj' delta_kk' B_alpha,beta.
Q_A is the counting actual forward-gradient energy. The true domain is
U=U_0^g,U_0=im(d_1^*) for the flat forward curl and its counting adjoint,
d0=dim_R U_0=(D-1)(V-1). Internal coordinates are in the specified fixed basis.
For each internal channel take its own integer rectangular partition, shared
across D spatial components, listing all canonical component means
C_(alpha,k,S)z=V^(-1/2)|S|^(-1/2)sum_(i in S)z_(k,alpha)(i).
There is an explicit such C with full output count
N_out<=L_D[V+g V/n^2] and normalized coercivity
Q_A(z)/V+K||Cz||^2>=c||z||^2/V for ALL fields z,
with finite K=c+4DH=c(1+4Dn^2).
The minimum ACTUAL rank r=rank(C|_U) among restoring canonical maps is
Theta_D(V+g V/n^2), uniformly over all n>=2,g>=1.
No sharp penalty order for the newly covered g<n^2 range is claimed here.
The older238/237 sharp cn^2 assertion remains valid in their stated range.

2. Two ranges already supplied by proved constructions.
If g>=n^2,238 constructs all-size rectangles with N_out<=C_D g V/n^2
and full-field2c kernel margin, hence the stated penalty. The proof derives
the local Neumann gap and channel allocation for actual g, not g=V.
Since V<=gV/n^2, its count has the desired form.
If g<4096, use singleton partitions in EVERY channel. Their count DgV
is <=4095D V, and normalized K=c suffices since Q_A>=0. The larger common
K also suffices. These two cases cover their overlap consistently, including
g=1 and all small n. A bounded constant threshold does not conceal g growth.
It remains to construct the map when 4096<=g<n^2; necessarily n>sqrt(g)>=64.

3. Coarse bands plus ONE fine tail, not a rounded channel per fine scale.
Set s=sqrt(g), k=floor(log_2 s), so k>=6,2^k<=s<2^(k+1),2^k<n.
Let Pi_j be the Cartesian floor-quantile partitions of the n-torus for
j=0,...,k: endpoints floor(t n/2^j), omitting empty intervals. Let P_j
be counting block-average projections and L_j=ceil(n/2^j).
These partitions are nested, P_0 global averaging, and E_j=P_(j+1)-P_j
for0<=j<k are orthogonal projections. Set E_tail=I-P_k. Then E_tail
is orthogonal to every coarse E_j and sum_(j<k)E_j+E_tail=I-P_0.
The same elementary Neumann path/tensor argument as236/238 gives
||E_j w||^2<=(L_j^2/4)E(w) for j<k, and
||E_tail w||^2=||w-P_kw||^2<=(L_k^2/4)E(w).             (239.1)
The tail estimate is ONE parent-variance estimate, not the sum of all finer
band estimates. Therefore it carries no hidden log n charge. The finite
path spectrum4sin^2(pi t/(2L)) gives gap>=4/L^2; tensor the parent
rectangles and add only nonnegative inter-parent/wrap edges. No coefficient,
regional-gap assumption, changed domain or nonlocal observation is introduced.

Allocate r_j=ceil(5g L_j^2/(8n^2)) DISTINCT channels to child Pi_(j+1)
for j<k, and r_tail=ceil(5g L_k^2/(8n^2)) OTHER channels to SINGLETONS.
Every leftover channel receives the root. Coarse groups and tail group are
disjoint. Crucially, only the tail group uses finest singleton means.

4. Prove the revised integer budget fits.
Let m=k+1. For k>=6,2^(k-2)>=k+1 by induction (16>=7 at6, doubling
preserves the next inequality). Thus m<=s/4. The finite geometric bounds
give sum_(j=0)^k L_j^2<=4n^2/3+4n+m. Consequently S=sum_(j<k)r_j+r_tail
satisfies S/g<=5/6+5/(2n)+5m/(8n^2)+m/g.
Since n>s and m<=s/4, this is at most
5/6+93/(32s)<=5/6+93/2048<1.                            (239.2)
This includes EVERY ceiling and the tail's own integer allocation. Thus S<=g-1
and consecutive disjoint groups exist. Also L_k<=n/2^k+1 gives
g L_k^2/n^2 <=(s/2^k+s/n)^2 <(2+1)^2=9,
so r_tail=ceil(5g L_k^2/(8n^2))<=6.                     (239.3)
There is no assumption that g is square or dyadic; s is only a real bound.

5. Full-field kernel margin with each channel charged once.
Kernel partition sums imply every component of z has mean0. Split
f=sum_alpha v_alpha z_alpha,s_alpha=z_alpha-v_alpha f; the orthogonal norm
identity holds and Q_A(z)=aE(f)+T, T=H sum_alpha E(s_alpha).
For a coarse group, child means vanish, giving E_j s_alpha=-v_alpha E_j f.
For a tail channel z_alpha=0 everywhere, so E_tail s_alpha=-v_alpha E_tail f.
Use239.1 on the DISTINCT groups, with its appropriate parent bound, to obtain
T>=H sum_(j<k)[4r_j/(gL_j^2)]||E_j f||^2
    +H[4r_tail/(gL_k^2)]||E_tail f||^2
 >=(5/2)c[sum_(j<k)||E_j f||^2+||E_tail f||^2]=(5/2)c||f||^2.
Every strong channel energy is charged at most once, including the entire
fine tail as one term. Separately zero means and the labelled torus gap
4sin^2(pi/n)>=16/n^2 give T>=16c sum_alpha||s_alpha||^2.
The convex combination4/5 and1/5 proves Q_A>=2c||z||^2 in the FULL kernel.
For the full block projection R, ||Cz||^2=||Rz||^2/V. With x=(I-R)z,y=Rz,
PSD polarization and ||L_A||<=M=4DH give Q_A(z)>=Q_A(x)/2-Q_A(y)
>=c||x||^2-M||y||^2. Add(c+M)||Rz||^2 and divide by V. This proves
the stated all-field penalty, without assuming R preserves coexactness.

Together with Section2, choose

7. Actual lower bounds: retain both, even when g is fixed.
For any internally channel-separable linear map restoring on U with any
finite penalty Phi(0)=0,238's rederived general-g kernel/trace argument gives
r>=b_D g V/n^2, b_D=(D-1)/(4(1+8D)). It uses row-space projections
INSIDE U_0, so is valid for allg>=1 and not only the old g>=n^2 range.
A SECOND lower bound is necessary when g<n^2: T0f=(v_alpha f)_alpha is an
isometry from U_0 into U and Q_A(T0f)=aE(f)<=c||f||^2/4.
If C T0 f=0 for any nonzero f, restoration would force Q_A(T0f)>=c||f||^2,
a contradiction. Thus C T0 is injective and r>=d0>=(D-1)V/2.
Combining r>=a_D V,a_D=(D-1)/2 and r>=b_D g V/n^2 yields
r>=[min(a_D,b_D)/2](V+gV/n^2).                          (239.4)
These bounds apply to the canonical class, and Section6 supplies its actual
upper. Therefore both its minimum restoring rank and this successful explicit
map's actual rank have the claimed Theta_D(V+gV/n^2) order uniformly in g.

8. Boundaries and retained limitations.
The equalityg=V and thresholdg>=n^2 are both absent from this theorem. The
observation-class restriction, fixed internal basis, canonical weights, exact
positive constant coefficient family and finite actual flat coexact domain
remain essential. Full capped budget is still exactly
F=D^2V[1+(g-1)min(1,alpha_D n^(-D))]<=D^2(V+alpha_Dg),
so h+F=O_D(V+g); no tail multiplicity or budget regime is silently removed.
For fixed g this rank order is merely O_D(V), not a universal additive-rank
obstruction. The normalized sufficient penalty here is O_D(cn^2); its optimal
order for the new g<n^2 map has NOT been proved by this result. Arbitrary
