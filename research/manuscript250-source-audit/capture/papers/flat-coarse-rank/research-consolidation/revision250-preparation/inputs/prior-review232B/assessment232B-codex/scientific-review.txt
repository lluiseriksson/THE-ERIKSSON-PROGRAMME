IV. Component-adapted escape, Section 5
For actual component-block-diagonal coefficients the full spectrum is the disjoint union of the D-by-D block spectra. Applying the h=1 matrix theorem to each actual real component, then taking the maximum finite penalty, proves a_D*h+b_D*F_c. There is no spatially varying diagonalization of fields; that would change gradients.

On the identical family (36), only two real components are weak. All others have H, and 4*sin^2(pi/n)>=16/n^2, together with the displayed sigma/c bound, gives H*lambda_1>=c. Root means suffice for strong components on the full field space, and every root mean vanishes on U. The adapted restricted rank is therefore O_D(m), whereas common partitions require O(m^2). The proof first gives an all-field estimate and then restricts it; it does not pretend spatial components are independent coexact input spaces. It asserts neither an arbitrary coupled-matrix additive bound nor optimal adapted rank.

Young's inequality spends c of that margin to control the cross term, leaving c with K=c+(4DH)^2/c. A mere c-kernel margin would not justify this step; the present proof correctly uses 2c. With g=V, the upper actual rank O_D(n^(2D-2)) matches Appendix B. VII. Objections and limitations to preserve
1. Priority is unresolved. The primary-source portions checked genuinely contain the acknowledged ring and thresholded-tree/path mechanisms. Scalar completeness is not scalar novelty. I did not exhaustively search for predecessors of the coexact rank formula or coupled example, and cannot certify either is new. A specialist literature judgment remains appropriate before editorial priority claims.
2. Free finite penalties limit practical importance. Negative depth is paid by K and Appendix C allows a large K. Economical rank with arbitrarily poor conditioning is not a robust sensing or control guarantee. Useful penalty scales or applications would strengthen relevance; the paper's present honesty does not remove this limitation.
3. Growing g=V and the fixed internal basis are indispensable qualifications of the separable gap. Common and adapted results also concern different observation classes. No general mixed-observation obstruction, fixed-g impossibility or arbitrary coupled additive theorem is established.
4. The universal rank lower bound is sharp across an attaining family, whereas coefficient-profile results are only sharp in order on special families. There is no general per-profile rank formula, leading coefficient or global spectral-count identity.
5. No external physical/continuum producer or reduction is provided. These finite unit-spacing component-gradient results demonstrate no Yang–Mills or continuum mass-gap conclusion. This limits relevance without creating a request for speculative bridging.
6. Section 7's roughly two pages of workflow and predecessor-assessment history could move to an immutable supplement, retaining a concise AI disclosure and exact-artifact distinctions. Preserve all records and original verdict referents. Its explicitly dated source-freeze statements about unobserved future rendering are factually scoped, but a short present-artifact note would reduce confusion in this now-rendered draft.
7. Appendix C chooses a real eigenbasis by existence. This suffices mathematically, but reproducible execution would need tie-breaking at degenerate modes and an actual supplied implementation. No implementation or computational performance is established by this reading.
