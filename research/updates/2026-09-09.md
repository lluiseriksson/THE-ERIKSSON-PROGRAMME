## Source cards

### Efficient Quantum Simulations of Yang-Mills theory with Maximal-tree Gauge

- Source: [arXiv:2608.27267v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27267v1); Li, Tianyin, Li, Ying-Ying, Wang, Xiaoyang, Xing, Hongxi.
- Dates: first 2026-08-27; latest 2026-08-27. **background**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: v1: abstract and maximal-tree gauge construction.
- Reported result: Li and collaborators use maximal-tree gauge and field-amplitude digitization to obtain polynomial resource bounds for Hamiltonian Yang-Mills simulation through QSVT.
- Limits: Hamiltonian simulation complexity is distinct from an exact Haar edge-coordinate equivalence, Euclidean reflection positivity, and a continuum mass gap. First posted 27 August; a September news mention is not a new submission.

### Non-Abelian string melting and thermalization in an open lattice gauge theory

- Source: [arXiv:2609.04117v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04117v1); Verde, Erick Parra, Cataldi, Giovanni, Kühn, Stefan, Magnifico, Giuseppe, Halimeh, Jad C..
- Dates: first 2026-09-03; latest 2026-09-03. **new-in-trailing-week**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: v1: model, thermalization discussion and strong-dephasing reduction.
- Reported result: A tensor-network study couples 1+1D SU(2) gauge theory with matter to a gauge-preserving Lindblad bath. It finds nonmonotonic relaxation and derives an exclusion-process description in a strong-dephasing limit.
- Limits: This is an open-system finite/truncated lattice setting, not pure 4D Euclidean Yang-Mills. A Liouvillian relaxation rate is not automatically a Hamiltonian gap, and the strong-dephasing expansion has its own regime.

### Uniform Partition-Function Estimates for Coulomb Modulated Energy at All Positive Temperatures

- Source: [arXiv:2609.00867v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00867v1); Wang, Zhenfu, Zhao, Xianliang.
- Dates: first 2026-09-01; latest 2026-09-01. **background**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: Abstract and submission history.
- Reported result: Wang and Zhao report particle-number-uniform partition-function bounds for centered Coulomb-type modulated energies in a locally square-integrable regime, with a second-chaos Fredholm-determinant limit.
- Limits: Positive-real-parameter integrability does not establish complex zero-freeness. Particle-number uniformity is not automatically RG-scale, support or volume uniformity in the Yang-Mills activity norm. First posted 1 September.

### The prescribed Hermitian-Yang-Mills flow I

- Source: [arXiv:2606.21062v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21062v2); Xiong, Zhiyao, Yang, Xaokui, Yau, Shing-Tung.
- Dates: first 2026-06-19; latest 2026-09-05. **revised-in-trailing-week**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: Abstract and submission history.
- Reported result: The prescribed Hermitian-Yang-Mills flow paper establishes convergence for a class of prescribed Hermitian tensors; a parabolic comparison principle supplies the uniform C0 estimate.
- Limits: This is a flow of Hermitian metrics on holomorphic bundles, not Wilson lattice gradient flow. Read the precise tensor and manifold assumptions before importing a comparison principle. Version 2 is dated 5 September; version 1 is from June.

### On Quasiparticles within the Refined Gribov-Zwanziger Model

- Source: [arXiv:2609.04423v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04423v1); Garcia, Felipe F., Capri, Marcio A. L., Mintz, Bruno W..
- Dates: first 2026-09-03; latest 2026-09-03. **new-in-trailing-week**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: Abstract and submission history.
- Reported result: The refined Gribov-Zwanziger effective model is reexamined through PT symmetry, with a proposed interpretation of quadratic excitations in the real-mass-pole case.
- Limits: An effective gauge-fixed propagator and real poles do not establish reflection positivity for gauge-invariant Wilson observables, OS reconstruction, or a physical mass gap.
