# Quantifier consequence for consolidation — analytic pending formal family endpoint
For fixed d, Nc and c>0, the cutoff-existence endpoint selects one k>=3. For every integer n>=k and every m>=1, the explicit nonnegative profile with amplitude (m-1)n^(d+2) has exact directional positive-total mean m. If the original local nonlinear assumptions hold with an observation space F, the candidate finite-tail theorem forces
n^(d+2) <= k^(d+2) (dim F + (d+2)^2 (Nc^2-1)).
Consequently a family with fixed positive c and bounded observation dimension cannot satisfy these assumptions for all sufficiently large n for this profile. Radii, delta and penalty constants may vary with n; the finite-tail proof does not require their uniformity. This consequence follows by unboundedness of n^(d+2), since d+2>=2, but is not a separately checked Lean family theorem here. It refutes a proposed uniform-in-profile rank bound inferred from mean alone; it does not refute the infimum-over-profiles mean-budget theorem. No independent novelty claim; consolidate with the same existing paper dossier.
