[arXiv:1410.2798](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1410.2798), introduction (printedp1)
and Proposition4 (printedp10, PDF index9), as served on2026-10-31.

volume and explains why the initial cube bounds do not suffice there.

establishing positivity. Our actual flat divergence-zero sector and arbitrary
finite-output observation are a specified different set of hypotheses. No
proof identifies all of Dimock's constraints or operator scaling with these
hypotheses, and no contradiction to his result is claimed.

The source confirms substantial classical context, not originality of our
exact statement or independent certification of its Lean proof. This was a
focused scope check, not an exhaustive priority search. Integer selection,
Fourier counting and rank arguments are classical. Original scientific
priority remains unestablished; this unit provides scoped formal transport
