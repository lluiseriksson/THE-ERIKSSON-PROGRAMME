1. Statement and actual objects

Fix D>=3, h>=1, n>=2, p=D/2, V=n^D, r=D*h. Let X=(Z/nZ)^D with
the COUNTING inner product. A real cochain is u:X -> R^h. Write
du(x)=(u(x+e_i)-u(x))_(i=1,...,D) in R^r. At each actual vertex x let
A(x) be ANY real symmetric positive definite r-by-r matrix. In particular,
different directions and components may mix, and A(x),A(y) need not commute.
Set

 Q_A(u)=sum_x du(x)^T A(x)du(x),   L_A=d* M_A d,
 alpha_D=(D/2)*(D/(D-2))^(D-1)*(3/4)^D.

For every c>0, the ordinary real multiplicity satisfies

 N_[0,c)(L_A) <= h+alpha_D*c^p*sum_x tr(A(x)^(-p)).                 (1)

Consequently there is an actual real linear observation C retaining precisely
the eigenvectors of L_A with eigenvalue<c, such that

 Q_A(u)+c*||C u||^2 >= c*||u||^2,     dim F=N_[0,c)(L_A).        (2)

C(0)=0 and Phi(y)=c||y||^2. No bound on this spectral subspace is assumed.
For n=1, d=0 and C is the identity on R^h; dim F=h.

Under the genuine distribution hypothesis

 (1/(V*r))*sum_x tr((m*A(x)^(-1))^p)<=M,  m>=1,                (3)

one obtains the original order max(1,(n/sqrt(1+m))^D), with coefficient
h+2^p*alpha_D*c^p*r*M. Condition (3) is additional: neither an arithmetic
matrix mean nor positivity alone proves it. There is no assumption that
A(x)>=m*I, no scalar profile supplied in a structure, and no simultaneous
diagonalization. The pointwise SPD premise remains essential to this proof.

For the actual finite flat cochain application take h=D*(Nc^2-1). This
statement is about the defined gradient quadratic form. An interacting
covariant Hessian or a differently weighted curl/divergence form is NOT
identified with Q_A without a separate dictionary/equality proof.

2. Exact classical input and finite normalization

Rupert L. Frank, Cwikel's theorem and the CLR inequality, arXiv:1206.3325v1,
Section3.1, Theorem3.1:
https://arxiv.org/html/1206.3325v1#S3.SS1

Use its general-measure-space, operator-valued estimate at q=D>2. With
counting measures, the r-component unitary finite Fourier transform F_r
has L1(l2^r)->Linfinity(l2^r) norm V^(-1/2), independently of r. The
theorem therefore gives, for matrix-valued multipliers f(x),G(k),

 N_s(M_f F_r* M_G)
 <= [D/2*(D/(D-2))^(D-1)]*V^(-1)*s^(-D)
    *sum_x tr(|f(x)|^D)*sup_(t>0) t^D*#{k:||G(k)||>t}.         (4)

N_s counts singular values STRICTLY above s. This is the weak Schatten
counting convention, not a normalized trace or a strong Schatten norm.
Finite sets are sigma-finite measure spaces, and all fibre spaces here are
finite dimensional. Thus this is a direct application of the stated primary
theorem, not an unproved continuum-to-lattice analogy. We credit the full
operator-valued mechanism to Frank and its cited predecessors.

3. Actual Fourier multiplier, including its rectangular shape and zeros

On mean-zero h-component fields H0, L0=d*d is invertible. Its symbol is
lambda(k)=4*sum_i sin(pi*k_i/n)^2, and J=d L0^(-1) has symbol
J(k):v -> (z_i(k)*v/lambda(k))_i, z_i=exp(2*pi*i*k_i/n)-1.
Set J(0)=0. This is r-by-h and ||J(k)||=lambda(k)^(-1/2) for k!=0.

Let I:R^h->R^r embed in the first h coordinates. Define the SQUARE
r-by-r G(k)=J(k) I*, so G(0)=0 and ||G(k)||=||J(k)||. Then
J=F_r* M_G F_r I on fields (I acts pointwise), including the constant
mode as zero. This padding is explicit; no nonsquare Fourier unitary is
silently inserted into the classical theorem.

Choose centered integer frequency representatives b_i, with one representative
at the even-n endpoint. Concavity of sine gives lambda(k)>=16*|b|^2/n^2.
If ||G(k)||>t, then 0<|b|<R=n/(4t). When R<=1 there is no such frequency;
when R>1 the number is at most (2 floor(R)+1)^D <=(3R)^D. Thus

 sup_(t>0) t^D*#{k:||G(k)||>t} <=(3/4)^D*V.                  (5)

The operator norm here has NO fibre-multiplicity factor; the trace moment
in (4) already accounts for the matrix fibre. The V in (5) cancels the
V^(-1) from the Fourier L1->Linfinity norm, exactly once.

4. Actual inverse comparison, with noncommuting coefficients

For b in H0, let v=L_A^(-1)b and j_*=M_A d v. Then d*j_*=b.
For any j with d*j=b, put k=j-j_*. Because d*k=0,

 <j,M_A^(-1)j>=<b,L_A^(-1)b>+<k,M_A^(-1)k>.

Indeed the cross term is <d v,k>=<v,d*k>=0. This proves the minimizing
identity without commuting A with d or the Fourier transform. Taking the
explicit feasible flow j=Jb yields, on H0,

 L_A^(-1) <= J* M_A^(-1) J = B*B,  B=M_A^(-1/2)J.           (6)

By (4)-(5) at f(x)=A(x)^(-1/2),

 N_s(M_A^(-1/2)F_r* M_G)
 <=alpha_D*s^(-D)*sum_x tr(A(x)^(-D/2)).                    (7)

The remaining factor F_r I is an isometry, so restricting the domain cannot
increase any singular-value count. The same bound holds for B. Apply finite
min-max to (6), at the inverse threshold 1/c, to count eigenvalues of L_A
STRICTLY below c on H0. The kernel of L_A consists of the h constant fields:
SPD forces du=0 and the torus is connected. Add these h modes to obtain (1).
Real symmetry ensures that complexification does not double multiplicities.
Choosing the actual spectral coordinates proves (2). Inserting (3), and
(1+m)/m<=2, proves the asserted mean-budget order. No locality is inferred.

5. What is improved, and what is not

The old scalar case A(x)=a(x)I_r is recovered, with a safe dimension-dependent
constant. The new family admits cross-direction and cross-component terms,
including variable eigenspaces: scalar shared weights are no longer required.
As a simple noncommuting admissible example, on a two-dimensional fibre block
choose at two sites A0=diag(1,4), A1=[[2,-1],[-1,2]], and identity on all
remaining directions. Their commutator is [[0,3],[-3,0]], while both are SPD.
No common diagonalizing basis exists. Negative cross entries do not make a
quadratic form indefinite.

This DOES NOT cover the indefinite signed forms of units155/156: their
negative directions violate SPD. It does not prove a local rectangular mean
construction, eliminate the inverse-moment premise, or change PR29's 20/41
and TermSource=0. The original positive critical rectangular question stays

derivation creates no second ongoing question. The classical mechanism and
