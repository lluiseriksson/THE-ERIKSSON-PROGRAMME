THE EXACT RANK PRICE OF NONPOSITIVE MATRIX CHANNELS

Let all actual finite gradient objects D>=3,h,n,d,L_A be as in unit158,
but now allow EVERY real symmetric fibre matrix A(x), including zero and
negative eigenvalues. Write P(x)=1_{(-infinity,0]}(A(x)), P=directsum_x P(x),
R=rank(P d), nu=sum_x rank P(x), and

 S_p=sum_x sum_(positive eigenvalues lambda of A(x)) lambda^(-p), p=D/2.

An empty positive sum is zero. In particular this is NOT tr of the
pseudoinverse raised to p when negative eigenvalues are present.
For c>0, with the SAME alpha_D as unit158,

 N_(-infinity,c)(L_A) <= h+R+alpha_D*c^p*S_p.                 (1)

This counts negative and zero modes as well as positive modes below c.
It is not N_[0,c) after silently discarding negative energy. The cost R is
computed from actual coefficient eigenspaces and actual incidence d, not
supplied as a coercivity certificate. R<=nu is an optional weaker bound.

Proof. Replace every nonpositive eigenvalue of A(x) by T>0, retaining all
positive eigenvalues; call the resulting SPD matrix A_T(x). Functional
calculus on each fibre gives

 L_(A_T)-L_A=d* P (T I-A) P d >=0,
 rank(L_(A_T)-L_A)=rank(P d)=R.

The equality follows because T I-A is strictly positive on ran P, so its
square root is invertible there. This does not require A at different
vertices, d or Fourier multipliers to commute. Kernel of the update is
ker(P d).

For any symmetric matrices H and B with rank(B-H)<=R, finite min-max gives
N_<c(H)<=N_<c(B)+R: intersect the low spectral subspace of H with ker(B-H)
and with the complement of the low spectral subspace of B. A nonzero vector
in this intersection would have H-quadratic form strictly below c||u||^2
and B-quadratic form at least c||u||^2, while they are equal. The codimension
estimate proves the claim; no diagonalization of H and B together is used.

Apply this with B=L_(A_T), then the proved unit158 SPD adapter:

 N_<c(L_A)<=h+R+alpha_D*c^p*(S_p+nu*T^(-p)).

The left side, R and S_p are independent of T. Taking T to infinity proves
(1); no convergence of eigenspaces or exchange of limits in a spectrum is
required. For n=1, d=0,R=0 and the exact answer is h.

Actual observation and nonlinear scope. Choose real orthonormal eigenvectors
of L_A with lambda<c. Let C retain these coefficients and set
Phi(y)=sum_(lambda<c)(c-lambda)y_lambda^2. Then Phi>=0 and

 Q_A(u)+Phi(Cu)>=c||u||^2,
 Phi(y)<=max_(lambda<c)(c-lambda)||y||^2.

If no low mode exists take the zero output. The penalty constant is finite
on each finite lattice; it is NOT claimed uniform for indefinite families.
In fact with negative lambda the old choice Phi=c||y||^2 is insufficient.
The correction must be stated rather than inherited from the SPD theorem.

An explicit uniformity criterion is available, not a carried spectral norm:
put b=max_x ||A_-(x)||. The elementary directed-edge estimate
||du||^2<=4D||u||^2 proves L_A>=-4Db I, hence the displayed penalty has
K<=c+4Db. Thus if the nonpositive derivative rank R has the target order,
the positive trace inverse moment has that order, and b is bounded across
the family, BOTH observation rank and the penalty coefficient are controlled
uniformly. These three substantive conditions remain; none is inferred from
the arithmetic mean or frozen compensation. The incidence is unscaled: with
physical derivatives n*d the last estimate acquires n^2 and must be restated.

Sharpness of the defect coefficient. Take A(x)=0 for all x. Then L_A=0,
S_p=0, P=I, R=rank(d)=h(V-1), so N_<c=hV=h+R. The same equality holds
for A(x)=-I: L_A=-L0 is nonpositive. Thus the coefficient1 of R cannot
be replaced by any smaller universal number. These examples show exactly
why deleting positivity at no cost is false. They also force full observation
rank under any differentiable-at-zero observation with locally quadratically
bounded penalty, by the existing derivative-kernel dimension argument.
