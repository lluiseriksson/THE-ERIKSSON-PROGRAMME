2. Exact inertia reduction, including dependencies and zero eigenvalues

Write N0=h(V-1). The symmetric block matrix

on the mean-zero domain plus R^q has two legitimate Schur complements.
Eliminate W^-1>0: inertia(M)=inertia(H|H0)+(q,0,0).
Eliminate mL0>0: inertia(M)=(N0,0,0)+inertia(W^-1-Gamma/m).
The second reduced matrix is C/m and has the same inertia as C. Therefore,
using order (positive,negative,zero),

 n_-(H)=n_-(C),
 n_0(H)=h+n_0(C),
 n_+(H)=N0-q+n_+(C).                                     (2)

The block eliminations are congruences: for [[A,B],[B*,D]] with invertible A,
multiply by [[I,-A^-1 B],[0,I]] and its transpose to obtain
diag(A,D-B*A^-1B). This explicitly proves the inertia step. No row
independence, disjoint-cycle, simultaneous diagonalization or nonsingular H
premise is required. C is constructed from actual coefficients and incidence;
checking it is not a promise that it is positive.

In particular H is PSD iff C is PSD. H is PSD with exactly the h constant

finite form equality and block algebra. For q=1 this recovers162's sharp
