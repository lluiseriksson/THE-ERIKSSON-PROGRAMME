EXACT RECTANGULAR PENALTY AND THE COEXACT CRITICAL RESIDUE

1. General finite residue lemma (classical Schur/resolvent mechanism)
Let L=L* act on a finite real Hilbert space E, let C:E->R^q be a
coisometry, and let K=ker C, O=ran C*. Suppose the smallest eigenvalue
of the actual compression L_K is c*>0. Write
 L-c*I=[[A0,X],[X*,B0]] on K direct-sum O,
with A0>=0 and let P be the orthogonal projection onto ker A0.
For delta>0 and c=c*-delta>0 the minimum nonnegative k in
 L+kC*C >= cI
is exactly
 k(delta)=max{0,lambda_max(X*(A0+delta I)^-1 X-B0-delta I)}.
Consequently
 lim_(delta->0+) delta k(delta)=||PX||^2.
If PX!=0, there is no finite penalty at c* and
 k(delta)=||PX||^2/delta+O(1).
If PX=0, penalties remain bounded and converge to the finite critical
minimum max{0,lambda_max(X*A0^dagger X-B0)}.

Proof. Complete the square on the strictly positive kernel block
A0+delta I, as in165. Let A+ denote A0 restricted to (ker A0)^perp.
Its smallest eigenvalue is positive unless that space is zero, in
which case the following remainder is simply zero. Spectral splitting
gives the EXACT identity
 (A0+delta I)^-1=delta^-1 P+(A++delta I)^-1(I-P).
The second term is uniformly bounded and converges to A0^dagger.
Multiplication on both sides by X*,X leaves the leading positive matrix
delta^-1 X*PX and a bounded self-adjoint remainder. The variational
formula for lambda_max gives an error bounded by the norm of that
remainder. Its leading largest eigenvalue is ||PX||^2. If PX=0 the
whole matrix instead converges to X*A0^dagger X-B0. Continuity of the
largest eigenvalue and max{0,.} proves all claims. The impossibility
at c* with PX!=0 is the critical-kernel compatibility criterion165.
This is a finite-dimensional application of classical tools, not a
claim of a new general Schur theorem or independent original priority.
