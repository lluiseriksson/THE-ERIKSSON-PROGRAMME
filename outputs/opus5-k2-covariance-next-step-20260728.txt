# Design verdict up front

Everything about the δ‑tail collapses to **one budget identity**. If Y(·,t) is holomorphic and bounded by M on the disk |δ| ≤ ρ, then with q = δ_max/ρ = 10⁻³/ρ:

```
|Y(δ) − Σ_{k≤5} Y_k δ^k|  ≤  M · q⁶/(1−q)          for all 0 ≤ δ ≤ 10⁻³
```

With margin ~0.1 and a deliberately crude M = 10⁴ this needs only **ρ ≥ 7·10⁻³**. Your measured coefficients (Y₃≈2.85, Y₄≈11.58, Y₅≈57.48 → ratios 4.06, 4.96) predict a true singularity near |δ| ≈ 0.2, i.e. **~30× more radius than required**. So the tail is not a hard estimate; it is a *zero‑freeness* certificate. That reframing is the whole recommendation, and it also settles the 8·c_min pain: a truncation charge η on R enters the direct route as 8c_min·η/δ but enters the disk route as 8c_min·η/ρ times q⁶/(1−q), a relief factor q⁷/(1−q) ≈ 10⁻⁷ at q = 0.1.

## Route 1 (rank 1) — complex‑δ disk, Cauchy, on the covariance‑normalised form

**Owns: the δ‑remainder.** Consumes KD > 0 as an input.

**1. Rearrangement.** Never form 4(KD·HDF − KF·HDD)/(δ·KD²). Push the δ‑cancellation *inside the integral* and drop 1/δ from the outside entirely:

```
Φ(x,δ) = ((H/K)D − 1/(4c))/δ ,   A = F/D ,   w_δ = K·D
S_f(δ) = ∫_B f·w_δ dx + (exterior)
Y(δ) = 4·(S_{AΦ}S_1 − S_A S_Φ) / S_1²
```

This is your identity with the 1/δ absorbed pointwise; the mandatory 4 is preserved and all four S are O(1) full moments with no 0/0.

**2. Remainder inequality — nothing is differentiated.**

```
Y(δ) − Σ_{k≤5}Y_kδ^k = (δ⁶/2πi) ∮_{|w|=ρ} Y(w) dw / (w⁶(w−δ))  ≤  M q⁶/(1−q)
```

Cauchy's inequality is precisely the legal device that manufactures all‑order derivative control from a **sup of the function itself**. No |·| is ever differentiated, and no cumulant combinatorics appears.

**3. Killing the dependency blowup — and killing the complex quadrature.** There is no coefficientwise arithmetic anywhere: heads are your exact closed forms, tail is one scalar. Better, **M requires no complex integration**. From pointwise additive majorants |X(x,δ) − X(x,0)| ≤ ρ·Λ_X(x) for X ∈ {K,D,H,F}, impose the **½‑annulus condition** ρΛ_X(x) ≤ ½X(x,0) for X ∈ {K,D}. Then pointwise |X/X₀| ∈ [½,3/2] and |arg(X/X₀)| ≤ π/6, hence |arg(w_δ/w₀)| ≤ π/3 and

```
Re S_1(δ) ≥ ¼·cos(π/3)·S_1(0) − tail = ⅛ S_1(0) − tail        (lower bound on |S_1|)
|S_f(δ)|  ≤ (3/2)² ∫_B |f|_max(ρ) w₀ dx + tail                  (upper bounds)
M ≤ 4(S̄_{AΦ}S̄_1 + S̄_A S̄_Φ)/S_1(0)²·64·(9/4)²
```

Every quantity fed to M is a **real, positive, δ=0‑weighted integral with no cancellation**, needed only as a one‑sided bound — grid 12 suffices where the head needs grid 48. Note the additive (not logarithmic) majorants avoid the circularity of assuming |∂_δ log X| ≤ S on a disk where X's zero‑freeness is what you are proving.

**4. Finite certificate data, per (coarse) t‑cell.** (i) Λ_K, Λ_D, Λ_H, Λ_F as closed‑form Bessel majorants (via I_ν′ = (I_{ν−1}+I_{ν+1})/2); (ii) one ρ(t) from the ½‑annulus predicate, verified as a boolean on an x‑cover with t as an interval; (iii) five real enclosures S_1(0) two‑sided, S̄_A, S̄_Φ, S̄_{AΦ} upper only; (iv) exterior + Bessel‑truncation majorants **re‑derived uniformly on |δ| ≤ ρ**; (v) the closed‑form M q⁶/(1−q). Head and tail t‑covers decouple: the heads are 1‑D closed forms and can be swept finely; the tail needs 2‑D work only on a coarse cover.

**5. Likely fatal obstruction.** A = F/D requires D zero‑free on B, and the ½‑annulus condition requires inf_B D(x,0) > 0 relative to ρΛ_D. If D has a zero or near‑zero anywhere in [0,12]² (corners, or the region the exterior splice touches), ρ is forced to 0 there and the route dies locally. Second candidate: if δ tunes a *decay rate*, the disk crosses into a divergent half‑plane and the exterior majorant has no complex continuation.

## Route 2 (rank 2) — real‑axis Taylor model with pointwise δ‑cancellation

**Owns: the fallback if analyticity is one‑sided, and the mandated independent second witness.**

**1. Rearrangement.** Same Φ, but build TM₅ of Φ *pointwise* from proved φ₀…φ₅ plus remainder; integrate to get TM₅ of each S; then TM‑divide by S_1² as S_1(0)⁻²(1+u)⁻² with u small, expanded as an explicitly truncated geometric reciprocal (per your class rule 8 — no `sympy.series`).

**2. Inequality.** Integral‑form Taylor remainder, δ⁶/5! · ∫₀¹(1−s)⁵ ∂_δ⁶(integrand)(sδ) ds, with |·| taken **after** differentiating the exact analytic integrand and **before** integrating a positive majorant. Order of operations is the whole legality argument.

**3. Blowup control.** Two exact, free moves: (a) the pointwise cancellation above — the 4.9e8 at grid 12 is the signature of cancelling *after* integration, where any additive moment error is amplified by 1/δ = 10³ without bound as δ↓0; (b) **exact re‑centering**, A → A − a₀, Φ → Φ − g₀ with rational a₀,g₀ from a numerical pre‑pass. Covariance is shift‑invariant exactly, so S_{AΦ}S_1 − S_AS_Φ stops being a difference of large near‑equal numbers.

**4. Data.** ~24 coefficient integrals ∫φ_k A^j w₀ per t‑cell, plus one majorant for sup_{B×[0,10⁻³]} |∂_δ⁶Φ|.

**5. Fatal obstruction.** ∂_δ⁶ of the quotient (H/K)D needs Faà di Bruno on a reciprocal; naive Leibniz on 1/K produces k!·C^k with C set by inf K, and if that C is what Route 1's ρ would have been, you get the *same* estimate done by hand and lossier. This route is dominated unless complex continuation genuinely fails.

## Route 3 (rank 3) — probability/covariance, **retargeted**

Do not use this for the δ‑tail; it cannot beat q⁶. Use it for **KD > 0** and to convert the 0.29‑vs‑0.37 head margin into headroom.

**1./3. The sharp fix for 0.29.** Law of total covariance with the **weighted L²(P_c) projection onto affines**, not the affine interpolant:

```
Cov(A,G) = Σ_c w_c Cov_c(A,G) + Cov(Ā_c, Ḡ_c)
Cov_c(A,G) = Cov_c(ℓ_A,ℓ_G) + Cov_c(e_A,e_G)      (cross terms ≡ 0 by orthogonality)
```

Cov_c(ℓ_A,ℓ_G) is *exact* from the cell's weighted moments (∫_c w, ∫_c x_i w, ∫_c x_i x_j w — positive integrals, stable). Grüss is then applied only to residuals with Δ(e) ≲ h²‖∇²‖/8, i.e. the within‑part drops from O(h²) to **O(h⁴)** — plausibly ~300× at h = 0.25 — and the Grüss factor ¼ is applied where it is actually sharp. Residual error is then dominated by O(N) aggregation of per‑cell quadrature widths, which wants **analyticity‑based Chebyshev/Bernstein cells with one shared global modulus majorant**, not more cells and not higher real derivatives.

**2.** Grüss/Cauchy–Schwarz on residuals only; no differentiation of bounds.

**4.** Per cell: weight moments up to order 2, the 2×2 moment matrix, Hessian majorants for A and Φ. For KD > 0: box lower bound vs. exterior tail majorant, one scalar per t‑cell.

**5. Fatal obstruction — and an audit fork.** The projection constants involve the inverse cell moment matrix; cells where w concentrates at a corner are near‑degenerate and the bound blows up. More seriously: **if the KD integrand is not pointwise positive on B**, P_δ is a signed measure, and Grüss and Cauchy–Schwarz are simply inapplicable — the 0.29 number would then be unsound, not merely loose. Settle this fork before spending anything else here.

## Highest‑value single lemma to attempt first

**Does the δ = 0 identity R ≡ 1/(4c) survive Bessel truncation?** If R_N|_{δ=0} = 1/(4c) exactly at truncation order N, every truncation charge acquires an explicit factor δ, the 1/δ amplification vanishes, and the 8·c_min correction is neutralised in *all three* routes simultaneously. If it survives only as 1/(4c) + η_N, the fallback is to absorb η_N into the head and charge only the difference. This is a cheap check with disproportionate payoff.

## Steps requiring genuinely new analytic lemmas (flagged, not assumed)

- **L1** Holomorphy of S_f on |δ| ≤ ρ: pointwise continuation of the integrand *plus* a δ‑uniform integrable majorant on the exterior (Morera + Fubini). Small but real.
- **L2** Explicit Λ_K, Λ_D, Λ_H, Λ_F. Mechanical, must be written and committed.
- **L3** The truncation‑identity lemma above.
- **L4** The t ↓ 0 endpoint: the box must grow or the kernel be rescaled. Route this through your existing sinc lemma on (0, t*(β)] rather than re‑deriving; the δ‑tail must inherit that cover, not invent one.
- **L5** inf_B D(x,0) > 0 (or an independent bound on A). Route 1 and Route 3 both die without it.

## Falsification diagnostic

Certify ρ first. If the ½‑annulus predicate returns ρ ≪ 10⁻² while the empirical coefficient growth still says ρ_true ≈ 0.2, the majorants Λ are lossy — **repair Λ, do not change route**. If instead ρ_true itself is near 10⁻³, the expansion in δ is not the right object at all and the head/tail split should be abandoned in favour of a uniform‑in‑δ enclosure.

I have not verified any of the closed forms, the measured radii, or the sign structure of D and KD against the repo; the above is architecture conditional on your stated measurements.
