\textbf{Publication corrected.}\par
This note replaces the title and headline framing of ai.viXra:2602.0038.  The
preserved version 2 manuscript follows this page unchanged.

\textbf{Exact claim retained.}\par
For the simplified quadratic Gribov--Zwanziger lattice measure defined in the
preserved manuscript, the zero-momentum propagator obeys the stated
volume-uniform bound, and its thermodynamic-limit value is computed.  The
result is a propagator bound for that simplified measure.

\textbf{Claim withdrawn from the title.}\par
Finiteness of $D(0)$ is only the necessary condition identified in Definition 4.
It does not by itself prove exponential clustering, a transfer-operator spectral
gap, a mass gap for the simplified measure, or a mass gap for the Wilson
Yang--Mills theory.  Version 2 already says this in its abstract, introduction
and Section 5.4; the old title nevertheless continued to advertise ``Mass Gap''
and ``A Non-Perturbative Proof''.  Those words are withdrawn.

\textbf{Evidence class.}\par
The analytic theorem is confined to the stated quadratic model.  Numerical
checks reported by the manuscript are supporting, author-reported checks and
are not promoted here to an independent certificate.  No continuum-limit or
Clay-problem conclusion is claimed.

\textbf{Provenance.}\par
Pages 2--11 of this replacement reproduce the ten pages of public version 2
with identical rendered content and unchanged page-content streams.  They remain available to preserve the
full correction history of version 1 and version 2.  Where their old title
conflicts with this page, this scope correction controls.
