## Reading status labels

**Recorded core result** means a theorem within the stated import closure and
checkpoint. **Research branch** means the work is not integrated into `main`.
**Cold-sealed** refers to the exact fresh-checkout gate cited by the research
record. **Diagnostic PASS** is limited to the diagnostic actually run.
**PRE-VALIDATION** and **open** are not proof claims.

For hRpoly, use the [September snapshot](HRPOLY-STATUS.md) before older campaign
plans. No continuum construction or Clay solution is claimed; the historical
distance shorthand remains **~0% (<0.1%)**.
