Date: 2026-07-23
Status: candidate-only; no promotion to G2 or G6

## Frozen attempt

The high-order CWIN3/2 probe was run for the previously uncovered beta slice

\[
  \beta\in[3259/32,3261/32]=[101.84375,101.90625].
\]

The command used the existing production certifier
`scripts/certify_bulk_beta_taylor_scaled_sign_rows_cwin3p2_high.py` with
`--unit gap_101p84375_101p90625`, order-30 Taylor data, and the prescribed
high-precision settings.  It ran for approximately 558 seconds and stopped
without emitting a certificate:

```
RuntimeError: bulk failure near t=3.125678020954915
```

The failure is inside the requested interval, before a complete row cover was
constructed.  Therefore the attempted slice supplies no admissible rows and
does not alter the beta-union topology.

## Independent gate result

`beta_union_complete: false`, a terminal gap ending at `1000/9`,
`relay_status: RELAY_LEMMA_UNPROVED`, and `promotion: NONE`.  The Cauchy-budget
audit independently reports `M_SUPREMUM UNSUPPLIED` and
`NO_H_TAIL_PROMOTION`.

This incident is evidence against treating a narrow sign probe as a closure
mechanism.  It does not bear on the truth of the Surface Theorem, and it does
not justify changing G2, G6, or the manuscript's terminal banner.
