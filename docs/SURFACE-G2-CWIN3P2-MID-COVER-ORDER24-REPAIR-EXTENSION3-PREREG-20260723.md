**Status:** preregistered extension; quarantined evidence only.

This extension covers beta units `[255/4,64]` and `[64,257/4]` after the
order-22 route. The frozen contract is `CWIN=3/2`, beta Taylor order 24,
t order 25, 180-bit Arb precision, beta width `1/4`, and minimum t width
`1/100000`. Fresh production/replay transcripts must be byte-identical.
