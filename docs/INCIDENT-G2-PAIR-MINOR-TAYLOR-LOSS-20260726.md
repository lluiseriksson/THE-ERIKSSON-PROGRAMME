# Pair-minor evaluator still loses the bulk scale (2026-07-26)

The exact Wronskian pair identity was tested as a replacement for the global
product subtraction,

```text
W = 2 * sum_{m<n} (a_m b_n-a_n b_m)
              * ((m+n) sin((n-m)t) - (n-m) sin((m+n)t)).
```

At the difficult stress point `beta=110`, `t=3.12225`, a 200-digit
independent multiprecision evaluation gives

```text
W = -2.025434492090947...e-116.
```

The direct Arb pair sum, with beta fixed exactly, 500-bit arithmetic, 220
modes, and a t-box of width `1e-8`, gives an outward enclosure approximately

```text
[-6.3793076793e-17, +6.3793076548e-17].
```

The individual Bessel minors are tight; the loss is generated by independent
interval trigonometric widths across the pair sum.  Consequently, replacing
the product subtraction by pair minors does not solve the bulk problem.  A
successful judge must factor the positive denominator/edge scale or carry a
shared t-Taylor/covariance representation all the way through.

This is a design obstruction only.  It carries no G2, K2, K4, S1'''/S2''', or
G6 promotion and does not contradict the Surface Theorem.
