the independent validator and the relay audit accept it.

## Fixed lane

* beta target: `[853/8,1000/9]`;
* `CWIN=3/2`, beta Taylor order 40, t Taylor order 50;
* Arb precision 300 bits, `MIN_DT=1/100000`;
* every unit has independent production and replay transcripts, exact
  rational endpoints, strict outward-rounded upper bounds, and a manifest.

## Deterministic width rule

The first two units have width `1/16`.  After two consecutive passes, the
next two units use width `1/8`; after two more consecutive passes, the next
units use width `1/4`, capped at `1/4`.  A failed unit is retained as an
incident and the immediately following unit is its left/right half-width
subdivision.  No width change may be selected from an unregistered result.
The final unit is truncated exactly at `1000/9`; no unit may cross that
endpoint.  Every production command, unit name, and rational interval is
recorded before execution in the session log.
