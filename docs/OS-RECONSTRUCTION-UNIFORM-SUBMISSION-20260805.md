## Exact scope

Inside the stated anisotropic Dobrushin window, the manuscript records a
machine-checked chain from finite Gibbs sums to the reconstructed transfer
operator, a single extent-independent positive mass controlling projected
operator norms and connected time correlations, normalization by a
positive-cone denominator floor, the finite-time stationary Markov
interpretation and the corresponding susceptibility bound.

The manuscript explicitly does **not** construct an infinite-volume transfer
operator, an `L -> infinity` state or compatibility maps between spatial
extents. It does not claim boundary independence beyond the stated positive
time terminations, a continuum or Wightman theory, `SU(N)`, or any consequence
for the four-dimensional Yang--Mills mass-gap problem. The analytic mass input
is inherited from the Dobrushin corollary; the paper's contribution is the
reconstruction, exact identification and volume-independent normalization.
