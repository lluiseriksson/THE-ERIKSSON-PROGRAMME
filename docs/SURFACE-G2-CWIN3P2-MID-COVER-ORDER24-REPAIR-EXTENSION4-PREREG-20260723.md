# G2 order-24 repair extension 4: units 64--65

**Status:** preregistered extension; quarantined evidence only.

The order-24 repair covers beta units `[257/4,129/2]` and `[129/2,259/4]`.
The frozen parameters are `CWIN=3/2`, beta Taylor order 24, t order 25,
180-bit Arb precision, beta width `1/4`, and minimum t width `1/100000`.
Production and replay must be byte-identical. The sign rows remain
quarantined and cannot promote `H_tail`, G2, G6, K2, or K4.
