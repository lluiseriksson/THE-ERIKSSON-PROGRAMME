`tr(x*y^-1)` as the sum of four matrix-entry Gram terms.

eight features gives the exponent `(beta/2) Re tr(x*y^-1)`.

quadratic form. `su2WilsonCrossing_isHaarPSDKernel` then closes the exact
kernel, not a real or diagonal slice.

is no hidden representation-dimension factor to audit at this endpoint.
