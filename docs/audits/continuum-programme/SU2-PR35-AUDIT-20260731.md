## Claim-level audit

### B1 — trace/entry identity: PASS

At lines 181-196,
`su2_trace_mul_inv_eq_sum_entries` proves

```text
trace(x*y^-1) = sum_i sum_j x_ij * conjugate(y_ij).
```

The proof uses the special-unitary star/inverse coercion and matrix
conjugate-transpose rule. It has the correct index order and conjugates the
second feature.

This is a coefficient-matrix identity, not a character expansion. The
producer correctly does not call the four entries irreducible characters or
claim Peter-Weyl.

### B2 — finite-rank complex quadratic form: PASS

Lines 40-51 define a finite sum

```text
K(x,y) = sum_i c_i phi_i(x) conjugate(phi_i(y)),  c_i >= 0.
```

Lines 76-175 prove, for continuous complex `F`,

```text
integral_x integral_y conjugate(F(x)) K(x,y) F(y)
  = sum_i c_i * |integral_x conjugate(phi_i(x)) F(x)|^2.
```

The right-hand side is a real nonnegative number. Integrability is discharged
from continuity, compact support on a compact space, and finite measure. The
proof handles arbitrary complex observables and the star placement is
consistent.

The theorem is more general than Haar measure. The name `IsHaarPSDKernel`
does not add invariance or probability normalisation; those are not used in
the proof.

### B3 — Wilson crossing kernel: PASS as a definition; PSD BLOCKED

Lines 199-207 define

```text
su2WilsonCrossingKernel beta x y
  = exp((beta/2) * Re trace(x*y^-1))
```

and prove only its pointwise positivity. That theorem is true but must not be
confused with positive semidefiniteness of the kernel.

An exact search at the audited SHA found no declaration connecting
`su2WilsonCrossingKernel` to either `IsFiniteRankPositiveKernel` or
`IsHaarPSDKernel`. The Taylor/Fock expansion, nonnegative coefficients,
uniform convergence, and passage of the quadratic form to the limit are all
missing. Status: **BLOCKED**, with those declarations as the missing data.

### G1 — Haar conventions: BLOCKED

`SUN_StateConstruction` supplies the normalized probability measure
`sunHaarProb 2`, but the new theorem is quantified over an arbitrary finite
measure and never instantiates that measure. No step presently requires left,
right, or inversion invariance.

The missing data are an instantiated SU(2) quadratic-form theorem and the
exact invariance used by the future gluing/gauge-fixing proof. No invariance
is inferred from the import alone.

### G2 — reflection, splitting, and gluing: BLOCKED

The new file has no reflection map, half-space support, `Half x Cross x Half`
type, full-configuration assembly map, inverse map, boundary identification,
product-Haar equality, Wilson-weight factorisation, or crossing multiplicity
proof.

The PR body explicitly says those items are future work and that the existing
finite `ZMod N` splitting API is not an SU(2) instantiation. This honest scope
statement passes prose audit; it does not discharge any geometry gate.

### G3 — endpoint and full OS claim: BLOCKED

There is no nonzero finite-lattice SU(2) endpoint, no reflected observable,
and no theorem identifying a reflected quadratic form with a full Wilson
expectation. Accordingly the artefact proves neither the OS axiom nor a
transfer-matrix statement.
