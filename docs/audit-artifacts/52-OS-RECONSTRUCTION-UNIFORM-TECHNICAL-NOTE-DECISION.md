# (52) Editorial disposition after the 5.23/10 reassessment

Decision: retain this manuscript as a technical note; do not publish it as a
separate main paper in its current form.

The reassessment credits a genuine formal improvement but places the result at
5.23/10.  The remaining limitation is mathematical rather than presentational:
the mixed endpoint follows from the existing finite-volume operator-gap bound,
the Dobrushin window is unchanged, and no thermodynamic or continuum limit is
constructed.  The note also overlaps substantially with *The Reconstructed
Theory Has One Mass*.

The stronger publication path is therefore to transfer the mixed definition,
exact reconstructed identity, arbitrary-pair decay bound, and explicit
zero-time proof into *The Reconstructed Theory Has One Mass*.  Until that
consolidation is carried out, this artifact is a citable technical record of
the checked interface improvement.
