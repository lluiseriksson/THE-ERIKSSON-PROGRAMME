We coupled omissions of hosts whose records list the same headline on the same date for the same directed pair. We applied Unicode normalization and case folding, extracted alphanumeric word tokens, and required at least eight words. Repetitions of the same title and date count once for each host pair. Hosts connected by qualifying pairs form a single omission unit, including transitive links. We tested two cutoffs fixed before calculation. The rule measures headline co-appearance and does not establish editorial ownership or identity of the full articles.
