## Scope boundary

The manuscript proves the stated all-time `O(|lambda|)` bound for the displayed
compactly supported Dirac form factor, together with the stated Fermi-golden-rule
positivity and Araki--Wyss regularity conditions. The sharper `O(lambda^2)`
estimate remains conditional on additional reduced-resonance hypotheses. This
release record does not upgrade that conditional statement.
