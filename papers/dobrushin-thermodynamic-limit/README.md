# Dobrushin thermodynamic limit and quasi-local C-star state paper

Source: `dobrushin_thermodynamic_limit.tex`.

The manuscript describes the Lean-checked D-7/D-9 chain from finite
two-specification comparison to a positive normalized infinite-volume state
on the quotient of finite-support site cylinders.  It proves equality of the
free and periodic limits and invariance of the state under the full additive
`Z^2` action.  It then constructs the intrinsic uniform norm, an explicit
complex star algebra, its concrete commutative C-star closure, and a positive
norm-one extension of the state.  Arbitrary finite-set Gibbs kernels and their
exact finite-volume tower identity are also formalized.

The exact D-9 Lean source anchor cited by the manuscript is
`f587b35001f4ae87c7fe0383662864df6bc9a1c0`.  The paper does not yet claim the
infinite-volume DLR fixed-point identity, completed `Z^2` covariance,
arbitrary Følner exhaustions, a beyond-Dobrushin parameter region, or a
Yang--Mills consequence.
