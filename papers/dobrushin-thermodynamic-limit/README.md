# Dobrushin thermodynamic limit and covariant state paper

Source: `dobrushin_thermodynamic_limit.tex`.

The manuscript describes the Lean-checked D-7/D-8 chain from finite
two-specification comparison to a positive normalized infinite-volume state
on the quotient of finite-support site cylinders.  It proves equality of the
free and periodic limits and invariance of the state under the full additive
`Z^2` action.

The exact Lean source anchor cited by the manuscript is
`6ce506bb6ad627e793485631a12f1271423f2bd4`.  The construction is algebraic: it
does not claim a complex C-star completion, a DLR theorem for arbitrary
Følner shapes, or a Yang--Mills consequence.

The final rendered PDF is released at
`output/pdf/dobrushin_thermodynamic_limit.pdf`.  See `RELEASE-MANIFEST.md` for
the frozen source commit, build commands, timings, byte counts, and hashes.
