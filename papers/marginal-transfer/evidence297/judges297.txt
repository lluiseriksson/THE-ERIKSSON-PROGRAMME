Question: for each fixed 0<rho<1, 1<s<(1+rho)/(1-rho), and B>1,
can strictly positive mean-one densities r_L with ||r_L||_s<=B have
an exponentially vanishing normalized gap of sqrt(r_L) P_rho sqrt(r_L)?
Mechanism: two antipodal product tilts plus a positive uniform component;
move the spectrum to P_sqrt(rho) r_L P_sqrt(rho), then compare a two-block
Gram matrix with two identical leading eigenvalues and exponentially small
off-diagonal norm. All norms use uniform probability on the Boolean cube.

G1: construction strictly positive, exactly normalized, and Ls bound uniform
in L for every fixed B>1, with no hidden lower bound or L-dependent s/rho.
G2: exact one-coordinate Walsh matrix and tensor factorization.
G3: small-tilt leading eigenvalue >1 precisely on the strict subcritical side;
give a legitimate derivative argument, no numerical evidence in its place.
G4: cross square-root norm strictly less than the leading eigenvalue, with
equality cases checked; no assumed orthogonality of the two product states.
G5: AB/BA spectral equivalence, multiplicities and dimension-two case checked.
G6: first and second eigenvalue estimates plus the positive base term produce
an explicit exponential gap upper bound with constants independent of L.
G7: exactly the original transfer operator, not heat-bath or another generator.
G8: compare to296 sufficient bound; endpoint stays open, no marginal or
finite-range/physical claims and no unestablished original-priority claim.

Falsifiers: any failed normalization/norm bound; cross norm equality; a
nonpositive second Gram eigenvalue; an invalid AB/BA multiplicity argument;
or a sign error in the small-tilt threshold. Abandon this construction if
one is found and cannot be repaired without changing the target. Retain
negative result; do not tune an already failed gate. One separate full-source
audit, frozen SHA, no inbound messages. No local mathematical computation.

manuscript for production and exact-PDF review; no new minor standalone paper.
