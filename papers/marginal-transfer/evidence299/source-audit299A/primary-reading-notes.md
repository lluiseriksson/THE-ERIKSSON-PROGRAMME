# Targeted primary-source comparison

[Aida, An estimate of the gap of spectrum of Schroedinger operators which generate hyperbounded semigroup, author preprint dated February 9, 2001](https://www.ms.u-tokyo.ac.jp/~aida/paper/preprint/handai3.pdf). Read the setup, assumptions A1-A6, additive form definition and Theorem 10 with its surrounding estimates. The framework includes a derivation/chain-rule hypothesis, an additive potential and ground-state/weak-Poincare inputs. No operator-and-hypothesis identification with the present multiplicative Boolean transfer congruence was established. This is now a directly accessed primary comparison; earlier reports' access limits remain historical facts. It neither refutes the present proof nor supplies novelty clearance.

[Biskup and Koenig, Eigenvalue order statistics for random Schroedinger operators with doubly-exponential tails, author manuscript dated April 21, 2015](https://www.wias-berlin.de/people/koenig/www/PPP4PAM-CMP-final.pdf). Read the operator setting, Proposition 2.2, Lemma 4.5 and their proofs in Section 4.3. Lemma 4.5 bounds the second eigenvalue by principal values on its two sign regions. Equation (4.29) then uses additivity of the potential budget and a half-budget choice, producing a log-two term. This is a close classical precedent for the two-sign-budget mechanism. Its additive lattice operator and exponential potential budget are not the exact multiplicative cube statement audited here. Citation of this precedent is appropriate in a later exposition; no priority for the general mechanism can be claimed.

No third-party full text is included in this evidence directory. These targeted reads do not establish originality of the exact threshold or boundary family; no inference is drawn from absence of search results.
