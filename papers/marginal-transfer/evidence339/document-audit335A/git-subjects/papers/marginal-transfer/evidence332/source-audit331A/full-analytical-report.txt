Define the positive margins d_1=a_s K-2-1/(2K)-log K and d_2=a_s K-(1/2)log(e C_s K). Their fixed positivity, rather than a diverging u/m ratio, is used below.
For any prescribed sequence k/sqrt L->C in (0,infinity), its smallest odd majorant satisfies k<=m<=k+1. Hence m/sqrt L->C, m->infinity and m+1<=L eventually. No monotonicity of k or rate of convergence to C is required. The finite cutoff may depend on rho,K,C,b and the sequence. Before that cutoff r=1 has mean one, floor at least b, norm 1<B*, and every nonconstant Fourier coefficient zero, even if k=L. Thus every-dimensional requirements are compatible with asymptotic choices. An unused early delta can be arbitrary in (0,1); it is not needed to define these patched densities.
