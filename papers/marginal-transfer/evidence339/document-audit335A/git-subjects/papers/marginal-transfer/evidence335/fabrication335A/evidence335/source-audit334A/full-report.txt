C2 — CRITICAL TAIL BUDGET: PASS

Since r_b is nonnegative, Jensen on the entire probability space gives Er_b^s>=(Er_b)^s>=(1-2M^(1-s))_+^s. Disjoint supports give exact additivity Er^s=Er_b^s+Er_t^s. Therefore ||r_t||s<=B_M=[2-(1-2M^(1-s))_+^s]^(1/s), uniformly in L,r.

It does not imply ||r_t||s->0 or uniform integrability at exponent s. The proof correctly leaves a possible full unit in the tail; replacing it by zero would falsely assert rigidity throughout the finite-C regime.
