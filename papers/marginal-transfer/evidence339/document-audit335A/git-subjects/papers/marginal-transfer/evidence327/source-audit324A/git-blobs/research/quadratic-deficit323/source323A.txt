STATEMENT AND NORMALIZATION
On the uniform probability cube {-1,1}^L, fix 0<rho<1, s=(1+rho)/(1-rho), p=2s/(s-1)=1+1/rho>2 and Q=P_sqrt(rho). Let r>=0, E r=1, ||r||_s<=B with B independent of L. Suppose the weighting law r u_L has exactly uniform marginals on every subset of at most k coordinates, where k is an integer, 0<=k<=L. Set S=Q M_r Q and T=M_sqrt(r) P_rho M_sqrt(r). There exists c_p>0, depending only on p, such that
 ||S-P_rho|| <=2(B+1)sqrt(L+1) exp[-c_p(floor(k/2)+1)^2/L]
for 0<=k<L. If k=L then r=1 and S=P_rho exactly. In particular the weaker bound with exponent -c_p k^2/(4L) holds for all0<=k<=L. If k_L^2/[L ln(L+1)] tends to infinity, then the norm difference tends to zero, lambda1(T)->1, lambda2(T)->rho, and gap(T)=1-lambda2/lambda1 tends to1-rho. L>=1 is implicit. No constant is claimed uniform as rho approaches0 or1.

Their homogeneous moment bound reads ||f_j||_p<=2^{L psi(p,j/L)/p}||f_j||_2 for homogeneous degree j<=L/2. The function psi(p,x) is continuous on[0,1/2], psi(p,0)=0, strictly concave for p>2, with derivative (p/2)log2(p-1) at zero. These are cited external theorems, not results of this programme.

1. QUADRATIC DEFICIT DERIVATION
Put z=1-2delta and A(z)=((1+z)^p+(1-z)^p)/2. Write h(x)=-x ln x-(1-x)ln(1-x), using natural logs. The constants in Proposition2.6 cancel, yielding exactly
 ln(2) psi(p,x)=ln A(z)-(p/2)h(x)-p x ln z,
 x=z A'(z)/(p A(z)).
For any fixed real p>2, A is positive and even analytic near z=0. Direct binomial expansion followed by ln(1+t) gives
 ln A(z)=a2 z^2+b4 z^4+O(z^6),
 a2=p(p-1)/2,
 b4=p(p-1)(p-2)(p-3)/24-p^2(p-1)^2/8
   =-p(p-1)(p^2+p-3)/12.
With v=z^2 and a=p-1, b=4b4/p, the implicit equation becomes
 x=a v+b v^2+O(v^3).
Since a>0, the analytic inverse function theorem applies near0:
 v=x/a-b x^2/a^3+O(x^3),
 ln v=ln x-ln a-b x/a^2+O(x^2),
 ln A=(p/2)x-b4 x^2/a^2+O(x^3).
Also h(x)=-x ln x+x-x^2/2+O(x^3). Substitution cancels x ln x and the linear constants, giving
 ln(2) psi(p,x)=(p/2)ln(p-1)x+[p/4+b4/(p-1)^2]x^2+O(x^3)
 =(p/2)ln(p-1)x-p^2(p-2)x^2/[12(p-1)]+O(x^3).
All remainders here are for fixed p as x decreases to0. Thus
 Delta_p(x):=x ln(p-1)/2-ln(2)psi(p,x)/p
 =p(p-2)x^2/[12(p-1)]+O(x^3).
In particular the limiting coefficient is strictly positive for every fixed p>2. It vanishes at p=2, consistent with psi(2,x)=0; p=2 is excluded from the theorem.

2. GLOBAL QUADRATIC LOWER BOUND AND ALL LEVELS
Let y=min(x,1-x) and a_p(x)=Delta_p(y)+(x-y)ln(p-1)/2 for x in[0,1]. Strict concavity of psi proves Delta_p(y)>0 for0<y<=1/2. Hence a_p(x)>0 for every x>0, including a_p(1)=ln(p-1)/2. For x near0 the preceding calculation gives a_p(x)/x^2 tending to C_p=p(p-2)/[12(p-1)]>0. Thus this quotient extends continuously and positively to the compact interval[0,1]. Its minimum c_p is positive. This defines an existence constant, not an unproved explicit global value C_p. Consequently a_p(x)>=c_p x^2 throughout[0,1].
For degree j<=L/2 the external homogeneous bound and rho=1/(p-1) imply
 ||Q f_j||_p<=exp[-L a_p(j/L)]||f_j||_2
 <=exp[-c_p j^2/L]||f_j||_2.
For j>L/2 multiply f_j by total parity chi_[L]. This preserves all Lp norms and maps the degree to L-j; applying the bound there, then the original noise multiplier rho^(j/2), gives the identical estimate. This does not assert commutation between parity and Q. At j=L the parity-reduced degree is0 and the same expression holds.
Let m=floor(k/2), Pi project onto degrees<=m, and H=I-Pi. For arbitrary f, orthogonality in L2, triangle inequality in Lp and Cauchy--Schwarz across the levels give
 ||Q Hf||_p<=sum_{j=m+1}^L exp[-c_p j^2/L]||f_j||_2
 <=sqrt(L+1) exp[-c_p(m+1)^2/L]||f||_2.
Ordinary endpoint hypercontractivity gives ||Q||_(2->p)<=1; also ||QPi||_(2->p)<=1.

3. MARGINAL CANCELLATION AND OPERATOR ESTIMATE
Write h=r-1 and D=Q M_h Q. Exact weighting-law marginals imply hhat(A)=0 for all|A|<=k, including the constant coefficient. Matrix elements of Pi D Pi are rho^((|A|+|C|)/2) hhat(A symmetric_difference C), zero because |A symmetric_difference C|<=2m<=k. Therefore Pi D Pi=0 and D=H D+Pi D H.
For unit u,v, Holder with1/s+2/p=1 bounds
 |<u,H Dv>|=|E[h(QHu)(Qv)]|
 <=(B+1)sqrt(L+1)exp[-c_p(m+1)^2/L].
The other block uses QPi u and QHv and has the same bound. Adding yields the stated norm estimate. If k=L all nonconstant coefficients of r vanish, so the conclusion is exact with no tail argument needed. Since m+1>=k/2, the weaker bound holds uniformly. Its logarithm is bounded by ln[2(B+1)]+(1/2)ln(L+1)-c_p k^2/(4L), tending to minus infinity under the claimed sufficient condition.
Finally S and T are the two square products AA* and A*A, where A=Q M_sqrt(r), so their full eigenvalue lists agree including zeros. Norm perturbation of the two leading eigenvalues1,rho of P_rho gives the actual top-two eigenvalue limits. Mean one gives lambda1>=1. This proves the normalized gap limit even if r has zeros.

SCOPE / HYPOTHESIS REMOVED / REMAINING GAP
The positive linear-fraction hypothesis of321 is replaced by the weaker proved sufficient condition k^2/(L ln(L+1))->infinity, with the same dimension-independent critical norm budget B, including B=2^(1/s). No uniform integrability of r^s, density floor, supercritical moment, positivity beyond nonnegativity, or local dependence is assumed. These are weighting-law marginals, not Perron stationary marginals. The result is a finite Boolean transfer-operator statement, not a physical mass gap or continuum bridge.
Together with319 collapse for every fixed k=floor(L^alpha),alpha<1/2, this brackets the polynomial scale. It does NOT establish a sharp square-root threshold, necessity of the logarithm, rigidity for k merely greater than sqrt(L), or any assertion at k comparable to sqrt(L). Do not remove the logarithm without new proof. The constant c_p may deteriorate with p. Novelty of this spectral application remains unestablished by the targeted literature check; all inherited moment input is explicitly credited.
No locally executed numerical test or Lean verification is claimed. The algebra and functional analysis above are the evidence proposed for terminal source audit. Preserve all failures. Group this improvement with319/321 in322 rather than issuing a separate paper.
