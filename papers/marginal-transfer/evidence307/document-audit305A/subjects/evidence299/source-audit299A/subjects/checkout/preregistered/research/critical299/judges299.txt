No rate claim at the critical budget, no physical/local/marginal-matching conclusion for the collapse family.

Hypotheses: fixed rho, critical s, fixed B>=1; normalized uniform measure; actual multiplicative transfer congruence, descending eigenvalues with multiplicity; at least two total states. The fixed-floor counterexamples may replace a finite prefix with r=1.
