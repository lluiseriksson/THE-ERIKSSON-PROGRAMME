UNIT300 — CRITICAL MOMENT TAIL CONTROL
Registered before acceptance/audit of the candidate, after preparatory unreviewed reasoning. Not a claim of blind discovery. Unit299's mathematical question is closed; its publication continues separately.

Question: can a uniform critical-moment tail bound restore a dimension-free normalized transfer gap for arbitrary finite total critical budget?
Setting: uniform Boolean cubes, fixed 0<rho<1, s=(1+rho)/(1-rho), r>=0, Er=1, T=sqrt(r)P_rho sqrt(r). Actual descending eigenvalues including zeros and multiplicities; gap=1-lambda2/lambda1.
Candidate: if a fixed K>=1 satisfies E[r^s 1_{r>K}]<=8^(-s), then with B_K=(K^(s-1)+8^(-s))^(1/s), gap>=min(1/2,2*8^(-s^2)*K^(1-s^2)/B_K)>0. No optimum claim. Consequently uniform integrability of critical moments suffices, including a fixed Orlicz moment bound with a diverging extra factor. This is an additional tail hypothesis, not proof that a bounded critical moment suffices.
Mechanism: truncate each second-eigenvector sign part after multiplying by sqrt(r); endpoint hypercontractivity controls the discarded tail, interpolation and reverse hypercontractivity force a positive cross term, and sign compression bounds the actual spectral gap.
Falsifier: a valid family satisfying one fixed K bound with collapsing gaps, an invalid truncated norm estimate, wrong interpolation/reverse exponent, or failure to control actual lambda1/lambda2. Numerical agreement is not proof.

G1: Establish active-support/Perron/sign handling, rank one, repeated second eigenvalues, lambda1>=1 and lambda1<=B_K without assuming uniform-mean-zero eigenvectors.
G2: Prove endpoint tail estimate ||Q sqrt(r)1_{r>K} h||2<=1/sqrt(8) for ||h||2=1, Q=P_sqrt(rho). Derive total moment bound from Er=1.
G3: For lambda2>=1/2 derive ||Q f_low||2>=1/sqrt(8) for each normalized sign part. Show ||f_low||2<=sqrt(K), ||f_low||_(1+rho)>=1/sqrt(8), and interpolation exponent theta=1/s^2 with a=1-rho, including zero functions and quasi-norm powers.
G4: Derive cross term D>=8^(-s^2)K^(1-s^2) by reverse hypercontractivity and positivity. Verify compression eigenvalue difference D/(uv), uv<=1/2 and the normalization by lambda1. Handle lambda2<1/2 separately.
G5: State uniform integrability and a quantitative Orlicz consequence with all quantifiers. Clearly distinguish a new tail assumption from eliminating all critical integrability restrictions. Do not infer an optimal constant or necessity/equivalence of UI.
G6: In the exact299 diluted balanced family show r_L->1 in probability but E r_L^s->2, and for every fixed K>1 the tail moment tends to1. Bind the prior family to its exact source; no change of construction or unexplained eigenvalue premise.
G7: Compare existing primary operator-tail/uniform-integrability results and distinguish density tails under the base measure from operator tails under the Perron measure. No novelty by failed search, no independent/human/formal/local/physical claim. Preserve negative cases.

Abandonment: a failed estimate blocks the displayed constant/theorem; revise only with an explicitly new candidate under unchanged judges.
