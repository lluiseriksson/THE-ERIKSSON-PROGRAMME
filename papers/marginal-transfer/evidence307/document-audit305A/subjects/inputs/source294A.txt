1. QUESTION, CONVENTIONS AND RESULT

The owner's13November instruction asks to cross the closed papers rather
than indefinitely extend one auxiliary calculation. We combine the literal
marginal-input framework of parity_positive (revision215) with the positive
diagonal transfer congruences of congruence_spectrum. Q251 is parked, still
open; no previous route counter is reset. One active question here:
Can exact product marginals through a positive fraction of all coordinates
certify a volume-uniform gap for a fixed-coupling positive Ising transfer?

Let Omega_L=F_2^L, Hamming distance d(x,y), beta>0 fixed and q=exp(-2beta).
The symmetric kernel M_beta(x,y)=q^d(x,y) is a positive scalar multiple
of exp(beta sum_i (-1)^(x_i+y_i)). Its tensor factors [[1,q],[q,1]] are
positive definite. For a strictly positive probability mu on Omega_L put
 A_mu=diag(sqrt(mu)) M_beta diag(sqrt(mu)).
Let lambda_1>lambda_2>=...>0 be its eigenvalues. PF strictness holds since
all entries are positive. Write gap(A_mu)=1-lambda_2/lambda_1.
For uniform u_L, tensorization gives lambda_2/lambda_1=(1-q)/(1+q)=tanh beta,
independent of L. A positive scalar normalization does not change ratios.

We prove: for every beta>0 there are alpha,R,gamma>0 such that for all
sufficiently large L a strictly positive mu_L has ALL labelled marginals
on <=floor(alpha L) coordinates exactly uniform product, while at least
2^ceil(RL)-1 non-Perron eigenvalues satisfy
           lambda_j/lambda_1 >=1-7 exp(-gamma L).
The Perron Doob stationary law pi_L ALSO has exactly those same marginals.
Thus weighting-law marginals, stationary-law marginals, or both together
cannot certify a positive volume-uniform transfer gap on this class.
This concerns arbitrary positive weights, not fixed-range Gibbs weights.

A negative control is essential: the single parity family from215, despite
its arbitrarily small heat-bath gap, has transfer ratio at most tanh(2beta)
for every L>=2 and every |t|<1. Its heat-bath conclusion cannot simply be
relabeled as a transfer conclusion. We prove that too, with the full blocks.

2. CLASSICAL CODE INPUT WITH A SELF-CONTAINED EXISTENCE PROOF

Let h(p)=-p log_2 p-(1-p)log_2(1-p). Fix 0<alpha,delta<1/2 and0<R<1 with
             h(alpha)<R<1-h(delta).
For k=ceil(RL), a binary linear code C of dimension k, minimum nonzero
weight at least delta L, and dual minimum weight >floor(alpha L) exists
whenever k<L and
        2^(k-L(1-h(delta)))+2^(-k+L h(alpha))<1.       (CODE-GATE)
This gate holds for all sufficiently large L by the strict inequalities.

Proof. Take a uniformly random k by L binary matrix G. For fixed nonzero
v in F_2^k, vG is uniform in F_2^L. Union bound over v shows that the chance
some vG has weight <delta L is at most2^(k-L(1-h(delta))). This event
includes a rank failure (vG=0); its absence makes G injective on messages.
For fixed nonzero w in F_2^L, Gw is uniform in F_2^k. Union bound over
weights1,...,floor(alpha L) bounds the chance some such w has Gw=0 by
2^(-k+L h(alpha)). The two bounds summing to <1 prove joint existence.
We used sum_(j<=pL) binom(L,j)<=2^(L h(p)) for p<1/2. To see it, set
z=p/(1-p)<1; every z^j with j<=pL is >=z^(pL), so the binomial sum is
at most z^(-pL)(1+z)^L=2^(Lh(p)). Strict-weight events are bounded by
this same inclusive sum. All floor/ceiling conventions are thus paid.

For every set S of at most floor(alpha L) columns, G_S has rank |S|:
a nontrivial relation would give a nonzero dual word supported in S.
Uniform codewords therefore project uniformly onto F_2^S. Conversely,
this argument identifies exactly the standard code/orthogonal-array
mechanism; neither the code existence nor k-wise independence is new.

3. A STRICTLY POSITIVE WEIGHT AND A FINITE SPECTRAL BOUND

More generally let C be ANY binary linear code of size m>=2 and minimum
nonzero weight d_C, with every coordinate projection of size <=r surjective.
Let0<eta<=1/2 and set
     mu=(1-eta) Uniform(C)+eta Uniform(Omega_L).
All entries are positive and every <=r marginal is exactly uniform.
Put a=(1-eta)/m+eta/2^L and s=(m-1) exp(-2beta d_C).
We claim, for every2<=j<=m,
  0<1-lambda_j(A_mu)/lambda_1(A_mu)
       <=2s+2m(sqrt(eta)+eta).                        (FINITE)
The lower strict inequality follows from PF and positivity; it need not
be quantitatively uniform. The upper bound can be vacuous at small L.

Proof. On the coordinate subspace indexed by C, the principal block is
A_CC=a M_CC. Its diagonal entries are a; each off-diagonal entry in M_CC
is <=exp(-2beta d_C). The symmetric row-sum bound gives all eigenvalues
of M_CC in[1-s,1+s]. For completeness the operator norm of M_CC-I is
at most its maximum absolute row sum s, e.g. by Cauchy--Schwarz with
row/column absolute sums. Principal interlacing yields lambda_m(A)>=a(1-s).
Every j<=m obeys the same lower bound. Also lambda_1(A)>=a by a coordinate
Rayleigh vector, even if1-s is negative.

Split A into C and C-complement. The outside block is positive semidefinite,
so its norm is at most its trace, which is eta(1-m/2^L)<=eta. The cross
block B satisfies
 ||B||^2<=||B||_F^2
   <=(sum_C mu)(sum_outside mu)<=eta,
since every M_xy^2<=1. The off-diagonal block matrix [[0,B],[B*,0]] has
norm ||B||; hence the difference from diag(a M_CC,0) has norm at most
sqrt(eta)+eta. Therefore lambda_1(A)<=a(1+s)+sqrt(eta)+eta and
lambda_1-lambda_j<=2as+sqrt(eta)+eta. Divide by lambda_1>=a and use
a>=(1-eta)/m>=1/(2m) to obtain FINITE. No limiting eigenvalue argument,
hard-support identification, numerical experiment or assumed gap is used.

4. LINEAR-ORDER MARGINALS AND EXPONENTIALLY MANY SLOW MODES

Fix beta>0. Choose delta=1/4 and
 R=min{[1-h(1/4)]/2, beta/(4 ln2)} >0.
Choose any0<alpha<1/2 with h(alpha)<R, for example h(alpha)<R/2.
Let gamma=beta/2-R ln2 >=beta/4>0.
Take codes from section2 for all sufficiently large L, m=2^ceil(RL),
and set eta=exp(-2beta L)/m^2. This is positive and <=1/4.
Since d_C>=L/4 and m<=2 exp(R ln2 L),
 2s<=4 exp(-gamma L).
Further,
 2m(sqrt eta+eta)=2 exp(-beta L)+2 exp(-2beta L)/m
                 <=3 exp(-beta L)<=3 exp(-gamma L).
FINITE gives the stated7 exp(-gamma L) upper bound for every2<=j<=m.
There are m-1 such nonleading eigenvalues. Their multiplicities are counted
normally, with no assertion they coincide. The whole tensor matrix has
2^L eigenvalues and k<L ensures these indices exist.

A concrete symbolic parameter choice at beta=1 is delta=1/4,R=1/16,
alpha=1/256, gamma=1/2-(ln2)/16. Both entropy inequalities can be verified
analytically or by a separately certified computation; this optional choice
is NOT an input to the theorem and no numerical validation is claimed here.
The main theorem uses only the displayed entropy inequalities and CODE-GATE.

5. THE ACTUAL PERRON STATIONARY LAW, NOT JUST THE INPUT WEIGHT

Choose the PF eigenvector v>0 with sum_x v_x^2=1. Define
 P(x,y)=A_mu(x,y) v_y/(lambda_1 v_x), pi(x)=v_x^2.
The eigenvector equation gives row sum1, and
 pi(x)P(x,y)=v_x A_mu(x,y)v_y/lambda_1=pi(y)P(y,x).
Thus P is a strictly positive reversible transition matrix with stationary
pi; it is similar to A_mu/lambda_1, so its nontrivial eigenvalues and
its discrete-time spectral gap are exactly the ratios above.

For every c in C, addition x->x+c preserves mu, Hamming distance and A_mu.
The associated permutation commutes with A_mu. It takes normalized positive
PF vectors to normalized positive PF vectors; uniqueness gives v_(x+c)=v_x.
Thus pi is C-translation invariant. If projection C->F_2^S is surjective,
then every translation on F_2^S is the image of a c in C. The S-marginal
of pi is invariant under all translations, hence is exactly uniform.
Therefore BOTH mu and pi have the required labelled product marginals.
For uniform weight, v is uniform and pi=u_L, and P has ratio tanh beta.
No claim pi=mu is made or used in the constructed nonuniform case.

6. NEGATIVE CONTROL: PARITY SLOW HEAT-BATH DOES NOT IMPLY SLOW TRANSFER

Use spin characters chi_S on Omega_L and the strictly positive law
mu_t=2^-L(1+t chi_[L]), |t|<1, from revision215. Let rho=tanh beta in(0,1).
The unweighted row-normalized transfer P_beta=M_beta/(1+q)^L acts on
chi_S by rho^|S|. Up to an overall scalar the weighted congruence is
D_t P_beta D_t, D_t=diag sqrt(1+t chi_[L]). It is similar, using invertible
D_t, to P_beta D_t^2. Multiplication by chi_[L] takes chi_S to chi_(S^c).
Consequently the unordered complement pair {S,S^c} has coefficient block
  [[rho^s, t rho^s],[t rho^(L-s),rho^(L-s)]], s=|S|,
and eigenvalues
 (z_s +/- sqrt(z_s^2-4Delta))/2,
 z_s=rho^s+rho^(L-s), Delta=(1-t^2)rho^L.
These2^(L-1) blocks exhaust the spectrum with multiplicity. They are
positive since Delta>0 and z_s>0. The largest z_s is z_0=1+rho^L.
Among proper pairs the largest is z_1=rho+rho^(L-1). The plus-root is
increasing in z, while the minus-root in the empty/full pair is <=rho^L,
and every proper plus-root is >=rho^min(s,L-s). In particular the s=1
plus-root is >=rho. Thus for L>=2 the Perron root is the s=0 plus-root,
and lambda_2 is the s=1 plus-root. Upper roots of other pairs are smaller;
all their lower roots are no larger than their upper roots.

For z>=2sqrt Delta, the plus-root divided by z is
(1+sqrt(1-4Delta/z^2))/2, an increasing function of z. It follows that
 lambda_2/lambda_1 <= z_1/z_0
       =(rho+rho^(L-1))/(1+rho^L) <=2rho/(1+rho^2)=tanh(2beta).
The penultimate step writes the ratio as rho(1+b)/(1+rho^2 b) with
b=rho^(L-2)<=1; it increases with b. Equality in the limiting t->1
and L=2 shows the uniform bound is sharp as a supremum. This holds even
though revision215's single-site heat-bath gap tends to zero at fixedL.
The two dynamics are genuinely different. A direct parity-only transfer
proposal is therefore rejected, not recycled as an alleged consequence.

7. LITERAL CERTIFICATE CONSEQUENCE AND SCOPE

Define M_r(nu)=(nu_S: S subset[L], |S|<=r), retaining coordinate labels.
At fixed beta, suppose a lower-gap certificate is a function of L,beta,
M_floor(alpha L)(mu) and/or M_floor(alpha L)(pi), and is valid for every
strictly positive weight in this transfer class. On the constructed laws
its input equals that of the uniform product law at the same L. Its value
must therefore be <=7exp(-gamma L) whenever it claims a valid lower bound.
It cannot certify a positive constant independent of L at the product input.
This does NOT say all functions of marginal input vanish, or that the actual
product chain is slow; its gap is exactly1-tanh beta. Full conditionals,
density lower bounds, operator entries or code membership are extra input.
The construction permits exponentially small weights and many-body spatial
interactions. It does not refute a theorem with uniform positivity constants,
fixed-range Gibbs structure, local generators or other absent assumptions.
No continuum Yang--Mills, physical mass gap, spatial mixing or entropy-
tensorization theorem is asserted. The many-mode conclusion is spectral;
no metastable-set or mixing-time asymptotic beyond its consequences is
claimed without a separate proof.
