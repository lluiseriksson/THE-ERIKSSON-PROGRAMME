2. A UNIFORMLY POSITIVE SELECTED RESPONSE ON ALL V-VALUED ROWS

Let K=c n^5 T R0 T* restricted to W. Since R0 is componentwise and the cut
selects one point in each of the two layers of each component, K is block
diagonal with N blocks on V plus V:
                  K_j=[[A0,B_j],[B_j^t,A0]].
All blocks are real. In the unitary two-dimensional transform,
   A0=n^-2 sum_(theta!=0) 3P(theta)/Lambda(theta),
   B_j=n^-2 sum_(theta!=0) zeta_j(theta) S(theta)P(theta)/Lambda(theta),
where |zeta_j|=1 is the phase for the two selected points; conjugating S
instead is harmless if the two layers are interchanged. Frequencies pair
under conjugation, so A0 is real symmetric and B_j real. The spatial trace
of P is ONE, not two. Normalization is n^-3 H^-1=(c n^5)^-1; the residual
n^-2 comes from the unitary component Fourier transform.

For each nonzero theta, D has both eigenvalues in (0,6], so D^-1>=I/6.
The two layer evaluation phases have modulus one; after replacing D^-1 by
I/6 the quadratic form is diagonal in the two row vectors. Moreover
   n^-2 sum_(theta!=0) P(theta) = (n^2-1)/(2n^2) P_V.                 (AVG)
To prove this identity, conjugation makes the sum real. Permuting the three
coordinates of z, then dividing by its third coordinate, permutes the
nonzero transverse frequency pairs bijectively modulo n. The associated
spatial permutation conjugates P. The sum therefore commutes with all
three-coordinate permutations, annihilates 1, and is scalar on V (a real
matrix commuting with all such permutations has constant diagonal and
constant off-diagonal). Its trace is n^2-1, giving (AVG).
Consequently, on W,
   kappa I<=K<=K_n I,   kappa=(1-n^-2)/12>=1/16.                    (ELL)
The upper bound follows from R0<=R and input(iii). Longitudinal row vectors
parallel to 1 are annihilated by T R0 T*. The actual M is NOT replaced by K;
K will only provide a lower bound under minimization.

3. THE PAIR-SUM/PAIR-DIFFERENCE CROSS BLOCK IS UNIFORMLY BOUNDED

Let a,b be unique centered frequency angles in [-pi,pi), with rho=|a|+|b|.
An explicit spanning vector for {1,z}^perp is
   w=(exp(-ib)-1, 1-exp(-ia), exp(-ia)-exp(-ib)), ||w||^2=Lambda.
Its three entries have a common factor -2i, real amplitudes, and phases
   exp(-ib/2), exp(-ia/2), exp(-i(a+b)/2).
Thus every phase difference has absolute value at most rho/2. Entrywise
Im(P_jk) is bounded by rho |w_j w_k|/(2Lambda). The Frobenius norm then gives
                  ||Im P(theta)||<=rho/2.
No smooth choice of a unit polarization or division by a coordinate is used.
Because transpose, not adjoint, is relevant to the real B_j,
   ||(B_j-B_j^t)/2||
        <= n^-2 sum_(theta!=0) |S| ||Im P||/Lambda
        <= (3/2)n^-2 sum rho/Lambda.                               (CROSS1)
The evaluation phase zeta_j never needs a distance estimate.

For centered integer residues r,s, a=2pi r/n,b=2pi s/n, put m=max(|r|,|s|).
The chord inequality gives Lambda>=4(a^2+b^2)/pi^2. Also
   rho/(a^2+b^2)<=n/(pi m).
There are at most8m frequency pairs in shell m and 1<=m<=n/2; the Nyquist
boundary uses fewer points, not more. Hence
   n^-2 sum rho/Lambda <= (pi^2/4)*(4/pi)=pi.
Combining with(CROSS1) yields
                   ||(B_j-B_j^t)/2||<=3pi/2<=2pi.                  (CROSS)
This bound is uniform over every original pair separation and permutation cut.

4. PAYING OPTIMIZATION OVER EVERY PAIR DIFFERENCE

Change row coordinates orthogonally to plus=(odd+even)/sqrt2 and
minus=(odd-even)/sqrt2. This is only an analysis of the ORIGINAL row space,
not a modified observation map. K_j has blocks
   X_j=A0+(B_j+B_j^t)/2,
   Y_j=A0-(B_j+B_j^t)/2,
   Z_j=(B_j^t-B_j)/2,
with the off-diagonal block Z_j up to an irrelevant sign.
For fixed plus vector a_j, minimization over its minus vector gives
   <a_j,L_j a_j>,  L_j=X_j-Z_j Y_j^-1 Z_j^t.
By(ELL), Y_j>=kappa I and kappa I<=L_j<=K_n I. The latter lower bound follows
also by minimizing kappa(||a_j||^2+||minus||^2).
By(CROSS) the trace of the correction on the two-dimensional V is at most
   2(2pi)^2/kappa<=128pi^2.
Input(v), or the spatial trace tr(P_V P(theta))=1 in the Fourier formula,
gives tr X_j=g0+G(o_j,e_j)>=h_N/(8pi^2)-1. The omitted transverse zero
optical branch pairs to zero with the equal plus vector, so it adds no term.
Therefore
           tr L_j>=h_N/(8pi^2)-1-128pi^2.                          (LOCAL-OPT)

5. PAYING THE REMAINING GLOBAL MEAN DIRECTIONS

Let Lblock=directsum_j L_j on V^N. E0 corresponds to mean-zero plus vectors.
After minimizing every minus vector, the remaining allowed E0-perpendicular
directions are exactly the two-dimensional common-plus mean space (and the
longitudinal null rows, which have no K energy or E0 pairing).
The effective operator S0 for minimizing K with fixed P_E0 alpha=y is the
Schur complement of Lblock by this mean space. Its compression part has trace
at least sum_j tr L_j-2K_n. Its subtracted PSD correction has rank at most2
and is bounded above by that compression, hence norm at most K_n and trace
at most2K_n. This uses positivity of the Schur complement, not a rank heuristic.
It follows that
   tr S0 >= N{h_N/(8pi^2)-1-128pi^2}-4K_n,
   ||S0|| >= tr S0/[2(N-1)].                                      (MEAN)
All means and pair differences have been optimized, not set to zero by fiat.

6. PAYING ROTATION TO THE ACTUAL POSITIVE-WEIGHT USEFUL SPACE

For any positive definite K on W and subspace E' of W, elementary constrained
minimization gives
   min_(P_E' alpha=y)<alpha,K alpha>
       =<y,(P_E' K^-1 P_E'|E')^-1 y>.
Indeed K alpha must lie in E'; solving for its multiplier proves both the
formula and attainment. Let S_E(K) denote this inverse compression.
Both E and E0 have dimension d and projection distance at most epsilon<1.
Principal two-plane rotations give an orthogonal O on W with O E0=E and
||O-I||<=2epsilon: on a principal angle theta, ||O-I||=2sin(theta/2)
<=2sin(theta), while sin(theta)<=epsilon. Use identity on the common/rest space.
On E0 the two inverse compressions differ by at most
   ||P_E0(O^t K^-1 O-K^-1)P_E0||<=4epsilon/kappa.
Their inverses have norm at most K_n by(ELL); the inverse difference identity
therefore gives
   ||O^t S_E(K) O-S0||<=4epsilon K_n^2/kappa
                         <=128K_n^2/(48n^2-1).                    (ROT)
The comparison is between operators on the identified equal-dimensional
spaces, not between singular ambient inverses.

7. TRANSFER TO ALL ORIGINAL SINGLETON COEFFICIENTS

Put Mhat=c n^5 M on the full original 3n-dimensional real row space.
It dominates K extended by zero on W-perpendicular, and Mhat<=K_n I.
Thus ker Mhat is contained in W-perpendicular and E is contained in range
Mhat. For any alpha with P_E alpha=y, its W component has the same pairing
and its K energy equals the full extended K energy. Taking minima gives
   Sigma>=S_E(K); also Sigma<=K_n I by the admissible choice alpha=y.
For completeness the minimum defining Sigma is attained even if Mhat is
singular. The operator P_E Mhatdagger P_E|E is positive definite, because E
lies in range Mhat. With lambda=(P_E Mhatdagger P_E|E)^-1 y,
alpha=Mhatdagger lambda is feasible, and its energy is <y,lambda>.
Every feasible perturbation h has P_E h=0, so <h,Mhat alpha>=<h,lambda>=0;
PSD then proves minimality. Hence
               Sigma=(P_E Mhatdagger P_E|E)^-1.                    (SHORT)
Combining(MEAN),(ROT) proves(OPT), with every E-perpendicular singleton
direction included. The minimization can only use the original singleton
coefficients, not hidden prefix/long-cell rows.

For(SIMPLE), K_n<=4h_N<=4N for n>=4: 1/(6n^2-1)<=4h_N/13 pays the small term.
In(OPT), N/[2(N-1)]<=1 and is at least1/2. Thus its first part is at least
   h_N/(16pi^2)-(1+128pi^2)-2K_n/(N-1).
The last fraction is at most16. The rotation term is at most
   2048N^2/(192N^2-1)<=11,
since64N^2>=11. This proves the stated SIMPLE bound for every n>=4 even.
Since h_N>=log(N+1), the optimized response is unbounded, even after all
singleton corrections. For a proposed fixed upper constant C0, every included
n with log(n/2+1)>16pi^2(C0+28+128pi^2) contradicts that bound. These sizes
are only an explicit sufficient threshold, not an estimated sharp transition.

8. ACTUAL SINGLETON-KERNEL COUNTEREXAMPLE, NOT THE FULL CANONICAL KERNEL

actual high cancelling field

   <f,Bf>=<T_L l,Mdagger T_L l>=c<y,Sigma^-1 y>=c/beta.

   Q(w)-c||w||^2=<l,(A_L-cI)l>+<f,Bf>
                         <=-7c/8+c/beta.
