Question registered before inspecting historical settlement values. Among all live British Demand Flexibility Service half-hour windows in July–September 2026 with a positive published requirement, does the aggregate signed requirement-to-settlement shortfall arise predominantly before procurement or after it? Use the official versioned-by-digest NESO summary source and stratify by direction. Compare duration-weighted sums of R-P (required minus procured) and P-S (procured minus settled). Negative values represent surplus. Keep zero as real and blank as unobserved. Restrict the main estimate to complete windows, report excluded windows, classify zero procurement separately and desist from a population claim if any settlement is missing. Only compare directions actually present. Report a direction as dominated at procurement if A>B and A+B>0. Witnesses against that conclusion include A<=B or no positive net shortfall. Abandon extrapolation to data centres, to individual providers and to counterfactual energy savings. An alternative including windows with both volumes positive is diagnostic only.
