SOURCE351A — Uniform spectral rigidity of disjoint-parity Doob transforms
Analytical candidate, parent /root fabrication, 2026-11-14.
No formal/numerical verification, separate audit, priority or publication claim.

1. SETTING AND EXACT CLAIM
Fix 0<rho<1, n>=1, m>=2, and r>=0 integers. Partition L=nm+r Boolean coordinates into n disjoint blocks of size m and r residual coordinates. Let z_i(x) be the product of the spins in block i. Let H:{-1,1}^n -> (0,infinity) be completely arbitrary, possibly depending on L with no amplitude bound. Set h(x)=H(z(x)); multiplying h by a positive scalar changes none of the Markov conclusions. Write P=P_rho for ordinary product Boolean noise with Walsh multiplier rho^|S|, g=Ph, Z=E hPh, and
 K(x,y)=P(x,y) h(y)/g(x), pi(x)=h(x)g(x)/Z.
All expectations use uniform probability. pi is a density and is stationary for K. Put N=E(h/g), r_h=(h/g)/N. (Residual-coordinate count r and weighting density r_h are distinct.)

Let theta=rho^m, ell=(1-theta)^n, u=(1+theta)^n,
 alpha=ell/u,
 beta=(rho+rho^(m-1))/(1-theta),
 epsilon=(1-rho^2)rho^(m-1)/(1-theta).
Let lambda_2(K) be the largest eigenvalue on the orthogonal complement of constants in L2(pi dmu), and gamma=1-lambda_2(K). L>=2, so this space is nonzero.

THEOREM351 (candidate).
(A) pi has every labelled marginal of degree at most m-1 exactly uniform. This follows from its algebraic symmetry, without any moment/floor assumption.
(B) Every eigenvalue of K is real and nonnegative; its simple top eigenvalue is1. Uniformly over all positive H,
 max(0,rho-epsilon) <= lambda_2(K) <= min(1,max(1-alpha,beta)),
 hence max(0,1-max(1-alpha,beta)) <= gamma <= 1-rho+epsilon.
The lower gap bound may be trivial for small m; the asymptotic statement below does not use such a bound as positive.
(C) Pointwise alpha^2 <= r_h/pi <= alpha^(-2). Thus for every fixed s>0,
 alpha^(2s) E pi^s <= E r_h^s <= alpha^(-2s) E pi^s.
(D) Along ANY sequence m->infinity and n rho^m->0, with arbitrary residual r and arbitrary positive H at each dimension, gamma -> 1-rho uniformly over H. Also alpha->1, so the moment comparison factor tends to1 for each fixed s. In particular the critical stationary cap E pi^s<=2 implies the actual uniform weighting bound E r_h^s<=2 alpha^(-2s), eventually bounded, in this entire family. It does NOT transfer the exact numerical cap2.

There is no stationary floor or moment hypothesis in(A),(B) or the pointwise comparison(C). This is an exclusion of an explicitly defined construction class, NOT a solution of the gap question for all exact-marginal densities. It includes the disjoint-parity macro construction used in349, and allows arbitrary H on arbitrarily many macro bits under the stated scaling. It does not include every code, every low-degree-matching density, or the block-majority construction of344.

2. QUOTIENT NOISE AND STATIONARITY
For a uniformly distributed x, its parity vector z is uniform on the macro cube. Conditional on x, Boolean noise multiplies each spin by independent signs of mean rho, so the parity multiplier on each size-m block has mean theta. The block multipliers are independent; residual coordinates do not affect z. Therefore
 (PF(z))(x)=(B_theta F)(z(x)),
where B_theta is ordinary n-bit noise. In particular g(x)=G(z(x)), G=B_theta H.
Relative to macro uniform probability, its kernel is
 b_0(z,w)=product_i(1+theta z_i w_i),
so ell<=b_0<=u. Let a=E_macro H>0. Then
 a ell <= G(z) <= a u.                                      (2.1)
The induced macro Doob kernel is
 K_0(z,w)=2^(-n)b_0(z,w) H(w)/G(z),
which is stochastic. Its stationary density is Pi(z)=H(z)G(z)/Z and the full pi is Pi(z(x)); conditional on z all original x are uniform. Reversibility follows directly:
 pi(x)P(x,y)h(y)/g(x)=h(x)P(x,y)h(y)/Z,
symmetric in x,y under uniform probability. Strict positivity of P and h makes K strictly positive and irreducible.

The usual similarity to a positive semidefinite matrix can be checked directly. Let a_x=h(x)/g(x)>0 and S=M_sqrt(a) P M_sqrt(a). Conjugation of K by M_sqrt(hg) gives S. Product noise P has eigenvalues rho^|S|>0 and hence is positive definite; S is positive definite. Thus K has real positive eigenvalues, with simple Perron eigenvalue1. The weak nonnegativity formulations in the theorem remain valid. No limiting singular h is allowed at any finite dimension.

3. EXACT MARGINALS
Let c_i be the full coordinate set of block i, and C be their binary span under symmetric difference. A function of the parity vector has a Walsh expansion supported on C, because every macro character product_{i in I} z_i is the original character chi_(union_{i in I} c_i). Every nonempty such union has degree at least m. Products and P preserve this subalgebra, so pi=hPh/Z also has support in C. Its mean is1; therefore E pi chi_S=0 whenever1<=|S|<=m-1. Fourier inversion on any coordinate subset of at most m-1 coordinates gives exactly its uniform marginal. This applies to arbitrary H, not only product or bounded-height H.

4. DECOMPOSITION OF THE FULL CHAIN, NOT ONLY THE QUOTIENT
The Fourier characters partition into cosets of C in the binary subset group. For a representative t, define
 V_t={chi_t(x) F(z(x)): F arbitrary on the macro cube}.
These spaces span all functions, because their Walsh supports are the disjoint sets t+C. They are also mutually orthogonal in L2(pi dmu): pi is C-supported, so for t+v outside C the uniform expectation of chi_(t+v) times any C-supported function iszero. On each V_t, multiplication by h,g and their inverses preserves the space, as does P. Thus K is block diagonal across these spaces, with each block self-adjoint in the restricted stationary inner product. Within a fixed sector the norm of chi_t F is exactly the macro L2(Pi) norm of F, since chi_t^2=1 and Pi depends only on z.

For each block let j_i be the number of coordinates of t in block i, and let d be the number of residual coordinates in t. On a macro Walsh character indexed by I,
 P[chi_t chi_(union_(i in I)c_i)]
 =rho^(d+sum_(i notin I)j_i+sum_(i in I)(m-j_i))
   chi_t chi_(union_(i in I)c_i).
Consequently P on V_t is chi_t times a real signed macro convolution B_t whose kernel relative to macro uniform probability is
 b_t(z,w)=rho^d product_i(rho^j_i+rho^(m-j_i) z_i w_i).       (4.1)
The induced K block is
 (K_t F)(z)=E_w[b_t(z,w)H(w)F(w)]/G(z).                     (4.2)
For t=empty this is exactly K_0.

Representatives may be chosen with j_i=0 whenever the original j_i is m, by adding c_i; this only replaces F by a macro character and leaves the sector unchanged. Thus a nontrivial sector either has d>=1 or has some1<=j_i<=m-1. Other j_i are0. This observation is essential: bounding only K_0 would not bound the full chain.

5. NONTRIVIAL SECTORS
For0<rho<1, the function x -> rho^x+rho^(m-x) is convex on[1,m-1], so its maximum on that interval is rho+rho^(m-1). For a nontrivial block1<=j<=m-1,
 |rho^j+rho^(m-j) z_i w_i|/(1+theta z_i w_i)
 <=(rho+rho^(m-1))/(1-theta)=beta.                         (5.1)
A block with j=0 has ratio exactly1. Residual coordinates contribute rho^d.

If beta<1, a nontrivial sector therefore satisfies |b_t|<=beta b_0: if it has any nontrivial blocks, the product of their factors is at most beta; if it has none but d>=1, rho^d<=rho<beta. Equation(4.2) gives pointwise
 |K_t F|<=beta K_0|F|,
and since K_0 is stochastic, the induced supremum norm of K_t is at most beta. Every eigenvalue in a nontrivial sector is therefore at most beta in absolute value. This argument uses no H bounds.

If beta>=1, no claim that a product of many beta factors is <=beta is made. Instead the already proved global Markov/Perron bound lambda<=1 gives lambda<=min(1,max(1-alpha,beta))=1 directly. This separate case avoids an invalid product bound for small blocks. Thus for the nontrivial sectors the advertised combined upper bound is valid in both cases.

6. QUOTIENT CONTRACTION
Let nu(w)=2^(-n)H(w)/a. This is a probability measure. From b_0>=ell and G<=a u,
 K_0(z,w)>=alpha nu(w).
Since0<alpha<1, write K_0=alpha 1 nu^T+(1-alpha)R with R stochastic. For a real nonconstant eigenfunction F of K_0 with eigenvalue lambda!=1, its oscillation osc(F)=maxF-minF ispositive. Markov averaging cannot increase oscillation, so
 |lambda| osc(F)=osc(K_0 F)<=(1-alpha)osc(F).
Thus every nonconstant eigenvalue in the quotient sector is at most1-alpha in absolute value. Constants are its only eigenvectors at1 by strict positivity. Combining this with section5 proves the upper bound for lambda_2 of the FULL K, not merely a lumped chain.

7. A NEAR-RHO MODE AND THE TWO-SIDED GAP LIMIT
Choose t to consist of a single coordinate in block1 and no others. Since m>=2, t is not in C, so V_t is orthogonal to constants under pi. Formula(4.1) gives
 b_t-rho b_0
 =(rho^(m-1)-rho^(m+1)) z_1 w_1 product_(i>=2)(1+theta z_i w_i).
Hence |b_t-rho b_0|<=epsilon b_0 pointwise. Applying(4.2) to F=1 gives
 |K_t1-rho|<=epsilon K_0 1=epsilon.
The original test function f=chi_t has stationary norm1 and stationary mean0. Its Rayleigh quotient is
 <f,Kf>_(pi)=E_Pi[K_t1]>=rho-epsilon.
The variational characterization for the self-adjoint K therefore gives lambda_2>=rho-epsilon. Positive semidefiniteness adds lambda_2>=0. This proves the lower eigenvalue bound; it does not infer an eigenfunction from an approximate pointwise value without a variational argument.

If m->infinity, theta->0, beta->rho and epsilon->0. If also n theta->0, then
 -log alpha=n log[(1+theta)/(1-theta)]->0.
For example, for theta<=1/2 the logarithm is at most4theta, proving the limit directly. Thus alpha->1 and max(1-alpha,beta)->rho<1. Both bounds squeeze lambda_2 to rho, equivalently gamma to1-rho. Every estimate is uniform over H and the number of residual coordinates. No amplitude, stationary cap, floor or normalization constant enters these bounds.

8. ACTUAL WEIGHTING COMPARISON
Under the macro stationary measure, E_Pi(G^(-2))=N/Z. By(2.1),
 1/(a^2 u^2) <= N/Z <= 1/(a^2 ell^2).
The identity r_h/pi=Z/(N G^2) therefore yields
 (ell/u)^2 <= r_h/pi <= (u/ell)^2,
which is exactly(C). Raising positive quantities to any fixed s>0 and integrating proves the moment inequalities. No Jensen step at s<1 is needed. In particular for s=(1+rho)/(1-rho), Epi^s<=2 gives Er_h^s<=2alpha^(-2s). When n rho^m->0 this tends to2 as an upper bound, but finite-dimensional values above2 remain permitted and agree with349. Neither(C) nor(D) asserts exact r_h matching.

9. RELEVANCE, NEGATIVES AND LIMITS
For example m can grow on the order of sqrt(L), n=floor(L/m), and r=L-nm; then n rho^m->0 for every fixedrho. Automatic exact matching reaches degree m-1. Choosing m>=floor(C sqrt(L))+1 makes this a legitimate construction class for the programme's matching condition, whenever the partition exists (for fixedC it does for all sufficiently largeL). All positive macro H are covered, including many-level families and arbitrarily high amplitudes. Imposing the programme's additional floor and cap only restricts this class; it cannot invalidate the bounds. Therefore this class cannot furnish a collapsing-gap or divergent-critical-moment counterexample. The two-block parity construction of349 is a special case, with unused coordinates treated as residual.

The obstruction beyond this class is concrete. General exact marginal matching of pi only removes low-degree Walsh coefficients; it does not force Fourier support in the span of disjoint full-block parities. General linear codes likewise need not have the product kernel(4.1). Theorems351(A)-(D) do not extend to those classes without another argument. No statement about the original gap problem, Yang--Mills, the continuum, formal verification or historical originality follows.

Two tempting incorrect shortcuts were explicitly avoided:
- Quotient minorization by itself misses all nontrivial Fourier sectors of the full chain.
- Multiplying beta bounds and then replacing beta^q by beta is valid only when beta<1; the small-m case uses the trivial global spectral bound instead.
