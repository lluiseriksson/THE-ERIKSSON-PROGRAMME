3. ORIGINAL OPERATOR, LOW-REGION MASS, AND KILLING

The assumptions are L>=1, 0<rho<1, h>0, g=P_rho h,
E_mu(hg)=1, pi=hg>=b with 0<b<1, and uniform cube measure mu. The
Doob kernel K=M_(1/g) P M_h is Markov. Its reversible probability is
nu=pi mu, and conjugacy by sqrt(pi) gives S=M_sqrt(h/g) P M_sqrt(h/g).
Noise P is self-adjoint positive definite and contractive; the Markov
conjugacy establishes that S is a positive semidefinite contraction.
In particular S^2<=S is legitimate. The Perron vector is sqrt(pi).

The constant Walsh component gives (E h)^2<=E hPh=1, and stochasticity
gives E g=E h<=1. For B={h<a}, 0<a<=b/4, one therefore has
nu(B)=E 1_B hg<=a E g<=a. Since pi>=b, mu(B)<=nu(B)/b<=a/b<=1/4.
These bounds do not require the critical cap or matching. They are not
interchangeable with an unproved assertion that rare sets have small killed
norm. C=B^c has at least three quarters of the cube states; for L=1 the
integer count forces both states into C. Thus the trace always has at least
two states and its nonconstant spectral gap is well defined.

On B, U=h/g=h^2/pi<=a^2/b=theta. The inequality theta<=b/16<1/16
is correct. Extending a B-supported vector by zero and using ||P||<=1
proves 0<=A=S_BB<=theta I. This is a bound on the actual killed
operator, derived from the stationary floor. It says nothing analogous
about {g small}, where h/g can be large. Conjugacy carries the bound to
K_BB on L2(nu restricted to B); this measure is not renormalized in the
block calculations.

When B is nonempty and the initial law is nu conditioned on B, the survival
probability for entrance time T_C is
<1_B,K_BB^n 1_B>_nu/nu(B). The operator bound gives at most theta^n.
At n=0 both sides equal one, consistent with the chain starting inside B.
Summing tails for a positive integer entrance time gives E T_C<=1/(1-theta).
This is a conditioned-stationary average, not a uniform starting-state
bound. The explicit restriction B nonempty avoids division by zero.

4. TRACE MARKOV CHAIN AND BLOCK INEQUALITIES

The Neumann series for (I-K_BB)^-1 converges, because its spectral radius
is at most theta<1. Expanding K_CB K_BB^n K_BC enumerates excursions
which spend n+1 visits in B between visits to C. The additional K_CC
term includes a direct next visit. This is the trace chain, not reflection
or killing. The equation (I-K_BB)1_B=K_BC1_C, followed by the C row-sum
equation, proves K^C1_C=1_C. Reversibility follows from the original
reversible block operators. Its invariant probability is nu(.|C).

On the corresponding uniform C coordinate space, scalar normalizations
cancel and its self-adjoint conjugate is
T=S_CC+D*(I-A)^-1 D, D=S_BC. Each summand is positive semidefinite.
P(x,y)>0 for all x,y when 0<rho<1 and h>0, so K_CC is strictly positive
entrywise. Hence the trace is irreducible and has a simple Perron eigenvalue
one; other eigenvalues lie in [0,1). No negative-eigenvalue or absolute-gap
ambiguity arises. Both t=gap(K^C) and gamma=gap(K) lie in (0,1].

Taking the B diagonal block of S^2<=S gives A^2+DD*<=A. Congruence by
(I-A)^-1 gives HH*<=A(I-A)^-1 for H=(I-A)^-1D. Because A commutes
with its functions, spectral calculus gives ||H||^2<=theta/(1-theta).
Similarly R=(I-A)^-1/2D has RR*<=A<=theta I. Equality of the nonzero
singular values, or the definition of operator norm, then gives
R*R<=theta I on C. Thus 0<=T-S_CC=R*R<=theta I has the correct
operator domain and orientation. There is no unjustified commutation of D
with A. Empty B produces zero correction and K^C=K throughout.

5. HARMONIC EXTENSION AND TWO-SIDED GAP TRANSFER

All norms in this argument use the restricted unnormalized measures unless
the explicitly normalized conditional expectation is written. The isometry
given by sqrt(pi) transfers the bound on H to
H_K=(I-K_BB)^-1 K_BC. For e(phi)=(H_K phi,phi), the B component of
(I-K)e(phi) vanishes. Expanding the quadratic form of e(phi)+w, with w
supported on B, cancels its two cross terms and leaves precisely
<phi,(I-K^C)phi>_(nu|C)+<w,(I-K_BB)w>_(nu|B).
This is the exact Schur complement identity R2.

For arbitrary f, take c=E_(nu_C) f_C and phi=f_C-c. Then phi is centered
in the correct C measure, f-c=e(phi)+w, and
E(f)>=t||phi||_C^2+(1-theta)||w||_B^2.
The bound on H_K gives ||e(phi)||^2<=||phi||_C^2/(1-theta).
Variance is the minimum over constants, so using this particular c and
then the triangle inequality gives
Var_nu(f)<=(||phi||_C/sqrt(1-theta)+||w||_B)^2.
Weighted scalar Cauchy--Schwarz against the preceding two energy terms
has factor 1/((1-theta)t)+1/(1-theta). Its reciprocal is exactly
(1-theta)t/(1+t). This proves the lower bound of R1. The argument covers
phi=0 and w=0, and does not require e(phi) to have zero global mean.

6. EFFECTIVE BASE KERNEL, SUB-MARKOV PROPERTY, AND CONDITIONAL DENSITY

For nonempty B, V=diag(1/U_B)-P_BB is symmetric positive definite:
diag(1/U_B)>=theta^-1 I and P_BB<=I. The stated factorization of its
inverse has the correct order,
V^-1=M_U (I-P_BB M_U)^-1.
The product P_BB M_U has norm at most theta in the uniform coordinate
space, so its nonnegative Neumann series converges and V^-1 is entrywise
nonnegative. No inference from positive definiteness alone to inverse
entrywise positivity is made. The diagonal of the inverse is positive.

Since g_B=(1/U_B)h_B=P_BB h_B+P_BC h_C, one gets
Vh_B=P_BC h_C and hence P_eff h_C=g_C. An explicit Doob calculation is
I-K_BB=M_(1/g_B) V M_(h_B), so
(I-K_BB)^-1=M_(1/h_B) V^-1 M_(g_B).
Insertion into the trace formula cancels the B multipliers and gives
K^C=M_(1/g_C)[P_CC+P_CB V^-1 P_BC]M_(h_C).
Thus R3--R4 concern the actual trace, not a chosen replacement chain.
Symmetry and positive semidefiniteness of P_eff follow from the compression
and the positive congruence. Its entries are strictly positive because the
P_CC entries are strictly positive.

Let v=V^-1 P_BC1_C. Since V1_B-P_BC1_C=1/U_B-1_B>=0 and V^-1>=0
entrywise, 0<=v<=1_B. Therefore P_eff1_C=P_CC1_C+P_CBv<=1_C.
For nonempty B, U_B<=theta<1 makes the right-hand difference strictly
positive componentwise. The positive inverse diagonal ensures v<1_B;
P_CB is entrywise strictly positive, giving P_eff1_C<1_C pointwise.
The strict sub-Markov conclusion is valid and is appropriately restricted
to nonempty B. It is compatible with K^C being stochastic, because its
Doob denominator is g_C=P_eff h_C, rather than an assumed constant row sum.

The stationary probability of the trace is nu_C. Relative to normalized
uniform measure mu_C its density is pi_C=pi|C mu(C)/nu(C). Since
mu(C)>=3/4 and nu(C)<=1, pi_C>=3b/4. If the original cap is imposed,
E_muC pi_C^s=mu(C)^(s-1)nu(C)^(-s) E_mu[1_C pi^s]
<=2/(1-a)^s, using s>1 and nu(C)>=1-a. The normalization factors and
exponents are correct. The floor in fact holds without the cap; the
candidate presents both estimates together under a conditional sentence,
which does not make a false implication. The early statement that the cap
is unnecessary for sections 2--5 is read as referring to the trace/gap
results: the final cap inheritance is expressly conditional.
