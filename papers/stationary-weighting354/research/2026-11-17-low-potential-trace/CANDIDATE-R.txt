1. Scope and outcome

This note derives a uniformly transient low-potential region from the original
stationary floor, and eliminates it by the classical trace-chain construction.
It gives an exact effective kernel and two-sided quantitative gap transfer.
It does not prove the remaining core gap, derive a positive minimum of h on
the whole cube, or solve the stationary-gap conjecture. The classical trace
construction is not claimed as novel. This is a bounded analytic reduction,
not a separate paper, PDF assessment, formal verification or publication.
No original hypothesis has been removed. In particular, moving the unresolved
gap to a trace chain is not counted as proving that gap or removing a premise.

2. Original model and the region that can actually be controlled

Let L>=1, 0<rho<1, and let mu be uniform probability on {-1,1}^L.
Write P=P_rho, h>0, g=Ph, E_mu(hg)=1, pi=hg>=b, 0<b<1,
nu=pi mu, U=h/g. The actual reversible kernel is
  K(x,y)=P(x,y)h(y)/g(x).
Its self-adjoint conjugate on L2(mu) is S=M_sqrt(U) P M_sqrt(U).
It is a positive semidefinite contraction, with Perron vector sqrt(pi).
Indeed conjugacy gives the Markov contraction, and P has Walsh eigenvalues
rho^|A|>0. The stationary density cap E pi^s<=2 and exact labelled matching,
s=(1+rho)/(1-rho), may be imposed but are not needed for Sections 2--5.

Fix 0<a<=b/4, put B={h<a}, C=B^c and theta=a^2/b<=b/16<1/16.
The constant Walsh coefficient in E hPh gives (E h)^2<=1, so E g=E h<=1.
Consequently
  nu(B)=E[1_B h g]<=a E g<=a,
  mu(B)<=nu(B)/b<=a/b<=1/4.
Thus C is nonempty and has at least two states, including when L=1.
On B the original floor gives
  U=h^2/pi<=a^2/b=theta.
For A=S_BB acting on the B coordinate space this implies
  0<=A<=theta I.
Proof: <f,Af>=<sqrt(U)f,P sqrt(U)f><=||sqrt(U)f||^2<=theta||f||^2.
This is an actual killed-operator bound derived from the floor, not inferred
from rare mass. It is for small h, not small g. Confusing these two regions
would incorrectly extend this result to the unresolved high-ratio set.
In the unnormalized L2(nu|B) space the same bound holds for K_BB by conjugacy.
For a chain started from nu conditioned on B, if B is nonempty, it follows
that P(T_C>n)<=theta^n and E T_C<=1/(1-theta), n>=0, where T_C is the first
entrance time in C. This averaged assertion is not a worst-start bound.
Indeed P(T_C>n)=<1_B,K_BB^n 1_B>_nu/nu(B).

3. The trace chain, not the reflected or killed chain

If B is nonempty define
  K^C=K_CC+K_CB(I-K_BB)^(-1)K_BC.
The inverse is its convergent nonnegative Neumann series. This is the chain
observed at successive visits to C, allowing immediate C-to-C steps. Its
rows sum to one: (I-K_BB)1_B=K_BC 1_C, and the original row-sum identity on C
then applies. It is reversible for nu_C=nu(.|C). Conjugating by sqrt(pi) on C
(the constant normalization of nu_C cancels) gives
  T=S_CC+D^*(I-A)^(-1)D,   D=S_BC.
The operator is positive semidefinite, since S_CC>=0 and the added term is
positive. The chain is irreducible because P and h are strictly positive.
Its simple eigenvalue 1 has eigenvector sqrt(pi)|C, and all other eigenvalues
lie in [0,1). Put t=gap(K^C) and gamma=gap(K). Both lie in (0,1].
When B is empty all formulas have their empty-block interpretation and K^C=K.

There are useful uniform operator bounds beyond invertibility. From S^2<=S,
the B diagonal block gives
  D D^*<=A-A^2=A(I-A).
Set H=(I-A)^(-1)D and R=(I-A)^(-1/2)D. Congruence therefore yields
  H H^*<=A(I-A)^(-1)<=theta/(1-theta) I,
  R R^*<=A<=theta I.
In particular
  ||H||^2<=theta/(1-theta),
  0<=T-S_CC=R^*R<=theta I.
These are statements about the exact original S and its actual trace. No
operator-norm claim is made for a surrogate selected independently of h.

4. Gap comparison with all variance normalizations retained

The quantitative conclusion is
  (1-theta)t/(1+t) <= gamma <= t.                         (R1)
In particular (15/32)t <= gamma <= t. Thus uniform full gaps in this original
family and in its derived trace family are equivalent, with fixed constants.
This equivalence alone does not prove either one.

Here is a self-contained variational proof. Work with functions in L2(nu)
and its B,C orthogonal decomposition, with restricted but UNNORMALIZED
measures nu|B and nu|C. Let L0=I-K. For a function phi on C its harmonic
extension is e(phi)=(H_K phi,phi), where H_K=(I-K_BB)^(-1)K_BC.
The operator norm bounds for H carry to these spaces by conjugacy.
Completing the square in the quadratic form gives, for w supported on B,
  <e(phi)+w,L0(e(phi)+w)>_nu
    =<phi,(I-K^C)phi>_(nu|C)+<w,(I-K_BB)w>_(nu|B).       (R2)
This follows by expanding: the B harmonic equation cancels both cross terms,
and the remaining C block is the Schur complement. Hence no additive
error, uncontrolled excursion term or changed stationary variance is dropped.

For any f subtract the constant c=E_(nu_C) f|C. Write f-c=e(phi)+w with
phi=f|C-c centered for nu_C. Let E=<f,(I-K)f>_nu. By (R2),
  E>=t||phi||_(nu|C)^2+(1-theta)||w||_(nu|B)^2.
Also ||e(phi)||_nu^2<=||phi||^2/(1-theta). Thus
  Var_nu(f)<=||f-c||_nu^2
     <= (||phi||/sqrt(1-theta)+||w||)^2
     <= [1/((1-theta)t)+1/(1-theta)] E,
where the last step is scalar weighted Cauchy--Schwarz. This proves the
lower bound in (R1), including phi=0 or w=0.

For the upper bound choose a nonconstant t-eigenfunction phi of K^C,
centered for nu_C. Set f=e(phi). Its energy equals t||phi||_(nu|C)^2.
For every constant c,
  ||f-c||_nu^2>=||phi-c||_(nu|C)^2>=||phi||_(nu|C)^2.
Minimizing in c proves Var_nu(f)>=||phi||_(nu|C)^2. The full-chain Rayleigh
quotient is therefore at most t. This proves gamma<=t without an assumption
that the harmonic extension has global mean zero.

5. Exact effective base kernel and the obstruction to importing J

For B nonempty let
  V=diag(1/U|B)-P_BB,
  P_eff=P_CC+P_CB V^(-1)P_BC.                            (R3)
V is positive definite. In fact diag(1/U)>=theta^(-1)I and ||P_BB||<=1;
its inverse is nonnegative, as can also be seen from
  V^(-1)=M_U (I-P_BB M_U)^(-1)
on B, with the nonnegative series converging since ||P_BB M_U||<=theta.
The block identity g_B=P_BC h_C+P_BB h_B gives V h_B=P_BC h_C.
Consequently
  P_eff h_C=g_C,
  K^C=M_(1/g_C) P_eff M_(h_C).                         (R4)
Formula (R3) follows as well by inserting the Doob factors in the trace
series. P_eff is symmetric and positive semidefinite: P_CC is positive
semidefinite and the other summand is a positive congruence. It is strictly
positive entrywise because P_CC is.

P_eff is generally SUB-Markov, not the Boolean noise kernel on a smaller
cube. To check the asserted direction, write v=V^(-1)P_BC 1_C. Since
  V1_B-P_BC1_C=(1/U|B)-1_B>=0
and V^(-1) is nonnegative, 0<=v<=1_B. Therefore
  P_eff1_C=P_CC1_C+P_CB v<=P_CC1_C+P_CB1_B=1_C.
If B is nonempty, U<1 on B, strict positivity implies v<1_B and hence
P_eff1_C<1_C pointwise. Thus one must not use P_eff1=1, its uniform
stationarity, Boolean hypercontractivity or the Walsh matching identities
of P as if they survived unchanged on C. Although h_C>=a is now true,
the positive-background theorem J is NOT directly applicable to (R4).

With mu_C=mu(.|C), the trace stationary density is
  pi_C=pi|C * mu(C)/nu(C).
If the original critical cap holds then
  pi_C>=b mu(C)/nu(C)>=3b/4,
  E_(mu_C) pi_C^s
     =mu(C)^(s-1) nu(C)^(-s) E_mu[1_C pi^s]
     <=2/(1-a)^s.
These inherited estimates do not restore exact labelled matching on the
conditioned state space, which need not even be a cube. They do not close
the gap proof. No extra moment, background-kernel inequality or trace gap
is being reclassified as an original hypothesis already established.

6. Controls, failures, and the next bounded step

Q's special point has h(x0)->infinity and g(x0)->0, so it lies in C for
large L; this proof does not delete the unresolved high-ratio mechanism.
The already established good full gaps for Q and P imply good trace gaps
by gamma<=t, not by new numerics or an invented pointwise denominator floor.
K/L have low h at rare sites, but this note uses the floor to prove their
low-h killed control; it does not deduce the full gap from rare mass alone.

The tempting attempted shortcut was: remove B, use h_C>=a, apply J. It fails
because P_eff1<1 when B is nonempty; its required background and noise
inequalities have not been established. This failed inference is retained.
The exact open task is a conductance or energy/variance estimate for (R3)--(R4)
derived from the original full-cube floor/cap/matching identities. Replacing
it with the premise gap(K^C)>=c would merely rename the original problem.
R is useful for disposing of low-h excursions and locating the remaining
obstruction, but is conservatively not counted as a substantial resolution
of the high-ratio route. The nonadvancing-route counter becomes 2, following
the Q integration unit. After one more nonadvancing unit change mechanism
or identify the concrete mathematical obstruction. Only one question remains.
