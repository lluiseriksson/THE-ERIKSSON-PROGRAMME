BOUNDED QUESTION AND SCOPE
The single active research question remains the stationary-doob-gap question.
This unit tests one possible regularization step within that question: does
h -> P_tau h, followed by energy normalization, preserve exact stationary
matching under the original stationary floor and critical moment cap?
We prove that it does not. The stationary floor is always preserved; in the
counterexample the moment cap also survives, but even a first marginal fails.
This is an actual Doob construction, not an unrelated abstract density.
It does not exhibit a collapsing gap, remove any original hypothesis, or
settle the general gap question. It belongs with the grouped L/M results,
not in a separate paper containing a small corollary.

Conventions: uniform probability mu on {-1,1}^L; E denotes its expectation.
chi_S(x)=product_{i in S} x_i. P=P_rho is the Boolean noise kernel,
P chi_S=rho^{|S|} chi_S, with fixed 0<rho<1. For positive h normalized by
E hPh=1, the actual kernel is K_h(x,y)=P(x,y)h(y)/(Ph)(x), with stationary
density pi=hPh. The original assumptions used here are pi>=b, E pi^s<=2,
s=(1+rho)/(1-rho), 0<b<1, and E pi chi_S=0 for 1<=|S|<=k.
All inequalities between functions are pointwise unless a norm is specified.

PROPOSITION N.1 (floor preservation, without a matching assumption)
Let R=P_tau, 0<=tau<=1, H=Rh, Z=E HPH, and h_tau=H/sqrt(Z).
Then pi_tau=h_tau P h_tau >= b whenever pi>=b.
Proof. Noise kernels commute, so HPH=(Rh)R(Ph). Cauchy--Schwarz in
each probability row of R gives (Rh)R(Ph)>=(R sqrt(hPh))^2>=b.
Walsh Parseval gives Z=sum_S rho^{|S|} tau^{2|S|} hhat(S)^2 <=
sum_S rho^{|S|} hhat(S)^2=1. Here the constant term has multiplier 1,
including tau=0. Z>0. Thus pi_tau>=b/Z>=b. This proves the assertion.
No general preservation of the moment cap is asserted by this proposition.

THEOREM N.2 (exact matching fails after every nontrivial smoothing)
Fix rho,b as above, L>=3 and 1<=k<=L-2. There exists a positive,
energy-normalized actual potential h such that:
  (a) pi=hPh has every labelled marginal of order <=k equal to uniform;
  (b) pi>=b and E pi^s<=2;
  (c) for every 0<=tau<=1, pi_tau>=b and E pi_tau^s<=sqrt(2)<2;
  (d) for every 0<tau<1, E pi_tau chi_{j} is nonzero for a specified j.
In particular the regularization fails to preserve the original admissible
class even though the floor and cap hold before and after regularization.

PROOF, INCLUDING EXISTENCE OF THE ACTUAL POTENTIAL
Choose A of size m=k+1 and j outside A; set B=A union {j}.
Set alpha=rho^m and beta=rho^{m+1}=alpha*rho, and fix the positive number
  epsilon=min{1/8, (1-b)/4, (log 2)/(64s)}.
This choice is independent of L and k. Define
  w(x)=1+epsilon(chi_A(x)+chi_B(x)).
It has mean 1, w>=1-2epsilon>=max{3/4,(1+b)/2}>b, and its only nonzero
nonconstant Walsh coefficients are at A and B, both of degree >k.
Thus all the stated low-degree coefficients vanish. Fourier expansion of
each coordinate-marginal indicator shows this is equivalent to the stated
labelled marginal equality.

We construct h on a four-point quotient, thereby avoiding a dimension
dependent inverse bound. Put y=chi_A(x) and z=x_j. Under uniform mu these
are independent uniform signs. Boolean rho-noise induces on (y,z) the
product kernel Q=P_alpha on y tensor P_rho on z. Indeed the independent
sign flips on the disjoint sets A and {j} have means alpha and rho. Hence
P[f(chi_A,x_j)]=(Qf)(chi_A,x_j) for every function f on these four points.
Q is strictly positive, symmetric, stochastic and positive semidefinite,
with eigenvalues 1, alpha, rho and beta.

For completeness the required inverse result is proved here, specializing
the already audited lemma inversestationary in the grouped source.
For any strictly positive mean-one four-point w, define on R^4
  F(v)=1/2 sum_{a,d} mu_4(a) Q(a,d) exp(v_a+v_d)
       -sum_a mu_4(a) w(a) v_a.
Its Hessian is positive definite: all exponential terms are convex and
the positive diagonal terms are strictly convex in every coordinate.
Writing M=max v_a and d0=1/2 min_a mu_4(a)Q(a,a)>0 gives
F(v)>=d0 exp(2M)-M. Thus a sublevel set has M bounded above. If some
coordinate tends to minus infinity with all coordinates bounded above,
the strictly positive linear weights force F to infinity. Sublevel sets
are therefore bounded. The unique minimizer solves exp(v)Qexp(v)=w.
It defines the unique positive solution g, and the implicit function
theorem gives smooth dependence along a positive path w_t.

Apply this construction to w_t=1+t epsilon(y+yz), 0<=t<=1. This path
is at least 1/2. Let g_t Q g_t=w_t, g_0=1. The actual reversible kernel
J_t(a,d)=Q(a,d)g_t(d)/(Qg_t)(a) has stationary probability nu_t=w_t mu_4
and spectrum in [0,1]. Positivity of the spectrum follows from its
self-adjoint conjugate M_sqrt(g_t/Qg_t) Q M_sqrt(g_t/Qg_t); the upper
bound follows from its being a Markov kernel. Since nu_t>=1/8, set
T_t=(I-J_t)/2. Its absolute row sum is 1-J_t(a,a)<=1, and its L2(nu_t)
norm is at most 1/2. Consequently
  ||T_t^r||_{infinity->infinity} <= min{1,sqrt(8)*2^{-r}}.
The first bound uses the row norm. The second uses ||f||_2<=||f||_infty
and |f(a)|<=||f||_2/sqrt(min nu_t). The Neumann series yields
  ||(I+J_t)^(-1)||_infinity
  <=1/2 [2+sum_{r>=2} sqrt(8)*2^{-r}] <=2.
Differentiating log(g_t Qg_t)=log w_t gives
  (I+J_t) d(log g_t)/dt = epsilon(y+yz)/w_t.
The right hand side has infinity norm <=4epsilon. Integrating gives
  ||log g_1||_infty <=8epsilon=:eta <=(log 2)/(8s).
Lift h(x)=g_1(chi_A(x),x_j). Then hPh=w exactly, E hPh=1, and
  exp(-eta)<=h<=exp(eta).
Existence, exact matching, and normalization have therefore been proved,
not introduced as extra assumptions on a purported potential.

For every tau in [0,1], H=P_tau h and PH lie in the same interval.
Thus exp(-2eta)<=Z<=exp(2eta), and pi_tau=HPH/Z<=exp(4eta).
It follows that E pi_tau^s<=exp(4s eta)<=sqrt(2). Proposition N.1
proves pi_tau>=b. This includes tau=1, which is the original h.

Every function on the four-point quotient has the unique expansion
  h=c+d chi_j+e chi_A+f chi_B, with c=E h>0.
Multiplying this expansion by its P-image gives the following exact
coefficients, using chi_A chi_B=chi_j:
  (1+rho)c d+(alpha+beta)e f=0,                         (N1)
  (1+alpha)c e+(rho+beta)d f=epsilon,                   (N2)
  (1+beta)c f+(rho+alpha)d e=epsilon.                   (N3)
If e=0, (N1) implies d=0, contradicting (N2). If f=0, (N1) again
implies d=0, contradicting (N3). Hence e f is nonzero.
The smoothed potential is exactly
  H=c+tau d chi_j+tau^m e chi_A+tau^{m+1} f chi_B.
Its product HPH has chi_j coefficient
  (1+rho)c tau d+(alpha+beta)tau^{2m+1}e f.
Using (N1) and dividing by Z proves the explicit identity
  E pi_tau chi_j = (alpha+beta)e f (tau^{2m+1}-tau)/Z.  (N4)
All factors other than the final difference are nonzero, and for
0<tau<1 with m>=2 the difference is strictly negative. This proves (d)
without a perturbative remainder or a numerical nonzero test.
At tau=0 the density is uniform; at tau=1 it is the original matched
density. Neither endpoint is claimed to break matching. QED.

GROWING MATCHING REGIME AND LIMITATIONS
For any fixed C>0, k_L=min{L,floor(C sqrt(L))} satisfies 1<=k_L<=L-2
for all sufficiently large L. Apply N.2 in those dimensions and take h=1
in the finitely many remaining dimensions. This supplies a family obeying
the original growing matching regime but failing first-marginal matching
after every fixed nontrivial smoothing in all sufficiently large dimensions.
No dimension-uniform lower bound on the size of the nonzero defect is
claimed; alpha tends to zero as k grows. Exact equality, not approximate
agreement, is the hypothesis refuted by smoothing. The family has a fixed
positive minimum potential and is not a collapsing-gap construction.

MECHANISM, FALSIFIER AND ABANDONMENT
Mechanism: the two high-degree characters have a low-degree symmetric
difference. Their contribution to the stationary product cancels a low
potential coefficient before smoothing. Noise weights these two terms by
different powers, so the nonlinear cancellation is destroyed.
Falsifier of this proof: failure of quotient existence/stability, a wrong
coefficient in N1--N4, or a nontrivial tau with N4 zero despite e f nonzero.
The proposed exact-admissibility-preserving potential-smoothing route is
abandoned if the counterexample passes first analytic audit. No further
search on that unconditional invariance assertion is needed.
Next bounded test within the SAME active gap question: smoothing the
stationary density itself and reconstructing its unique potential preserves
matching and the floor/cap, but a quantitative actual spectral comparison
must be proved rather than assumed. Alternatively work directly on small
Ph regions. No extra hypothesis on Ph may be disguised as elimination.
