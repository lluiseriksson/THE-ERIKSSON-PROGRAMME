\begin{abstract}
For half-correlated Boolean noise we characterize recovery of the full ordered spectrum after a reversible Doob transform by one-step returns inside a single exceptional set. The set consists of vertices where the potential divided by its one-step noise average exceeds an exponential threshold in the order of stationary marginal matching. With a bounded stationary moment of order above three the criterion holds for arbitrary potentials without a positive uniform density floor. On quotients with two disjoint coordinate bases it holds at every moment order above $5/3$, including the critical order $15/7$. We recover all eigenvalues with multiplicity from ordinary noise and the chain killed outside the exceptional set. A family with bounded stationary densities disproves an alternative test by unnormalized Schatten norms of any finite order prescribed from the matching order. The paper also gives sufficient recovery estimates using fractional base packing and code weight enumerators, gap bounds without matching, and examples separating control of the leading bands from control of the whole spectrum.
\end{abstract}
