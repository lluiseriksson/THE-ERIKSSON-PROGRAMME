% Source supplement for Rank defects and spectral stability of Boolean Doob chains.
\section{Fractional basis packing and a direct inverse moment}

Use the notation of the rank paper, with $P=P_{1/2}$, $Q=P_{1/\sqrt2}$,
$g=Ph$, $\pi=hg$, $E\pi=1$ and $U=h/g$. Let $A$ have full row rank
and let $\pi$ be a function of $Ax$. By uniqueness of positive symmetric
scaling, $h$ and $g$ are functions of $Ax$ as well. A fractional packing of
weight $t$ assigns weights $w_J\ge0$ to column bases $J$ of $A$, with
$\sum_Jw_J=t$ and $\sum_{J\ni i}w_J\le1$ for each input column $i$.
Repeated columns have separate capacities.

Suppose $\|\pi\|_p\le B$ for fixed $2\le p<\infty$, and the stationary
marginals match the uniform law through degree $k$. If $t$ is fixed and
\[
       \frac1p+\frac{2}{4^t-1}<\frac{2^t-1}{2^t+1},                 \tag{P1}
\]
then the entire ordered spectrum differs from that of $P$ by at most
$C_{B,p,t}e^{-c_{p,t}k}$, with $c_{p,t}>0$. No stationary minimum
uniform in dimension is assumed. In particular, at $p=2$ it suffices that
$t>\log_2(2+\sqrt5)$, so three disjoint full bases suffice. At $t=2$
it suffices that $p>15/7$, which includes the programme's stationary
moment endpoint. The strict boundary in (P1) is not decided.

\paragraph{Allocating noise time.}
On the quotient, the noise contributed by column $i$ acts by retaining
or translating by that column. Its multipliers are $1$ and $\lambda$.
Thus two factors in the same column multiply their correlations.
Allocate a factor of correlation $\lambda^{w_J}$ in each column of $J$
to basis $J$, omitting zero weights. The unused capacity is another
commuting noise factor. Each basis acts as ordinary product noise on the
entire quotient in its own coordinates. Successive applications of
forward hypercontractivity give contraction from $a>1$ to
$1+(a-1)\lambda^{-2t}$. For a positive input and $0<a<1$, reverse
hypercontractivity gives the lower bound from $a$ to
$1-(1-a)\lambda^{-2t}$, including negative target orders. This follows
by using the successive equality exponents of the classical inequalities.
If an intermediate order vanishes, use the geometric mean by continuity;
all functions are strictly positive on the finite quotient. Leftover
noise contracts forward norms and increases the generalized reverse norm
by Jensen. These steps use the usual time convention for Boolean noise
(\href{https://doi.org/10.5802/aif.357}{Bonami} and
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.1210}{Mossel, Oleszkiewicz and Sen,
Remark 1.13}). Fractional base packing itself is classical, for example
\href{https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ESA.2026.57}{Dynamic Matroids,
Base Packing and Covering}.

\paragraph{Start with the potential.}
The normalization $\|Qh\|_2=1$ implies $\|g\|_2\le1$. Since $E\pi=1$
and $\|\pi\|_2\le B$, Cauchy--Schwarz gives
$\mu\{\pi\ge1/2\}\ge1/(4B^2)$. Also $\mu\{g>4B\}\le1/(16B^2)$.
On an intersection of measure at least $3/(16B^2)$, we thus have
$h=\pi/g\ge1/(8B)$. For every fixed $a>0$, this bounds $\|h\|_a$
from below solely in terms of $a,B$. In particular, for $0<q<4^t-1$,
use $a=1-(1+q)4^{-t}$ and the reverse estimate on $Ph$, directly, to obtain
$\|g^{-1}\|_q\le C_{B,t,q}$. This spends the full noise time on the
potential and requires neither a floor on $\pi$ nor matching in this step.

\paragraph{Ambient operator comparison.}
Choose $q<4^t-1$ and $s>1$ so that
\[
 \frac1p+\frac2q<\frac1s<\frac{2^t-1}{2^t+1}.
\]
Then $U=\pi/g^2$ is uniformly bounded in $L^v$ for
$1/v=1/p+2/q<1/s$. For any nonnegative nonzero quotient weight $W$,
the unique positive unit leading eigenvector of $Q M_W Q$ is invariant
under all translations in $\ker A$. Forward hypercontractivity on this
vector, with $2s/(s-1)<1+2^t$, bounds $\|Q M_W Q\|\le\|W\|_s$
on the full cube. Zero weights satisfy the same bound.

The matching-energy estimate of the rank paper gives
$\|g-1\|_2\le C_B2^{-k/2}$. On
$E_k=\{|g-1|>2^{-k/8}\}$ the measure is at most $C_B2^{-3k/4}$.
H\"older bounds $\|(U-\pi){\bf1}_{E_k}\|_s$ by a constant times
$\mu(E_k)^{1/s-1/v}$. Outside $E_k$, $|U-\pi|\le C2^{-k/8}\pi$
for large $k$. The multiplier bound applied to $|U-\pi|$ therefore
gives exponential decay of $\|Q M_{U-\pi}Q\|$.

By the strict inequality for $1/p$, take $\lambda>1/\sqrt2$ sufficiently
close to $1/\sqrt2$ that $2p/(p-1)\le 1+\lambda^{-2t}$; take $\lambda<1$.
Set $\theta=1/(\sqrt2\lambda)<1$. The same positive multiplier argument
gives $\|P_\lambda M_{|\pi-1|}P_\lambda\|\le B+1$, hence the same
bound for its signed counterpart. In $Q=P_\lambda P_\theta$ split the
$P_\theta$ factors at degree $j=\lfloor k/2\rfloor$. The low-low term
against $\pi-1$ is zero by matching; the other three terms each have norm
at most $(B+1)\theta^{j+1}$. Thus $\|QM_UQ-P\|$ decays exponentially.
The two Gram products of $M_{\sqrt U}Q$ identify the spectrum of the
first operator with that of the stationary Doob chain. The claimed
comparison follows by min--max. For $p=2$, writing $u=2^t$, (P1)
reduces to $u^2-4u-1>0$. For $t=2$ it reduces to $1/p<7/15$.

\paragraph{Separation from the four-family rank estimate.}
In each of $n\ge2$ independent rank-two blocks use five columns of
types $(1,0),(0,1),(1,1)$ with multiplicities $2,2,1$. Assign total
weights $3/2,1/2,1/2$ to the three distinct pairs of types in the order
$(1,2),(1,3),(2,3)$, spreading each uniformly over the available copies.
Each column uses exactly its unit capacity. Normalize the block weights
to probabilities, take their product across blocks, and multiply by $5/2$.
This is a fractional packing of global bases of weight $5/2$.
The coordinate count $5n$ and the basis size $2n$ also give the matching
upper bound. The full code forces the four-family rank defect to be at
least $\lceil 2n-5n/4\rceil$ by the support formula of the rank paper.

For an explicit matched law, choose $z$ in the code nonzero in every block
and a basis $e_1,\ldots,e_{2n}$ consisting of words supported on single
blocks. Take $0<\epsilon_n<1$ tending to zero and set
\[
 \pi=1-\frac{1-\epsilon_n}{2n+1}
       \left(\chi_z+\sum_{i=1}^{2n}\chi_{z+e_i}\right).
\]
Every nonzero word in one block weighs at least three. All displayed
characters therefore have degree at least $3(n-1)$. They are distinct,
their differences span the code, and $\pi$ has mean one and takes values
between $\epsilon_n$ and two, attaining the lower bound at the origin.
Finite positive symmetric scaling realizes this law. Taking $k=3n-4$
makes the old four-family bound vacuous, whereas (P1) gives exponential
recovery at $p=2$. The example separates the two sufficient estimates
on the intrinsic code. It makes no exclusivity claim about all other
stability methods. The unrestricted endpoint without quotient information
remains open.
