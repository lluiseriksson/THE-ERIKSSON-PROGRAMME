# Conditional recovery on the fractional-packing boundary

Research note following section 7 at programme source 6b0f3b6a5990912514fc980ab18f30abd4ca876f. This is a subsequent analytic calculation in the drafting chat. It is outside the frozen public submission and its assessment.

Use the notation and positivity assumptions of section 7. All norms and integrals use uniform probability on the cube. Fix a packing of weight t and a stationary moment p >= 2 on the equality line of (P1). Set q = 4^t - 1 and s = (2^t+1)/(2^t-1), so 1/s = 1/p + 2/q. Suppose the matching order k diverges, the sequence of densities satisfies sup E[-log pi] <= D, and pi^p is uniformly integrable. Then the entire ordered spectrum recovers. A uniform pointwise floor is unnecessary. These are additional tail hypotheses at equality. The unrestricted boundary is open.

Since E g = E h <= ||Qh||_2 = 1, Jensen gives E log g <= 0 and E log h = E log pi - E log g >= -D. The reverse quotient noise inequality from section 7, applied at positive input order a and output order 1-(1-a)4^t, passes to input order zero on each finite cube. Thus ||1/g||_q <= exp(-E log h) <= exp(D). No exchange of a limit with a supremum over dimensions occurs. The zero-order norm is the geometric mean. The Boolean reverse inequality and continuity of generalized norms are recorded by Mossel, Oleszkiewicz and Sen, equation (1.2) and Remark 1.13. https://arxiv.org/pdf/1108.1210

Let B bound ||pi||_p, let E_k = {|g-1| > 2^(-k/8)} and let omega(d) be the supremum of ||pi 1_A||_p over all family members and events with uniform measure at most d. Uniform integrability implies omega(d) tends to zero. Matching gives mu(E_k) <= C_B 2^(-3k/4). For U = pi/g^2, Holder at the exact exponents yields ||U 1_Ek||_s <= exp(2D) omega(mu(E_k)). Also ||pi 1_Ek||_s <= omega(mu(E_k)) and outside E_k the multiplier difference has s-norm <= C B 2^(-k/8). The positive multiplier bound holds at equality: 2s/(s-1) = 1+2^t, the included endpoint of the ordinary forward contraction. It therefore bounds the whole operator Q M_(U-pi) Q. For Q M_(pi-1) Q there is still strict slack since 1/p < 1/s. The existing low/high matching argument gives exponential decay. Min-max now bounds the ordered error by C exp(-ck) + C_D omega(C_B 2^(-3k/4)).

A quantitative instance is sup E[pi^p log(e+pi)^alpha] <= M for fixed alpha > 0. For an event of measure d < 1/2, split at height d^(-1/(2p)). The expectation of pi^p restricted to the event is <= sqrt(d) + M/[log(e+d^(-1/(2p)))]^alpha. Substitution gives error O((1+k)^(-alpha/p)). At t=2 the included p is exactly 15/7.

Stress check on the extra lower-tail hypothesis. Take n independent quotient coordinates, each represented by two copies of its column. The maximum packing weight is two, by counting columns. Let z be the all-ones quotient frequency and e_i the coordinate frequencies. For n>=3 set pi=1-(1-eps)/(n+1) times the sum of the characters at z and z+e_i, meaning pi = 1 - [(1-eps)/(n+1)](chi_z + sum_i chi_(z+e_i)). Its mean is one, its values are in [eps,2], and the nonconstant Fourier degrees on the ambient cube are 2n or 2n-2. The differences of the frequencies span the entire quotient. At the zero quotient, with mass 2^(-n), pi=eps. With eps=exp(-4^n), E[-log pi] >= 2^n-log 2, although every finite positive moment is uniformly bounded. This refutes deducing the log bound from matching and a positive moment. It does not disprove recovery for this family.

Next research test: seek a bound on the joint upper-density and inverse-noise tail on E_k without global reverse entropy. A counterexample must retain the fixed p bound, prescribed packing and diverging matching order with a nonvanishing ordered discrepancy. A failure of an inverse-moment estimate alone would refute only that proof route. This note has no separate publication or score.

## An alternative bound allowing exponentially growing peaks

There is also a route without a reverse entropy bound, valid on either side of (P1). Set a=(2^t-1)/(2^t+1), s=1/a and q0=4^t-1. Suppose p>=2, the p-norm of the stationary density is uniformly bounded, a>1/p and a>2/q0. Write sigma=limsup log(||pi||_infinity)/k in natural units. If sigma < (log 2)(a-2/q0), the ordered spectral error decays exponentially. This is a quantitative peak-growth assumption. It permits arbitrary decay of the stationary minimum.

Proof. Choose r<q0 and beta>0 sufficiently close to q0 and zero, respectively, that r>2s and sigma < (log 2-2 beta)(a-2/r). Use a slightly larger exponent than sigma in the eventual bound on ||pi||_infinity. As in section 7, the lower level set of h of uniformly positive measure gives ||1/g||_r <= C via reverse hypercontractivity, with no lower bound on pi. For E={|g-1|>exp(-beta k)} the matching argument gives mu(E)<=C exp[-(log 2-2 beta)k]. On E,
    ||U 1_E||_s <= ||pi||_infinity ||1/g||_r^2 mu(E)^(a-2/r),
    ||pi 1_E||_s <= B mu(E)^(a-1/p).
Both decay exponentially. On its complement ||(U-pi)1_(E^c)||_s<=C B exp(-beta k). The quotient multiplier bound at the included forward endpoint controls the operator with multiplier U-pi. The separate signed pi-1 term has exponent slack because a>1/p, so the paper's low/high factorization applies. This proves the estimate by min-max.

For t=2 and p=2 this permits limsup log_2 ||pi||_infinity / k < 7/15. The strict bulk criterion in the submitted paper needs p>15/7 for two bases. This extension therefore covers additional second-moment input under an explicit growth cap. A slowly diverging height bound need not itself yield any fixed additional moment. For a linear quotient of dimension m each atom has mass 2^-m, hence ||pi||_infinity <= B 2^(m/p). At t=2,p=2 a sufficient rank-only condition is limsup m/k<14/15. These constants are sufficient; no matching necessity is claimed.

Separation example for t=2,p=2. For m tending to infinity take n=m^3 quotient bits, each coordinate column repeated twice. Split the n quotient bits into m disjoint blocks of length m^2. Let S be the event of even parity on every block, with mass u=2^-m. Start with pi0=1+u^(-1/2)(1_S-u). Its nonconstant Fourier support consists of unions of whole blocks. Choose y0 outside S, let z be the all-ones quotient frequency, and define
    W(y)=(chi_z(y-y0)+sum_i chi_(z+e_i)(y-y0))/(n+1),
    pi(y)=pi0(y) - (1-sqrt(u)-eps_m)W(y),
where 0<eps_m<1-sqrt(u) tends to zero. The mean of W is zero, |W|<=1 and W(y0)=1. Thus pi has mean one, is everywhere at least eps_m and attains this minimum. Its L2 norm is uniformly bounded. On S its height is comparable to u^(-1/2), so every fixed p>2 has diverging moment by the lower bound c_p u^(1-p/2). On the ambient cube each block parity has degree 2m^2; the extra characters have degree 2n or 2n-2. The chain therefore matches through k=2m^2-1. The height satisfies log_2 ||pi||_infinity = m/2+O(1) = o(k).

Every z+e_i coefficient is nonzero since this frequency is never a union of whole blocks for m>=2. The z coefficient of pi0 has modulus sqrt(u); the added z coefficient has modulus (1-sqrt(u)-eps_m)/(n+1). These orders differ for large m. Thus z and all the z+e_i belong to the support; their differences span the entire quotient. Its intrinsic packing is exactly two. The rank defect of a four-family packing is at least ceil(n-2n/4), by counting the total number of columns, while k=o(n). The submitted paper's strict (P1) and four-family sufficient bounds therefore do not certify this fixed-packing example. Positive symmetric scaling realizes pi on the finite cube. The new peak-growth estimate gives exponential-in-k comparison for all its ordered eigenvalues. This is a separation of explicit bounds, not a claim of priority over every method.
