1. Bounded question, result and nonclaims
Can the original stationary floor, critical moment cap and exact labelled matching force a dimension-uniform positive lower bound on g=P_rho h? The preceding K/L family only refuted a positive minimum for h: its g was bounded below near1. Here this different denominator premise is refuted by an actual admissible family, with its entire spectrum also classified.

Fix 0<rho,b<1, C>0, s=(1+rho)/(1-rho), and k_L=min(L,floor(C sqrt(L))). There exist positive potentials htilde_L, normalized by E htilde_L P_rho htilde_L=1, with actual stationary densities pitilde_L satisfying
  pitilde_L>=b, E pitilde_L^s<=2,
  E pitilde_L chi_S=0 for every 1<=|S|<=k_L,
in every dimension L>=1. At the all-plus point x0,
  (P_rho htilde_L)(x0)->0, htilde_L(x0)->infinity,
  pitilde_L(x0)->1, [htilde_L/(P_rho htilde_L)](x0)->infinity.
Nevertheless, with U_tilde=htilde_L/(P_rho htilde_L),
  ||pitilde_L-1||_s ->0, ||U_tilde-1||_s ->0,
and every ordered eigenvalue of the actual K_htilde_L approaches the corresponding eigenvalue of P_rho, uniformly in the index with exponentially small error in L. In particular gap(K_htilde_L)->1-rho. The same exponential claim applies to the two norm errors; the pointwise rates are made explicit below.

This refutes a derived uniform Ph floor and shows that a small-denominator/high-ratio point is not sufficient for collapse. It does not disprove a localized cut-flow or killed-operator estimate, a direct Rayleigh comparison or the original gap conjecture. No original hypothesis is eliminated. Group with K/L/M and N/O/P, not a separate small paper.

2. Elementary concentration used in the construction
If X_1,...,X_L are independent Bernoulli variables with arbitrary parameters and S=sum X_i, then for a>0
  Pr(S-E S>=aL)<=exp(-2a^2 L),
  Pr(S-E S<=-aL)<=exp(-2a^2 L).                    (Q1)
For completeness, for one Bernoulli variable let F(lambda)=log E exp(lambda(X-E X)). Then F(0)=F'(0)=0 and F''(lambda) is the variance of a tilted Bernoulli law, at most1/4. Integrating twice gives F(lambda)<=lambda^2/8 for every real lambda. Independence and exponential Markov with lambda=4a (or -4a) give(Q1). No equal-parameter assumption is used. This is the classical Hoeffding bound, proved here rather than claimed new.

3. Two separated noise shells and a rare return region
Write p=(1-rho)/2, q=p+rho p=(1-rho^2)/2, and a0=(1+b)/2.
Choose
  delta=min{rho p/16,rho^2/16}>0, r0=2delta/rho.
Thus r0<=p/8, q-p=rho p>=16delta and 1/2-q=rho^2/2>=8delta.
Let d(x) be Hamming distance from x0. Define
  B_L={x: |d(x)/L-p|<=delta},
  A_L={x: |d(x)/L-q|<=2delta},
  D_L={x: (P_rho 1_B_L)(x)>1-a0}.
Here D_L is a set, not the inverse-stability constant of source L. A_L and B_L are disjoint since q-2delta>p+delta. For a noise step from x, d(Y) is a sum of independent Bernoulli variables, with mean
  E_x d(Y)=pL+rho d(x).                            (Q2)
On B_L this mean divided by L lies in [q-rho delta,q+rho delta]. Therefore(Q1) gives, uniformly over x in B_L,
  P_rho(x,A_L)>=1-2exp(-2delta^2 L).               (Q3)
Also P_rho(x0,B_L)>=1-2exp(-2delta^2 L).
If d(x)/L>=r0, the mean in(Q2) is at least p+2delta, whereas B_L lies below p+delta. Thus
  P_rho(x,B_L)<=exp(-2delta^2 L).
For all sufficiently large L this is <=1-a0; hence
  x0 in D_L subset {x:d(x)/L<r0}.                  (Q4)
The inclusion x0 in D_L follows from the preceding lower bound at x0, eventually with strict >1-a0. Because r0<p-delta and r0<q-2delta, D_L is disjoint from A_L and B_L. All later statements concern sufficiently large L; early dimensions are filled at the end.

Put
 c=min{2delta^2, 2(p-r0)^2, 2(q-2delta-p)^2,
       2(1/2-q-2delta)^2, 2(1/2-p-delta)^2,
       2(1/2-r0)^2}>0.
Under uniform measure mu, d(x) is Binomial(L,1/2). Under P_rho(x0,.), it is Binomial(L,p). The inclusions above and(Q1) give the following common exponential bounds:
  mu(A_L)<=exp(-cL), mu(B_L)<=exp(-cL), mu(D_L)<=exp(-cL),
  P_rho(x0,A_L)<=exp(-cL), P_rho(x0,D_L)<=exp(-cL),
  P_rho(x0,B_L^c)<=2exp(-cL),
  inf_{x in B_L} P_rho(x,A_L)>=1-2exp(-cL).        (Q5)
All constants are fixed before L; no concentration theorem for dependent coordinates is invoked. The set D_L is defined deterministically by the exact kernel, not by a numerical estimate.

4. Actual three-level potential and a pointwise stationary floor
Set epsilon=c/(16s), M_L=exp(epsilon L), E_L=A_L union D_L, and define
  H_L=M_L on E_L, H_L=M_L^{-1} on B_L, H_L=1 elsewhere.
These are disjoint cases. Put G_L=P_rho H_L, R_L=H_L G_L, Z_L=E R_L and h_L=H_L/sqrt(Z_L). Always M_L^{-1}<=H_L,G_L<=M_L.
For x in E_L, R_L(x)>=M_L M_L^{-1}=1.
For x in B_L, (Q5) gives G_L(x)>=M_L P_rho(x,A_L), so R_L(x)>=1-2exp(-cL)>=a0 eventually.
For x outside E_L union B_L, x is not in D_L, hence P_rho(x,B_L)<=1-a0. Since H_L>=1 off B_L,
  G_L(x)>=1-P_rho(x,B_L)>=a0,
and H_L(x)=1. Therefore R_L>=a0 at EVERY point.    (Q6)
This is an actual stationary floor computation. In particular no arbitrary density/kernel pair or missing shell boundary is used.

5. Small denominator and critical moment control
At x0 in D_L, H_L(x0)=M_L. Splitting the noise expectation over B_L,E_L and their complement yields
  G_L(x0)=M_L^{-1} P_rho(x0,B_L)+P_rho(x0,(B_L union E_L)^c)
              +M_L P_rho(x0,E_L).
Consequently by(Q5),
  P_rho(x0,B_L)<=M_L G_L(x0)
       <=1+2M_L exp(-cL)+2M_L^2 exp(-cL)->1.       (Q7)
The two tail terms vanish because 2epsilon<c. Thus G_L(x0)~M_L^{-1}, R_L(x0)->1, and H_L/G_L(x0)~M_L^2. Normalization is handled below.

For every finite r>=1, disjoint supports and(Q5) give
  d_{r,L}:=||H_L-1||_r
       <=M_L(2exp(-cL))^{1/r}+exp(-cL/r).          (Q8)
Noise is an L^r contraction by Jensen/invariance, so ||G_L-1||_r<=d_{r,L}. Holder and HG-1=(H-1)G+(G-1) imply
  ||R_L-1||_s<=d_{2s,L}(1+d_{2s,L})+d_{2s,L}
              =2d_{2s,L}+d_{2s,L}^2.             (Q9)
Since epsilon<c/(2s), d_{2s,L} and this bound decay exponentially. Hence Z_L->1 exponentially, pi_L=R_L/Z_L has ||pi_L-1||_s exponentially small, and E pi_L^s->1. From(Q6), pi_L>=a0/Z_L. With b1=(a0+b)/2>b, we have pi_L>=b1 eventually. This is strict slack for the exact correction, and the cap2 holds eventually.
At x0 normalization gives h_L(x0)~M_L and (P_rho h_L)(x0)~M_L^{-1}, pi_L(x0)->1. These are energy-normalized actual quantities.

6. Full spectral convergence despite a vanishing denominator
The scale-invariant ratio is U_L=H_L/G_L=h_L/(P_rho h_L). Although G_L has no fixed positive floor, its proved bound G_L>=M_L^{-1} and(Q8) give
  ||U_L-1||_s <=M_L ||H_L-G_L||_s
              <=2M_L d_{s,L}
              <=2*2^{1/s} exp(-(c/s-2epsilon)L)
                   +2 exp(-(c/s-epsilon)L).       (Q10)
Both exponents are positive. This tradeoff uses the rarity of both nonconstant regions and the deliberately slow growth of M_L. It does not assume the pointwise denominator bound that the example refutes.
For clarity the standard actual-chain connection is repeated. Put Q=P_sqrt(rho). The reversible conjugate of K_h is S=M_sqrt(U) P_rho M_sqrt(U). Taking X=M_sqrt(U) Q gives S=XX* and T=X*X=Q M_U Q, similar because X is invertible. Thus K_h and T have identical full spectra. Boolean hypercontractivity gives ||Qf||_{2s'}<=||f||_2, since 2s'=1+rho^{-1}. Holder therefore yields
  ||Q M_v Q||_{2->2}<=||v||_s,
and hence ||T-P_rho||<=||U_L-1||_s. Min--max controls every ordered eigenvalue with multiplicity. In particular gap(K_h)->1-rho exponentially. This is the same classical sandwich as M, with a DIFFERENT denominator argument(Q10); M's near-one pointwise denominator bound does not hold here.

7. Exact labelled correction and actual positive reconstruction
Use the proved inverse-stationary and multiplicative-stability lemma of L (CANDIDATE-L.txt, Sections2--4). It states that a positive density w on the cube has a unique positive h with hP_rho h=w. For a path w_tau=w+tau e with E e=0 and w_tau>=b,
  ||log(h_1/h_0)||_infinity<=D_L^{scale}(b)||e||_infinity/b,
  D_L^{scale}(b)=[ceil((L+log2(1/b))/2)+2]/2=O(L).
The lemma follows from strict convexity and the L-infinity resolvent bound for (I+K)^{-1}, using nonnegative spectrum, not a gap premise. We use its full-space statement, not a code-algebra restriction.

By(Q9) and normalization ||pi_L-1||_1<=||pi_L-1||_s decays exponentially. Define EXACTLY
  e_L=-sum_{1<=|S|<=k_L}(E pi_L chi_S) chi_S,
  pitilde_L=pi_L+e_L.
For large L, N_L=sum_{j=1}^{k_L} binomial(L,j)<=k_L L^{k_L}, with log N_L=o(L). Therefore
  xi_L:=||e_L||_infinity<=N_L||pi_L-1||_1
decays exponentially after reducing the exponent. Orthogonality gives E pitilde_L=1 and all specified Fourier coefficients exactly zero. Because pi_L>=b1>b and xi_L->0, the entire path pi_L+tau e_L has floor b. Also ||pitilde_L-1||_s->0 exponentially, giving E pitilde_L^s<=2 eventually.
Let htilde_L be its unique positive reconstruction. The initial inverse is h_L by uniqueness, and
  eta_L:=||log(htilde_L/h_L)||_infinity
         <=D_L^{scale}(b)xi_L/b
decays exponentially. Thus exp(-eta_L)h_L<=htilde_L<=exp(eta_L)h_L and, by positivity, the same multiplicative comparison holds for P_rho htilde_L versus P_rho h_L. This preserves every pointwise limit and asymptotic in(Q7):
  htilde_L(x0)~exp(epsilon L),
  (P_rho htilde_L)(x0)~exp(-epsilon L),
  pitilde_L(x0)->1, U_tilde(x0)~exp(2epsilon L).
Moreover exp(-2eta_L)U_L<=U_tilde<=exp(2eta_L)U_L, whence
  ||U_tilde-1||_s
    <=exp(2eta_L)||U_L-1||_s+exp(2eta_L)-1.         (Q11)
This still decays exponentially. The full-spectrum sandwich of Section6 applies to the actual corrected chain without any further assumption. Fill all finitely many earlier dimensions with htilde_L=1. Then all original constraints hold for every L and every asserted limit/rate holds eventually.

8. Scope, novelty checks and review context
Q is a concrete new obstruction within this programme to deriving a uniform positive Ph minimum. It also refutes a uniform pointwise bound on h/Ph. These conclusions are compatible with critical-L^s closeness of that ratio, and even full-spectrum convergence. A single dangerous point is not a killed-operator or cut-flow obstruction. The general stationary gap and critical weighting-moment questions remain open. Keep PR29=20/41 and TermSource=0.
The next bounded mechanism must control aggregate return/energy on small-Ph, high-h/Ph sets, rather than prohibit those points. A falsifier must be an actual family satisfying the original constraints; an abstract reversible matrix does not suffice. Do not promote this new family to gap collapse. Preserve the three-unproductive-unit rule; if this proof is accepted, the denominator-bridge refutation is substantial progress, not merely a new plan.
