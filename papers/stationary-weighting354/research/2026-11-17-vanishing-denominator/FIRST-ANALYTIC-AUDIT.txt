4. Concentration, including different Bernoulli parameters

The proof of Q1 is valid for independent, not necessarily identically distributed Bernoulli variables. For each centered Bernoulli variable, the second derivative of its log moment-generating function is the variance under exponential tilting, at most 1/4 for every real lambda. With zero value and first derivative at zero, integrating twice gives F(lambda)<=lambda^2/8, also for negative lambda. Summing over independent variables gives the log-MGF bound L lambda^2/8. Exponential Markov at lambda=4a yields exp(-2a^2 L); using lambda=-4a gives the lower tail. No binomial equal-parameter hypothesis is hidden in the shell-transition application.

For a rho-noise step from a fixed cube point x, coordinates remain independent. A coordinate positive at x is negative after the step with probability p=(1-rho)/2; one negative at x remains negative with probability 1-p. The expected number of negative coordinates is therefore p(L-d(x))+(1-p)d(x)=pL+rho d(x), precisely Q2. This validates use of Q1 from arbitrary x, where the Bernoulli parameters are generally different.
