3. RARE SET AND TRANSITION PROBABILITY

alpha <= (L/4)/[(1/2-q)^2 L^2]
      = 1/[4L(1/2-q)^2] -> 0.
Discarding D=0 only makes alpha smaller. Strict/non-strict threshold choices do not invalidate this upper bound.

Under the row P(x_0,.), D is Binomial(L,p). The complement of A is the disjoint union of D=0 and D>qL. Its first probability is u=(1-p)^L. On the second event D-Lp>(q-p)L, so Chebyshev gives
1-r <= u + p(1-p)/[L(q-p)^2] -> 0.
Thus r->1 and u->0. No exponential estimate for alpha is required by the proof: alpha->0 suffices for every chosen power. Also u+r<=1 exactly since the two row events are disjoint.

For a point y at distance d from x_0,
