1. Claimed result and nonclaims
Fix rho in (0,1), b in (0,1), C>0, and s=(1+rho)/(1-rho). Put k_L=min(L,floor(C sqrt(L))). There exist actual positive Boolean Doob potentials htilde_L with
  E htilde_L P_rho htilde_L=1,
  pitilde_L=htilde_L P_rho htilde_L>=b,
  E pitilde_L^s<=2,
  E pitilde_L chi_S=0 for every 1<=|S|<=k_L,
for every L, while
  ||pitilde_L-1||_s ->0,
  ||htilde_L-1||_{2s}->0,
  htilde_L(x_0)->0,
  (P_rho htilde_L)(x_0)->infinity.
Thus min htilde_L->0 even under the ORIGINAL exact stationary hypotheses. There is NO claim that these gaps collapse, no disproof of the original gap conjecture, no critical weighting-moment claim, and no three-level assertion for the corrected potentials. The theorem refutes the candidate bridge 'original hypotheses imply a fixed normalized pointwise minimum', thereby demonstrating that J's extra assumption cannot be eliminated by deriving it in this manner.

2. Finite symmetric scaling: existence and uniqueness proved here
Let P be a strictly positive symmetric stochastic matrix on n points with uniform measure mu=1/n. Given any strictly positive density w with E_mu w=1, there is a unique h>0 satisfying h Ph=w pointwise.
To prove this, for v in R^n minimize
  F_w(v)= (1/2) sum_xy mu(x)P(x,y) exp(v_x+v_y)
             - sum_x mu(x) w(x) v_x.
This function is strictly convex: every diagonal summand has strictly positive second derivative in its coordinate, and all other exponential terms are convex. It is coercive. Indeed with m=max v and d=min_x mu(x)P(x,x)/2>0,
  F_w(v)>=d exp(2m)-m,
since the positive linear weights mu w sum to 1. Thus a sublevel set has m bounded above (and below). If m is bounded above but some coordinate v_i tends to minus infinity, its positive linear weight makes -sum mu w v tend to plus infinity while the other coordinates contribute a bounded negative amount. Hence sublevel sets are bounded; continuity gives a minimizer, unique by strict convexity. The gradient equation is
  partial_i F_w=mu(i)[exp(v_i)(P exp(v))_i-w_i]=0.
Thus h=exp(v) is the required unique positive solution. It automatically has energy one. No numerical matrix-scaling algorithm is invoked.
For a smooth path w_tau>0, the Hessian is positive definite, so the implicit function theorem and uniqueness yield a smooth path v_tau=log h_tau on the entire compact parameter interval. Local continuations agree by uniqueness; existence at every tau was just proved.

3. A quantitative infinity-norm resolvent bound
Assume additionally P is positive semidefinite. For the actual Doob kernel K from h and stationary nu=w mu, K is reversible and has spectrum in [0,1]. Indeed its L2(nu) conjugate is M_sqrt(h/Ph) P M_sqrt(h/Ph), positive semidefinite; stochasticity supplies the upper bound.
Let nu_* = min_x nu(x)>0, A=nu_*^{-1/2}, and m=ceil(log_2 A). Define T=(I-K)/2. Its maximum absolute row sum is 1-K(x,x)<=1, hence ||T||_{infinity->infinity}<=1. On L2(nu), ||T||<=1/2. For ||f||_infinity<=1,
  ||T^j f||_infinity <= min{1, A 2^{-j}}.
The second bound uses evaluation |g(x)|<=||g||_{L2(nu)}/sqrt(nu_*), and ||f||_{L2(nu)}<=1.
The series
  (I+K)^{-1}=(1/2) sum_{j>=0} T^j
converges in finite dimension, since ||T||_2<=1/2. Since A<=2^m,
  sum_{j>=0} min{1,A2^{-j}}
   <= m + sum_{j=m}^infinity A2^{-j} <=m+2.
Consequently
  ||(I+K)^{-1}||_{infinity->infinity} <= (m+2)/2.       (L1)
This elementary logarithmic estimate uses positivity of the spectrum, not a spectral gap at 1. The operator T is not asserted to be Markov; its absolute-row estimate is proved directly.

4. Stability of the inverse stationary map
For a smooth path w_tau=h_tau P h_tau, differentiating log w gives
  d log w_tau/dtau = (I+K_tau) d log h_tau/dtau.        (L2)
Suppose w_tau >= b_0>0 on a cube of dimension L. Then nu_*>=b_0 2^{-L} and (L1) yields the uniform bound
  D_L(b_0)=[ceil((L+log_2(1/b_0))/2)+2]/2.
In particular for w_tau=w+tau e, E e=0, w_tau>=b_0,
  ||log h_1-log h_0||_infinity
      <= D_L(b_0) ||e||_infinity/b_0.                 (L3)
Equation (L3) follows by integrating (L2) and |d log w_tau/dtau|<=||e||_infinity/b_0. This controls a nonlinear reconstruction on the full state space without constructing a surrogate chain or assuming a contraction of the original noise kernel in sup norm. All logarithms except log_2 are natural.

5. The K family has exponentially small low moments
Use K with its floor parameter b_1=(1+b)/2 in place of b, the same fixed s, and its choices p,q,A,alpha,delta,u,M,t,H,Z,h,pi. The root's beta is then (1+b_1)/2. Eventually pi>=b_1 and E pi^s->1.
Here alpha is exponentially small, not merely small. To check this without an external concentration input, choose a small lambda>0 and use the exponential Markov inequality for D~Binomial(L,1/2):
  alpha<=Pr(D<=qL)
       <=[exp(lambda q)(1+exp(-lambda))/2]^L.
The bracket equals 1 at lambda=0 and its logarithmic derivative there is q-1/2<0. Thus choose a fixed lambda with bracket a in (0,1), proving alpha<=a^L. Also u=(1-p)^L and delta=2^{-L} decay exponentially.
From K's pointwise upper and lower estimates for R=HPH,
  ||R-1||_1 <= 2M alpha+M^2 alpha+u+(1-beta)delta
            =2 alpha^{1-1/(4s)}+alpha^{1-1/(2s)}
                 +u+(1-beta)delta.
All exponents of alpha are positive. Thus there exist constants c>0,A_0<infinity (fixed in L) with ||R-1||_1<=A_0 exp(-cL). Since |Z-1|<=||R-1||_1 and Z->1,
  ||pi-1||_1 <= Z^{-1}||R-1||_1+|Z^{-1}-1|
              <= A_1 exp(-cL)                       (L4)
for all sufficiently large L, after increasing A_1.

6. Exact Fourier correction
Let
  e_L=-sum_{1<=|S|<=k_L} (E pi_L chi_S) chi_S,
  w_L=pi_L+e_L.
Each nonconstant Walsh coefficient has absolute value at most ||pi_L-1||_1. The number of terms N_L is subexponential: for large L with k_L<L,
  N_L=sum_{j=1}^{k_L} binomial(L,j)<=k_L L^{k_L},
so log N_L=O(sqrt(L) log L)=o(L). By (L4),
  epsilon_L=||e_L||_infinity<=N_L A_1 exp(-cL),
and L epsilon_L->0. By orthogonality, E w_L=1 and
  E w_L chi_S=0 for every 1<=|S|<=k_L
EXACTLY. Also w_L>=b_1-epsilon_L>=b eventually; along pi_L+tau e_L the same floor b holds. Thus this correction preserves positivity, the requested floor, and mean one. Since ||pi_L-1||_s->0 and ||e_L||_s<=epsilon_L,
  ||w_L-1||_s->0, E w_L^s->1,
so the cap 2 holds eventually.

7. Reconstructing actual potentials and retaining the defect
The Boolean noise P_rho is strictly positive, symmetric stochastic and positive definite: its Walsh eigenvalues are rho^{|S|}>0. Apply Section 2 to the path w_tau=pi_L+tau e_L. Its initial h is exactly K's normalized h by uniqueness. Let htilde_L be the unique terminal potential with htilde_L P_rho htilde_L=w_L. Since the path has floor b, (L3) gives
  eta_L=||log(htilde_L/h_L)||_infinity
           <=D_L(b) epsilon_L/b ->0.
Hence exp(-eta_L) h_L <= htilde_L <= exp(eta_L) h_L pointwise. Because h_L(x_0)->0, htilde_L(x_0)->0. Because P is positive and (P h_L)(x_0)->infinity,
  (P htilde_L)(x_0)>=exp(-eta_L)(P h_L)(x_0)->infinity.
Moreover
  ||htilde_L-h_L||_{2s}<=(exp(eta_L)-1)||h_L||_{2s}->0,
using ||h_L-1||_{2s}->0 from K. Thus ||htilde_L-1||_{2s}->0.
The constructed htilde has energy E w_L=1. Its actual Doob stationary density is exactly w_L, which has the prescribed exact labelled marginals, floor and cap. Fill all finitely many earlier dimensions with htilde=1; this meets every original hypothesis for every L, and does not alter any asserted limit.

8. Consequences and boundaries
This produces a fully admissible family outside every fixed positive-minimum class. The finite-dimensional inverse construction is an existence proof with quantitative stability, not a formal executable certificate. It does not preserve three levels, nor is that needed. Small finite-norm errors were NOT simply declared to be zero: the Fourier correction gives exact equalities for the density, and symmetric scaling reconstructs the actual positive potential without losing the exceptional minimum.
No gap collapse is shown. In particular, the necessary condition from J is attained but is not sufficient. Original stationary-gap and endpoint weighting questions remain open, and PR29 remains20/41 with TermSource=0. This is a useful counterexample to an attempted hypothesis-elimination bridge, not elimination of an original hypothesis and not a Yang--Mills spectral-gap claim.
Next mathematical mechanism must bypass uniform minima: control actual cut flow or localized high-ratio killed return directly, or show a concrete actual gap-collapse construction. Reattempting 'exact matching forces h>=a' is now excluded if this proof passes audit.
