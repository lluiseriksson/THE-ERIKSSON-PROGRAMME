2. Complete low-degree column bound

Let R=Pi_<=d and Z=I-R. Under 1<=d<=min{k,L-1}, R includes the constant and all degree-one characters; Z has positive dimension. In particular L>=2. The A column estimate at the empty character is valid, as was checked in the A audit, and all columns with |S|<=d are bounded by

3. Bound (B.1), including the off-diagonal norm

Since P commutes with R and Z, the difference between T and RPR+ZTZ has block matrix
  [ R(T-P)R   RTZ ]
  [ ZTR         0 ].
The upper-left block has norm at most eps_d by restriction of the full column bound. RTZ=R(T-P)Z has norm at most eps_d by restriction of the adjoint row bound. Write this block as B. The self-adjoint off-diagonal matrix [0 B; B* 0] has square diag(BB*,B*B), hence its norm is ||B||. Therefore the triangle inequality gives

4. Ordered eigenvalues and (B.2)

Consequently the ordered eigenvalues of K are the same as those of the self-adjoint positive T, with multiplicity. Also 0<=T<=I. Compression to the nonzero high-degree space gives 0<=H_d<=I there; its norm is its largest eigenvalue and is at most one.

The low block RPR has a simple largest eigenvalue1 from the constant character and second eigenvalue rho from the degree-one characters, because d>=1. The full block diagonal spectrum is the multiset union of that low spectrum and the high-block spectrum. Its largest eigenvalue is one, and its second is exactly max{rho,||H_d||}. If a high eigenvalue were one, it would contribute the second copy of one and the same formula would still hold. 5. Growing window and precise equivalence

For the growing d_L of A, M_d>=1 eventually and
  sqrt(1+M_d)<=sqrt(2)sqrt(M_d).
Hence eps_d<=sqrt(2)E_Ld and tends to zero with the exponential bound already proved in A. The range d_L<=L-1 holds for all sufficiently large L because d_L=O(sqrt(L)/log L); finite initial dimensions are not the asymptotic assertion.

The equivalence can be written without ambiguity as
  liminf_L gap(K_L)
   =1-max{rho,limsup_L ||H_(d_L)||},
where rho is fixed in (0,1) and gap(K_L)=1-lambda_2(K_L). Indeed (B.2) has a vanishing error; for a bounded scalar sequence x_L, limsup max{rho,x_L}=max{rho,limsup x_L}. Therefore an eventual dimension-uniform positive gap, meaning liminf_L gap(K_L)>0, holds if and only if limsup_L ||H_(d_L)||<1. No convergence of the high-block norms is assumed for this equivalence.

If the actual gaps collapse along the entire sequence, lambda_2(K_L)->1. Then (B.2) yields max{rho,||H_(d_L)||}->1. Since rho<1 is fixed, this forces ||H_(d_L)||->1. Oscillatory near-collapse along a subsequence is separately captured by the limsup formulation and is not incorrectly described as convergence of every high-block norm. The converse full-sequence implication also follows from (B.2), although B only needs the displayed necessary direction.

6. Failed Holder/hypercontractive step

For a high-degree unit vector f and any finite t>1, u>=0 gives the valid Holder estimate
  <f,Tf>=E[u|Qf|^2]<=||u||_t ||Qf||_(2t')^2.
For real functions this is the displayed square form in B. The available finite exponents are t_n<s. Conjugation t -> t/(t-1) is strictly decreasing, so t'>s'. Since
  s'=(1+rho)/(2rho),  2s'=1+1/rho,
one obtains 2t'>1+1/rho. Ordinary scalar Boolean hypercontractivity for Q=P_sqrt(rho), from L2 into L^(2t'), requires
  rho <= 1/(2t'-1).
But the preceding strict inequality gives 1/(2t'-1)<rho. The proposed use of that particular unrestricted scalar norm map is therefore inadmissible. At t=s it would be at the admissible equality, but that critical moment is not supplied by the finite subcritical bootstrap.

The high-degree support of f is not, by itself, a license to invoke the failed unrestricted hypercontractive inequality. A separately proved restricted-tail estimate or a different exploitation of u=h/Ph could be relevant; B correctly leaves those possibilities open. This audit accepts the stated failed admissibility check, not a universal impossibility theorem for all methods using Holder, hypercontractivity, matching or high-degree information. No actual counterexample to a critical bound or the target gap is furnished.
