METHOD DEVELOPMENT AND NEGATIVE FINDINGS --341, retained without a gap claim
1. A direct extension of338's unrefined entropy/conductance estimate was not used. Its rare stationary-mass loss remains real. No assumption about weighting marginal or critical moment control was imported.
2. An initial route used the stationary floor to estimate inverse potentials: under energy1, pi>=b implies h^(-1)<=Ph/b. Hypercontractivity of Ph from Qh would control a negative moment of h; further Jensen/smoothing would control inverse Ph moments. This appeared to provide an interpolation route to TV convergence, but added the floor and complicated exponents. It was abandoned before becoming a stated theorem when the exact PSD reciprocal lemma N=E h/(Ph)<=1 was found. No inverse-moment theorem is needed or certified by source341.
3. The productive mechanism tests stationary matching against the degree-k truncation of Ph. Cauchy under h and a hypercontractive tail yield a self-consistent variance inequality, rather than assuming h is already regular. Keeping the sqrt(1-a^2) tail factor improves the preliminary bound to the stated epsilon^2/(1+epsilon^2).
4. The PSD reciprocal bound was direction-sensitive: N<=1 means lambda=1/N>=1, not lambda<=1. The source derives the direction by a positive Perron eigenvector and the isospectral Q M_u Q Rayleigh quotient. Cauchy under pi separately gives N>=a^2. Both directions are necessary for the final bound.
5. It was tempting to promote convergence of fixed low-degree compressions and lambda1 to a gap bound, or to assert that near-unit eigenvectors escape every fixed degree. Neither implication was established. Cross and high blocks remain uncontrolled. Source341 explicitly does not claim those conclusions.
6. Total-variation/Hellinger convergence does not logically supply critical L^s control. The generic concentrated-density diagnostic in section6 records why; it is not a valid h/Ph stationary counterexample. A separate structural moment/tail estimate or a direct stationary rare-cut argument is still needed.
7. No admissible collapsing family was found, and no uniform lower bound for the original normalized gap was proved. The present unit nevertheless proves a new-to-this-route quantitative reduction, eliminating the possibility of a nontrivial limiting Perron normalization or order-one stationary/weighting TV discrepancy under the given moment/matching hypotheses. Originality beyond this workflow remains unestablished.
