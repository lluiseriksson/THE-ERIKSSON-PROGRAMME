1. EXACT MODEL AND REUSED PREMISE

2. FINITE-PATH DENSITY: EXACT CANCELLATION

For a fixed positive integer N and a path (x_0,...,x_(N-1)), the probability under initial law eta is
P_eta^(N)(x_0,...,x_(N-1)) = eta(x_0) product_(i=0)^(N-2) K(x_i,x_(i+1)).
For the same transitions and initial law nu it is the same product multiplied by nu(x_0).

All states have positive nu mass because mu is positive on every cube vertex and pi>0. Moreover Boolean noise at rho=1/2 has positive transition probability between every pair of vertices, and positive h preserves this in K. Thus every path has positive reference probability. Taking the ratio gives exactly
dP_eta^(N)/dP_nu^(N) = eta(X_0)/nu(X_0).

The ratio is evaluated only at the initial point. There is no product of N initial-density factors, no factor b^(-N), and no change in the transition kernel. For N=1 the transition product is empty and the same identity holds. Eta may have zero masses, in which case the density is zero on the corresponding paths. There is no lost absolute-continuity case.

If eta(x)<=M nu(x) for all x, then for every nonnegative path functional Z,
E_eta-start Z = E_nu-start[(eta(X_0)/nu(X_0)) Z] <= M E_nu-start Z.
This follows by summing the nonnegative terms of the finite path space. No independence, reversibility, stationarity of eta or moment cancellation is needed for this comparison itself. Reversibility/PSD were needed upstream for F, not newly assumed for G's density argument.
