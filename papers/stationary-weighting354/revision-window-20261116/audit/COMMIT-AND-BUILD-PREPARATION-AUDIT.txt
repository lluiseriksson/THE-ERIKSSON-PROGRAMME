MATERIAL PREPARATION DEFECT: FAILURE EVIDENCE LOST ON SIZE REJECTION

If all-page PNGs make window-evidence exceed 50 MiB, finish writes upload_ok=false and exits 64. The only upload step then cannot run. build-full.log was redirected into runner-temp, so the full compile logs, exact exit files, input hashes, comparison record and other diagnostic artifacts disappear when that runner is disposed. The Actions console merely reports the size error; it is not a copy of those redirected logs. The same outcome can occur on a failed run that leaves an oversized directory.

This does correctly prevent over-cap uploading; it does not fake success. However it violates the stated preservation of full logs and failures, and makes a foreseeable rendering/size failure unnecessarily unauditable. This is why the execution-preparation verdict is correction required even though the inactive commit is safe to preserve.

Suggested correction, before requesting the exception: keep a compact, explicitly bounded diagnostic bundle separate from page/PDF payloads; preserve full build and postprocessing logs, actual phase exits, source/tool hashes and the size-failure explanation even if page images cannot be retained. Reserve its capacity within the same total ceiling. A permitted console fallback for complete diagnostics can be explicit where artifact capacity is unavailable; do not truncate a log while calling it complete. Oversized page payloads must remain a failed/incomplete rendering-evidence outcome, not silently disappear behind a success label. If even the diagnostic data exceed the ceiling, fail with a truthful preservation limitation and a defined permitted recovery route. No cap increase, paid upload or quota bypass is recommended.
