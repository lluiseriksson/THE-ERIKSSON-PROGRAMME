2. A CLOSED NEGATIVE RESULT INSIDE THIS EXACT KERNEL CLASS

Proposition. Fix q in (0,1). For every integer L>=1 let
 H_L(sigma,tau)=q^{d_H(sigma,tau)}, R_L=(1+q)^L, z_L=q^L,
 t_L=z_L^2/R_L.
Give the two constant configurations (+,...,+) and (-,...,-) weight1 and give
all other configurations weight t_L^2. All weights are strictly positive.
Let D_L=diag(sqrt(w_L)), S_L=D_L H_L D_L and A_L=D_L^2 H_L.
Write their eigenvalues in decreasing order as lambda1>lambda2>=...>0;
A_L is similar to S_L and has the same eigenvalues. Define the relative modulus
gap delta_L=1-max_{mu!=lambda1}|mu|/lambda1=1-lambda2/lambda1.
With eta_L=2 z_L^2+z_L^4/R_L, one has
 (2 z_L-eta_L)/(1+z_L) <= delta_L
    <= (2 z_L+eta_L)/(1+z_L+eta_L),
and consequently delta_L/(2 q^L) ->1 as L->infinity.
In particular strict positivity for every L and every source weight does not
imply any positive volume-uniform relative gap over this allowed class, even
when beta=-log(q)/2>0 is fixed once and for all.

Proof.
(a) H_L is the tensor product of L copies of [[1,q],[q,1]]. Equivalently the
Walsh characters chi_J(sigma)=product_{j in J}sigma_j form an orthogonal basis
and have eigenvalues (1-q)^{|J|}(1+q)^{L-|J|}>0. This follows by separating each
sum over tau_j. Thus H_L is positive definite. D_L is invertible and S_L is
positive definite by congruence. Also D_L^{-1} A_L D_L=S_L. All eigenvalues are
positive, so the non-Perron maximum in modulus is the ordinary second eigenvalue.

(b) All row and column sums of H_L equal R_L, by factoring sum_tau q^{d_H}.
For a real vector x, Cauchy-Schwarz with weights H_ij gives
 |(H x)_i|^2 <= R_L sum_j H_ij |x_j|^2.
Summing i proves ||H x||_2^2<=R_L^2||x||_2^2. Equality holds on the constant
vector, hence ||H||_{2->2}=R_L. This is an operator-norm statement, not an
entrywise or unspecified-norm estimate.

(c) Let P be the orthogonal projection onto the two constant configurations,
Q=I-P and D=P+t_L Q. Put B=P H P. On range(P), B=[[1,z_L],[z_L,1]]; on its
orthogonal complement it is zero. Its two largest eigenvalues are1+z_L and
1-z_L. This also covers L=1, where Q=0 and there is no complement.
The exact decomposition
 S-B=t_L(P H Q+Q H P)+t_L^2 Q H Q
therefore implies ||S-B||<= (2t_L+t_L^2)R_L=eta_L.

(d) The normalized sum of the two corner coordinate vectors has Rayleigh
quotient1+z_L for S, so lambda1>=1+z_L. The perturbation norm and the maximum
Rayleigh quotient of B give lambda1<=1+z_L+eta_L.
Every vector in range(P) has Rayleigh quotient at least1-z_L. This two-dimensional
space intersects the orthogonal complement of a top eigenvector nontrivially;
the Rayleigh principle therefore gives lambda2>=1-z_L.
For the upper bound, restrict to the orthogonal complement of the corner-sum
vector. B has Rayleigh quotient at most1-z_L on that codimension-one space,
and S has quotient at most1-z_L+eta_L. The min-max characterization gives
lambda2<=1-z_L+eta_L. No uncontrolled continuity or eigenvalue calculation is
used; these are exact variational bounds.

(e) For completeness the top eigenvalue is simple: a unit Rayleigh maximizer
can be replaced by its entrywise absolute value. Strict positivity of S makes
its image strictly positive, hence there is a strictly positive top eigenvector.
A second top eigenvector orthogonal to it would have both signs; replacing
that vector by its absolute value strictly increases its Rayleigh quotient,
a contradiction. Thus delta_L>0 for every L>=1. Symmetric diagonalization and
similarity also give algebraic simplicity in this specified class; this is an
analytic statement here, not a new Lean theorem or a claim about every generic
nonsymmetric positive kernel's recorded formal endpoints.

(f) Dividing the two bounds on lambda2 by the appropriate positive bounds on
lambda1 yields exactly the displayed two-sided bounds on delta_L. Since
eta_L/z_L=2z_L+z_L^3/R_L ->0, both bounds divided by2z_L tend to1.
For fixed beta>0, K_beta,L=exp(beta L) H_L with q=exp(-2beta).
Multiplying the whole matrix by this positive scalar changes no eigenvalue ratio,
so this is the actual sourceWeightedKernelL class from Section1, not a separate
2x2 surrogate or a restatement with a gap hidden in the hypotheses. QED.

3. LIMITS AND ADVERSARIAL CHECKS

The source weights vary with L and globally distinguish the two constant spin
configurations. They are not asserted to be a fixed-gamma nearest-neighbour
Ising weight. Their positive lower bound tends to0. This proposition does not
refute a uniform gap under stronger, separately verified weight hypotheses,
does not settle the printed fixed-(beta,gamma) Ising families, and proves no
interacting/continuum Yang-Mills conclusion. PR29 stays20/41,TermSource0.
For L=1, P=I and the exact gap is2q/(1+q), consistent with all stated bounds.
The excluded endpoint q=1 (beta=0) is different: K has every entry1, so A=w*1^T
has eigenvalue sum(w) and otherwise0; its relative modulus gap is1 for L>=1.
This directly warns against interpreting the old title as saying that every
allowed weighted family necessarily loses its uniform gap.
No finite numerical grid is used to prove the limiting assertion above.
No hypothesis is counted as eliminated; this is an explicit counterexample
to a stronger uniform conclusion, proved within the existing positive-weight
class. It is to be consolidated with the existing paper, not counted as a new
paper or a physical spectral gap.

5. CORRECTION TO QUALITATIVE VERSUS QUANTITATIVE LANGUAGE

For any fixed finite matrix with at least two eigenvalues, strict separation
implies an existential positive gap by taking the maximum of finitely many
non-Perron moduli. The old phrases 'no quantitative content' or 'no information
whatever' are too strong. The actual limitation is no explicit certified modulus
with controlled dependence on L and the specified weight family. Section2 now
supplies a proved obstruction for an explicit arbitrary-source-weight sequence,
while supplying no conclusion for a fixed local-interaction Ising family.
