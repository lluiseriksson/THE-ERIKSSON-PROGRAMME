\section{Exact identification against the Gibbs sums}
\label{sec:measure}
The bridge from the raw measure to the operator is a chain of
\emph{identities} --- no bound appears until the gap is invoked, and
none of the statements below needs the window: they hold at every real
$\beta$, every $\lambda \ne 0$, every positive weight.

\begin{lemma}[B0b, \lean{act\_symWeighted\_eq\_smul\_act\_tilt}]
$\mathrm{act}\,S\,u = \lambda\cdot \mathrm{act}\,(\mathrm{tilt})\,u$
for $\lambda \ne 0$, and iterating
(\lean{act\_symWeighted\_iterate\_eq\_smul\_tilt}, via the scalar
transport lemmas \lean{act\_smul\_fun},
\lean{act\_iterate\_smul\_fun}):
$(\mathrm{act}\,S)^{[N]} u = \lambda^N \cdot
(\mathrm{act}\,\mathrm{tilt})^{[N]} u$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[B0a, \lean{act\_iterate\_eq\_opOf\_pow}]
$(\mathrm{act}\,M)^{[n]} u = (\mathrm{opOf}\,M)^n(\mathrm{toLp}\,u)$
pointwise, for any kernel matrix $M$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{theorem}[B1, \lean{gibbsPathSum\_eq\_inner\_pow}]
For $w > 0$, $\beta \in \mathbb{R}$, $\lambda \ne 0$, $N \in
\mathbb{N}$ and real observables $A, B$:
\[
\mathrm{gibbsPathSum}\,w\,\beta\,N\,A\,B
  \;=\; \lambda^N\,
  \bigl\langle (\mathrm{opOf}\,(\mathrm{tiltKernel}\,w\,\beta\,
  \lambda))^N\, (Q A),\; Q B \bigr\rangle ,
\]
an exact identity: the unnormalised Gibbs two-point sum \emph{is}
$\lambda^N$ times a matrix element of the reconstructed operator's
powers, taken between dressed observables.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[B2, \lean{gibbsPartition\_eq\_inner\_pow}]
Under the same hypotheses, the partition function is the same shape at
the dressed constant:
$\;\mathrm{gibbsPartition}\,w\,\beta\,N = \lambda^N
\ip{\hat T^N (Q\mathbf 1)}{Q\mathbf 1}$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}
Dividing B1 by B2 cancels $\lambda^N$, so the \emph{normalised}

\section{Mixed clustering on a generic Hilbert space}
\label{sec:mixed}
The clustering machinery is stated for an arbitrary real Hilbert
space $H$ (a \lean{section} with
\lean{[NormedAddCommGroup H] [InnerProductSpace \(\mathbb{R}\) H]
[CompleteSpace H]}), so the three lemmas below are reusable against
any transfer operator packaged as a \lean{VacuumTransfer} --- in
particular they are exactly the consumer shape of the
\lean{GappedTransfer} hypothesis of the abstract frameworks in
Section~\ref{sec:prior}.

\begin{lemma}[\lean{norm\_sub\_inner\_smul\_le}]
For $\lVert\Omega\rVert = 1$:
$\lVert v - \ip{\Omega}{v}\,\Omega\rVert \le \lVert v\rVert$
(Pythagoras; removing the vacuum component does not grow the norm).
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[\lean{pow\_apply\_norm\_le}]
$\lVert S^n v\rVert \le r^n \lVert v\rVert$ whenever
$\lVert S\rVert \le r$, $r \ge 0$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{theorem}[\lean{mixed\_connCorr\_bound}]
Let $T$ be a \lean{VacuumTransfer} with vacuum $\Omega$, and suppose
$\lVert \mathrm{projectedTransfer}\,T\,\Omega\rVert \le r$ with
$r \ge 0$.  Then for all $u, v \in H$ and all $n \in \mathbb{N}$
\emph{including $n = 0$}:
\[
\bigl|\ip{u}{T^n v} - \ip{\Omega}{u}\,\ip{\Omega}{v}\bigr|
  \;\le\; \lVert u\rVert\,\lVert v\rVert\, r^n .
\]
The $n = 0$ case is the Pythagoras lemma; $n \ge 1$ goes through the
in-tree \lean{projected\_pow\_succ}.
\end{theorem}

This strictly extends the lane's earlier diagonal clustering
(\lean{os\_reconstruction\_uniform\_clustering}, $u = v$, constant

observables, and the time-zero correlator is bounded too --- the

\section{The division-safe six-term connected bound}
\label{sec:sixterm}
The one step the previous version named open is now a theorem, in two
layers.  The generic layer lives in the same arbitrary-Hilbert-space
section as the mixed bound:

\begin{theorem}[\lean{six\_term\_connected\_bound}]
Let $T$ be a \lean{VacuumTransfer} with vacuum $\Omega$, let
$0 \le r \le 1$ with
$\lVert\mathrm{projectedTransfer}\,T\,\Omega\rVert \le r$, and write
$F(u,v) := \ip{u}{T^N v}$.  Then for all $q_A, q_B, q_1$ and all $N$:
\[
\bigl| F(q_1,q_1)\,F(q_A,q_B) - F(q_A,q_1)\,F(q_1,q_B) \bigr|
\;\le\; 6\,\lVert q_A\rVert\,\lVert q_B\rVert\,\lVert q_1\rVert^2\,
r^N .
\]
\end{theorem}

The proof is a ring identity in $E$-terms
($E(u,v) := F(u,v) - \ip{\Omega}{u}\ip{\Omega}{v}$): the pure product
terms cancel \emph{exactly}, six terms survive, each carrying at least
one $E$ factor bounded by Theorem~\lean{mixed\_connCorr\_bound}, and
the two $E\!\cdot\!E$ terms spend one factor through $r^N \le 1$.  The
constant $6$ is the honest count of surviving terms, not an optimised
one.

The model layer consumes it at the dressed observables, \emph{in raw
Gibbs terms}:

\begin{theorem}[\lean{os\_reconstruction\_connected\_uniform}]
Let $\beta, \gamma \in \mathbb{R}$ and $0 < \alpha < 1$ with
$2\tanh|\beta|+2\tanh|\gamma| \le \alpha$.  There is \emph{one}
$m > 0$ such that for \emph{every} spatial extent $L$ there are
$\lambda > 0$, $\Omega > 0$ with the fixed-point equation, and for
every time depth $N$ and all real observables $A, B$:
\[
\bigl| Z_N\, S_N(A,B) - S_N(A,\mathbf 1)\, S_N(\mathbf 1,B) \bigr|
\;\le\; \lambda^{2N}\,
\bigl(6\,\lVert QA\rVert\,\lVert QB\rVert\,\lVert Q\mathbf 1\rVert^2
\bigr)\,(e^{-m})^N ,
\]
where $S_N = \mathrm{gibbsPathSum}$, $Z_N = \mathrm{gibbsPartition}$,
and $Q{\cdot} = \mathrm{toLp}(\mathrm{dress}\,{\cdot})$.
\end{theorem}

This is the normalised bound multiplied through by the positive scale
of $Z_N^2$.  To divide it one needs a lower bound for
$\ip{\hat T^N Q\mathbf 1}{Q\mathbf 1}$ that does not degrade with $N$,
and that is the content of the next section.

\section{The positive cone: a denominator floor uniform in $N$}
\label{sec:cone}
A spectral argument gives the floor only along the even powers: writing
$Q\mathbf 1 = \ip{\Omega}{Q\mathbf 1}\Omega + q^\perp$ one has
$\ip{\hat T^N Q\mathbf 1}{Q\mathbf 1}
= \ip{\Omega}{Q\mathbf 1}^2 + \ip{S^N q^\perp}{q^\perp}$ with
$S$ the projected operator, and the correction is sign-controlled only
for $N$ even ($S^{2k}$ is positive semidefinite; $S^{2k+1}$ is not).
The obstruction is an artefact of the method, not of the object: the
kernel here is entrywise \emph{positive}, so the order-theoretic route
closes at every $N$.

\begin{lemma}[\lean{tiltKernel\_pos}]
For $w>0$ and $\lambda>0$, $\mathrm{tiltKernel}\,w\,\beta\,\lambda$ is
entrywise positive --- at \emph{every} real $\beta$, since
$\mathrm{spatialKernel}$ is a product of positive bond factors
(\lean{spatialKernel\_pos}).  The vacuum is positive too
(\lean{vacOf\_pos}).
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[cone propagation, \lean{opOf\_pow\_ge\_smul\_fix}]
Let $M$ be entrywise nonnegative with $\mathrm{opOf}\,M\,\Omega =
\Omega$.  If $c\,\Omega \le v$ entrywise then $c\,\Omega \le T^n v$
entrywise, for every $n$.  (Induction on $n$; the step is
$\sum_\tau M_{\sigma\tau}(c\,\Omega_\tau) \le
\sum_\tau M_{\sigma\tau}(T^n v)_\tau$ and the fixed-point equation.
No parity, no spectral input.)
\end{lemma}

\begin{theorem}[the floor, \lean{inner\_pow\_floor}]
Let $M$ be entrywise nonnegative, $\Omega$ the normalised positive
fixed vector, $v$ entrywise positive.  Then there is $f>0$ with
$\;f \le \ip{T^n v}{v}\;$ for \emph{every} $n$.  Explicitly
$f = c^2\lVert\Omega\rVert^2$ where $c = \min_\sigma
(v_\sigma/\Omega_\sigma) > 0$ is the in-tree finite minimum
\lean{minWeight}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[\lean{os\_reconstruction\_normalised\_clustering}]
In the window there is \emph{one} $m>0$ such that for \emph{every}
spatial extent $L$: the partition function is positive at every depth,
and there is a constant $C(A,B)$ --- depending on the observables and
on $L$, but \emph{not} on the time depth $N$ --- with
\[
\Bigl| \frac{S_N(A,B)}{Z_N}
  - \frac{S_N(A,\mathbf 1)}{Z_N}\,\frac{S_N(\mathbf 1,B)}{Z_N} \Bigr|
\;\le\; C(A,B)\,(e^{-m})^N ,
\qquad
C(A,B) = \frac{6\,\lVert QA\rVert\,\lVert QB\rVert\,
  \lVert Q\mathbf 1\rVert^2}{f^2} .
\]
\end{theorem}

The three quotients are the expectations of the normalised Gibbs
measure: $S_N(A,B)/Z_N = \langle A(X_0)B(X_N)\rangle$,
$S_N(A,\mathbf 1)/Z_N = \langle A(X_0)\rangle$ and
$S_N(\mathbf 1,B)/Z_N = \langle B(X_N)\rangle$.  So the statement is
that the connected two-point function \emph{of the measure} decays at
the volume-uniform rate, with a time-independent prefactor.  This is
the step the earlier version of this manuscript named as open; it is
now a theorem, and the honest residue is stated in
Section~\ref{sec:honesty}: $C$ still depends on $L$.

\section{What is not claimed}\label{sec:honesty}
No Wightman axioms, no OS$\to$Wightman analytic continuation, no
continuum limit, no $SU(N)$, no statement about the Clay problem.  The
window is the Dobrushin window; outside it the programme's own
kill-test (repository, Addendum 610) shows the standard-cone Birkhoff
route is projectively blind to the site weight, which is why no claim
beyond the window appears here.  The model is the $\mathbb{Z}_2$
Ising slice; ``reconstruction'' means the elementary finite-volume
site-form construction (the quotient content lives on half-chain
observables, Section~\ref{sec:provenance}), and the theorems are
about its \emph{spectrum and its exact relation to the Gibbs sums},
not about the construction being difficult.  The connected bound is
printed in both forms, division-free (Section~\ref{sec:sixterm}) and
normalised (Section~\ref{sec:cone}); the residue there is precise and
we state it rather than blur it: the mass $m$ is uniform in the
spatial extent $L$, but the \emph{constant} $C(A,B)$ of the normalised
bound is not --- it carries $1/f^2$ with $f = c^2\lVert\Omega\rVert^2$
and $c$ the minimum ratio $Q\mathbf 1/\Omega$, which may degrade as
$L\to\infty$.  So this paper proves a volume-uniform \emph{rate} with
per-extent constants, not a volume-uniform bound; making $C$ uniform
in $L$ as well is the next step, and it is a genuinely new estimate
rather than a further identification.
