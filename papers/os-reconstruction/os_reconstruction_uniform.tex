\begin{abstract}
For the spatial $\mathbb{Z}_2$ (Ising-slice) system in the Dobrushin
window $2\tanh|\beta| + 2\tanh|\gamma| \le \alpha < 1$, we machine-check
in Lean~4 that the Osterwalder--Schrader (site-form) reconstruction of
the transfer operator carries \emph{one} mass: there is a single $m>0$
such that for \emph{every} spatial extent $L$ the reconstructed
(site-form) transfer operator is unitarily conjugate --- by the explicit
$\sqrt{w}$ boundary dressing --- to the symmetrised kernel whose
projected norm the Dobrushin corollary bounds by $e^{-m}$, and every
connected correlator of the transported transfer operator decays as
$\lvert\mathrm{connCorr}\,T\,\Omega\,v\,n\rvert \le \lVert v\rVert^2
(e^{-m})^n$ --- constant per-observable, rate alone uniform in the
volume.  All quantifiers live in the Lean statements; the prose here
copies the kernel's cut.  To situate the claim in the 2026 landscape of
machine-checked constructive field theory: the abstract construction
(reflection-positive data $\to$ physical Hilbert space $\to$
self-adjoint contractive transfer operator) has been machine-checked,
sorry-free, in the Harvard formalization programme, \emph{with the
spectral gap carried as an undischarged hypothesis}
(\lean{GappedTransfer}); the Osterwalder--Schrader axioms have been
machine-checked for the free field; and measure-side Dobrushin
clustering has been machine-checked for lattice Yang--Mills.  What we
add, and what to our knowledge no machine-checked artefact yet
contains, is the combination \{concrete interacting model, gap
\emph{proved} rather than hypothesised, uniform in the spatial
volume\} on the reconstructed operator --- i.e.\ a concrete discharge
of exactly the hypothesis shape the abstract frameworks carry.  We do
not claim Wightman reconstruction, a continuum limit, $SU(N)$, or any
progress on the Clay problem.
\end{abstract}
