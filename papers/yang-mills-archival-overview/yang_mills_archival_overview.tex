\begin{abstract}
We present an archival technical synthesis and handoff specification for the
Yang--Mills formalization programme inside \texttt{THE-ERIKSSON-PROGRAMME}
(archival release \texttt{yang-mills-archive-v1.0}). The repository pins
Lean~4 (\texttt{v4.29.0-rc6}) and \texttt{mathlib}
(\texttt{07642720480157414db592fa85b626dafb71355b}) and maintains a
continuously verified core module, \texttt{YangMillsCore}, whose build and
axiom oracle pass in GitHub Actions with zero \texttt{sorry} declarations,
zero project-specific axioms, and every headline declaration restricted to
Lean's three foundational axioms
\texttt{[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]}.

Within this verified core, the programme establishes: (i) a reusable
Koteck\'y--Preiss and Mayer--Ursell polymer cluster-expansion engine
(including Penrose's tree-graph inequality and zero-free polydiscs);
(ii) finite-volume and volume-uniform strong-coupling Wilson-loop area laws
and unconditional exponential decay of truncated plaquette correlations for
$\mathrm{SU}(N)$ lattice gauge theory without invoking Peter--Weyl, together
with fixed-spacing thermodynamic limits; (iii) exact $\mathbb{Z}_N$
centre-symmetry selection rules and fundamental Haar/Schur identities;
(iv) machine-checked modified-Bessel/Amos ratio bounds and global ratio
monotonicity for killed von Mises bridges; (v) complete Osterwalder--Seiler
reflection-positivity and transfer-operator chains for $\mathbb{Z}_2$
lattice gauge systems; and (vi) certified negative obstructions isolating
exact walls in block-Poincar\'e coercivity, flat lattice Hodge rank, spatial
coupling, and sine-series monotonicity.

We state the limits with equal precision. No four-dimensional continuum
limit ($a \to 0$), no physical $\mathrm{SU}(N)$ Osterwalder--Schrader or
Wightman reconstruction, and no solution to the Clay Millennium Yang--Mills
Mass Gap problem is claimed; the repository's historical Clay-distance
shorthand remains ${\sim}0\%$ ($<0.1\%$). Three open fronts are isolated and
documented for future researchers and automated provers: the concrete
Balaban/Dimock physical activity estimate \texttt{hRpoly} (frozen in draft
PR~\#29 at $20/41$ terminal producers and $\mathrm{TermSource}=0$), physical
$\mathrm{SU}(N)$ reflection positivity and transfer-operator gaps beyond
finite-cylinder geometry (R1/R2), and continuum/OS--Wightman reconstruction.
All accompanying preprints in the AIRR archive (\url{https://airr.science/})
and reproduction steps are indexed in one citable record.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope, Verification Standard, and Epistemic Boundary}

Formalizing constructive quantum field theory in an interactive theorem
prover exposes two distinct failure modes: silent logical gaps
(\texttt{sorry} or unproved \texttt{axiom} declarations) and vacuous
formalizations whose top-level statements hold for degenerate models
(such as trivial gauge groups or coordinated dummy scalings). Early phases
of \texttt{THE-ERIKSSON-PROGRAMME} encountered both; the mature repository
enforces a strict archival discipline verified automatically on every push
and pull request to \texttt{main}:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Pinned toolchain and dependencies.}
  \texttt{lean-toolchain} pins \texttt{leanprover/lean4:v4.29.0-rc6};
  \texttt{lakefile.lean} and \texttt{lake-manifest.json} pin \texttt{mathlib}
  to commit \texttt{07642720480157414db592fa85b626dafb71355b} and fix all
  transitive dependency revisions.
  \item \textbf{Automated compilation and axiom oracle.}
  GitHub Actions workflow \texttt{lean-build.yml} builds
  \texttt{YangMillsCore} (recorded core count: $8466$ Lake jobs in
  \texttt{project-state.json}) and runs \texttt{oracle\_check.lean},
  requiring every \texttt{\#print axioms} readout to depend on nothing
  beyond \texttt{[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]}.
  \item \textbf{Zero-sorry and zero-core-axiom textual guard.}
  Workflow \texttt{ci.yml} runs \texttt{scripts/check\_consistency.py}, a
  comment-aware scanner forbidding \texttt{sorry} across the proof tree and
  forbidding \texttt{axiom} declarations across the verified-core source
  directories (\texttt{L0\_Lattice}, \texttt{L1\_GibbsMeasure}, \texttt{KP},
  \texttt{ClayCore}, \texttt{Paper}, \texttt{RG}, and the core
  \texttt{P8\_PhysicalGap} Haar/compactness modules). Missing analytic
  theorems are carried strictly as explicit theorem hypotheses
  (\texttt{HYPOTHESIS\_FRONTIER.md}), never as axioms.
  \item \textbf{Explicit Clay-distance boundary.}
  The programme does \emph{not} prove existence of a four-dimensional
  continuum Yang--Mills theory on $\mathbb{R}^4$, Osterwalder--Schrader or
  Wightman axioms for $\mathrm{SU}(N)$ ($N \ge 2$), or a continuum mass gap.
  The assembly lemmas in \texttt{YangMills/Paper/ClusteringToGap.lean} are
  explicitly documented as real-sequence exponential-decay lemmas; their
  physical role is conditional on genuine gauge-theoretic IR and UV inputs.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Certified Obstructions and Negative Results}

A central contribution of the programme for future researchers is a body of
machine-checked negative theorems ruling out tempting but mathematically
false shortcuts:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Volume-uniform Poincar\'e walls}
  (\texttt{ARR-2026-3Q0VH7PMNV8C9VQE}): obstructs flat and
  fluctuation-sector block-Poincar\'e routes to Combes--Thomas coercivity in
  lattice Yang--Mills.
  \item \textbf{Flat lattice Hodge factorized-penalty and coarse-rank walls}
  (\texttt{ARR-2026-627PF1B4KK9VTRYT}, \texttt{ARR-2026-7EPM3V3DP796BBRR},
  and expanded revision dossier \texttt{papers/flat-coarse-rank/}):
  exact-kernel Fourier families obstruct every penalty factored through a
  block map in a flat lattice gauge form.
  \item \textbf{Spatial coupling and Birkhoff obstructions in $\mathbb{Z}_2$
  slices} (\texttt{ARR-2026-4X084GVAYF87PB3F},
  \texttt{ARR-2026-6M8PMVHTMH8YZ9ND}, \texttt{ARR-2026-1A608PTGTC9XX9JZ}):
  isolates where elementary uniform-vacuum and extent-independent spectral
  arguments break under spatial coupling.
  \item \textbf{Parity barriers for decoupling inequalities}
  (\texttt{ARR-2026-5HRC3PS9HZ9WDAA5}): no comparison functional of bounded
  marginal order can certify uniform decoupling.
  \item \textbf{Failure of Biernacki--Krzy\.z for sine series}
  (\texttt{papers/wronskian-reduction/}): explicit counterexample
  $a=(3,4,3)$, $b=(3,2,1)$ showing coefficient-ratio monotonicity cannot
  certify the sign of the antisymmetrized sine-sum Wronskian.
  \item \textbf{Two-transporter Haar projection design no-go}
  (\texttt{docs/SU2-TWO-TRANSPORTER-NOGO-20260731.md}): two free
  Haar-integrated transporters absent from the observable project the
  reduced $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ quadratic form to the trivial sector.
\end{itemize}

\section{Open Frontiers for Continuation}

Future human teams or autonomous theorem provers picking up this archive
should start from \texttt{HORIZON.md}, \texttt{HYPOTHESIS\_FRONTIER.md}, and
\texttt{YangMills/CLOSURE.md}. Three concrete frontiers remain open:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Physical UV activity estimate (\texttt{hRpoly}), frozen in
  draft PR~\#29.}
  Construct the concrete Balaban/Dimock cluster-expansion-with-holes
  remainder decay bound for the actual gauge RG operator. At the frozen
  September 2026 checkpoint (\texttt{docs/HRPOLY-STATUS.md}), the checklist
  records $20/41$ terminal \texttt{PreEq136} producers,
  $\mathrm{TermSource}=0$, physical window~15 compatible but not attained,
  and uniform physical $(B_0,\delta_0)$ plus regional transport (F5) open.
  \item \textbf{Physical $\mathrm{SU}(N)$ reflection positivity and
  transfer-operator spectral gap at fixed spacing.}
  Extend the verified R1/R2 finite $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ cylinder geometry and
  product-Haar measure to genuine complex Gram-matrix positivity retaining
  all crossing plaquette weights, a strictly positive centered norm, and a
  physical transfer operator (\texttt{docs/POST-HRPOLY-CONTINUUM-AUDIT-20260906.md}).
  \item \textbf{Four-dimensional continuum limit ($a \to 0$) and
  Osterwalder--Schrader / Wightman reconstruction (M4--M5).}
  This remains open mathematics well beyond current formalization
  infrastructure: removing the ultraviolet regulator while retaining a
  non-perturbative positive mass gap for $\mathrm{SU}(N)$ ($N \ge 2$) and
  constructing the continuum Hilbert space and fields.
\end{enumerate}

Late-2026 open research branches on GitHub (not merged into
\texttt{YangMillsCore}) preserve additional work on concentric
integer-shell energy control and flat cochains (PRs~\#180--\#190) and
Boolean Doob chain retention criteria (PRs~\#292, \#299).

\section{Reproducibility and Citation}

To reproduce the verified Lean~4 core from scratch (on Windows, pass
\texttt{-c core.longpaths=true} due to nested historical audit paths under
\texttt{papers/marginal-transfer/}):

\begin{verbatim}
git clone -c core.longpaths=true \
  https://github.com/lluiseriksson/THE-ERIKSSON-PROGRAMME.git
cd THE-ERIKSSON-PROGRAMME
git checkout yang-mills-archive-v1.0
lake exe cache get
lake build YangMillsCore
lake env lean oracle_check.lean
python scripts/check_consistency.py
\end{verbatim}

The code is released under \texttt{AGPL-3.0-or-later} with machine-readable
citation metadata in \texttt{CITATION.cff} and \texttt{.zenodo.json}.
