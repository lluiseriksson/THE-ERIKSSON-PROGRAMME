specification and continuation handoff for the Yang--Mills Lean~4 formalization inside
\texttt{THE-ERIKSSON-PROGRAMME} at archival release \texttt{yang-mills-archive-v1.0}

material objection raised by hostile reviews of v1 and v2, this v3 revision supplies exact
fully-qualified Lean~4 theorem statements and complete hypotheses (including the complex
modulus and all four KP inequalities in the exact-action Wilson-loop area law, the
unspannable-chain behavior of \texttt{chainAreaA}, the $\beta > 0$ hypothesis and literal

compiled-only modules, and a reconciled cross-reference of all 33 companion
mathematical-physics repository folders to their 33 live AIRR identifiers.

The verified core proves fixed-spacing lattice results only: a Koteck\'y--Preiss /

area laws and exponential clustering of truncated two-plaquette correlations without

\section{Certified Negative Results and Exact Obstruction Classes}\label{sec:obstructions}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Volume-uniform Poincar\'e walls (\texttt{ARR-2026-3Q0VH7PMNV8C9VQE}):}
  on the unit-spacing periodic torus $\Lambda_{LN'} = (\Z/LN'\Z)^d$ ($d \ge 3$, $N_c \ge 2$,
  internal space $\R^{N_c^2-1}$ with concrete matricial $\mathfrak{su}(N_c)$ adjoint model),
  let $K_0 = D_1^\dagger D_1 + D_0 D_0^\dagger$ be the flat lattice Hodge operator
  ($\langle A, K_0 A\rangle = \|\mathrm{curl}\,A\|^2 + \|\mathrm{div}\,A\|^2$) and let $Q$
  be the \emph{unscaled} line-integral block map satisfying $Q(A_v) = L\,A_v^{\mathrm{coarse}}$
  on direction-wise constants. The formalized Poincar\'e predicate
  \texttt{FlatGaugeHodgePoincare d L N' Nc rho CP} is the \emph{unweighted} inequality
  \[
    0 < C_P \quad\wedge\quad
    \forall A,\quad \|A\|^2 \le C_P\,\bigl(\langle A, K_0 A\rangle + \|QA\|^2\bigr),
  \]
  which feeds coercivity $c = \min(1,a)/C_P$ to the penalized covariance
  $K = K_0 + a Q^\dagger Q$ ($a > 0$). Two walls refute uniformity as block side grows:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \emph{Constant-sector wall:} on side $LN'$ for any fixed $N' \ge 1$, testing on
    $A_v$ ($\langle A_v, K_0 A_v\rangle = 0$, $\|A_v\|^2 = (LN')^d\|v\|^2$,
    $\|QA_v\|^2 = L^2(N')^d\|v\|^2$) forces $C_P \ge L^d/L^2 = L^{d-2} \to \infty$ as
    $L \to \infty$ (this refutes uniformity in $L$, not in $N'$ at fixed $L$).
    \item \emph{Fluctuation-sector wall:} restricting to cochains orthogonal to constants on
    even block side $L = 2M$ (fine side $(2M)N'$, any fixed $N' \ge 1$), a transported
    half-period square-wave mode satisfies $\|QA\|^2 \le (2M)^{-1}\|A\|^2$ and
    $\langle A, K_0 A\rangle = 8((2M)N')^{-1}\|A\|^2$. Because the Poincar\'e predicate carries
    unit weight on $\|QA\|^2$ and $N' \ge 1$, the Rayleigh numerator is at most
    $\bigl(\frac{8}{(2M)N'} + \frac{1}{2M}\bigr)\|A\|^2 \le \frac{9}{2M}\|A\|^2$, forcing
    $C_P \ge 2M/9 \to \infty$ as $M \to \infty$ for every $N' \ge 1$, $d \ge 3$, $N_c \ge 2$,
    and every adjoint model.
  \end{itemize}

  \item \textbf{Flat lattice Hodge factorized-penalty and coarse-rank walls}
  (\texttt{ARR-2026-627PF1B4KK9VTRYT}, \texttt{ARR-2026-7EPM3V3DP796BBRR}, consolidated
  dossier \texttt{papers/flat-coarse-rank/}): on $X_n = (\Z/n\Z)^d$ ($n = \ell N'$,
  $d \ge 2$, $N_c \ge 2$, unit-spacing forward-difference curl $\mathrm{d}A$ and backward
  divergence $\delta A$, $\langle A, K_{0,n} A\rangle = \|\mathrm{d}A\|^2 + \|\delta A\|^2$)
  with the averaged line-integral block map
  $(Q_\ell A)_i(y) = \ell^{-d}\sum_{x \in B_y}\sum_{k=0}^{\ell-1} A_i(x + k e_i)$, pick
  distinct directions $p \neq q$, orthogonal internal unit vectors $u,v \in \R^{N_c^2-1}$,
  $\iota(z) = (\operatorname{Re} z) u + (\operatorname{Im} z) v$, and define for $\ell \ge 2$
  the explicit transverse $\ell$-periodic Fourier witness:
  \[
    A^{(\ell)}_i(x) := \mathbf{1}_{i=p}\,\iota\!\bigl(e^{2\pi i x_q / \ell}\bigr).
  \]
  Because $\sum_{r_q=0}^{\ell-1} e^{2\pi i r_q / \ell} = 0$ and shifts along $p \neq q$
  leave $x_q$ unchanged, $Q_\ell A^{(\ell)} = 0$ identically; meanwhile
  $\delta A^{(\ell)} = 0$, $\|A^{(\ell)}\|^2 = (\ell N')^d$, and
  $\langle A^{(\ell)}, K_{0,n} A^{(\ell)}\rangle = (\ell N')^d \cdot 4\sin^2(\pi/\ell)$
  (nonzero, decaying as $O(\ell^{-2})$ — not in $\ker K_{0,n}$). Consequently, for
  \emph{every} penalty $\Psi_\ell(Q_\ell A)$ satisfying $\Psi_\ell(0) = 0$ (including any
  factorized quadratic form $Q_\ell^* M_\ell Q_\ell$ and nonlinear penalties),
  $C_P(\ell) \ge (4\sin^2(\pi/\ell))^{-1} \ge \ell^2/(4\pi^2) \to \infty$ as
  $\ell \to \infty$. Beyond fixed $Q_\ell$, \texttt{UniformPhysicalCoarseRankWall.lean}
  constructs a low-frequency real subspace $S_R$ of dimension $2(2R+1)^d$ with
  $\langle A, K_{0,n} A\rangle \le (4\pi^2 d R^2/n^2)\|A\|^2$, ruling out uniform coercivity
  from any fixed-dimensional linear coarse measurement plus uniformly bounded-rank fine repair.
