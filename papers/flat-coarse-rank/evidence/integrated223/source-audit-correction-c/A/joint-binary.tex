\label{sec:binary200}

\subsection{A growing-dimensional discrepancy}
Use the family of Theorem~\ref{thm:boundary195-aggregate} on the subsequence
$n=s^{2D}$, $H=s^4$, with even $s\ge2$ and $D\ge4$. Put $d=D-1$,
$m=n/2$ and $T=(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^d$. The exact actual mean and
moment bounds there still hold: $\frac23H\le\mu<H$, $1\le a\le2\mu$
and $1\le M_a<3$.
For $y\ne0$ retain $R_y=\{(0,y),(1,y)\}$, and let $U$ be their union.
Define two distinct quadratic forms on real traces:
\[
 S_U(u)=\min_{f|_U=u}E_a(f),\qquad
 B_{\rm sep}(u)=\sum_{y\ne0}Q_{R_y}(u|_{R_y}).
\]
The grounded-block argument above makes $S_U$ well defined with constant
kernel; it is positive definite on the counting-mean-zero trace hyperplane.
The separate summands minimize different global fields.
Write $C_d=d3^{d-1}/2$, $K_d=3+2C_d$ and $\beta_D=4+2\pi^2d$.
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:jb200-rank}
For each fixed $A>0$ and all sufficiently large even $s$, any real-linear
maps $C:\mathbb R^U\to F$ and $V:\mathbb R^U\to\mathbb R^U$, and any
function $\Phi$ with $\Phi(0)=0$, satisfying
\[
 B_{\rm sep}(u)\le A S_U(u)+\Phi(Cu)+\langle u,Vu\rangle
\]
for all traces, or on a neighborhood of zero, must obey
\[
 \rank C+\rank V\ge L_s=(2s^{D-2}+1)^{D-1}-1
 \ge n^{(D-2)(D-1)/(2D)}.
\]
No symmetry or positivity of $V$, or continuity of $\Phi$, is required.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The five-change comparison in the preceding theorem gives the individually
minimized pair form $g_y(v_0-v_1)^2$ with $g_y\ge H/K_d$; hence
$Q_{R_y}=(g_y-1)(v_0-v_1)^2$.
Set $R=s^{D-2}$, so $R^2=n/H$ and $R<n/2$. The real Fourier space on $T$
with frequencies $|k_i|\le R$ has dimension $(2R+1)^d$: the frequency
box is conjugation symmetric and contains no nonzero Nyquist character.
Evaluation at zero is a nonzero functional since constants belong to the
space. Its kernel $G_R^0$ therefore has dimension $L_s$.
For $g\in G_R^0$ put $u_g(0,y)=g(y)$ and $u_g(1,y)=-g(y)$ for $y\ne0$.
These traces are mean zero; the map is injective because $g(0)=0$, and
$\|u_g\|^2=2\|g\|^2$. For $H\ge2K_d$,
\[
 B_{\rm sep}(u_g)=4\sum_{y\ne0}(g_y-1)|g(y)|^2
 \ge (H/K_d)\|u_g\|^2.
\]
Here the coefficient $g_y$ of the pair form is distinct from the field $g$.

Let $\sigma(t)=1$ on $0,m+1,\ldots,n-1$ and $-1$ on $1,\ldots,m$.
The extension $f_g(t,y)=\sigma(t)g(y)$ is globally mean zero, regardless of
the mean of $g$. Its longitudinal energy is exactly
$8\|g\|^2+4(H-1)|g(0)|^2=8\|g\|^2$. Its transverse energy is
\[
 \sum_y\big[(n-2)H+2+(H-1)\mathbf1_{y=0}\big]
       \sum_{l=1}^d|D_lg(y)|^2\le nH E_T(g).
\]
The derivatives $D_lg(0)$ have not been set to zero. Fourier support gives
$E_T(g)\le4\pi^2dR^2\|g\|^2/n^2$. Therefore
\[
 S_U(u_g)\le E_a(f_g)\le(8+4\pi^2d)\|g\|^2
                      =\beta_D\|u_g\|^2.
\]
When $H>AK_d\beta_D$, every nonzero vector in this $L_s$-dimensional
trace space violates $B_{\rm sep}\le A S_U$. If the combined rank of
$C,V$ were less than $L_s$, their joint kernel in that space would contain
a nonzero vector. Both repair terms vanish there. Scaling that vector
toward zero also handles a neighborhood-only inequality. Finally
$L_s\ge s^{(D-2)(D-1)}$ gives the displayed power of $n$.
\end{proof}
The same subspace gives at least $L_s$ generalized eigenvalues of
$B_{\rm sep}/S_U$ at least $H/(K_d\beta_D)$ on the mean-zero trace
hyperplane, by finite-dimensional min--max. This necessity is not shown
sharp or sufficient. Its exponent is below the allowed $n^{D-1}$ count
for this family. It does not rule out growing-rank repairs or selectable
partitions, and does not address $D=3$ through this comparison.

\subsection{Binary corollary of the existing threshold construction}
The following is an explicit specialization of
Section~\ref{ap:sec:spatial-new}, not a new construction.
\begin{proposition}\label{thm:jb200-binary}
Let $D\ge3,n\ge3$ be integers, $c>0,H\ge1$, $p=D/2$, $N=n^D$,
and let $a:(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^D\to\{1,H\}$ have exact mean $\mu$
and $N^{-1}\sum_x(\mu/a(x))^p\le M$. There is a nonwrapping integer
rectangle partition with normalized scalar means
\[
 (Pf)_R=\frac{\sum_R f}{\sqrt{N|R|}},\qquad
 \|f\|_N^2=N^{-1}\sum_X|f|^2,
\]
satisfying, for every real scalar $f$,
\begin{align}
 N^{-1}E_a(f)+c\|Pf\|_2^2&\ge c\|f\|_N^2,\label{eq:jb200-penalty}\\
 \rank P&\le2^p\big[(1+\sqrt c)^D+2DM\big]
 \max\{1,(n/\sqrt{1+\mu})^D\}.\label{eq:jb200-count}
\end{align}
Here $E_a(f)=\sum_{x,i}a(x)|D_if(x)|^2$ uses positive-start weights.
No upper envelope $a\le2\mu$ is required; $H$ need not be attained.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Use the threshold construction of Section~\ref{ap:sec:spatial-new} with
$\tau=H$. Its geometric and internal-energy argument uses only $a\ge1$;
the standing upper envelope in that section is unused in this argument.
For $H>1$ the set $\{a<H\}$ consists of coefficient-one vertices, whose
actual moment contribution gives $\#\{a<H\}\le MN/\mu^p$.
For $H=1$ that set is empty. Thus the earlier rectangle count, with
$B_c=(1+\sqrt c)^D$ and $1\le\mu\le H$, gives
\[
 J\le B_c\max(1,N/H^p)+2DMN/\mu^p
 \le(B_c+2DM)\max(1,N/\mu^p).
\]
Dividing the scalar version of~\eqref{ap:eq:globalmeans} by $N$ gives
\eqref{eq:jb200-penalty}, since $P=C_{\mathcal P}/\sqrt N$.
The $J$ rows have disjoint nonempty supports, so their rank is $J$.
Finally $1+\mu\le2\mu$ proves~\eqref{eq:jb200-count}.
The singleton branch of the earlier construction also covers $H<c/4$.
\end{proof}
For constant $f=1$, both squared normalized norms equal one and the energy
is zero, so $K\ge c$ is necessary in this convention; the construction
attains $K=c$ without claiming minimum rank. For $r$ independent real
components the means have $rJ$ scalar coordinates, not $J$.
The two-plane comparison family above is included: a failed prescribed
comparison coexists with successful selectable observations.

\subsection{A failed extension to intermediate values}
For even $n$ put $a=H/2$ on half the vertices and $a=H$ on the rest, with
$H\ge2$. Then $\mu=3H/4$, $a\le2\mu$, and
\[
 M_a=\tfrac12[(3/2)^p+(3/4)^p]
\]
is independent of $H$. Nevertheless the attempted unit-cost bound
$|\{a<H\}|\le M_aN/\mu^p$ would require $H^p\le2^p+1$, and fails for
larger $H$. The actual moment does not pay unit cost per intermediate
vertex. Rounding these coefficients down to one changes the hypotheses.
This is a falsifier of that cardinal lemma, not of the target partition
statement: the same field is uniformly strong at scale $H/2$. A derived
multilevel construction remains necessary for the unrestricted question.
