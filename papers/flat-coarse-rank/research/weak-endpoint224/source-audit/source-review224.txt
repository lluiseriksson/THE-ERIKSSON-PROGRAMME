1. ACTUAL COEXACT FIELD AND ALL BOUNDARY STARTS (lines 39-61)

The backward differences commute on the periodic lattice, proving divergence zero; each difference telescopes to zero mean. A unit internal vector exists for every N_c>=2.
More explicitly, the one-coordinate lattice sequence f(r)=p(r/L) is supported at r=1,...,L-1. Its backward difference is supported at r=1,...,L. A_1 uses that backward difference in coordinate 2 and f in all other coordinates; A_2 uses it in coordinate 1. Thus both components vanish on every lower face and outside {0,...,L}^D. If a forward derivative is nonzero, either its start or endpoint meets the support. The only potentially exterior incoming start in its derivative coordinate is -1, whose endpoint is a zero lower face, so it contributes nothing. Upper outgoing starts at L can contribute and are inside the box. There is no uncharged high-coefficient incoming edge. Translating this reasoning gives precisely the coefficient-start support needed in section 6. Periodic wrapping adds zero because the boxes lie strictly away from the wrap.
