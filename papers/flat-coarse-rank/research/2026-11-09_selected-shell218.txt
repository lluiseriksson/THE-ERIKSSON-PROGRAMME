Selected unit shells with actual disjoint rectangular observations
Analytical research supplement218 to the existing shell dossier, not a paper.

0. Question and scope fixed before verification

The sole main question remains the unrestricted critical rectangular problem
of Section27. This unit asks a bounded subquestion: can the original critical
inverse moment itself select a thin shell and supply actual disjoint
rectangles with a proved local gap, rather than an assumed annular gap?
The mechanism is finite radial averaging, a first-exterior-coordinate face
partition and the already proved counting Sobolev inequality. A falsifier is
an actual positive starting-site profile for which the stated selected shell
or face estimate fails. Abandon this mechanism if its moment-to-face bound
fails; do not replace the criterion after seeing a failure. Even success
does not partition or count observations on the complement of the shell.

1. Statement, all normalizations explicit

Fix D>=3, d=D-1, p=d/2, P=D/2, q=2d/(d+2), an integer L>=1,
a center z in Z^D and a scale mu>0. Work in a nonwrapping copy of
Omega={x: L<=||x-z||_infinity<=2L-1}. All coefficients a(x)>0 are actual
original starting-site coefficients. Put b(x)=mu/a(x),

  B=sum_{x in Omega} b(x)^P.

There is an explicitly selected k in {L,...,2L-1} and a partition of
A_k={x:||x-z||_infinity=k} into exactly 2D nonempty axis-aligned integer
rectangles F_{i,s}, i=1,...,D, s=+/-1, such that, for every real f,

 (1) sum_F sum_{x in F}|f(x)-mean_F f|^2
      <= C_D B^(2/D)/mu * sum_F E_{a,F}(f)
      <= C_D B^(2/D)/mu * E_{a,A_k}(f).

Every E_{a,F} sums only retained internal positive-axis edges
(x,x+e_j), with coefficient a(x), once each. No endpoint averaging,
off-shell energies or optimal extensions are supplied. Here one explicit
choice is

 K_2=2;
 for d>=3, c_d=d(1-2^(-1/d)), alpha_d=2(d-1)/d,
 q'=2d/(d-2), K_d=2 alpha_d d^(1/q')/c_d;
 C_D=4 K_d^2 3^(2d/q) d^(2/d).

In particular C_3=2592. These constants are upper bounds, not optimized.
The conclusion holds coordinatewise for real vector fields without an
extra coordinate-count factor. It does not require invariant coefficients,
a preassigned good shell, an assumed local moment, or an annular gap.

If c C_D B^(2/D)/mu<=1, the normalized counting means
q_F(f)=|F|^(-1/2) sum_F f give the actual local penalty estimate

 (2) E_{a,A_k}(f)>=c sum_{A_k}|f|^2-c sum_F|q_F(f)|^2.

This is a rank-2D local observation family per scalar coordinate. It is
not a rank bound for an observation family on the whole ambient domain.

2. The coefficient-driven selection and disjoint faces

For each candidate radius j, set W_j=sum_{A_j} b^P. The L shells are
pairwise disjoint and have union Omega, hence sum_j W_j=B. Choose the
least radius minimizing W_j. It satisfies W_k<=B/L. This is a finite,
coefficient-only rule; it does not inspect f or its energy.

In coordinates relative to z, define

 F_{i,s}= [-k+1,k-1]^(i-1) x {s k} x [-k,k]^(D-i).

Each has dimension d, with every tangential side of length either 2k-1
or 2k+1, including singleton sides at k=1. A shell point belongs to the
unique face determined by the first coordinate whose absolute value is k.
This proves exact disjointness, covering and nonemptiness; these are not
the overlapping slabs in the earlier shell proof. Each face has at most
(2k+1)^d<=(4L)^d sites. Internal face edges are disjoint retained shell
edges. Discarding edges between faces only decreases energy.

3. Actual tangential counting Sobolev estimate

The counting cube inequality in source216 Section2 (its complete cube
dependency is source202, now the sobolev.tex and shells.tex sections of
frozen revision217 at commit2c881a5e8f31cad5643a49cb205fe8eb3156955d)
states for a d-dimensional cube Q and every g:

  V_Q(g)<=K_d^2 (sum_{e internal Q}|dg(e)|^q)^(2/q).

For d=2 this is the coarea/median endpoint q=1,K_2=2; for d>=3 it is
the projection/isoperimetry/coarea/power proof with the above K_d.
No new graph or coefficient theorem is assumed here.

Here is the needed finite face adapter, including k=1. Translate a face
to R=product_{j=1}^d {0,...,ell_j-1}, ell_j in {2k-1,2k+1}. Let
s=2k+1. Fold each interval {0,...,s-1} onto its ell_j-site interval by
the endpoint-repeating sequence
0,1,...,ell_j-1,ell_j-1,...,1,0,0,1,... .
Every original vertex is visited at least once and at most three times,
since ell_j<=s<=3ell_j. For ell_j=1 the map is constant, and its
multiplicity is s<=3 (this case occurs only at k=1).
Every original internal interval edge has at most three preimages: for
k>=2,s<=ell_j+2, so the initial forward traversal and at most two
further steps cross each edge at most three times; for k=1 the possible
ell_j are1 or3, and the assertion is immediate. This deliberately loose
bound also covers ell_j=s.

Let g~ be the folded extension to Q_s. For any constant m,
sum_Q|g~-m|^2>=sum_R|g-m|^2, so after minimizing the left at its own
mean, V_Q(g~)>=V_R(g). Every folded nonconstant cube edge maps to an
actual internal face edge, possibly reversing its orientation, with
total multiplicity at most 3^d. This step is UNWEIGHTED. Therefore

 (3) V_F(f)<=H_d (sum_{e internal F}|df(e)|^q)^(2/q),
     H_d=K_d^2 3^(2d/q).

The face is a d-dimensional graph even though embedded in Z^D. Only d
positive tangential directions occur. Reversing folded edges does not
transport weights: original positive starting-site coefficients enter
only in the next step. Singleton faces have variance and energy zero.

4. Original weights and the critical-to-face moment conversion

Holder on the ORIGINAL retained face edges gives, with p=q/(2-q)=d/2,

 (sum_{e in F}|df|^q)^(2/q)
 <=E_{a,F}(f) (sum_{e in F} a(start e)^(-p))^(1/p)
 <=d^(1/p)/mu E_{a,F}(f) (sum_{x in F} b(x)^p)^(1/p).

Each vertex starts at most d retained tangential edges. No incoming
starting sites are omitted; the site sum is an upper bound on all the
actual starts, not a replacement weight on the observed vertex.

Since p<P, finite Holder on F gives

 (4) (sum_F b^p)^(1/p)
       <= |F|^(1/p-1/P) (sum_F b^P)^(1/P)
       <= (4L)^(2/D) (B/L)^(2/D)
       =4^(2/D) B^(2/D)<=4 B^(2/D).

The cancellation of L uses d(1/p-1/P)=2/D. In particular a global
moment is not simply asserted to be a uniformly bounded local average;
the shell selection and counting factors are both retained. Combining
(3)-(4) proves the first inequality in(1), face by face with the same
constant. Summing uses disjoint internal edges; no gluing of separately
minimized harmonic costs or common-refinement margin is needed.
Equation(2) follows from (1), c*tau<=1 and the exact identity
sum_A|f|^2=sum_F V_F(f)+sum_F |q_F(f)|^2.

5. Scale check and the precise remaining obstruction

The B^(2/D)/mu order cannot be replaced by o(L^2)/mu on homogeneous
profiles with mu fixed as L grows. Indeed, a=mu gives b=1 and
B=(4L-1)^D-(2L-1)^D<= (4L)^D. For every candidate k and f(x)=x_2-z_2,
the face F_{1,+} alone has variance
(2k+1)^d*((2k+1)^2-1)/12. The shell has at most2D(2k+1)^d vertices,
and E_{a,A_k}(f)<=mu |A_k|. Hence

 sum_F V_F(f)/E_{a,A_k}(f)
 >=((2k+1)^2-1)/(24D mu)
 >=L^2/(6D mu)>=B^(2/D)/(96D mu).

This is a genuine all-selected-radii scale check, not a numerical optimal
constant calculation. It uses f on the entire shell, not an isolated
face with ignored cross-face energy.

Most importantly, a unit shell contains only order L^(D-1) sites. The
selected2D rectangles do not cover the interior or the unselected layers.
Repeating this construction independently on order L shells costs order
L observations; that cost cannot be hidden as a dimension-only constant.
The local normalized observations on the chosen shell are derived, but
a coefficient-driven covering/charging theorem with the original
target-order count on the whole domain is STILL MISSING. We do not claim
that the shell is a global partition, that arbitrary holes are covered,
or that no finite penalty can work elsewhere.

Thus the bounded subquestion removes two actual local inputs (supplied
good-shell moment and disjoint-face conversion) for one selected shell.
It removes no hypothesis of the unrestricted main theorem. Earlier
refuted harmonic-cost additivity and stopped common-refinement routes
remain stopped; publication217 did not reset their counters.

6. Originality, relevance and verification boundary

Radial pigeonhole selection, finite reflection, Sobolev and Holder are
classical. This exact starting-site/disjoint-face specialization is a
derived local input in the existing dossier, not established new priority.
Primary comparison: Andres--Deuschel--Slowik, arXiv:1306.2521v5,
Section3.1, Proposition3.5 and its proof explicitly use unweighted
Sobolev plus Holder to obtain weighted estimates; their conductance and
cutoff setup is not this finite face theorem. Birman--Solomyak1967,
Math.USSR-Sbornik2:295-317, Sections1-2, studies adaptive cube partitions
and a set-function theorem; no unsupported zero-smoothness partition
estimate is imported. This limited comparison is not an exhaustive
novelty audit and establishes neither priority nor global applicability.
