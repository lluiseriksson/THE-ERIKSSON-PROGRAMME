2. Elementary 3D point-anchored rectangular Neumann lemma.
Let Q be a product of three finite paths of integer lengths ell_i, with
1<=ell_i<=L and ell_i>=L/2, L>=1 an integer. Count vertices/edges in the
actual path convention. Let N=ell1 ell2 ell3 and E_Q be sum of squared
differences over the internal nearest-neighbor edges once. For ANY corner b
and real scalar f with f(b)=0, we claim
                    ||f||_Q^2<=113 L^3 E_Q(f).       (247.1)
If N=1 this is immediate. Here is a proof, including the point trace constant.
The normalized cosine eigenvectors on a length ell path have squared point
values <=2/ell (the constant mode has1/ell), eigenvalues
4sin^2(pi k/(2ell)),0<=k<ell. Tensor products phi_k form an orthonormal
counting basis on Q, with |phi_k(b)|^2<=8/N and nonzero eigenvalues
 lambda_k>=4 sum_i k_i^2/ell_i^2>=4|k|^2/L^2.
This includes length1 factors, which have only their constant mode. The finite
cosine identities are checked by the path matrix with endpoint diagonal1 and
interior diagonal2, or by the elementary cosine difference identity.

For nonzero nonnegative integer triples let r=max k_i. At shell r>=1 there
are at most(r+1)^3-r^3=3r^2+3r+1<=7r^2 triples and |k|^2>=r^2.
As every k_i<L, sum_(k!=0)1/|k|^2<=7L. Thus
 G_Q(b,b):=sum_(k!=0)|phi_k(b)|^2/lambda_k
           <=(8/N)(L^2/4)7L=14L^3/N<=112.           (247.2)
No continuum Green estimate is imported. Cauchy--Schwarz in this finite
eigenbasis gives |f(b)-mean_Q f|^2<=G_Q(b,b) E_Q(f)<=112 E_Q(f).
Since f(b)=0, N|mean f|^2<=112N E_Q<=112L^3 E_Q. The first nonzero path/
tensor eigenvalue is >=4/L^2, so ||f-mean f||^2<=L^2 E_Q/4.
Orthogonal constant/mean-zero splitting proves247.1 because
112L^3+L^2/4<=113L^3. The lemma also holds for any observed vertex with the
same point-value bound; only corners are used below. Every constant and count
is derived in the finite box. No probabilistic oracle or computation occurs.

3. ALL strong directions give actual three-dimensional component coordinates.
Fix an original internal channel and let its strong-source parity be A
(odd for channel1, even for channel2). For each s in Z/nZ of parity A,
parameterize the source layer and its target layer by y in(Z/nZ)^3:
 x_s(y)=(y1,y2,y3,s-y1-y2-y3) modulo n,
 u_s(y)=z(x_s(y)), v_s(y)=z(x_s(y)+e4).
Here z is one ORIGINAL scalar spatial/internal component; there is no
internally mixed observed variable. x_s has coordinate sum s and x_s+e4 has
sum s+1. Every vertex lies in exactly one such paired layer for this channel,
and the n/2 different s pairs are disjoint. This uses even n through wraps.

The strong +e4 edge is u_s(y)--v_s(y). For i=1,2,3 the strong +ei edge is
 u_s(y)--v_s(y+ei), because x_s(y+ei)+e4=x_s(y)+ei modulo n.
These are ALL4 actual strong outgoing directions, with their original labels.
Thus the strong graph energy in one component is exactly
 E_s=sum_y[|u_s(y)-v_s(y)|^2
                   +sum_(i=1)^3|u_s(y)-v_s(y+ei)|^2].
For n=2 opposite forward terms in the induced y torus may share undirected
neighbors, but remain separately counted actual labels in the inequalities.
No labelled-edge factor is discarded or inserted.

Let E_3(u_s)=sum_(y,i)|u_s(y+ei)-u_s(y)|^2 on the labelled3D torus.
Insert v_s(y+ei) and use |p+q|^2<=2|p|^2+2|q|^2. Summing gives
 E_3(u_s)<=6sum_y|u_s(y)-v_s(y)|^2
              +2sum_(y,i)|u_s(y)-v_s(y+ei)|^2<=6 E_s. (247.3)
The rung term repeats3 times, and this factor is explicitly paid.
The source-weighted positive weak terms are merely nonnegative and will be
ignored; H sum_s E_s is an actual lower part of Q_A for this scalar channel.

5. Realize arbitrary anchors with canonical original rectangles.
For each original channel there are A_alpha=(n/2)m^3 distinct source anchors:
different cell corners are distinct, and different layers s are disjoint.
Their total across both channels is A=n m^3<=524288 n^2.
Start with the whole original chart {0,...,n-1}^4 as one rectangle. Insert
each anchor, in lexicographic original-coordinate order. If not yet singleton,
take its unique containing current rectangle. Slice before and after its
coordinate in direction1, retaining at most two nonempty side boxes; do the
same in directions2,3,4 within the remaining middle box. What remains is the
anchor singleton. At most8 side boxes plus one singleton replace one rectangle,
so the net cell increase is at most8 per insertion. Empty boxes are omitted.
All boxes stay original axis-aligned integer products, disjoint and covering
the chart. Previously isolated singletons are untouched; later anchors may
already be singleton and then cost nothing. No wrap, rotated observed field,
diagonal-slab average or assumed partition-size oracle is used.

For each channel the final partition has <=1+8A_alpha cells, shared over all4
