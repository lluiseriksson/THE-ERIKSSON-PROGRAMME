1. ACTUAL POSITIVE DIAGONAL PROFILE AND EXACT WEAK CHANNEL COUNT
For every m>=1, n=3m+3>=6 and all center coordinates are in the fixed chart. The first-coordinate center range is from 1 to 3m-2, strictly away from a periodic wrap. The other coordinates equal one. Incoming starts x_j-e_i remain in the chart, with coordinate zero allowed.
For each fixed direction i, the two selected starts x_j and x_j-e_i are distinct. Across different centers their first-coordinate sets are contained in {3j,3j+1}, disjoint for distinct j, so no direction-labelled channel is duplicated across centers. Within one center the D outgoing starts are at the same site but in distinct direction channels; the D incoming channels are likewise distinct from those outgoing and from each other. Consequently exactly 2Dm channels, including multiplicity in the canonical (direction,component) basis, have value a0. This verifies lines 27-38 with the required incoming orientation.
The constants alpha_D and sigma are strictly positive and finite for D>=3, c>0. Thus a0=min{c/(8D),sigma/2}>0. H=sigma r n^D>sigma because r=Dh>=3 and n>=6. All matrix eigenvalues are exactly either a0 or H, so the matrices are positive definite. No sign, rotation or nonlocality is required for the lower example. The general upper theorem may allow rotations and signs; this diagonal positive subfamily is sufficient to test its claimed rank dependence.

2. FULL CAPPED BUDGET, INCLUDING ALL STRONG CHANNELS
Write M=r n^D for the total number of local direction/component channels. As sigma^P=alpha_D c^P and a0<=sigma/2, each of the 2Dm weak positive channels has capped cost one. Each remaining channel has cost alpha_D c^P H^-P=M^-P, strictly below one. Hence exactly
 F_c(A)=2Dm+(M-2Dm)M^-P.
The selected channels form an actual subset of the M channels, so M-2Dm>=0. Since P=D/2>1 and M>=1, the strong contribution is at most M^(1-P)<=1. This proves the entire budget interval [2Dm,2Dm+1] in lines 44-46, rather than dropping the many strong channels. Every eigenvalue is positive, so nu_0=0. Increasing h increases H along with the total channel count; the strong tail remains bounded by one, uniformly in h. This h-dependent choice of H is permitted because the family concerns arbitrary finite profiles without a fixed coefficient envelope.

3. ACTUAL LOW SPACE WITH COMPLETE ORIGINAL STARTS
U_j is a one-site delta in component one, of counting norm one. For every direction i its forward difference is -e_1 at x_j and +e_1 at x_j-e_i. There are no other nonzero direction-labelled differences. Both actual original starts were assigned coefficient a0, and both outgoing and incoming energies are therefore charged. The centers and these edges are away from periodic wrap, so no additional wrap edge meets a nonzero endpoint.
It follows exactly that Q_A(U_j)=2D a0<=c/4. The U_j are orthonormal in counting units. Different centers are not nearest neighbors, and their nonzero direction-labelled gradient-start sets are disjoint, as proved in section 1. Since the matrices are diagonal, all mixed energy terms vanish; the stronger spatial start disjointness also makes this direct. Therefore for every U=sum_j t_j U_j,
 ||U||^2=sum_j t_j^2, and Q_A(U)=2D a0 sum_j t_j^2<=c||U||^2/4.
Thus L_m is an actual m-dimensional low space of general R^h-valued functions. It is not a coexact or divergence-zero construction, and cannot by this argument be used as an obstruction on those smaller domains.

4. RESTRICTION RANK, NORMALIZATION AND FINITE PENALTIES
For a partition with q=#R nonempty rectangles, restrict its actual normalized full-vector row C_B to L_m. It becomes
 C_B(sum_j t_j U_j)=V^-1/2 |B|^-1/2 (sum_(j:x_j in B) t_j)e_1.
Hence each rectangle supplies at most one scalar functional on the low space, even though its output contains h component coordinates. The restriction rank is at most q; indeed it is the number of partition pieces meeting a center, since their sets of center indices are disjoint. This confirms lines 69-70 and makes the geometry irrelevant to the rank bound.
If q<m, rank-nullity gives nonzero U in L_m with every observation row zero. Multiplying the normalized coercivity inequality by V gives c||U||^2<=Q_A(U), which contradicts the low-space bound. This is independent of how large a finite quadratic penalty coefficient K is chosen. The same direct contradiction applies to any penalty on these linear observations satisfying Phi(0)=0; differentiability, positivity or quadratic growth of that penalty is unnecessary. Common normalization of energy and norm by V changes none of these conclusions. Thus every successful common partition has q>=m.
On the full field domain the observation map has rank exactly hq, not just a padded codomain of that size. For each rectangle B choose one of its sites z. A field supported at z with value sqrt(V|B|)e_k has observation e_k in row B and zero in every other row. These fields demonstrate surjectivity onto all hq row coordinates. Consequently genuine full-space observation rank is at least hm for every successful partition. On L_m the rank requirement is only m; the additional h factor is a cost of retaining every component row on the common partition, not h independent low-space tests per rectangle.

5. h=m CONTRADICTION AND MATCHING PRODUCT ORDER
Setting h=m leaves the weak channel count unchanged and keeps the strong contribution bounded by one. Necessary full-space rank is at least m^2, while h+F_c(A)<=(2D+1)m+1. For fixed D and c their ratio tends to infinity with m. Thus no constant depending only on D can give the stated C_D(h+F) upper rank in this observation class; even allowing dependence on the fixed c would not rescue it.
For arbitrary h>=1 in this family, F_c(A)<=2Dm+1<=(2D+1)m gives hm>=hF_c(A)/(2D+1). This proves the displayed worst-case product lower bound. The225 analytic upper bound is O_D(h(1+F_c(A))) with some finite profile-dependent penalty, and its constants depend only on D for the dimension count. Along this family F>=2Dm>=2D, so 1+F is comparable to F. The two bounds therefore match in hF order in worst-case joint h,F growth, without determining sharp coefficients or a per-profile minimum. At h=1 the result reduces to linear dependence on F, as claimed.
