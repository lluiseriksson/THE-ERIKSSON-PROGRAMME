BOUNDED QUESTION AND EXIT
For D>=5, can arbitrary channel-separable linear observations restore
coercivity of positive constant coupled gradient forms with true coexact
restricted rank bounded by C_D(h+F)? The candidate answer is no.
Retained hypothesis: rows never mix distinct internal channels in the
specified fixed basis. Within a channel they may mix all sites and all D
spatial field components. No claim about all linear maps or D=4.
Mechanism: spread one weak internal polarization uniformly across g channels;
trace-average their row-space projections inside the actual coexact domain.
Falsifier: a wrong actual coexact dimension, projection not preserving that
domain, hidden multiplicity in the full budget, or a failed energy bound.
Abandon an uncorrectable mechanism, preserving its counterexample; after three
units without substantive progress change route. No basis-free restatement.

1. EXACT FINITE SETUP
Let D>=5, n>=2, X=(Z/nZ)^D, V=n^D, and g=V. Real D-vector fields
have counting inner product sum_{x,j} f_j(x)z_j(x). Write
partial_i^+f(x)=f(x+e_i)-f(x), and let d_1 be the actual forward curl.
Set U_0=im(d_1^*) and U=U_0^g. The codifferential is the actual counting
adjoint, not a substituted quadratic form. On the periodic flat torus,
d_0=(D-1)(V-1)=dim_R U_0. One direct verification: a nonzero frequency
has nonzero q=(exp(2 pi i k_i/n)-1)_i; curl q wedge has rank D-1,
and its adjoint image is the orthogonal complement of q. Zero frequency
contributes zero. Conjugate-frequency pairing gives the same real dimension.
In particular every component of every coexact field has zero global mean.
There are h=Dg real field components and r=Dh=D^2 g gradient coordinates.
All row-rank and projection statements below use the actual space U.

Fix c>0, a=c/(16D), H=c n^4, v=(1,...,1)/sqrt(g), and
B=H I_g-(H-a) v v^T. The coefficient matrix is constant in x and
A_{(i,j,alpha),(i',j',beta)}=delta_ii' delta_jj' B_alpha,beta.
It is strictly positive. It couples internal channels while preserving the
gradient-direction and spatial-component coordinates. Its eigenvalues at
each site are a with multiplicity D^2 and H with multiplicity D^2(g-1).
Define Q_A(u)=sum_{x,i,j,alpha,beta} B_alpha,beta
 partial_i^+u_j,alpha(x) partial_i^+u_j,beta(x).
Normalized norm is ||u||_V^2=V^-1||u||^2, and normalized energy V^-1 Q_A.

2. FULL CAPPED BUDGET, WITHOUT DROPPING STRONG MULTIPLICITIES
Use P=D/2 and alpha_D=(3/4)^D (D/2)(D/(D-2))^(D-1), exactly as226C.
The spectral cost at positive lambda is min(1,alpha_D(c/lambda)^P).
Let sigma=alpha_D^(1/P)c. Since sigma/c>=9/16 and a/c=1/(16D),
each weak eigenvalue costs1. Strong cost is at most alpha_D n^(-2D).
Thus the exact full budget is
 F=D^2 V[1+(g-1)min(1,alpha_D n^(-2D))]
 <=D^2 V+D^2 alpha_D, since g=V=n^D.
Consequently h+F <=(D+D^2)V+D^2 alpha_D. No negative eigenvalues,
uncontrolled depth, removed sites or omitted strong tail are involved.

3. CHANNEL-SEPARABLE OBSERVATIONS AND AN ACTUAL-DOMAIN PROJECTION
Let C=direct_sum_{alpha=1}^g C_alpha, where each C_alpha is any finite
real linear observation on a full D-vector field, restricted to U_0.
Only this internal separability is required; rectangular component means
in230 belong to this class. Set r_C=rank(C|U)=sum_alpha rank(C_alpha|U_0).
For each alpha let R_alpha be the counting orthogonal projection in U_0
onto the row-representer space of C_alpha|U_0. R=direct_sum R_alpha is
an orthogonal projection IN U, with rank r_C and ker R=ker(C|U).
These projections need not preserve locality, gradients or individual
spatial components. They do preserve U_0 because they are defined there.
This is a legitimate decomposition by internal channel; there is no false
decomposition of U_0 by its individual spatial field components.

Define the isometry T:U_0 -> U by (Tf)_alpha=v_alpha f. Then
 T^* R T=(1/g)sum_alpha R_alpha,
 trace(T^* R T)=r_C/g.
There is a real unit f in U_0 with
 t=||R Tf||^2 <= r_C/(g d_0).
Indeed the minimum Rayleigh quotient of the real symmetric positive
operator T^*RT is at most its trace divided by d_0. Here d_0>0.
Take u=(I-R)Tf. It lies in the actual coexact U, satisfies Cu=0,
and has ||u||^2=1-t by orthogonal projection and ||Tf||=1.

4. ENERGY AND NECESSARY TRUE RANK
For every torus vector field, sum_i ||partial_i^+z||^2<=4D||z||^2,
since shifts are isometries. On the weak polarization,
 Q_A(Tf)=a sum_i||partial_i^+f||^2<=4Da.
Since 0<A<=H I,
 Q_A(RTf)<=4DH||RTf||^2=4DHt.
The positive quadratic form inequality Q(x-y)<=2Q(x)+2Q(y) gives
 Q_A(u)<=8Da+8DHt.
Suppose an arbitrary penalty Phi on the observation output, with Phi(0)=0,
satisfies for every actual coexact z
 V^-1 Q_A(z)+Phi(Cz)>=c||z||_V^2.
Applying it to u and multiplying by V yields
 c(1-t)<=8Da+8DHt,
so t>=(c-8Da)/(c+8DH)=1/[2(1+8D n^4)].
Combining with the trace bound proves the necessary actual restricted rank
 r_C >= g(D-1)(V-1)/[2(1+8D n^4)].
This holds for nonlinear penalties too; only their value at zero was used.
There is no output-count versus actual-image-rank substitution.
The case t=1 is harmless; the same algebra remains valid even when u=0.

For fixed D>=5 and n tending to infinity, the lower bound is of order
n^(2D-4), whereas h+F is O_D(n^D). Their ratio diverges as n^(D-4).
Thus no constant depending only on D can bound the restoring restricted
rank by C_D(h+F) for this entire channel-separable class. The profile is
finite, constant, positive, and its internal dimension varies with n.

5. ESSENTIAL BASIS BOUNDARY: A CHEAP MIXED OBSERVATION EXISTS
The preceding theorem is not invariant under a change of the internal
separability basis. For the SAME coefficient profile allow cross-channel
mixing and observe Wz=sum_alpha v_alpha z_alpha at every site/component,
with rows V^-1/2 (Wz)_j(x). This map has actual restricted rank d_0.
Orthogonally split z=T(Wz)+z_strong within U. The split is constant in
space, hence commutes with forward differences and preserves coexactness.
All components of z_strong have zero global mean. The first positive
forward torus Laplacian eigenvalue is lambda_1=4sin^2(pi/n)>=16/n^2.
Therefore H lambda_1>=16 c n^2>=c, and
 Q_A(z_strong)>=c||z_strong||^2.
Adding c times the squared output norm controls the weak part:
 V^-1 Q_A(z)+c||C_weak z||^2>=c||z||_V^2.
The rank d_0 is O_D(V)=O_D(h+F). Thus the obstruction cannot be extended
to arbitrary linear observations, even on this exact family. A fixed
global internal rotation can expose the cheap weak channel. The result
identifies a row-class and chosen-basis restriction, not a universal
spectral-budget failure. At D=4 these powers do not yield divergence;
no four-dimensional or physical mass-gap conclusion is claimed.
