Weak axial filaments: a sharp repair cost for a prescribed internal certificate
Research supplement219 to the existing flat-coarse-rank dossier, not a paper.

0. Bounded question fixed before verification

The sole main question is still the original unrestricted critical rectangular
problem. Source218 derives one coefficient-selected unit shell and actual
disjoint face observations. A tempting global conversion is to decompose a
punctured cube by the first nonzero coordinate, discard all intercell edges,
and infer a uniform residual gap from the original critical moment. We test
that precise INTERNAL-ENERGY certificate, including coordinate-order choice.
The falsifier must retain actual mean, upper envelope and bounded critical
moment; failing only an unrelated moment surrogate is insufficient. Stop this
specific conversion if a genuine family defeats it. No inferred failure of
the actual full energy or of every allowed partition is permitted.

We construct such a family for every D>=3 and obtain a matching-order repair
rank. The same family has a proved one-rectangle solution for the actual
energy. This distinguishes a failed sufficient certificate from the main
problem. Earlier separately minimized harmonic-cost counterexamples are not
being recounted: the form below is the sum of actual retained internal edges,
with no minimizers or Schur complements.

1. Actual coefficient family and original hypotheses

Fix an integer D>=3 and s>=1. Put

 R=s^D, n=2R+1, X={-R,...,R}^D, N=n^D,
 A=s^2, H=n^2, w=2DR.

Interpret X either as a nonwrapping integer cube or as representatives of
the periodic n-site lattice. Let W consist of the 2D punctured coordinate
half-axes: x has exactly one nonzero coordinate. Then |W|=w. Define actual
positive starting-site coefficients a=A on W and a=H elsewhere, including
at the origin. With P=D/2 the exact mean and inverse critical mass are

 (1) mu=H-w(H-A)/N,
     S=sum_X a^(-P)=2D+1-w/N,
     M_a=N^(-1)sum_X(mu/a)^P=(mu/H)^P S<2D+1.

Indeed H^P=N, A^P=R, so the w weak sites contribute exactly2D to S.
Also N=(2R+1)^D>=2R*3^(D-1), hence
w/N<=D/3^(D-1)<=1/3 for D>=3. Since H>A>=1,

 1<=a<=H<=3mu/2<=2mu,  mu>=2H/3>=1.

Jensen applied to t^(-P) and mean a=mu gives M_a>=1. Thus every member
obeys the original coefficient hypotheses with the fixed M_D=2D+1.
The target-order factor is uniformly bounded:

 max(1,(n/sqrt(1+mu))^D)<=2^(D/2).

No rare-set density, moment or mean has been supplied independently of a.

2. The fixed family of canonical full-volume partitions

For any permutation pi of {1,...,D}, define the cells

 B_{i,+}: x_{pi(j)}=0 for j<i,
          1<=x_{pi(i)}<=R,
          -R<=x_{pi(j)}<=R for j>i;
 B_{i,-}: the same with -R<=x_{pi(i)}<=-1.

Together with {0}, these 2D+1 nonempty axis-aligned integer rectangles
partition X exactly: use the first nonzero coordinate in the order pi.
Let P_0 have the normalized COUNTING-L2 rows
q_B(f)=|B|^(-1/2)sum_B f; its exact scalar rank is2D+1.
Let

 E_int,pi(f)=sum_{B in partition} sum_{x,x+e_j in B}
                                      a(x)|f(x+e_j)-f(x)|^2.

Original positive-axis starts are retained, once each. Periodic wrap and
intercell edges are discarded only in E_int,pi. The actual full energy E_a
retains all nonwrapping edges, and optionally the additional nonnegative
periodic edges. Thus E_int,pi<=E_a, but the reverse comparison is not assumed.

The final pair B_{D,+},B_{D,-} are precisely the two weak half-axes of
coordinate pi(D). This holds for EVERY choice of pi, including a choice
depending on the coefficient profile. The family W includes all axes so
merely changing the coordinate order does not evade the obstruction.

3. The actual energy admits one rectangular mean

Let

 c_D=1/[64 D^2(2D+1)].

For every real f on X,

 (2) E_a(f)+c_D |N^(-1/2)sum_X f|^2>=c_D sum_X |f|^2.

Here E_a can be the full nonwrapping energy; adding periodic wrap preserves
the conclusion. Consequently the one-cell partition {X} solves this actual
family at a fixed positive c_D. Dividing(2) by N gives the original
probability-normalized convention, with its one row equal to mean_X f.

Proof, including a rational dimension-only constant. The complete counting
cube proof is sobolev.tex at source2c881a5e8f31cad5643a49cb205fe8eb3156955d.
For q=2D/(D+2), q'=2D/(D-2), alpha=2(D-1)/D and
c_iso=D(1-2^(-1/D)), it gives

 V_X(f)<=K_D^2(sum_internal |df|^q)^(2/q),
 K_D=2 alpha D^(1/q')/c_iso.

Bernoulli gives (1-1/(2D))^D>=1/2, hence c_iso>=1/2 and
K_D<=8 sqrt(D). Holder on ORIGINAL internal edges and the starting-site
count at most D gives

 V_X(f)<=K_D^2(D S)^(2/D) E_a(f)
        <=64D [D(2D+1)]^(2/D) E_a(f)
        <=64D^2(2D+1) E_a(f),

where 2/D<=1. Finally sum_X f^2=V_X(f)+|N^(-1/2)sum_X f|^2 proves(2).
No coefficient reflection, internal-certificate comparison or new unproved
gap is used. This is an analytic consequence of the proved cube estimate,
not a new formal execution claim or optimal gap constant.

4. A direct failed residual-gap certificate

Fix pi and use its positive final half-axis, indexed t=1,...,R. For R>=2
let f_t=t-(R+1)/2 there and set f=0 elsewhere. Then P_0f=0 and exactly

 E_int,pi(f)=A(R-1),
 ||f||_2^2=R(R^2-1)/12,
 E_int,pi(f)/||f||_2^2=12s^2/[R(R+1)] ->0 as s->infinity.

Thus there is no uniform positive residual gap for these canonical cells
under the bounded ORIGINAL critical moment, and no coefficient of ||P_0f||^2
can fix this failure. This is a statement about E_int,pi, NOT about E_a.

The omitted edges explain the difference rather than conceal it. At every
point of that weak half-axis, each of its D-1 transverse incoming positive
edges starts at a point with two nonzero coordinates and hence coefficient H.
Their other endpoint has f=0. They are all actual distinct nonwrapping edges,
so for every field supported on that half-axis, or on both final half-axes,

 (3) E_a(f)>=(D-1)H ||f||_2^2.

Their starts have not been reassigned the weak value A. The actual full
energy is uniformly strong on these fields, consistent with(2).

5. A growing-dimensional exact low internal-energy space

Put T_D=32D(D+1), ell=T_D s, and

 m_s=floor((R-1)/(4ell)).

Define a signed tent on integer t=0,...,4ell by

 g(t)=t                 for 0<=t<=ell;
      2ell-t           for ell<=t<=3ell;
      t-4ell           for 3ell<=t<=4ell.

It has zero endpoints, g(4ell-t)=-g(t), zero counting sum, and exact values

 (4) sum_t g(t)^2=2ell(2ell^2+1)/3,
     sum_{t=0}^{4ell-1}|g(t+1)-g(t)|^2=4ell.

For the first identity, the two triangular lobes give
4 sum_{j=1}^{ell-1}j^2+2ell^2; substitute the finite square-sum formula.
Every consecutive increment is1 or-1, proving the second identity.

On each of the final positive and negative half-axes, place m_s copies on
the intervals [1+4ell j,1+4ell(j+1)], j=0,...,m_s-1, using |x_{pi(D)}|
as index. Their nonzero supports are disjoint. Adjacent supports share at
most a zero endpoint, so their internal energy cross terms also vanish.
All intervals lie in [1,R]. Every tent has zero mean on its whole cell.
The resulting real span G_s has dimension2m_s and is contained in ker P_0.
For every f in this span,

 E_int,pi(f)=rho_s ||f||_2^2,
 rho_s=6A/(2ell^2+1)<=3/T_D^2<=c_D/4.

The last inequality follows from
T_D^2=1024D^2(D+1)^2>=768D^2(2D+1)=12/c_D,
since 4(D+1)^2>=3(2D+1). This is an exact all-size construction; if m_s=0
the lower bound is vacuous, and m_s grows of order s^(D-1) as s increases.
On the negative half-axis positive-axis orientation reverses the tent
index, but all retained line starts still have the SAME original value A.
Only this internal line calculation uses that constancy; transverse incoming
starts in the full energy still have coefficient H.

6. Sharp-order rank needed to repair this prescribed certificate

For any fixed pi, suppose real-linear maps C:R^X->F and V:R^X->R^X and
any function Phi with Phi(0)=0 satisfy, for all f, or in a neighborhood of0,

 (5) E_int,pi(f)+K||P_0f||^2+Phi(Cf)+<f,Vf>>=c_D||f||^2.

Here K is any finite scalar; no symmetry/positivity of V or continuity of Phi
is needed. Then

 (6) rank C+rank V>=2m_s=Omega_D(s^(D-1))=Omega_D(R^(1-1/D)).

If the combined rank were smaller, the joint kernel of C and V restricted
to G_s would contain a nonzero f. All three repair terms vanish there,
whereas E_int,pi(f)<=c_D||f||^2/4<c_D||f||^2. Scaling f toward zero proves
the neighborhood version. This is the elementary rank-nullity mechanism,
not an original general rank-repair principle. The lower bound is valid
for each pi, so it also applies to selecting any one of these coordinate
orders from a. It does not apply to arbitrary rectangular partitions.

The order is sufficient for this internal-certificate task by an explicit
refinement. In each B_{i,+/-}, retain its one weak coordinate half-axis and
partition its complement by the first nonzero later coordinate. This gives
2(D-i) STRONG integer rectangles per cell. Every such point has at least
two nonzero coordinates, hence actual coefficient H. Together there are
2D(D-1) strong rectangles. Split each of the2D weak half-axes into
R/s=s^(D-1) consecutive intervals of length s. Retain the singleton origin.
These are disjoint integer rectangles covering X, with exact total count

 (7) J_s=1+2D(D-1)+2D s^(D-1).

All strong cells have largest side at most n and coefficient H=n^2; all
weak intervals have length s and coefficient A=s^2. The derived product
interval variance bound V_B<=L_B(L_B-1) sum_internal|df|^2/4 therefore
gives E_{a,B}>=4V_B whenever V_B is nonzero. Singletons have zero variance.
Summing disjoint retained edges and the exact normalized-mean identity
proves the fixed-penalty inequality with coefficient K=c_D and these J_s
rows, even for the refined internal energy. That energy is no greater than
the base E_int,pi, so the same rows also supply a repair in(5), by taking
Phi(Cf)=c_D||P_ref f||^2 and retaining any nonnegative base penalty.
Since c_D<=1, there is no
division by a vanishing side or gap. Thus the matching repair order is
Theta_D(s^(D-1)); no optimal multiplicative constant is asserted.

7. What is refuted, and what remains open

This family has fixed original moment M_D and a bounded target-order factor,
and admits an actual one-rectangle solution. Nevertheless a natural
full-volume canonical decomposition, for EVERY coordinate order, needs
growing-rank repair if it relies only on its separated internal energy.
The order of this certificate loss is derived and matched constructively.
The route “transfer the shell gap to these full-volume cells and discard
their interfaces for free” is therefore STOPPED_BY_COUNTEREXAMPLE.

The example does not defeat the actual energy or every admissible partition;
it explicitly supplies a successful one. It neither removes a hypothesis of
the original unrestricted problem nor licenses an internal-energy conclusion
on coexact/divergence-constrained fields. It concerns the scalar sufficient
certificate; no interacting or continuum Yang--Mills assertion follows.
Source218's selected unit-shell theorem remains valid: its faces are nearly
balanced in their tangential dimensions, unlike the full-volume cells here.
Do not substitute this family for its coefficient-only selected-shell theorem.
