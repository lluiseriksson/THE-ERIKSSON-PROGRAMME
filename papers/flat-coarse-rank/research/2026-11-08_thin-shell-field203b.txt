THIN-SHELL203B: AN ACTUAL FIELD OBSTRUCTION FOR THE FIXED SLAB COVER
2026-11-08. Supplement to source203, kept in the same research dossier.

1. Exact scope
This strengthens source203 Section5 from a lower bound on its sufficient
tree quantity to an actual field falsifier of uniform squared-error gluing
for that same thin-shell slab cover. It is not a counterexample to a
thin-shell Poincare estimate, a different cover or the main critical
rectangle question. It is not a new paper, formal certificate or originality
claim. Source203 remains unchanged with its own frozen-source audit.

2. Setup and the field
Use the exact integer definitions of source203: D>=2, R>=1, 0<=r<R,
t=R-r, L=2R+1, J=2D, A=[-R,R]^D\[-r,r]^D (integer points),
and the J full-width face slabs S_(j,+),S_(j,-).
Each slab has v=t L^(D-1) points; slabs of different coordinates intersect
in theta=t^2 L^(D-2)=beta v, where beta=t/L lies strictly between0 and1/2.
Write N=|A|=L^D-(2r+1)^D=L^D-(L-2t)^D.
Define ONE scalar function on the whole shell, independent of any tree:
 f(x)=1 if x belongs to S_(1,+), and f(x)=0 otherwise.
This is a consistent pointwise assignment even at overlapping corners.

3. All local and global errors calculated exactly
For a0/1 field with h ones on a finite set of n points, its unnormalized
squared best-constant error is h(1-h/n), by direct expansion around h/n.
The root slab has f identically1, and the opposite first-coordinate slab
has f identically0. Each therefore has squared error0.
For every slab of coordinate j>=2 and either sign, exactly theta=beta v
points have f=1, since membership in S_(1,+) imposes the first-coordinate
condition, independently of the j condition. Its error is
 e_S(f)^2=beta v(1-beta).
There are J-2 such slabs, giving the exact sum
 sum_S e_S(f)^2=(J-2) v beta(1-beta)>0.                 (3.1)
Globally exactly v points have f=1, so
 e_A(f)^2=v(1-v/N).                                  (3.2)
The two first-coordinate slabs are disjoint subsets of A, each of volume
v. Thus N>=2v and 1-v/N>=1/2. Combining(3.1)-(3.2),
 e_A(f)^2 / sum_S e_S(f)^2
   =(1-v/N)/[(J-2)beta(1-beta)]
   >=1/[2(J-2)beta(1-beta)]
   >=L/[2(J-2)t].                                   (3.3)
All quantities use counting measure. No limit theorem, approximate count,
optimization, boundary extension or computational experiment is used.

4. Falsified assertion and precise limitations
Take r=R-1,t=1 and let R tend to infinity, with fixed D>=2. Then L grows
and(3.3) diverges. Consequently there is no finite dimension-only constant
C_D satisfying
 e_A(f)^2 <= C_D sum_S e_S(f)^2
for every one of these thin-shell slab covers and every field on its union.
This holds regardless of the root/tree selected: the function and the local
error sum do not involve a tree. It is stronger than divergent Q_T alone.
For unsquared errors the same obstruction follows from
(sum_S e_S)^2 <= J sum_S e_S^2: the ratio e_A/sum_S e_S is unbounded too.

It is not an additional fixed-rho counterexample to the generic adjacent-
overlap assertion discussed and refuted in201B, since here beta=t/L tends
to0. It also does not refute
the thick-shell bound203, where beta>=1/5 for R=2m,r=m. Indeed the upper
bound203 grows like L/t, consistent with(3.3); the deterioration order of
the squared-error gluing constant for this cover is therefore L/t, up to
dimension constants. This is an order statement for the gluing constant,
not the optimal internal-energy Poincare constant.

The field has many nonzero edge differences on the boundary of S_(1,+).
No claim that its energy is small is made. Transferring(3.3) to a spectral
or weighted-energy impossibility without a separate energy calculation
would be invalid. The slabs overlap and do not constitute an admissible
disjoint rectangle partition. A different coefficient-dependent partition
may work. Nothing here resolves the main rectangle or Yang-Mills problem.
