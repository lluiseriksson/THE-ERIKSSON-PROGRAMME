1. SPECTRAL CAP IDENTITY, INCLUDING EQUALITY AND NONPOSITIVE CHANNELS
For D>=3, P=D/2>1 and the displayed alpha_D is finite and strictly positive. Setting sigma=alpha_D^(1/P)c gives sigma^P=alpha_D c^P. For a positive eigenvalue lambda<=sigma, alpha_D c^P lambda^-P>=1 and its capped summand is exactly one. For lambda>sigma it is strictly less than one and equals alpha_D c^P lambda^-P. Every nonpositive eigenvalue is selected and contributes one via nu_0, without evaluating an inverse power at zero or a negative number. Thus F=N+alpha_D c^P sum_unselected lambda^-P holds exactly with multiplicity. Eigenvalues exactly at sigma are selected and contribute precisely one. In particular 0<=N<=F and |I|<=N.
Degeneracies at the cutoff are handled by the full spectral projector. This defines the replacement without choosing a preferred eigenbasis within a degenerate eigenspace. No common eigenbasis across different sites is needed. No external spectral counting inequality is used in this proof: the specific displayed positive alpha_D supplies the cap threshold and constants. Its historical attribution as a classical spectral constant is not a separate spectral-bound certification by this audit.

2. POSITIVE REPLACEMENT AND SCALAR BUDGET (lines 55-69)
T=sigma+c(1+N)>sigma. Selected channels become T>0 and all unselected channels were already >sigma>0, so A_T is positive definite. Its difference from A is positive semidefinite on the selected spectral subspace and zero on the complement. N=0 gives exactly A_T=A, with no replacement.
For a_T=lambda_min(A_T), pointwise v^T A_T v>=a_T||v||^2 for the complete Dh-dimensional stacked vector. This justifies the scalar energy domination even for matrices coupling all direction and internal coordinates. Also a_T^-P is the largest eigenvalue of A_T^-P and is no greater than its trace. Summing traces gives the unselected inverse powers plus N T^-P. Since T>=c(1+N), N(c/T)^P<=N/(1+N)^P<=1; the estimate is valid also at N=0. The identity in section 1 therefore proves exactly c^P sum_x a_T^-P<=(F-N)/alpha_D+1. The added one is loose but harmless and is carried into the stated count.

4. POINT ISOLATION, EXACT COUNT AND CAUCHY SCALING (lines 85-101)
Each bad start x and its D forward endpoints are included in Z=I union union_i(I+e_i), with periodic addition. Thus |Z|<=(D+1)|I|<=(D+1)N, regardless of repeated or coincident endpoints. Represent sites in the fixed chart {0,...,n-1}^D.
For a rectangle R containing z, the points in R other than z are partitioned by the first coordinate in which they differ from z and by the lower or upper side in that coordinate. Preceding coordinates are fixed to z, the current coordinate is an integer interval below or above z, and later coordinates range over their original intervals. Every resulting nonempty piece is a rectangle; there are at most 2D pieces. Adding {z} gives at most 2D+1 pieces in place of one, hence an increase of at most 2D, not 2D+1. Already isolated points lie in disjoint singleton members and remain untouched. Iterating in any fixed coefficient-only order, for example lexicographic order on Z, proves R<=R_0+2D|Z|. Periodic endpoints are simply their chart representatives, so a wrapping edge presents no obstruction to this refinement.
For each refined parent B with children B_k, its vector row equals sum_k sqrt(|B_k|/|B|) C_(B_k)u. The squared weights sum to one. Hilbert-space Cauchy therefore gives ||C_Bu||^2<=sum_k||C_(B_k)u||^2, including the exact common V^-1/2 normalization and all h components. Summing over parents proves line 99. Each z in Z is a distinct singleton row V^-1/2 u(z), so line 100 follows exactly. There is no hidden gradient observation or nonrectangular observation row.

5. FULL SIGNED CORRECTION, MIXING AND WRAPS (lines 103-120)
For N>0, L=max_selected(T-lambda) is finite and positive because there are finitely many finite eigenvalues. The pointwise operator inequality is 0<=A_T-A<=L Id, with zero correction away from I. It controls the entire rotated matrix correction, including every direction/internal cross term. It does not assume that individual cross terms have a sign or discard them separately.
Thus Q_(A_T)-Q_A<=L sum_(x in I) sum_i||u(x+e_i)-u(x)||^2. Applying ||v-w||^2<=2||v||^2+2||w||^2 gives line 111. For a fixed site z, its own bad-start contribution has multiplicity at most D, and there are at most D incoming direction-labelled starts x=z-e_i. Therefore the coefficient inside the brackets is at most 2D. Multiplying by 2L proves the displayed 4DL bound on sum_Z||u(z)||^2. It remains valid at n=2, where opposite oriented periodic edges may be parallel, because those are separately counted direction-labelled starts. At n=1 all differences vanish and the stated bound is harmless. No original wrap start, incoming endpoint, or negative-depth contribution is omitted.
After division by V the singleton row inequality bounds the complete correction by 4DL||C_ref u||^2. Starting from c||u||_normalized^2<=V^-1 Q_(A_T)+c||C_0u||^2, inserting the correction and the refinement inequality gives exactly
 V^-1 Q_A+(c+4DL)||C_refu||^2>=c||u||_normalized^2.
All of T,L, the partition and K=c+4DL are fixed by A and fixed parameters before u is chosen. Hence the penalty is finite and uniform in u. It can grow with the negative spectral depth; no profile-independent K, K=c for signed forms, or control of that growth by F is proved. For N=0, L=0 and K=c is justified.

6. COMPLETE OUTPUT DIMENSION AND WHAT IT MEANS (lines 122-129)
Combining R_0 and the refinement gives hR<=2Dh[1+B_D+(B_D/alpha_D)(F-N)+(D+1)N]. Both F-N and N are nonnegative, so their weighted sum is bounded by max{B_D/alpha_D,D+1}F. This is exactly the proposed count, with no lost h or D factor. On the full u-space, disjoint nonempty rectangle means are linearly independent component by component, so the observation map has rank hR as well as output dimension hR. On a constrained subdomain the rank can only decrease. Neither optimal rank nor a sharp spectral coefficient is established.
The optional all-singleton alternative works for any finite signed matrix profile because it measures the whole field and any finite-dimensional symmetric quadratic form has a finite lower bound relative to the counting norm. A sufficiently large finite quadratic penalty therefore restores c-coercivity. Selecting the smaller of the two coefficient-only partitions is legitimate. This optional observation is not needed for the main budget bound.
