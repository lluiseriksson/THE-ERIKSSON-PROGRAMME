matrix gradient coefficients control a coefficient-only partition into
integer rectangles, with actual normalized component means and some finite
quadratic penalty? No assumed geometric/local-gap/extension certificate.
Mechanism: positive spectral replacement, scalar minimum-eigenvalue

FINITE ACTUAL OBJECTS
D>=3,n>=1,h>=1,c>0; X=(Z/nZ)^D, V=n^D and r=Dh.
Use the actual common counting inner product, u:X->R^h, and forward starts
 du(x)=(u(x+e_i)-u(x))_(i=1..D) in R^r.
A(x) is any finite real symmetric r by r matrix; matrices at different
sites need not commute. Set Q_A(u)=sum_x du(x)^T A(x)du(x).
Norm and energy may both be divided byV. No weighted curl identity or
interacting/covariant dictionary is assumed or proved.

Write P=D/2 and the classical finite spectral constant
 alpha_D=(D/2)(D/(D-2))^(D-1)(3/4)^D >0.
Let nu_0 count all nonpositive local eigenvalues with multiplicity, and
 F_c(A)=nu_0+sum_(x,j:lambda_j(x)>0) min{1,alpha_D c^P lambda_j(x)^(-P)}.
These are actual local coefficients, not eigenvalues of the global operator.
All eigenvalues are finite; F is finite and nonnegative. Define
 sigma=alpha_D^(1/P)c,
 S_x=the local spectral subspace of A(x) for eigenvalues<=sigma,
 N=sum_x dim(S_x), I={x:dim(S_x)>0}.
Then exactly
 F=N+alpha_D c^P sum_(unselected channels) lambda^(-P).
There is no assumption on channel orientation, sign depth or shared eigenbasis.

Let b=2^D,J=2D,C_ext=1+J[2J(1+12J)+1],

The actual output dimension h times the number of rectangles is bounded by

1. ACTUAL POSITIVE LOCAL REPLACEMENT
Set T=sigma+c(1+N)>sigma. Replace each selected local eigenvalue byT,
leaving every unselected eigenvalue and local eigenspace unchanged; call
the actual resulting positive definite matrix A_T(x).
This is defined by the spectral projector of the original finite matrix;
degenerate eigenvalues at the cutoff are all selected, with no basis choice.
For N=0 no coefficient is changed. Set a_T(x)=lambda_min(A_T(x))>0.
The actual quadratic form satisfies
 Q_(A_T)(u)>=sum_x a_T(x) sum_i||u(x+e_i)-u(x)||^2.
This is pointwise matrix positivity, not a substituted weighted curl form.
Also lambda_min(A_T)^(-P)<=tr(A_T^(-P)), so
 c^P sum_x a_T(x)^(-P)
 <=c^P sum_(unselected)lambda^(-P)+N(c/T)^P
 <=(F-N)/alpha_D+1,
since P>=1 and N/(1+N)^P<=1. For N=0 the last+1 is merely harmless.

3. POINT ISOLATION REFINEMENT AND ITS EXACT COUNT
Let Z=I union union_(i=1..D)(I+e_i), with periodic addition for endpoints.
Then |Z|<=(D+1)|I|<=(D+1)N.
Refine the scalar partition so that every z inZ is a singleton rectangle.
For each point z not already a singleton, its current rectangle R minus{z}
is a disjoint union of at most2D integer rectangles: assign a point to its
first coordinate different fromz, then to the lower or upper side. Empty
pieces are omitted. The singleton plus those pieces replaceR, increasing
the spatial rectangle count by at most2D. Earlier isolated singletons stay
unchanged since partition members are disjoint. This works in a fixed
coordinate chart0,...,n-1 even when an original forward endpoint wraps.
After at most|Z| steps,
 R<=R_0+2D|Z|<=R_0+2D(D+1)N.
Normalized Cauchy on every refined parent rectangle gives
 ||C_0u||^2<=||C_refu||^2, with the same Euclidean h component norm.
For the isolated points, V^(-1)sum_(z inZ)||u(z)||^2<=||C_refu||^2.
No measurement of gradients or nonrectangular set is left in the output.
