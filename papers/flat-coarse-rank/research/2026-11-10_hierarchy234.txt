1. SETUP AND A LOWSPACE FOR ALL ITS VECTORS
Let X=(Z/nZ)^D,V=n^D,U0=im(d1*) for the actual forward curl, and
d0=(D-1)(V-1). Let U=U0^g with counting norm. Put c>0,a=c/(16D),
H=cn^2,v=(1,...,1)/sqrt(g),B=H I_g-(H-a)vv^T. At every site
A_(i,j,alpha),(i',j',beta)=delta_ii' delta_jj' B_alpha,beta.
This is exactly231B's constant positive profile, with normalized energy
V^-1 Q_A and full capped budget F<=D^2(1+alpha_D)V, h=Dg.
Define the isometry T:U0->U by (Tf)_alpha=v_alpha f. The difference bound
sum_i||D_i^+f||^2<=4D||f||^2 gives, for EVERY f in U0,
 Q_A(Tf)=a sum_i||D_i^+f||^2<=c/4 ||f||^2.
No selection of a few modes or spatial components is made in this lowspace.

2. EXACT UNRESTRICTED MINIMUM
If linear C:U->finite real output restores
 V^-1 Q_A(z)+Phi(Cz)>=c V^-1||z||^2 for every z in U,
with Phi(0)=0, then C restricted to T U0 must be injective. Otherwise a
nonzero Tf in its kernel would have energy at most c/4 times its norm and
contradict that inequality. Consequently rank(C|U)>=d0.

Let W=T* so Wz=sum_alpha v_alpha z_alpha, and observe every spatial
component of Wz at every site with factor V^-1/2. W maps U onto U0
because WT=I, hence this mixed observation has actual rank exactly d0.
The orthogonal split z=TWz+z_s remains coexact and commutes with all forward
differences because internal mixing is spatially constant. Its energy splits
into weak a and strong H contributions. Every coexact component has global
mean zero. The actual forward torus Laplacian gap lambda1>=16/n^2 gives
 Q_A(z_s)>=H lambda1||z_s||^2>=c||z_s||^2.
Also ||Cz||^2=V^-1||Wz||^2=V^-1||TWz||^2. Adding penalty c||Cz||^2,
and discarding nonnegative weak energy, proves the required inequality.
Therefore the minimum actual rank among ALL finite linear observation maps
with some allowed restoring penalty is EXACTLY d0, not merely O_D(V).
No nonlinear observation map rank is defined or asserted here.

3. EXACT COMMON-FULL-VECTOR MINIMUM
For any partition P of X into nonempty cells, let C0_P act on a D-vector
field by all normalized spatial-component means
 V^-1/2 |B|^-1/2 sum_{x in B} f_j(x), for B in P,j=1,...,D.
The common full-vector map on U is the literal direct sum
 C_P=direct_sum_{alpha=1}^g C0_P,
all using the SAME partition and row normalization. Hence
 C_P(Tf)=(v_alpha C0_P f)_alpha.
Every v_alpha is nonzero. If C0_P|U0 had a nonzero kernel f, C_P(Tf)
would vanish, contradicting the lowspace coercivity argument in section2.
Thus C0_P|U0 is injective for ANY restoring common map. Its actual rank is
d0, and rank(C_P|U)=g*d0 by the internal direct sum. This is the ENTIRE
dimension of U. No listed-output-count h*#P is substituted for actual rank.
The conclusion does not assert a minimal number of partition cells.

The singleton partition is common and rectangular. Its rows are z_j,alpha(x)
times V^-1/2, so squared output norm is V^-1||z||^2. Since Q_A>=0,
penalty c times that norm restores coercivity on U, and its actual rank is
g*d0. Therefore the minimum common full-vector restricted rank is EXACTLY
g*d0, both for arbitrary-cell partitions and for integer rectangular ones.
The necessity proof even applies when C0 is any same linear channel map,
repeated in every internal channel, but is not asserted for unequal maps.

4. ONE PROFILE, THREE CLASSES, AND LIMITS
Together with the separately proved233 result, the SAME positive profile
and actual coexact domain have minima

retaining all components has the direct-sum restriction just used. Nothing
