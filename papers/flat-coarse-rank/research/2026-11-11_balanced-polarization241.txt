2. Actual partitions and a greedy weighted allocation, not an assumed grouping.
Write w_alpha=v_alpha^2; these positive weights sum to1. For any successive
requested masses lambda_q>0, choose the shortest consecutive unused channel
group I_q whose mass M_q=sum_(alpha in I_q)w_alpha is >=lambda_q.
Each completed group has
       lambda_q <= M_q <lambda_q+Lambda/g,
and its cardinality obeys
       |I_q| <= g M_q/nu < (g lambda_q+Lambda)/nu.             (241.2)
If sum_q(lambda_q+Lambda/g)<1, all groups exist disjointly: before each
selection the unused mass exceeds the sum of remaining requested masses
plus their possible overshoots, in particular exceeds the current request.
The shortest prefix therefore reaches that request. This induction derives
the grouping from the actual v, including all indivisible-weight overshoots.
Every leftover channel will observe the root partition. No fractional channel
is permitted; no row is rescaled by v_alpha or by a free normalization.

6. Actual-rank lower bounds: recompute the weighted trace.
This part applies to every internally channel-separable linear restoring C
on U with ANY finite penalty Phi(Cu), Phi(0)=0; the penalty lower below
then specializes to canonical fixed weights and quadratic coefficients.
Let R_alpha now denote orthogonal row-space projections INSIDE U_0, let
R=direct-sum R_alpha, r=sum rank(R_alpha)=rank(C|U), and T0f=(v_alpha f)_alpha.
T0 is an isometry U_0 -> U. The compression is positive, and
       trace(T0^* R T0)=sum_alpha v_alpha^2 rank(R_alpha)<=Lambda r/g.
Choose a unit f in U_0 with t=||R T0f||^2<=Lambda r/(g d0).
Set u=(I-R)T0f, so Cu=0, ||u||^2=1-t, and u is ACTUALLY coexact.
The forward lattice bound E(w)<=4D||w||^2 and PSD quadratic polarization
give, as in231B but now with the weighted t,
       Q_A(u)<=2Q_A(T0f)+2Q_A(RT0f)<=8Da+8DH t.
Restoration at Cu=0 therefore yields
       c(1-t)<=c/2+8Dcn^2 t,
       r>=g d0/[2Lambda(1+8Dn^2)]
         >=[(D-1)/(4Lambda(1+8D))] gV/n^2.              (241.7)
There is also r>=d0>=(D-1)V/2, since Q_A(T0f)=aE(f)<=c||f||^2/4
makes C T0 injective on U_0. Combining these inequalities gives the matching
Theta_(D,nu,Lambda)(V+gV/n^2) restoring rank together with Section4.
The restriction to canonical maps is a subclass for this lower bound.

For canonical projection R with these fixed weights and quadratic K,
restoration of T0f gives K||RT0f||^2>=3c||f||^2/4.

with squared-singular-value sum at most its actual rank. Summing over a

       K>=3c g d0/(4Lambda r).

       K>=3(D-1)c min(g,n^2)/(16Lambda L_(D,nu,Lambda)).  (241.8)
Together with32cb this proves the simultaneous least-penalty order.
