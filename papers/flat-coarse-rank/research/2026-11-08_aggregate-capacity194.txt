QUESTION TESTED
After193 derived Q_R, a possible rescue is an aggregate energy bound
  sum_j f|_{R_j}^T Q_{R_j} f|_{R_j} <= C(D,M) E_a(f)
for disjoint actual rectangles R_j, each Q computed by eliminating the
entire complement of that individual rectangle. This unit refutes precisely
that bound at fixed M=3, for each D>=4. It does not refute an aggregate bound
with an explicit growing size/scale factor, a different joint elimination,
or the main selectable rectangular partition/counting theorem.

CONVENTIONS
X=(Z/nZ)^D, n even and n>=6, D>=4, d=D-1>=3, p=D/2,
N=n^D, T=(Z/nZ)^d. Write x=(t,y), with t in Z/nZ and y in T.
E_a(v)=sum_{x,i} a(x)|v(x+e_i)-v(x)|^2, counting each positive-start
edge once. All coefficients and fields are real. R_y={(0,y),(1,y)}.
These are pairwise disjoint actual product rectangles, not abstract blocks.
The harmonic boundary form Q_R is defined exactly as in193:
  min_{v|_R=u} E_a(v)=E_int,R(u)+u^T Q_R u.
Positive coefficients and connectedness give the unique minimizer on the
complement;193 proves this by grounding and completing the square.

1. A UNIFORM TRANSVERSE POINT-DIFFERENCE ESTIMATE, DERIVED HERE
On the unit-coefficient torus T define E_T(w)=sum_{y,l}|w(y+e_l)-w(y)|^2.
For every y,z and every real w,
  |w(y)-w(z)|^2 <= C_d E_T(w),  C_d=d*3^(d-1)/2.
To prove it, use normalized Fourier coefficients hat(w)(k)=n^(-d)
sum_y w(y) exp(-2pi i k.y/n), with representatives k_l in
{-n/2,...,n/2-1}. Nonzero eigenvalues are
  lambda(k)=4 sum_l sin^2(pi k_l/n)>=16|k|^2/n^2.
Fourier expansion, Parseval and Cauchy--Schwarz therefore bound the
point-difference constant by
  n^(-d) sum_{k!=0} |exp(2pi i k.y/n)-exp(2pi i k.z/n)|^2/lambda(k)
  <= n^(2-d)/4 sum_{k!=0}|k|^(-2).
For r=max_l|k_l|>=1 the number of representatives in its shell is at most
(2r+1)^d-(2r-1)^d <= 2d*3^(d-1)r^(d-1). Thus the last sum is at most
2d*3^(d-1) sum_{r=1}^{n/2} r^(d-3) <= 2d*3^(d-1)n^(d-2).
This proves the stated C_d, with no imported resistance estimate or hidden
numerical calculation. The d>=3 restriction is essential to this bound;
this proof makes no D=3 claim.

2. TWO WEAK CUT PLANES AND ONE STRONG HOLE
Set m=n/2 and H=n^(2/D), so H^p=n. Let a(t,y)=1 on both planes
t=0 and t=m, except a(0,0)=H. Set a=H at every other vertex.
The weak count is W=2n^d-1, and mu=N^(-1)sum a=(1-W/N)H+W/N.
Since W/N<2/n<=1/3, we have 2H/3<=mu<H, so 1<=a<=2mu.
The normalized critical inverse moment obeys
  M_actual=mu^p [W/N+(1-W/N)H^(-p)]
           < H^p W/N+1 < 3.
It is at least1 by Jensen. Thus a fixed admissibility bound M=3 covers
the whole family. No moment constant is allowed to grow with n.

3. EACH OF MANY TRACE PAIRS HAS LARGE OPTIMAL CONDUCTANCE
Fix y!=0. The only internal edge of R_y is the positive-start edge from
(0,y) to (1,y), of coefficient1. Its internal Neumann gap is exactly2.
Let K_d=3+2C_d. For an arbitrary global v, telescope its endpoint
difference along the following five changes:
 (0,y) -> (n-1,y) -> (n-1,0) -> (0,0) -> (1,0) -> (1,y).
The first, third and fourth changes are actual direction1 edges of
coefficient H, the first traversed backwards. The second and fifth are
point differences within the full transverse layers t=n-1 and t=1.
Those layers have coefficient H at all their positive transverse starts.
Section1 controls their squared differences by C_d times the corresponding
layer energy divided by H. The three single-edge energies and the two
entire transverse-layer energies are pairwise disjoint subsets of E_a(v):
y!=0 distinguishes the two incoming edges, and directions/layers distinguish
the others. Weighted Cauchy--Schwarz, with weights1,C_d,1,1,C_d, gives
  |v(0,y)-v(1,y)|^2 <= K_d E_a(v)/H.
This applies to every global extension. Its minimized two-point energy is
g_y|u(0,y)-u(1,y)|^2 by the symmetric two-by-two Schur form. Consequently
  g_y>=H/K_d,
  u^T Q_{R_y}u=(g_y-1)|u(0,y)-u(1,y)|^2.
Small n need not give a positive lower bound for g_y-1 from this estimate;
Q is nevertheless positive semidefinite, and the divergence argument uses
only the tail with H>=2K_d. No individual constant has been assumed.

4. ONE GLOBAL FIELD, MANY OVERLAPPING HARMONIC CHARGES
Take f(t,y)=1 for t=0,m+1,...,n-1 and f(t,y)=-1 for t=1,...,m.
It has global counting mean zero. Only direction1 edges starting at t=0
or t=m have nonzero differences. There is one such strong edge at (0,0),
with weight H, and 2n^d-1 other such weak edges with weight1. Therefore
  E_a(f)=4(2n^d-1+H).
For each y!=0 the retained endpoint difference is2. Thus for J=n^d-1
disjoint rectangles, section3 implies
  sum_{y!=0} f|_{R_y}^T Q_{R_y} f|_{R_y}
       >=4(n^d-1)(H/K_d-1).
Since H<=n^d, n^d-1>=n^d/2 and eventually H>=2K_d, their ratio satisfies
  [sum boundary costs]/E_a(f) >= H/(12K_d) -> infinity.
No C(D,3) can make the proposed aggregate energy inequality true.
The fields in different trace problems are optimized separately; disjoint
retained vertices do not make those global extension energies disjoint.
The lower-bound certificates use the same high-conductance hole and
transverse layers across different trace problems. This does not assert
that each actual minimizer must use that hole rather than the weak cuts.
It exposes the failed additivity assumption under test, not a failure of
the exact Schur identity from193.

5. LIMITS, COUNTING SCALE AND NEXT MECHANISM
This is an all-size scalar counterexample for D>=4; no D=3 analogue is
established here. Because 2H/3<=mu<H and H>=1, the original scale obeys
  2^(-p)n^d <= N/(1+mu)^p <= (3/2)^p n^d.
Thus J is of the same order as the permitted target count, not larger
than it by an unbounded factor. The example therefore supplies NO
counterexample to the main rectangle-count estimate. It only refutes
a size-independent aggregate harmonic-energy bound of the precise form
in the opening question. No aggregate estimate with a growing factor,
joint Schur reduction, partition-adapted boundary rule or alternate
choice of rectangles is excluded. There is no coexact/rank/physical
continuum conclusion, no positive hypothesis removal and no new PDF.

The single active main question remains the fully variable fixed-moment
actual-rectangle estimate. Abandon the independent-cell harmonic-cost
additivity route now: the explicit family is its falsifier. Three boundary
units192-194 have now isolated, rather than solved, the obstruction:
cellwise extension fails, near-one moments do not repair it, and disjoint
traces do not justify an energy sum. Change mechanism to a joint partition
or a direct local internal-energy construction whose quantitative count
is derived without this additivity premise. First compare a joint retained
set with separate-cell reductions on the present family. Do not package
the missing count as an assumed structure. After three units without
substantial advance on the new mechanism, document the blockage/change.
