2026-11-08. Same research dossier as201B/203/203B/204; no new paper.

1. Inventory, scope and theorem
The single main question remains the coefficient-only critical rectangle
partition problem. The local scalar shell mechanism is tested here.
204 proved direct unweighted energy control uniform in thickness;203B's
actual thin-shell local-error gluing obstruction remains valid. The
unweighted critical Sobolev estimate on arbitrary rectangles is ALREADY
proved in revision202/sobolev.tex and is NOT a new result of this note.
We reuse that proved estimate to derive a shell result and never assume
a pointwise coefficient bound comparable to the mean.

Let D>=3, p=D/2, q=2D/(D+2), J=2D. Let R>=1 and0<=r<R be integers,
t=R-r, L=2R+1, and A=([-R,R] intersect Z)^D minus([-r,r] intersect Z)^D.
Set N=|A|>0. Every field f:A->R and every positive coefficient a:A->(0,infty)
satisfy
 V_A(f) <= C_D L^2 [(1/N)sum_(x in A)a(x)^(-p)]^(1/p) E_a,A(f), (1.1)
where V_A is squared counting distance to its arithmetic mean, and
 E_a,A=sum_(x,x+e_j in A) a(x)|f(x+e_j)-f(x)|^2.
Only internal positive-axis edges are retained, each once, with their
ORIGINAL starting-site coefficient. No torus wrapping or outside energy.
C_D is finite and depends only on D; an explicit derived value is below.
No condition on thickness, contrast or pointwise mean-scale minimum appears.
All selections in the proof are fixed independently of f and a.

2. Exact existing box dependency and constants
Dependency: papers/flat-coarse-rank/integrated-revision/revision202/sobolev.tex
at commit8262c72573387d44d82359636f65ec635449a131, full section entitled
'A finite proof of the critical rectangle estimate'. Its displayed
rectangle-unweighted-derived theorem states, for every integer product box
B with v vertices and all coordinate lengths<=L,
 V_B(f)<= A_D L^2 v^(-2/D) (sum_(e internal B)|df(e)|^q)^(2/q). (2.1)
The dependency defines
 alpha=2(D-1)/D, c_D=D(1-2^(-1/D)), q'=2D/(D-2),
 K_D=2 alpha D^(1/q')/c_D,
 A_D=K_D^2 2^D 3^(2D/q+2).
It derives finite projection by entropy, relative edge isoperimetry,
layer-set/median Sobolev and power Holder, and endpoint-repeating reflection
with actual preimage multiplicities for every box aspect ratio. Thus(2.1)
is a proved existing lemma with a pinned proof, NOT a new input field or an
unverified shell extension theorem. Singleton coordinate sides are covered.
Its all-singleton case has zero variance. No weighted reflection is used.
Our shell slabs below have at least one long side L>=3, so no degenerate
all-singleton exception arises. Reproducing this box proof would duplicate
an already closed manuscript result and is deliberately avoided.

3. Actual shell cover and fixed tree
Use slabs S_(j,+)={x in[-R,R]^D:x_j in[r+1,R]},
S_(j,-)={x in[-R,R]^D:x_j in[-R,-r-1]}, on integer points.
They cover A; each has v=t L^(D-1)>0 vertices and is a product box.
N<=Jv because it is a cover. Slabs of distinct coordinates overlap in
t^2 L^(D-2)>0 points. Root at(1,+), attach all J-2 slabs of coordinates
other than1, and attach(1,-) to(2,+). This is a spanning tree of depth<=2.
Each tree edge joins distinct restricted coordinates. The construction is
valid at r=0,R=1 and t=1; translations preserve it, off-center holes and
wrapping identifications are outside the theorem.

4. Mean transport in the q-energy norm, with no overlap loss
For a field g on an L-site interval I and nonempty T subset I, Jensen and
Holder along the interval path yield
 |mean_I(g)-mean_T(g)|^q
 <= (1/(L|T|))sum_(i in I,k in T)|g(i)-g(k)|^q
 <= L^(q-1) sum_(interval edges)|dg|^q.                (4.1)
Every path uses at most L-1 edges; replacing its sum by all interval edges
only enlarges it. Averaging cancels L|T|, without a factor1/|T| left over.
For a product box B=I times F with w=|F| fibers, Jensen across the SAME
fibers gives
 |mean_B(f)-mean_(T times F)(f)|^q
 <= L^(q-1)/w sum_(directional edges of B)|df|^q.      (4.2)

Write m_i for the mean on slab S_i, and F_i=sum_(edges in S_i)|df|^q.
For neighboring slabs in the fixed tree, their intersection is obtained
from each by restricting ONE long coordinate of L sites to t sites, with
the other coordinate intervals unchanged. Hence w=v/L in(4.2). Their
actual intersection mean is identical from either side, proving
 |m_i-m_j| <= L v^(-1/q)(F_i^(1/q)+F_j^(1/q)).         (4.3)
No inverse-overlap volume or local-error gluing estimate is invoked.

5. Unweighted shell Sobolev derived before weighting
Put H=sum_i F_i and F_A=sum_(internal edges of A)|df|^q. Each F_i<=H.
By(4.3) a tree edge mean difference is at most2 L v^(-1/q) H^(1/q).
Every root path has at most2 edges, so
 |m_i-m_root|<=4 L v^(-1/q) H^(1/q).                  (5.1)
Use the root mean as a candidate constant on the shell, the cover, and
the exact decomposition about each local mean:
 V_A(f)<=sum_i[V_Si(f)+v|m_i-m_root|^2]
 <= L^2 v^(-2/D)[A_D sum_i F_i^(2/q)+16J H^(2/q)]
 <= (A_D+16J)L^2 v^(-2/D) H^(2/q).                  (5.2)
Here1-2/q=-2/D, and sum_i F_i^(2/q)<=H^(2/q) since2/q>=1.
Every slab-internal edge belongs to A; every A-edge belongs to at most J
slabs. Thus H<=J F_A. Also N<=Jv gives v^(-2/D)<=J^(2/D) N^(-2/D).
Consequently
 V_A(f)<= B_D L^2 N^(-2/D) F_A^(2/q),
 B_D=(A_D+16J) J^(2/q+2/D).                          (5.3)
This is uniform in thickness, derived from actual geometry and the proved
box lemma rather than postulating an annular Sobolev constant.

6. Original site weights: critical Holder and no minimum hypothesis
Orient every shell-internal edge e from its lower coordinate endpoint x
to x+e_j and set b_e=a(x)>0. Holder with exponents2/q and2/(2-q) gives
 F_A^(2/q)
  <= [sum_e b_e|df(e)|^2] [sum_e b_e^(-q/(2-q))]^((2-q)/q)
  = E_a,A(f) [sum_e b_e^(-p)]^(1/p),                (6.1)
because q/(2-q)=p=D/2. Each site starts at most D retained internal edges,
so sum_e b_e^(-p)<=D sum_A a^(-p). Combining with(5.3) proves(1.1) with
 C_D=D^(2/D) B_D.
Weights have not been reflected, symmetrized, averaged between endpoints,
or supplied outside A. This proves the full inverse-moment weighted bound
without importing a pointwise alpha lower bound or assuming a local gap.

If, for a positive parameter mu, the ACTUAL LOCAL moment obeys
 (1/N)sum_A (mu/a)^p<=M_A,
then(1.1) implies V_A<=C_D L^2 M_A^(1/p) E_a,A/mu.
The parameter mu need not be the shell mean. This is a conditional
corollary with an explicit local moment; a moment on a much larger ambient
domain does not automatically give the same local M_A on every shell.
For uniform a=H the coefficient factor is exactly1/H, as required.

7. What is and is not closed
The inverse-moment weighted comparison proposed as unreviewed in204 is
proved here for D>=3 and the stated concentric integer shells. The
pointwise mean-scale lower-bound premise and a supplied local shell gap
are not required for this estimate. No main rectangle theorem is proved:
choosing/counting a family of shells or disjoint integer rectangles using
only the GLOBAL critical moment remains a separate unproved link. This
note does not silently replace the global moment by local bounds.
The slabs overlap; they are not themselves a partition. The203B actual
field refutes a different local-error comparison and remains valid.
No off-center, wrapping torus, physical, coexact/color or continuum claim.
