corresponding diagonal entry B_alpha,alpha(x), evaluated at the source x.

2. Actual selected strong-edge cycles, including n=2 labels.
For channel1, strong outgoing coefficient H occurs at the odd-parity sites;
for channel2 it occurs at the even-parity sites. Fix one of these channels,
write A for its strong-source parity, and select ONLY the strong outgoing
edges in spatial directions e1 and eD. Every vertex has degree2 in this
selected undirected labelled graph: an A vertex has its two outgoing edges,
and a vertex of opposite parity has its two incoming edges. These directions
are distinct since D>=3. With n=2, distinct selected directions still give
distinct edges; there is no silent loss of labelled forward multiplicity.

For an A-parity base w0 define, for t in Z/nZ,
 w_t=w0+t(eD-e1), b_t=w_t+eD.
There are n distinct w_t (the translation has order n), n distinct b_t,
and parity separates the two sets. Selected edges are w_t--b_t (eD) and
 b_t--w_(t+1) (the +e1 edge from w_(t+1) to b_t).
Thus every component is exactly a2n-cycle with these alternating vertices.
Every vertex belongs to one such component; no selected edge leaves it.
The same construction applies to the opposite source parity for channel2.

Let Pi={x:x_D=0}. On each cycle exactly one w_t and one b_s belong to Pi.
Their indices satisfy s=t-1, since x_D(b_s)=x_D(w_s)+1. They are adjacent
through the selected +e1 edge w_t--b_(t-1). Hence Pi intersects EVERY cycle
in exactly two adjacent vertices. This uses a real coordinate plane, not
a diagonal slab or a rotated/mixed observation basis.

3. Strict Dirichlet margin on fields vanishing at Pi.
Suppose a scalar field v vanishes on Pi. On each selected2n-cycle its two
adjacent zero vertices leave 2n-2 free vertices in a path with zero endpoints.
For such a path the exact smallest Dirichlet eigenvalue is
 4sin^2(pi/[2(2n-1)]) >=4/(2n-1)^2,
by the finite sine eigenbasis and sin(t)>=2t/pi on[0,pi/2]. The edge joining
the two zero vertices contributes zero. The estimate applies also n=2.
Summing components and then both channels and all D vector components gives
 Q_A(z)>= [4H/(2n-1)^2]||z||^2=(c+delta_n)||z||^2,
 delta_n=c[4n^2/(2n-1)^2-1]
        =c(4n-1)/(2n-1)^2 >0,                       (245.1)
for ALL fields vanishing pointwise on Pi. Other strong edges and every weak
edge have nonnegative energy and were merely discarded, not replaced.
No Fourier approximation, continuum gap or unproved regional hypothesis is
used. The exact elementary Dirichlet formula supplies the strict margin.
