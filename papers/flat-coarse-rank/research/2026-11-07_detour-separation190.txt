1. Exact set-up and bounded research test
X=(Z/nZ)^D, D>=2, n>=3. Count each positive-start periodic edge(x,i) once. E0(u)=sum_(x,i)|u(x+e_i)-u(x)|^2. Ew and Es restrict starts to W and its complement. A coefficient a satisfies a>=H>0 outsideW and a>=0 inW; Ea is the actual start-weighted energy.
Periodic maximum-coordinate distance is max_j min(|x_j-y_j|,n-|x_j-y_j|). Distance2-separatedW means distinct weakvertices have distance>=2. This is stronger than absence of nearest-neighbour weakpairs.
Targeted subtest of the one existing fullyvariable critical-rectangle question: weaken the established detour radius3 to2 with its constant retained inD>=3, and test whether nearest-neighbour independence alone suffices. Mechanism: inverse edge-use enumeration and a coefficient-derived coexact witness. Falsifiers: allowed repeated strongedge use inD>=3, a weak bypass start, or a failed coexact/zero-energy identity. 2. Positive theorem: distance2 suffices
For D>=3,n>=3, distance2-separatedW implies Ew<=3Es, E0<=4Es and Ea>=HE0/4. For D=2 this construction gives Ew<=6Es, E0<=7Es and Ea>=HE0/7; no sharpness of6or7 claimed. Valid for real/complex and finite Hilbert-valued fields, componentwise for the actual one-cochain gradient form and its coexact restriction.
Proof. Let sigma cyclically permute allD directions. Replace a weak-start edge x->x+e_i by
 x -> x-e_sigma(i) -> x+e_i-e_sigma(i) -> x+e_i.
Its positive starts are x-e_sigma(i) in directions sigma(i) andi, and x+e_i-e_sigma(i) in direction sigma(i). All are at nonzero periodic distance1 from weakx and so are strong. The first edge is traversed backwards; its coefficient is at x-e_sigma(i). Individual path edges are distinct for n>=3.
For a fixed positive edge(y,k), possible weaksource/path types are
 A=y+e_k, with i=sigma^-1(k);
 B=y+e_sigma(k), with i=k;
 C=y-e_sigma^-1(k)+e_k, with i=sigma^-1(k).
Each type has at mostone actual path. A-B and A-C are nonzero differences with entries in{-1,0,1}. InD>=3 the three directions k,sigma(k),sigma^-1(k) are distinct, so B-C=e_sigma(k)+e_sigma^-1(k)-e_k also has this property. All three pairwise periodic distances are1, including n=3wraps. Thus at mostone source is weak and every strongedge is used at mostonce. Three-step Cauchy–Schwarz and summation give Ew<=3Es.
InD=2, A is incompatible with B andC, while B-C=2e_sigma(k)-e_k can have periodic distance2. At mostB andC coexist, so edge use is at mosttwo and Ew<=6Es. Add Es and use Ea>=HEs. This proves a coefficient-derived comparison, not a supplied comparison structure.
The unit-spacing torus Fouriergap gives E0>=4sin^2(pi/n)||u-mean(u)||^2>=16/n^2||u-mean(u)||^2. InD>=3, if4H/n^2>=c, one actual normalized global mean gives c||u||^2<=Ea(u)+c|X||mean(u)||^2. InD=2 the sufficient condition is16H/(7n^2)>=c. These are finite flat statements, not an interacting/continuum mass gap.

3. Negative theorem: nearest-neighbour independence fails, including on actual coexact forms
Take evenn=2m>=4,D>=2. Let s(x)=sum_jx_j modn in{0,...,n-1}, W={s even}. Every nearest-neighbour step flipsparity, so W has no nearest-neighbour pair. Set a=epsilon>0 onW and a=H>0 elsewhere. Only the limiting strong-graph statement allows epsilon=0.
For real z=(z_0,...,z_(m-1)) with sum_rz_r=0, define j(s)=ceil(s/2) modm and f_z(x)=z_(j(s(x))). Thus evenlayer2r and oddlayer2r-1 have valuez_r, including wrap0,n-1. Each residue layer has n^(D-1) vertices. Strong positive starts advance2r-1 to2r, giving Es(f_z)=0. Weak positive starts advance2r to2r+1, giving
 Ew(f_z)=D n^(D-1) sum_r|z_(r+1)-z_r|^2,

 Ea(f_z)/||f_z||^2=(D epsilon/2) sum_r|z_(r+1)-z_r|^2 / sum_r|z_r|^2 <=2D epsilon.

4. Critical moment, attribution and limits
