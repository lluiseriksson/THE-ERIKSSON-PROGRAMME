QUESTION, HYPOTHESES AND DISPOSITION
The family194/197 refutes an aggregate separate-elimination comparison,
and197 rules out its uniformly bounded-rank repair. Does this actual family
itself refute the selectable rectangular partition target? No. This note
constructs an admissible partition for every c>0, of target-order cardinality,
with the stronger global quadratic penalty coefficient K=c. It proves a
positive result for this prescribed structured family only. It does not solve
the unrestricted coefficient question. Group with192-194/197, no new paper.

1. DEFINITIONS AND EXACT MOMENT
Fix D>=4 and even integer s>=2. Set d=D-1, n=s^(2D), H=s^4,
p=D/2, m=n/2, N=n^D, X=(Z/nZ)^D. Use representatives0,...,n-1
in every coordinate; t is the first coordinate, y the remaining d.
Let a=1 on t=0 or t=m except a(0,0)=H; let a=H elsewhere.
E_a(f)=sum_(x in X)sum_(i=1)^D a(x)|f(x+e_i)-f(x)|^2.
Every positive-start edge occurs once. W=2n^d-1 sites have a=1;
mu=(1-W/N)H+W/N is the actual arithmetic mean. Since H^p=n and
W/N<2/n<=1/3, we have 2H/3<=mu<H, 1<=a<=2mu, and
M_actual=mu^p[W/N+(1-W/N)H^(-p)]<n W/N+1<3.
Convexity of z->z^(-p) gives M_actual>=1. Thus this is the same
fixed critical inverse-moment family, with M=3 as an upper bound.
The exceptional strong hole is retained, not deleted or averaged away.

2. ELEMENTARY INTERNAL RECTANGLE POINCARE BOUND
Let R be a product of D nonempty integer intervals, of lengths l_i<=L,
with only its nonwrapping internal nearest-neighbor edges. For real f put
bar f_R=|R|^(-1)sum_R f, and E_R^0=sum_internal_edges |D_i f|^2.
Then
  sum_R |f-bar f_R|^2 <= (L^2/4) E_R^0.                 (2.1)
For a path of l>=2 sites the exact variance identity is
  sum_j |w_j-bar w|^2 = (1/l)sum_(i<j)|w_i-w_j|^2.
By telescoping and Cauchy-Schwarz,
  |w_i-w_j|^2 <= (j-i)sum_(k=i)^(j-1)|w_(k+1)-w_k|^2.
Using j-i<=l and counting the k(l-k) pairs crossing edge k,
with k=1,...,l-1, proves the bound l^2/4 times the path energy.
For l=1 the variance is zero.

For completeness tensorization does not introduce a factor D. Let A_i
average in coordinate i, as an orthogonal projection on counting L2(R).
These projections commute. Decompose f into the mutually orthogonal
components [product_(i in S)(I-A_i) product_(i notin S) A_i] f.
The constant component has S empty. Each other component contributes once
to ||f-product_i A_i f||^2 and |S|>=1 times to
sum_i ||(I-A_i)f||^2. Thus variance <=sum_i ||(I-A_i)f||^2.
Apply the path bound on every coordinate fiber and sum. This proves(2.1),
including intervals of length1. The lemma concerns internal energy only;
no external harmonic extension or boundary-cost sum occurs.

3. AN ACTUAL INTEGER RECTANGULAR PARTITION
Fix c>0, b=max(1,sqrt(c)), and integer
  L=max(1,floor(sqrt(H)/b)).
We have 1<=L<=sqrt(H)<=n and L>=sqrt(H)/(2b). The last inequality
holds when sqrt(H)/b<1 since L=1, and otherwise follows from
floor x>=x/2 for x>=1.

Partition each of the two planes t=0,m into all n^d singleton vertices.
These are legal product rectangles; include (0,0) as a singleton as well.
The complement consists of the two coordinate slabs
  [1,m-1] x [0,n-1]^d, [m+1,n-1] x [0,n-1]^d.
Both are nonempty. Split each coordinate interval into consecutive chunks
of L sites, with one final shorter nonempty chunk when needed. Take products
of these chunks within each slab. All lengths are positive and <=L; the
result is a disjoint partition of every vertex of X, with no wrapped box.
Every start of every internal edge in a nonsingleton slab box has a=H.
Neither torus wrap edges nor edges crossing a box boundary are included in
its internal energy. Dropping them is legitimate since a>0.

If L=1 all boxes are singletons and their variances vanish. If L>=2,
then L<=sqrt(H)/b. By(2.1), on each slab box R,
  E_R^a=H E_R^0 >= (4H/L^2)sum_R|f-bar f_R|^2
                    >= c sum_R|f-bar f_R|^2.
Singleton boxes satisfy the same inequality with both variances zero.
The retained internal edges of distinct boxes are disjoint, so globally
  E_a(f) >= c sum_R sum_(x in R)|f(x)-bar f_R|^2.       (3.1)
This uses actual internal energies, not separate minimized exterior costs.

4. EXACT NORMALIZED MEANS AND A FINITE PENALTY
Put ||f||_N^2=N^(-1)sum_X |f|^2, and define one scalar coordinate per box
  (P f)_R=sqrt(|R|/N) bar f_R
          =sum_R f /sqrt(N |R|).
Thus P consists of normalized arithmetic means on the constructed boxes.
Their supports are disjoint and nonempty, so P has full row rank J=#boxes.
Orthogonal decomposition into cell means and cell-mean-zero fluctuations
is exact:
  ||f||_N^2=N^(-1)sum_R sum_R|f-bar f_R|^2+||P f||_2^2.
Combining with(3.1) gives, for EVERY real f, not only near zero,
  N^(-1)E_a(f)+c||P f||_2^2 >= c||f||_N^2.            (4.1)
Hence the finite penalty is exactly c times squared normalized-mean norm.
If an unnormalized row convention Q_R=sum_R f/sqrt(|R|) is used, the same
statement is E_a(f)+c||Q f||_2^2>=c sum_X|f|^2. No conventions are mixed.
In particular Pf=0 implies the actual spectral lower bound on that kernel.
This is a finite scalar graph inequality, not a physical mass gap.

5. INTEGER COUNT, INCLUDING ALL SMALL-s AND LARGE-c CASES
The exact number of boxes in this partition is
  J=2 n^d+2 ceil((m-1)/L) ceil(n/L)^d.
As L<=n, each ceiling here is at most 2n/L. Consequently
  J <=2 n^d+2^(D+1)n^D/L^D
     <=[2+2^(2D+1)b^D] n^D/H^(D/2),                  (5.1)
where H^(D/2)=n exactly. This estimate remains valid for L=1, in which
case the actual exact count is N. There is no large-s threshold depending
on c. With 1+mu<=2H, we obtain the original target scale
  J <= C(D,c) max{1,(n/sqrt(1+mu))^D},                (5.2)
  C(D,c)=2^(D/2)[2+2^(2D+1)max(1,c^(D/2))].
All constants are explicit and independent of s. The max is harmless; the
bound already holds without it. This note proves the scalar normalized-means
version. It does not silently identify scalar rank with a color-valued rank.

6. WHAT THIS ELIMINATES AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
This exact family, including the strong hole, cannot be a counterexample to
the scalar selectable-rectangle inequality at the stated count order:
(4.1)-(5.2) give a witness for every c>0. It is nevertheless a counterexample
to the separate harmonic-cost comparison and its bounded-rank repair197.
These claims coexist: choosing a different admissible partition is allowed,
and the new partition resolves the weak planes by singleton means.

No exact transverse invariance of a is needed in this proof: the strong hole
breaks it. That fact does not remove invariance from the previously proved
general invariant-coordinate theorem. This proof uses the much more special
geometry that all weak sites lie on two prescribed coordinate planes and the
complement is uniformly strong. Critical moment alone has not been shown to
supply that geometry, or to permit isolation of arbitrary weak sites at this
cost. The original question for all admissible coefficients remains open.
No D=3 claim, sharp count, universal optimality, coexact/interacting extension,
continuum theorem, new Lean result or physical hypothesis removal is claimed.
