\begin{abstract}
This appendix assembles one actual finite-flat mechanism: a repair supported
at a single lattice site has arbitrarily prescribed nonnegative directional
mean, yet vanishes on the kernel of a fixed-dimensional origin-difference
map. A constructed divergence-free low-energy band then forces observation
rank of volume order. We state the explicit integer-tail inequality and its
uniform bounded-rank consequence, identify their exact Lean declarations,
and preserve the failed and successful reproduction records. This is an
appendix for the existing coarse-information dossier, not a separate paper
or deposit. The mechanism and its quantifier closure are classical; no
independent novelty, interacting gauge-theory gap or continuum limit is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Objects and scope}
Let $D=d+2\geq2$, $n\geq1$, and $N_c\geq2$. Work on the periodic unit-spacing
lattice $X_n=(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^D$ with the actual real physical
one-cochain space used by the formalization. Its coefficient space $G$ has
real dimension $g=N_c^2-1$. Write $A_j(x)\in G$ for a field component and
$\Delta_i A_j(x)=A_j(x+e_i)-A_j(x)$ for the forward difference. All norms
and energies below use the repository's actual finite-flat normalization.
Write $\E_0(A)$ for its flat gauge Hodge form and $\E_w(A)$ for its
componentwise weighted repair form. The gauge condition is the actual
flat divergence constraint associated with the stated adjoint model $\rho$.

For $x_0\in X_n$ and $b\in\R$, define
\[
 w_{i,x,j}=b\,\mathbf1_{\{x=x_0\}},\qquad
 (\J_{x_0}A)_{ij}=\Delta_i A_j(x_0).
\]
The parameters $i,j$ both run over $D$ directions. Thus the codomain of
$\J_{x_0}$ has dimension $D^2g$, independently of $n$ and $b$.
This is the earlier site-gradient map in a different finite-product
packaging, not an independently introduced observation mechanism.

\begin{lemma}[Actual energy and exact mean]
For the actual concentrated weight,
\[
 \E_w(A)=b\sum_{i,j}\|\Delta_i A_j(x_0)\|^2.
\]
In particular $\E_w=0$ on $\ker\J_{x_0}$, for every real $b$.
If $m\geq1$ and $b=(m-1)n^D$, then $w\geq0$ and, for every $i,j$,
\[
 \sum_{x\in X_n}\max(1+w_{i,x,j},0)=n^D m.
\]
\end{lemma}
The last identity is an equality of actual directional sums, not a budget
certificate supplied as an extra hypothesis. It follows from the support
definition and the already derived repair identity. In particular $m=1$
gives the zero repair.

\section{Original local nonlinear data}
An observation is a map $C$ from the actual physical cochains to a real
finite-dimensional normed space $F$. Assume $C(0)=0$ and differentiability
at zero with derivative $L=DC(0)$. Let $\Phi:F\to\R$ obey
\[
 \Phi(y)\leq K\|y\|^2\quad(\|y\|<\delta),\qquad \delta>0.
\]
No differentiability of $\Phi$ is assumed. The local coercivity assertion to
be tested is, for some $c>0$ and $r>0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:local}
 c\|A\|^2\leq\E_0(A)+\E_w(A)+\Phi(C(A))
 \quad\text{if $\operatorname{div}_{\rho}A=0$ and $\|A\|<r$}.
\end{equation}
These are the original local nonlinear assumptions. No rank certificate,
band-dimension certificate, supplied injection or compressed-energy
certificate replaces them. In a family indexed by $n$, the observation,
$K$, $\delta$ and $r$ may vary with $n$.

\section{One mechanism, from a finite band to the integer tail}
The actual constructed positive coexact physical band of cutoff $R$ has
dimension
\[
 H_R=2R(2R+1)^{D-1},
\]
lies in the actual divergence kernel, and satisfies
\[
 \E_0(A)\leq\beta_{n,R}\|A\|^2,\qquad
 \beta_{n,R}=\frac{4\pi^2 D R^2}{n^2}.
\]
Its existence, dimension, divergence vanishing and energy bound are earlier
derived facts of the actual formal construction, not hypotheses of the
final rank theorem.

\begin{theorem}[Actual finite-band obstruction]\label{thm:band}
Suppose $2R<n$ and $\beta_{n,R}<c$. If \eqref{eq:local} holds with the
concentrated repair, then
\[
 H_R\leq\dim_{\R}F+D^2(N_c^2-1).
\]
The amplitude $b$ can be any real number.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Restrict $(L,\J_{x_0})$ to the actual coexact band. An element of its kernel
has zero repair energy and is invisible to the derivative of $C$. Along
$tA$, differentiability gives $C(tA)=o(t)$; the upper-quadratic penalty
therefore contributes at most $o(t^2)$. For sufficiently small $t$, the
original neighbourhood conditions apply. Dividing the local inequality by
$t^2$ gives $c\|A\|^2\leq\E_0(A)\leq\beta_{n,R}\|A\|^2$ in the limit.
The strict gap forces $A=0$. The restricted combined map is injective, so
rank-nullity and the actual codomain dimension yield the assertion.
\end{proof}

Choose $k\geq3$ so that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:scale}
 \frac{4\pi^2D}{k^2}\leq\frac c2.
\end{equation}
Such an integer exists for every $c>0$. For every integer $n\geq k$, put
$R=\lfloor n/k\rfloor$. The quotient estimates derive
\[
 0<R,\quad 2R<n,\quad
 \beta_{n,R}\leq\frac{4\pi^2D}{k^2}<c,\quad
 n^D\leq k^D H_R.
\]
The last comparison is a natural-number inequality; it is not restricted
to a divisibility subsequence.

\begin{theorem}[Full integer-tail rank and actual nonnegative profile]
Under the original local data, for $n\geq k$ and \eqref{eq:scale},
\begin{equation}\label{eq:volume}
 n^D\leq k^D\bigl(\dim_{\R}F+D^2(N_c^2-1)\bigr).
\end{equation}
For every $m\geq1$, the actual choice $b=(m-1)n^D$ is nonnegative and has
the exact directional positive-total mean $m$ stated in Lemma 1; the same
rank bound applies if its original local coercivity assertion holds.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Combine the derived quotient-volume comparison with Theorem~\ref{thm:band}.
The profile statements are the already proved actual energy/mean adapters.
\end{proof}
The finite additive cost $D^2(N_c^2-1)$ must be retained. Neither this
constant nor the chosen cutoff is asserted optimal.

\section{Uniform bounded-rank consequence}
\begin{theorem}[Explicit uniform tail]
Fix integers $d\geq0$ and $N_c\geq2$, a positive real $c$, and
a natural rank budget $q$. Choose $k$ by \eqref{eq:scale}, and set
\[
 N=\max\{k,\ k^D(q+D^2(N_c^2-1))+1\}.
\]
For every $n\geq N$, every $m\geq1$, every $x_0\in X_n$ and every
observation space of dimension at most $q$, the original local assertion
\eqref{eq:local} is false for the actual profile
$w_{i,x,j}=(m-1)n^D\mathbf1_{\{x=x_0\}}$ and the stated local data.
The threshold is independent of $m$, $C$, $K$, $\delta$ and $r$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If the assertion held, \eqref{eq:volume} and $\dim F\leq q$ would imply
$n^D\leq k^D(q+D^2(N_c^2-1))<n$. But $n\geq k\geq3$ and $D\geq2$
give $n\leq n^D$, a contradiction. The formal growth helper uses ordered
power monotonicity.
\end{proof}
The quantifiers permit local radii and penalty constants to change at
every $n$. A family with growing observation rank, or a coercivity constant
tending to zero, is not excluded. The underlying arbitrary-amplitude
obstruction is stronger than the intended nonnegative specialization;
the premise $m\geq1$ identifies that class but is not used in the rank
contradiction itself.

\section{Relation to the mean-budget question}
The relevant distinction is between an infimum over profiles and a statement
uniform over every profile with the same mean. The example above rules out
the latter form of bounded-rank saving. It does not refute the earlier
infimum-over-profiles scaling argument: a spatially constant repair and a
concentrated repair have different distributions despite equal means.
The block-mean upper construction and its tensorized variance estimate
remain analytic in the current dossier; they are not promoted here to Lean
endpoints. This appendix consolidates the actual lower-obstruction chain.

Fourier localization, finite-rank perturbations and rank-nullity are
classical. The uniform theorem is an elementary logical consequence of
the preceding actual volume inequality. The primary-source dependency
comparison establishes that it is not an independent new mechanism.
No exhaustive external priority search or new general spectral principle
is claimed. Relevance is limited to a precise, reproducible obstruction to
the proposed mean-only repair route in this finite-flat model.
