Q251 ROUTE NOTE: AN UNCUT-COORDINATE CELL OBSTRUCTION
11November2026. Bounded corollary/falsifier, not a new paper or new score.
The first exact250B PDF/4.50assessment remains unchanged and does not cover this note.

Question retained: can permitted original-channel canonical rectangles achieve
O(n) full count on the actual coexact D3 orthogonal checkerboard, or is its
current O(n(1+ln n)) upper logarithm necessary? This note settles neither option.
It rules out an explicit cheap class of proposed partitions without replacing
the actual coexact domain or assuming averaging preserves it.

Setup. D>=3, even n>=4, c>0, X=(Z/nZ)^D,V=n^D,g=2. All energies/norms below
use counting measure. Write t(x)=sum_i x_i mod n and p(x)=t(x) mod2.
P(x)=e1e1^T at even sources and e2e2^T at odd sources;
B(x)=H I2-(H-a)P(x), a=c/(16D), H=cn^2.
QB(z)=sum_(x,i,k) (partial_i z_k(x))^T B(x) partial_i z_k(x), with actual
forward starts and all original labels. Actual domain is U0^2, U0=im(d1*) for
(d1v)_ij=partial_i v_j-partial_j v_i, i<j. Canonical rows for channelalpha
are V^-1/2 |S|^-1/2 sum_(x in S) z_(k,alpha)(x), one nonempty original-chart
integer rectangle partition per channel, shared across spatial components k.

Statement. If, for either original internal channel r, EVERY rectangle in
that channel's partition has at least one full cyclic coordinate interval
{0,...,n-1}, then this observation map cannot restore targetc on actual U0^2
with any finite penaltyPhi(Cz) satisfyingPhi(0)=0. Different rectangles may
have different full directions. No condition on the other channel's partition
is needed, even if it observes every individual site. A restoring map therefore
needs, in EACH original channel, at least one cell proper in every coordinate.
This last necessary condition supplies no asymptotic count or logarithmic lower.

Explicit real witness. Let m=n/2>=2 and let p_r be the strong-source parity:
p_1=1 and p_2=0. For a residue t in{0,...,n-1}, set
 F_r(t)=cos(2pi floor(((t-p_r) mod n)/2)/m).
This is constant on each strong pair {p_r+2j,p_r+2j+1} modulo n, including
the odd-source wrap pair when r=1. Its sum over t is
2 sum_(j=0)^(m-1) cos(2pi j/m)=0; it is nonzero since F_r(p_r)=1.
Let spatial v=(1,-1,0,...,0), and set z_(k,r)(x)=v_k F_r(t(x)), with the other
internal channel identically zero. In particular z is real and nonzero.

Actual coexact membership, derived rather than supplied. Its mean vanishes
because every residue layer has n^(D-1) sites and sum_t F_r(t)=0. Its Fourier
support is the diagonal frequencies ell=(j,...,j); there is no zero frequency.
At such a nonzero frequency let xi_i=exp(2pi i j/n)-1 (the same for every i),
write u for the spatial Fourier coefficient of z in channelr, and put
 S=sum_i |xi_i|^2=D|exp(2pi i j/n)-1|^2>0,
 psi_ik=(xi_i u_k-xi_k u_i)/S for i<k.
Since u is a scalar multiple of v, sum_i conjugate(xi_i)u_i=0. With the stated
curl convention the adjoint coefficient in componentk is
 sum_(i<k) conjugate(xi_i)psi_ik - sum_(i>k) conjugate(xi_i)psi_ki
 = u_k - xi_k(sum_i conjugate(xi_i)u_i)/S = u_k.
All other frequencies and the other internal channel of psi are zero.
At conjugate frequencies xi and u conjugate; at a real Nyquist frequency they
are real. The constructed two-form is consequently real, and d1*psi=z.
Thus z belongs to the actual U0^2; no abstract divergence-free replacement,
external Hodge certificate or assumed coexact invariance of block means is used.

Actual low energy. Channelr has coefficientH precisely at sources of parityp_r.
There partial_i z is zero for every forward direction, since F_r(t+1)=F_r(t)
on that strong pair. The other channel is zero everywhere. Hence every actual
strong contribution vanishes, QB(z)=a E(z). For each periodic forward shift T_i,
||T_i z-z||<=2||z||, so E(z)<=4D||z||^2 and QB(z)<=c||z||^2/4.
No relabelling of incoming/outgoing weights, boundary approximation or continuum
estimate is involved. The n=2 case is deliberately excluded: its paired zero-mean
one-pair scalar space is zero, so this witness would not exist there.

Observation annihilation. For any cellS in channelr choose one full coordinate
j. Holding the others fixed, t(x) runs through every residue as x_j runs through
that coordinate, so sum_(x_j=0)^(n-1)F_r(t(x))=0. Sum over the remaining cell
coordinates; every spatial-component mean is zero. The other internal channel
was zero to begin with, so ALL actual observations vanish. Therefore
 V^-1 QB(z)+Phi(Cz)<=c||z||^2/(4V)<c||z||^2/V,
which contradicts targetc restoration on this nonzero actual coexact field.

Scope and use. This kills slab/cylinder/column proposals whose every cell in
one original channel leaves some coordinate wholly uncut, regardless of their
displayed row count or the finite penalty magnitude. It does NOT kill arbitrary
O(n)-cell partitions: all their cells may be proper in every coordinate.
It proves neither canonical coexact nlogn necessity nor canonical O(n) existence,
and does not transfer the ambient251 lower to coexact fields. It is a direct
specialization of the already constructed253 strong-kernel mechanism, with an
explicit real curl-adjoint preimage and elementary rectangle sum. Historical
novelty/independence is not claimed; no new numerical/formal test, paper, review
score, physical mass gap, continuum result or PR29/TermSource change follows.
