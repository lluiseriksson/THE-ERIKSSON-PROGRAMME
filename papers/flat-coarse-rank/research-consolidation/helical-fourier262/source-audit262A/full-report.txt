4. ACTUAL COEXACT IMAGE AND ZERO MODE

At a nonzero physical frequency, the true forward curl is d wedge on independent
j<l components. Its adjoint's j component is
sum_(l<j)conj(dl)A_lj - sum_(l>j)conj(dl)A_jl.
The contraction by conj(d) vanishes on this image, by antisymmetry. Conversely
for conj(d) dot v=0, insert A_jl=(dj vl-dl vj)/|d|^2. Both ranges of l yield
|dl|^2 vj-dj conj(dl)vl; adding them yields
(|d|^2 vj-dj sum_l conj(dl)vl)/|d|^2=vj.
The sign and conjugations are correct, and no factor2 enters because only
independent two-form components are counted. This proves BOTH directions of
the actual im(d1*) characterization. It is not an unjustified replacement by
an abstract divergence-free subspace.

All d components vanish only at physical zero, equivalent here to w=p=q=0.
At that frequency the adjoint is zero, so v=0 is necessary; a vacuous divergence
equation alone would be insufficient. The note explicitly imposes this exclusion.
For other frequencies denominator|d|^2 is positive. Set the zero-frequency
potential to zero and inverse transform the explicit nonzero-frequency potentials
to obtain the actual preimage. If field coefficients obey real conjugation,
d(-k)=conj d(k) makes these potentials obey it as well, including real Nyquist
frequencies. The construction applies separately to each original channel.

7. REAL VERSUS COMPLEX MINIMA

The physical coefficient form, curl-adjoint map and cell observations are real.
Write a complex actual-kernel field u=x+i y. Its actual preimage may also be
split into real/imaginary parts, so x,y are each actual coexact, and their
observations separately vanish. Q(u)=Q(x)+Q(y) and||u||^2=||x||^2+||y||^2.
At least one nonzero part has quotient no larger than the complex quotient.
The reverse inequality follows because real fields form a subset of complex
ones. Thus real and complex global minima coincide. A minimizing complex block
can therefore yield a real minimizing global field after pairing/splitting,
even when the individual block is not conjugation invariant.

Conjugation sends(w,p,q) to(-w,-p,-q). Because -h=h modulo n, it maps the
w block to the block with representative -w modulo n/2. Under this map
D is unchanged,S changes sign and M(-k)=conj M(k), so the energy/domain/
trace block family is stable. Some blocks map to themselves; others are
exchanged in pairs. The note's actual real/imaginary argument and reference
to conjugate blocks accommodate that fact and are correct.
