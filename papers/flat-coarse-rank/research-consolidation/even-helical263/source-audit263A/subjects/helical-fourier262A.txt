SHEAR AND UNITARY TRANSFORM
Let i=x1,y=x2-kappa*x1,z=x3 in Z_n. The shear is a bijection even though
gcd(kappa,n)=2. For each spatial component and original channel set
f(i,y,z)=u(i,y+kappa*i,z). Define
  fhat(w,p,q)=n^(-3/2) sum_(i,y,z) f(i,y,z)
             exp[-2pi*i*(w*i+p*y+q*z)/n].
All w,p,q range over Z_n. Norm is preserved by finite Fourier orthogonality.
The physical forward-difference multipliers are
  d1=exp[2pi*i*(w-kappa*p)/n]-1,
  d2=exp[2pi*i*p/n]-1, d3=exp[2pi*i*q/n]-1.
Reason: physical e1 sends (i,y,z) to(i+1,y-kappa,z), e2 sends y to y+1,
and e3 sends z to z+1. No continuum differential approximation occurs.

ACTUAL FOUR TRACE CONSTRAINTS
For one component/channel write P_i=f(i,0,0),
L2_i=sum_z f(i,0,z), L3_i=sum_y f(i,y,0), T_i=sum_(y,z) f(i,y,z).
The four cell sums are exactly P_i, L2_i-P_i, L3_i-P_i,
T_i-L2_i-L3_i+P_i. All cell normalization constants are nonzero;
thus every original observation vanishes iff P=L2=L3=T=0.
After the unitary n^(-1/2) Fourier transform in i their exact values are
  Phat(w)=n^(-1) sum_(p,q) fhat(w,p,q),
  L2hat(w)=sum_p fhat(w,p,0),
  L3hat(w)=sum_q fhat(w,0,q),
  That(w)=n*fhat(w,0,0).
These follow directly by summing exponentials, including their n factors.
Consequently the kernel constraints for EACH w, spatial r and channel are
  sum_(p,q) fhat_r(w,p,q)=0; sum_p fhat_r(w,p,0)=0;
  sum_q fhat_r(w,0,q)=0; fhat_r(w,0,0)=0.               (TRACE)
They are the actual finite observations, not arbitrary chosen Fourier rows.

ACTUAL COEXACT SUBSPACE, NOT AN ASSUMED SUBSTITUTE
At a nonzero physical frequency k=(w-kappa*p,p,q), d=(d1,d2,d3) is nonzero.
For the forward curl with independent j<l indices the symbol is d wedge.
Its adjoint image is exactly {v:sum_j conj(dj)*vj=0}.
To verify the reverse inclusion, let v satisfy this equation and set
  A_jl=(dj*vl-dl*vj)/|d|^2, j<l.
The jth component of (d wedge)^* A is
  sum_(l<j) conj(dl)*A_lj - sum_(l>j) conj(dl)*A_jl
  =(|d|^2*vj-dj*sum_l conj(dl)*vl)/|d|^2 = vj.
At the zero frequency the adjoint symbol is zero, so require v=0,
not merely divergence zero. Thus these frequency conditions characterize
the actual im(d1^*) in both directions. Apply them separately in each
original channel. The inverse transform of these potentials supplies the
actual coexact preimage; real fields follow by conjugate pairing.

EXACT SOURCE-WEIGHTED ENERGY PAIRS
Because kappa is even, source parity x1+x2+x3 equals i+y+z mod2.
Let h=(n/2,n/2,n/2) in (w,p,q), and pair k with k+h once.
Write theta=(2pi*(w-kappa*p)/n,2pi*p/n,2pi*q/n),
  D=sum_j |exp(i*theta_j)-1|^2,
  S=sum_j sin(theta_j), b0=(a+H)/2.
For channel1, strong on odd starts, b1=(a-H)/2; for channel2 b1=(H-a)/2.
Each spatial component has exactly the Hermitian pair energy matrix
  M_b1(k) = [[b0*D, 2i*b1*S],[-2i*b1*S,b0*(12-D)]].       (ENERGY)
Indeed the coefficient is b0+b1*(-1)^(i+y+z); at k+h each physical
theta shifts by pi since kappa is even, and
sum_j conj(exp(i*theta_j)-1)*(-exp(i*theta_j)-1)=2i*S.
The diagonal complement is sum_j|exp(i*theta_j)+1|^2=12-D.
The formula retains the actual weak coefficient and source weights.
It is summed over spatial r and the two ORIGINAL channels, with no
channel rotation or undocumented mixed observation.
