Cosco, Nakajima, Schweiger, Asymptotics of the p-capacity in the critical
regime, arXiv:2112.03661v2 (9 May 2023), actual primary PDF:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.03661 and https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.03661
Read PDF abstract, Section1.1 definition, Theorems1.1 and1.2, Remark1.3,
and Section1.3 paragraph on p=2 (pages1-2). The authors describe reciprocal
logarithmic capacity for p=d=2 as established, with graph energies and
point-to-boundary conditions. This is direct evidence that logarithmic
cutoff/capacity scaling itself is classical. These sections do not supply
our actual curl-adjoint field, checkerboard source weights, canonical cell
annihilation, or restricted row-count implication. This comparison is not
a proof of originality or an exhaustive search for equivalent theorems.
Our finite estimates are derived in257A; its correctness does not depend
on importing the sharp constant or asymptotic theorem from this source.
