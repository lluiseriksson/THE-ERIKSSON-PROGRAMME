6. FREQUENCY ALIASING AND BOUNDED OVERLAP, NOT FALSE ORTHOGONALITY

Since kappa=2 mod4, gcd(kappa,n)=gcd(kappa A,n)=2. Both X,Y lie in
[0,8T], with8T<n/4. Among base frequencies, lambda_r=p0-kappa A X

A partner of one base frequency cannot be another base frequency: it would

gives X'-X=n/4 modh, because any odd multiple of n/4 is n/4 modh.
But |X'-X|<=8T<n/4. This also shows that choosing a parity-pair representative
cannot conceal an alias. For fixed X,Y, J=q-p has a unique integer
