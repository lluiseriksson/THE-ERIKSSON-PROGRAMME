264A primary comparison supplement, 11 November 2026. Bounded reading only.

Efendiev, Galvis, Lazarov and Willems, Robust Domain Decomposition
Preconditioners for Abstract Symmetric Positive Definite Bilinear Forms,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1105.1131
Read the author-submitted abstract, not the full paper. It develops stable
decompositions and local-eigenfunction coarse spaces with contrast/mesh
robustness, with scalar elliptic, mixed, Stokes and Brinkman applications.
Thus broad claims of inventing spectral coarse spaces or contrast-robust
coarse corrections would be untenable. The abstract does not establish the
specific original-channel nonwrapping rectangle-map theorem in264A, and
its absence from the abstract does not establish novelty.

Bootland, Dolean, Nataf and Tournier, A robust and adaptive GenEO-type domain
decomposition preconditioner for H(curl) problems in three-dimensional
general topologies, v3 revised16 May2025:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.18783
https://arxiv.org/html/2311.18783v3
Read the abstract and bounded opening motivation through the contributions
paragraph; not the full theorem analysis, numerics or article. It treats
positive Maxwell finite-element systems, combines scalar-gradient near-kernel
information with local spectral enrichment, and discusses topological and
coefficient obstacles. It is relevant prior context, but is a different
operator, space and observation class from264A. In particular, no transfer
from our finite weighted coexact model to Maxwell/Yang-Mills follows.

No theorem of these papers is used to fill a gap in264A. These lookups do
not prove priority, comprehensive novelty or practical usefulness. A future
grouped manuscript must compare the precise operators, observations and
quantifiers before making a relevance claim. This is neither a new paper
nor a model score or peer review.
