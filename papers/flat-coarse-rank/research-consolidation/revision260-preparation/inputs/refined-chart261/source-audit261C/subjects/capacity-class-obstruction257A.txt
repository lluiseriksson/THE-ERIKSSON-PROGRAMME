THEOREM PROPOSED, WITH RESTRICTED DOMAIN EXPLICIT
Let n>=32 be divisible by4, V=n^3, c>0, a=c/48, H=cn^2.
On X=(Z/nZ)^3 use forward partial_h f(x)=f(x+e_h)-f(x),
delta_h f(x)=f(x)-f(x-e_h), and original orthogonal channels alpha=1,2.
Channel1 source weight b1(x)=H when sum(x) is odd, a when even;
b2 has opposite parity. Norms are original counting norms, and
 Q(z)=sum_(x,h,alpha) b_alpha(x)|partial_h z_alpha(x)|^2.
Each spatial channel lies in actual U0=im(d1^*) for the forward curl
(d1u)ij=partial_i u_j-partial_j u_i. No Hodge projection is used.

In channel1 let J_1,...,J_M2 and K_1,...,K_M3 be partitions of the two
cyclic coordinate sets into nonempty integer intervals. Intervals may wrap
around0, provided each is a consecutive cyclic interval; a whole cycle is
allowed. Use exactly the SAME J and K partitions at every x1=i.
Channel1 cells are {i} x J_j x K_k. Put q=M2*M3, R=n/4,
 L=log((R+1)/3)>0.
Use the original full vector component means
 C_(S,r,alpha)=(V|S|)^(-1/2) sum_(x in S) z_(r,alpha)(x).
The partition and number of rows in channel2 may be arbitrary: our witness
is identically zero in channel2. Channel1 has exactly3nq full rows.

If L>=2^22 q, then there exists an actual REAL nonzero z=(d1^*psi,0)
annihilating ALL these original means and satisfying
 Q(z)<=(7/16)c||z||^2<c||z||^2.
Thus arbitrary quadratic penalties on the means cannot restore c. Conversely,
recovery within THIS fixed-transverse-cut product-slice class requires
 q>L/2^22, hence channel1 row count >3nL/2^22.
This gives an order n log n necessary count only in this class, uniformly
over all cut positions and separations, including cutsets depending on n.
The threshold is enormous and not optimized. There is no practical claim.
It does NOT cover x1-dependent cutsets, nonproduct rectangle partitions,
general canonical means, mixed-channel measurement transformations, or the
global Q251 optimum. No physical spectral gap or continuum result follows.

1. EXACT COEXACT PREIMAGE AND CORNER TELESCOPING
For a real scalar Psi set phi=delta2 delta3 Psi and psi23=phi,
psi12=psi13=0. Since partial_j^*=-delta_j, direct adjoint calculation gives
 z1=(0,delta3 phi,-delta2 phi)
   =(0,delta2 delta3^2 Psi,-delta2^2 delta3 Psi)=P Psi.
This is membership in the actual image, not just a divergence constraint.
All components have zero full sum by periodic telescoping.
Let E2 be the M2 distinct terminal endpoints B_j of the cyclic J intervals,
and E3 the M3 terminal endpoints D_k of the K intervals. Their starting
endpoints minus1 are also the same terminal endpoint sets, because these are
partitions of a cycle. For a whole-cycle interval the two endpoints coincide
and the relevant telescoping difference is zero, which causes no problem.
Let E=E2 x E3: it has exactly q distinct transverse corner pairs.
Writing J=[A,B], K=[C,D] along their cyclic order, summing z2=delta2 delta3^2
Psi first in x2 and then x3 gives
 delta3Psi(B,D)-delta3Psi(A-1,D)
 -delta3Psi(B,C-1)+delta3Psi(A-1,C-1).
The z3 sum is the negative corresponding four-corner difference of delta2Psi.
These formulas apply separately at every x1=i, also across periodic seams.
Therefore it suffices that delta2Psi=delta3Psi=0 at every point of E for all i.

2. EXPLICIT LOW FIELD
Let t=pi*(x1+x2+x3)/2 and Psi0=sin(t), a real periodic function since4|n.
The period4 identities are delta_j sin(t)=sin(t)+cos(t),
delta2delta3 sin(t)=2cos(t), and
delta_j(2cos(t))=w(t), with values(2,-2,-2,2) on successive residue classes.
Hence z0=P Psi0=(0,w,-w), ||z0||^2=8V.
At every ODD source sum(x), w(x+e_h)=w(x), for each h=1,2,3.
All the original channel1 strong forward differences of z0 vanish exactly.
Also |Psi0|<=1 and |z0(x)|^2=8 at every site.

3. A SINGLE EXPLICIT TRANSVERSE CUTOFF
On Y=(Z/nZ)^2, let rho_p(y) be periodic l-infinity distance from p.
Define f_p(y)=min(1,max(0,log((rho_p(y)+1)/3)/L)).
Then 0<=f_p<=1; f_p=0 for rho<=2 and f_p=1 for rho>=R.
Write E_Y(f)=sum_(y,h=2,3)|f(y+e_h)-f(y)|^2, each forward edge once.
We prove, for every p,
 E_Y(f_p)<=64/L, and ||1-f_p||_Y^2<=4n^2/L^2.                 (1)
An edge with nonzero difference has smaller radius r with2<=r<R.
Radius changes by at most1 along a nearest-neighbor edge. The number of
forward edges crossing from radius r to r+1, disregarding their orientation,
is8r+4<=32r. Here r+1<=R<=n/4, so these are ordinary square boundaries in
local cyclic coordinates, with no distance seam or antipodal ambiguity.
The scalar increment is at most 1/((r+1)L), since log(1+u)<=u; clipping is
1-Lipschitz. Consequently
 E_Y(f_p)<=32/L^2 sum_(r=2)^(R-1) r/(r+1)^2
         <=32log(R+1)/L^2 <=64/L.
For the harmonic estimate use r/(r+1)^2<=1/(r+1), and
sum_(s=3)^R 1/s<=integral_2^R dt/t<=log(R+1).
The last inequality log(R+1)<=2log((R+1)/3)=2L holds for R>=8.

For the norm bound only rho<R matters. Put u=rho+1 and group all sites
with (R+1)/2^(k+1)<u<=(R+1)/2^k, k>=0. There are at most
4(R+1)^2/4^k sites in each such group: the containing square rho+1<=S
has at most(2S)^2 sites when S>=1, and none when S<1.
The deficit is at most (k+1)log2/L in that group. For rho<=2 this remains
true because 1-f_p=1<=log((R+1)/(rho+1))/L; for other rho it is equality
before using the dyadic upper bound. Thus
 ||1-f_p||^2<=4(R+1)^2 (log2)^2/L^2 sum_(k>=0)(k+1)^2/4^k
            <=16(R+1)^2/L^2 <=4n^2/L^2.
Indeed the series is80/27<4, log2<1, and R+1<=n/2.
This proves (1) without a numerical computation or external capacity theorem.

4. MINIMUM, NOT PRODUCT: LINEAR DEPENDENCE ON CORNER COUNT
Set eta(y)=min_(p in E) f_p(y). This is real, periodic, in[0,1], and zero
on every radius2 corner neighborhood. No separation of corners is required.
For any lists a_p,b_p of real numbers,
 |min_p a_p-min_p b_p|<=max_p|a_p-b_p|.
To see this, choose an index attaining the minimum of each list and compare
in the appropriate direction. Squaring and bounding max by sum gives
 E_Y(eta)<=sum_p E_Y(f_p)<=64q/L.
Also 1-eta=max_p(1-f_p), so
 ||1-eta||_Y^2<=sum_p||1-f_p||_Y^2<=4qn^2/L^2.              (2)
This is the improvement over the unreviewed product-cutoff handoff, whose
Cauchy-Schwarz estimate lost an extra factor q. No differentiability of
the minimum is assumed or needed on this finite lattice.

5. FINITE CONVOLUTION COMMUTATORS, IN ORIGINAL NORMS
Take Psi(x)=eta(x2,x3)Psi0(x), and z1=P Psi. At each corner eta is zero at
the corner and its backward e2 and e3 neighbors. Thus delta2Psi=delta3Psi=0
there for every x1, and Section1 proves exact invisibility of every mean.

For a transverse translation by (r2,r3), telescoping a path and the triangle
inequality in the counting l2 norm give
 ||T^r eta-eta||_Y <= (|r2|+|r3|) sqrt(E_Y(eta)).
This is valid for either shift sign and across a periodic seam; each unit
directional difference norm is <=sqrt(E_Y(eta)). The lift to X multiplies
norms by sqrt(n), since eta is independent of x1.
For any finite convolution A=sum_r a_r T^r, direct subtraction gives
 A(etaPsi0)-eta A(Psi0)=sum_r a_r(T^r eta-eta)T^r Psi0.
Using |Psi0|<=1 and the preceding translation bound yields
 ||A(etaPsi0)-eta A(Psi0)||_X
 <=sqrt(n E_Y(eta)) sum_r |a_r|(|r2|+|r3|).                 (3)
No coefficient commutation with the original source weights is used.

Each of the two nonzero P components is a product of three unit difference
operators: coefficient l1 sum<=2^3=8, transverse shift radius<=3.
Each partial_h P component is a product of four unit difference operators:
coefficient l1 sum<=16, transverse shift radius<=4, for every h. These
bounds remain valid with repeated translations or coefficients combined.
Apply (3) to the two P components, then square and sum:
 ||P(etaPsi0)-eta z0||^2<=2(8*3)^2 n E_Y(eta)=1152nE_Y(eta). (4)
For the six direction/nonzero-component pairs apply (3) to partial_h P.
On the original odd sources, eta partial_h z0 is EXACTLY zero by Section2.
Restricting the full norm commutator estimate to these sites gives
 sum_(odd x,h)|partial_h z1(x)|^2
 <=6(16*4)^2 n E_Y(eta)=24576nE_Y(eta).                     (5)
There is no replacement of odd source weights by target weights. The minimum
cutoff needs only first differences for (3), although P has third order.

6. NONZERO WITNESS AND ENERGY, INCLUDING ALL CONSTANTS
Use z1-z0=(eta-1)z0+[P(etaPsi0)-eta z0], (2), (4), and |z0(x)|^2=8:
 ||z1-z0||^2<=16n||1-eta||_Y^2+2304nE_Y(eta)
 <=64qV/L^2+147456nq/L
 <=(64q/L^2+147456q/L)V.                                  (6)
The final coarse inequality uses n<=V. If L>=2^22q and q>=1,
the coefficient in (6) is <=2^-38+9/256<2.
Since ||z0||=sqrt(8V), the reverse triangle inequality gives
 ||z1||>sqrt(8V)-sqrt(2V)=sqrt(2V), hence ||z1||^2>2V.
The strong contribution is, by (5), H=cn^2 and (2), at most
 24576cn^3 E_Y(eta)<=1572864cqV/L<=(3/8)cV
                         <(3/16)c||z1||^2.               (7)
For any counting-norm vector field f, each forward difference satisfies
||partial_h f||^2<=4||f||^2 by translation invariance and the triangle
inequality. Summing h=1,2,3 gives <=12||f||^2. Therefore the weak-source
contribution is <=12a||z1||^2=(c/4)||z1||^2.
Combining this with (7), setting the second channel to zero, proves
 Q(z)<(7/16)c||z||^2, in particular the stated weak inequality.
All fields and potentials here are explicit finite real functions. The
nonzero claim is proved by (6), rather than inferred from a nonzero potential.
Every quadratic form applied to the zero vector of means is zero, including
arbitrarily large size-dependent penalties, which proves the obstruction.

7. SCOPE, NONVACUITY AND NEXT FALSIFIABLE FRONTIER
For any fixed finite cutset sizes q, arbitrarily large multiples of4 satisfy
log((n/4+1)/3)>=2^22q. All intervals in each partition can be chosen nonempty
at such n, so the theorem is not vacuous. Arbitrarily nearby or unbalanced
cut positions are covered; the derived loss is linear in q.
L=log n-log12+o(1) gives the restricted n log n necessary row count.
This is an asymptotic obstruction in the specified original domain, not a
claim that the global canonical optimum is n log n. The fixed-cut assumption
is essential to the constructed x1-independent cutoff; union of different
cutsets over x1 can contain many more than q points.
Originality is separate from analytical correctness: finite logarithmic
cutoffs, capacity, minima and commutator arguments are standard mechanisms.
No priority conclusion or wider significance score is inferred. The precise
generalization beyond256 is from one grid to all fixed-transverse-cut
product-slice partitions, with derived logarithmic necessary count there.
