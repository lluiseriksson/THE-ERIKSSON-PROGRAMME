1. ACTUAL PAIRED SUM-LAYER FUNCTION SPACES
Put m=n/2 and t(x)=sum_i x_i mod n. Evenness makes parity of t agree with
actual site parity, including wrap labels. For each l=0,1 let F_l be the
space of real functions f:Z_n->R such that
 f(l+2j)=f(l+2j+1) for all j in Z_m (indices mod n), and sum_t f(t)=0.
The m pairs are disjoint and cover all n residues. For l1 the last pair is
{n-1,0}. A function is determined by m pair values b_j with sum_j b_j=0,
because sum_t f(t)=2 sum_j b_j. Hence dim_R F_l=m-1, including m1 where
the space is zero. This dimension is derived, not an assumed low-band size.
Every f(t(x)) has spatial mean n^(D-1)sum_t f(t)=0.

2. THE STRONG-NORMAL COORDINATES ARE AN INVERTIBLE PROOF MAP

det(S)^2=1-(s0 dot s1)^2=sin^2(theta)>0, so S is invertible. This is derived
from the actual two distinct weak lines; no external frame certificate or

Choose independently f_(l,r) in F_l for l=0,1 and r=1..D-1, and define

For each spatial r define the ORIGINAL internal vector field

Every original component is a function of t(x) alone.

3. MEMBERSHIP IN THE ORIGINAL COEXACT IMAGE IS PROVED
We give an actual finite Fourier preimage, not just assume a divergence
condition implies membership. Expand the scalar functions on Z_n in its

coefficient u=(u_1,...,u_D) satisfies sum_r u_r=0 because sum_r z_r=0.
Therefore sum_r conjugate(xi_r)u_r=0.

The actual forward-curl symbol is (d1u)_(ij)=xi_i u_j-xi_j u_i for i<j.
Expanding its counting adjoint yields

For the coefficients above the last term vanishes. Define actual Fourier
2-form coefficients

and psi0=0. Their adjoint curl equals u. Nonzero frequencies are finite
and the denominator is strictly positive, so this defines a genuine finite

of psi; inverse Fourier transform is REAL. At self-conjugate k=n/2 the
multiplier and coefficients are real and the same formula applies directly.
Consequently each original internal channel of z lies in actual im(d1*).
Thus W subset U0^2 with its stated exact REAL dimension. No unproved Hodge
