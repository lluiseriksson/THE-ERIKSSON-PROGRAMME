STATEMENT AND PRECISE CHANGE OF HYPOTHESIS
Let D>=3, even n>=2, c>0, X=(Z/nZ)^D,V=n^D. Original fields have D spatial
components and two internal channels, counting norm. At source parity l use
 B(x)=a v_l v_l^T+H s_l s_l^T,
where v_l,s_l form an orthonormal pair, a=c/(16D),H=cn^2. The two unoriented
weak lines are distinct, with principal angle theta in(0,pi/2]. All original
forward labels and their source weights remain fixed. Let
 Q(z)=sum_(x,i,r) (partial_i z_r)^T B(x)partial_i z_r,
 U=im(d1*)^2 for the actual original forward curl and counting adjoint.

Put delta=1-|s0 dot s1|=1-cos(theta)>0, p=D-1 and
 R=ceil(sqrt(D/(4delta))), L=min(n,2R+1).
There is an explicit unrestricted REAL linear map C with exactly
 Nout=2Dn L^(D-1)
displayed rows and normalized quadratic coefficient K=2c/delta, such that
 Q(z)/V+K||Cz||^2>=2c||z||^2/V
on ALL original ambient fields. Hence target c also holds there and on U.
At fixed D and fixed positive theta, Nout=O_(D,theta)(n) and K is independent
of n. The rows may mix the two original internal channels and are nonlocal
Fourier rows on diagonal strong components. They are NOT canonical original-
channel axis-aligned rectangle means. No claim of projection invariance on U
or equality of restricted rank and Nout is used.

In particular, the minimal unrestricted restoring ambient rank lies between
 Dn and 2Dn L^(D-1).
On actual U it lies between (D-1)(n-2) and 2Dn L^(D-1).
Thus both minima have order n as n->infinity at fixed D,theta. Exact minima
from253 at the orthogonal angle are not extended to arbitrary theta here.
The hypothesis theta=pi/2 is removed from the ORDER-n unrestricted upper,
by proving the needed high-mode control, not by assuming a comparison gap.
The original source family, finite-dimensional domain and fixed positive

1. A PROOF COORDINATE, NOT A CHANGE OF OBSERVATION CLASS OR ORIGINAL DOMAIN
Let S be the real2by2 matrix with rows s0^T and s1^T. Its Gram matrix has
diagonal1 and off-diagonal s0 dot s1. Its two eigenvalues are
1+|s0 dot s1| and delta=1-|s0 dot s1|. Therefore S is invertible and
 ||Sz||^2>=delta||z||^2
for the full original field norm, summing all sites and spatial components.
Set Y_(l,r)(x)=s_l dot z_r(x). This defines actual linear functionals on the
original field. The scalar Y_l is strongly weighted only on starts of parityl.
For every original difference the original orthonormal pair gives
 (partial_i z_r)^T B(x)partial_i z_r
 =a|v_l dot partial_i z_r|^2+H|partial_i Y_(l,r)|^2.
Dropping nonnegative weak terms yields Q>=H E_strong(Y), where E_strong
sums exactly the parityl starts in channel coordinateY_l. There is no loss
of weak/strong labels and no comparison based on an assumed frame certificate.

2. EXACT STRONG COMPONENTS AND THEIR TWO-LAYER SYMBOL
For each l and each residue s of parityl let
 G_(l,s)={x:sum_i x_i belongs to{s,s+1} mod n}.
These n/2 sets partition X for each l. For y in (Z/nZ)^p put
 x_s(y)=(y_1,...,y_p,s-sum_i y_i) modn,
 u(y)=Y_(l,r)(x_s(y)), t(y)=Y_(l,r)(x_s(y)+e_D).
These are bijections onto the actual two sum layers. Every strong forward
label stays in this component, including the wrap pair{n-1,0}. Its scalar
energy, with each original label retained including period2, is exactly
 E_s=sum_y|u(y)-t(y)|^2+sum_(y,i=1..p)|u(y)-t(y+e_i)|^2.
Rungs connect the two layers and rung/cross-edge paths generate all source
translations, so these are connected actual components.

For a counting-orthonormal Fourier character k of (Z/nZ)^p, write
 T_k=1+sum_(i=1..p)exp(2pi i k_i/n).
The two-layer Hermitian symbol has diagonalD and off-diagonal -T_k and its
conjugate, with eigenvalues D-|T_k| and D+|T_k|. Expanding squared moduli gives
 D^2-|T_k|^2=sum_(0<=i<j<=p)|exp(i theta_i)-exp(i theta_j)|^2,
theta0=0,theta_i=2pi k_i/n. Hence
 D-|T_k| >= (1/(2D))sum_(i=1..p)|1-exp(2pi i k_i/n)|^2.
Both this lower bound and the actual symbol are derived from original labels.

3. KEEPING A FIXED LOW-FREQUENCY CUBE, NOT ONLY THE CONSTANT MODE
Let |k_i|_n be distance of the residue to0 in Z_n and let
 A_R={k: |k_i|_n<=R for every i=1..p}.
It has exactly L^p elements and is closed under conjugation. On each actual
two-layer component, let P keep BOTH layer Fourier coefficients for k in A_R
and discard the others. This is a real counting-orthogonal projection on Y:
conjugate frequencies pair; any self-conjugate/Nyquist coefficients are real.
Its real dimension per component is 2L^p, including exactly one contribution
per real Nyquist coefficient, not two. Summing its orthogonal copies over
l,s,r gives an actual real orthogonal P on the full Y field space.

If A_R is the whole frequency torus, (I-P)Y=0 and the following tail inequality
is automatic. Otherwise some coordinate of every excluded k has
 R+1<=|k_i|_n<=n/2.
Since sin(pi q/n)>=2q/n for0<=q<=n/2,
 |1-exp(2pi i k_i/n)|^2>=16(R+1)^2/n^2.
Thus the smaller eigenvalue outside A_R is at least
 gamma=8(R+1)^2/(Dn^2).
The larger eigenvalue is >=D and also >=gamma: in the nonempty tail case
R+1<=n/2, so gamma<=2/D<=D. Finite Parseval, summed over all actual components,
now gives the DERIVED inequality
 E_strong(Y)>=gamma||(I-P)Y||^2.
We included both eigenbranches inside A_R to avoid any assumed basis choice,
branch projection regularity, missing layer mass or hidden Nyquist exception.

4. EXPLICIT NORMALIZED ORIGINAL-FIELD OBSERVATIONS AND PENALTY
Choose any explicitly enumerated real orthonormal character basis of A_R on
each source torus (constant, real sine/cosine pairs and real Nyquist modes).
Take separate copies on the two layers of every(l,s,r), extend each basis
function by zero off its original layer, and call them b_j. They form an
orthonormal real basis of rangeP in Y coordinates. Define on ORIGINAL fields
 (Cz)_j=V^(-1/2)<b_j,Y_(l,r)>.
The inner product is counting and Y_(l,r)=s_l dot z_r, so this specifies actual
linear rows in the original channel coordinates; it does not rotate an already
prescribed canonical map. There are2L^p rows per component, n/2 components
per l, two l, and D spatial components: Nout=2DnL^p. Norms satisfy exactly
 ||Cz||^2=||P(Sz)||^2/V.

Our chosen R gives (R+1)^2>=D/(4delta). Multiplying the proved tail gap by
H=cn^2 therefore yields H gamma>=2c/delta. In the full-cube case the same
conclusion on the zero tail holds without invoking a nonexistent outside mode.
Consequently
 Q(z)+VK||Cz||^2
 >=(2c/delta)||(I-P)Sz||^2+(2c/delta)||PSz||^2
 =(2c/delta)||Sz||^2 >=2c||z||^2.
Division by V proves the statement. P acts orthogonally on Y, not necessarily
on the original z norm. We only use its orthogonality in Y and the proved
singular-value bound to return to the original norm. No assertion that P or
S^-1 P S preserves U is needed: the inequality holds on the whole ambient
space before legitimate restriction to the actual U.

5. LOWER RANKS INSIDE THE ACTUAL ORIGINAL SPACES
For completeness we derive the lower subspaces, rather than supplying their
dimension. Let m=n/2. For each l let F_l consist of real f:Z_n->R constant on
the m strong pairs{l+2j,l+2j+1}, with zero sum. Its dimension is m-1.
For each spatial r<D choose independent f_(l,r) in F_l and set
f_(l,D)=-sum_(r<D)f_(l,r). Define
 z_r(x)=S^-1(f_(0,r)(sumx),f_(1,r)(sumx))^T.
The actual linear map is injective because S is invertible and sumx is onto.
Its real dimension is2(D-1)(m-1)=(D-1)(n-2). The fields have zero mean in
each original component and pointwise sum_r z_r=0. At every strong startl
the corresponding f_l is constant across that actual forward step, so all
strong normal differences vanish and Q(z)=aE(z)<=4Da||z||^2=c||z||^2/4.

These fields belong to actual U, not merely to a divergence-free replacement:
their nonzero original Fourier frequencies are (k,...,k), with common forward
multiplier xi_i=exp(2pi ik/n)-1. For any spatial coefficient u, sum_i u_i=0;
hence sum_i conjugate(xi_i)u_i=0. Put
 psi_ij=(xi_i u_j-xi_j u_i)/(sum_i|xi_i|^2),i<j.
Expansion of the actual unordered-curl adjoint gives d1*psi=u. There is no
zero frequency; conjugate and Nyquist symmetry make the inverse two-form real.
This explicitly proves actual coexact membership. A restoring linear map
with finite Phi(Cz),Phi(0)=0 must be injective on this entire low-energy space,
otherwise its nonzero kernel witness contradicts the target c inequality.
Thus its actual restricted rank is at least(D-1)(n-2), including zero at n2.

For the ambient lower, allow arbitrary pair values in every f_(l,r), with no
mean or spatial-sum constraint and all D spatial components independent.
S^-1 again injects these2Dm=Dn parameters. Strong differences still vanish
and Q=aE<=c||z||^2/4 on the whole subspace, including constants. The same
kernel argument gives ambient restoring rank>=Dn. These are actual ranks;
the constructed upper's displayed count only bounds rank from above.

6. WHAT IS REMOVED, WHAT IS NOT, AND FALSIFIERS
At an arbitrarily small fixed positive angle, keeping only strong component
means need not give the original norm margin. We do NOT assert that it does.
We keep a finite explicitly bounded collection of transverse low modes per
component. The new proof pays the comparison loss delta through the cutoff
R and coefficient K; at fixed theta both are n-independent. As theta->0,
delta->0, the count constant and K diverge. No uniform-angle conditioning,
all-angle exact minimum, canonical rectangle construction or practical solver
efficiency is claimed. At theta=0 S loses invertibility; that case is excluded
and is not obtained by an unjustified limiting argument.
