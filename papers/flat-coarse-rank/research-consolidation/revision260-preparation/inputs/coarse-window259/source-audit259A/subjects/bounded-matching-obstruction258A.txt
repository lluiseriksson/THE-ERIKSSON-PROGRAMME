THEOREM PROPOSED
Let n>=32,4|n,V=n^3,c>0,a=c/48,H=cn^2. On X=(Z/nZ)^3 use
 partial_h f(x)=f(x+e_h)-f(x), delta_h f(x)=f(x)-f(x-e_h).
Channel1 has source weight H at odd x1+x2+x3 and a at even sources;
channel2 has opposite parity, in the original orthogonal internal basis.
 Q(z)=sum_(x,h,alpha) b_alpha(x)|partial_h z_alpha(x)|^2,
 ||z||^2=sum_(x,alpha)|z_alpha(x)|^2.
The actual spatial domain in each channel is U0=im(d1^*) for the forward curl.

At each i in Z/nZ, choose nonempty cyclic interval partitions
 J_(i,1),...,J_(i,M2(i)) and K_(i,1),...,K_(i,M3(i)).
Their product q=M2(i)M3(i)>=1 is the SAME integer at all slices, but the
intervals, cut locations, and even the two factor counts may depend on i.
Channel1 cells are {i} x J_(i,j) x K_(i,k), with their original component means
 C_(S,r,1)=(V|S|)^(-1/2) sum_(x in S) z_(r,1)(x).
They give exactly3nq full channel1 rows. Channel2 cells can be arbitrary;
the constructed channel2 field is identically zero.

Let E_i be the Cartesian product of the two sets of terminal interval
endpoints at slice i. Thus E_i contains exactly q distinct transverse points.
Suppose for every cyclic adjacent pair i,i+1 there exists a BIJECTION
 beta_i:E_i -> E_(i+1)
whose matched points have periodic l1 distance <=B, for an integer B>=0.
Periodic l1 distance is the minimum |r2|+|r3| over integer representatives
of the difference modulo n. This is a concrete finite geometric condition.
No common translation, fixed cuts, global point labels or zero permutation
after one trip around x1 are required. Bijections may change at each step.
Put R=n/4, L=log((R+1)/3), M=max(4,3+B).

If L>=2^18 M^2 q, there is an explicit REAL nonzero z=(d1^*psi,0) such that
every original mean in BOTH channels is exactly zero and
 Q(z)<=(7/16)c||z||^2<c||z||^2.
Consequently no quadratic penalty on these observations restores c.
Recovery in this class requires
 q > L/(2^18 M^2), or 3nq > 3nL/(2^18 M^2).
For B bounded independently of n this is a necessary order n log n row count.
For growing B only the displayed quantitative tradeoff is asserted.
This is not a lower bound for arbitrary canonical or nonproduct partitions.
All constants and existence thresholds are deliberately unoptimized.

1. ACTUAL REAL PREIMAGE AND EXACT MEANS
For any real periodic scalar Psi, put phi=delta2delta3Psi and take the real
two-form psi23=phi, psi12=psi13=0. Direct adjunction of
 (d1u)23=partial2u3-partial3u2,
using partial_h^*=-delta_h gives
 z1=d1^*psi=(0,delta3phi,-delta2phi)
    =(0,delta2delta3^2Psi,-delta2^2delta3Psi)=:P Psi.
This is membership in the original image; no projection is used. Full
component sums vanish by periodic telescoping of a first difference.
At fixed i, summing z2 over J=[A,C] and K=[D,F] produces the four-corner
difference of delta3Psi at (C,F),(A-1,F),(C,D-1),(A-1,D-1).
The z3 sum is the negative four-corner difference of delta2Psi there.
All these points belong to E_i since each starting endpoint minus1 is the
preceding interval's terminal endpoint in that slice. A wrapping interval
obeys the same telescoping identity; a whole-cycle factor cancels exactly.
Thus all slice-i means vanish if delta2Psi=delta3Psi=0 on E_i. This argument
does not compare cut positions or factor counts between different slices.

2. MOVING MINIMUM CUTOFF AND ITS DEFICIT
For p in Y=(Z/nZ)^2 let rho_p be periodic l-infinity distance and
 f_p(y)=clip_[0,1](log((rho_p(y)+1)/3)/L).
The imported257A Section3 lemma gives, with original transverse counting norm,
 E_Y(f_p)=sum_(y,h=2,3)|partial_h f_p(y)|^2<=64/L,
 ||1-f_p||_Y^2<=4n^2/L^2.                                  (1)
Its hypotheses are exactly R>=8, R=n/4 and the L used here. Translations
preserve both estimates, including all cyclic seams.
Define the real scalar eta(i,y)=min_(p in E_i) f_p(y). It is periodic in i,
because the data are indexed by Z/nZ. It lies in[0,1] and vanishes within
transverse radius2 of every p in E_i. At each slice,
 1-eta(i,y)=max_(p in E_i)(1-f_p(y)),
so (1) gives ||1-eta(i,.)||_Y^2<=4qn^2/L^2. Summing the n slices yields
 ||1-eta||_X^2<=4qV/L^2.                                   (2)

3. DERIVE MOVING TRANSLATION BOUNDS FROM ACTUAL BIJECTIONS
First, any transverse shift u satisfies
 ||f_p(.+u)-f_p||_Y<=|u|_1 sqrt(64/L),                     (3)
by a shortest integer representative path, repeated triangle inequality,
translation invariance, and (1). Negative shifts and seams are included.
More generally
 ||f_(p')(.+u)-f_p||_Y<=(|u|_1+B) sqrt(64/L)
if p'=beta_i(p), because the two cutoffs are translates of the same radial
function and p'-p has an integer representative of l1 norm<=B.
For any two finite real lists indexed by the same set,
 |min a_p-min b_p|<=max_p|a_p-b_p|.
Apply this pointwise, use the bijection beta_i to index the second slice,
square, bound max by sum, then sum over y. It follows that
 ||eta(i+1,.+u)-eta(i,.)||_Y^2
 <=sum_(p in E_i)||f_(beta_i(p))(.+u)-f_p||_Y^2
 <=64q(|u|_1+B)^2/L.                                      (4)
For no longitudinal shift, the same proof uses the identity matching and
gives 64q|u|_1^2/L, without B. In particular the argument does not require
fixed point labels across three slices, let alone across the entire cycle.
At i=n-1, use the explicitly required matching beta_(n-1) to E_0; hence the
longitudinal seam is checked, without an assumption of trivial holonomy.

Let T^(r1,u)F(i,y)=F(i+r1,y+u). Summing (4) or its r1=0 variant over i gives,
for the ONLY longitudinal shifts needed below, r1 in {0,1},
 ||T^(r1,u)eta-eta||_X
 <=sqrt(64nq/L) (|u|_1+B r1).                              (5)
No estimate for unrestricted long longitudinal shifts is asserted or needed.
Equations(4)-(5) are derived from the geometric inputs, not assumed bounds
on a manufactured cutoff, weighted operator or coexact projection.

4. KEEP THE ORIGINAL LOW WAVE, NOT A MOVING PHASE
Take Psi0(x)=sin(pi*(x1+x2+x3)/2). The wave phase is the ORIGINAL total
coordinate; it is not transported with the cut positions. Its period4
identities give delta2delta3Psi0=2cos(pi*sumx/2), and
 z0=P Psi0=(0,w,-w), with w=(2,-2,-2,2) on total residues0,1,2,3.
Thus |z0(x)|^2=8, ||z0||^2=8V, and |Psi0|<=1. At every original odd source,
w(x+e_h)=w(x), for each h, so partial_h z0 vanishes there exactly.
Set Psi=eta Psi0, z1=P Psi. At each p in E_i the cutoff is zero at p and
its backward e2/e3 neighbors. Hence delta2Psi=delta3Psi=0 there, for that i.
Section1 proves exact invisibility of all channel1 means. Channel2 is zero.

5. FULL CONVOLUTION COMMUTATORS, WITH LONGITUDINAL MOTION INCLUDED
For any finite convolution A=sum_r a_r T^r, algebra gives exactly
 A(etaPsi0)-eta A(Psi0)=sum_r a_r(T^r eta-eta)T^rPsi0.
Since |Psi0|<=1, the l2 triangle inequality and (5) imply
 ||A(etaPsi0)-eta A(Psi0)||_X
 <=sqrt(64nq/L) sum_r |a_r|(|r2|+|r3|+B r1),              (6)
provided all its longitudinal shifts have r1 in{0,1}.
Each nonzero P component is a product of THREE transverse backward
differences: coefficient l1 sum<=8, r1=0, transverse radius<=3. Therefore
 ||P(etaPsi0)-eta z0||^2<=2*(8*3)^2*64nq/L
                         =73728nq/L.                     (7)
For partial2P and partial3P, the l1 sum is<=16, r1=0 and transverse
radius<=4. For partial1P, r1 is0 or1, the transverse radius is<=3 and
the coefficient l1 sum is<=16. Thus the radius term in (6) is<=M for all
six direction/nonzero-component pairs. On original odd sources,
eta partial_h z0=0, so restriction of the full norm bound yields
 sum_(odd x,h)|partial_h z1(x)|^2
 <=6*(16M)^2*64nq/L=98304M^2 nq/L.                        (8)
The original source weight has not been moved to a target. Equation(8)
does not treat eta as independent of x1: the B term in(5)-(6) explicitly
controls that change. No differentiability of the minimum is needed.

6. NONZERO FIELD AND ORIGINAL WEIGHTED ENERGY
Split z1-z0=(eta-1)z0+[P(etaPsi0)-eta z0]. By(2),(7), |z0(x)|^2=8,
and the two-term squared-norm inequality,
 ||z1-z0||^2<=16||1-eta||_X^2+147456nq/L
 <=(64q/L^2+147456q/L)V,                                  (9)
where the final coarse bound uses n<=V. Since M>=4, the hypothesis
L>=2^18M^2q implies L>=2^22q. As q>=1, the coefficient in(9) is at most
2^-38+9/256<2. The reverse triangle inequality gives
 ||z1||>sqrt(8V)-sqrt(2V)=sqrt(2V), so the actual field is nonzero.
Multiplying (8) by H=cn^2 gives the original strong contribution at most
 98304M^2 cqV/L<=(3/8)cV<(3/16)c||z1||^2.                 (10)
Here 98304/2^18=3/8 exactly. The weak-source contribution is at most the
full unweighted energy times a. Each forward difference has norm<=2||z1||,
so summing all three directions bounds it by12a||z1||^2=c||z1||^2/4.
Together with(10), Q(z)<7c||z||^2/16. This proves the stated weak bound.
All potentials and fields are finite, periodic and real; their membership,
invisibility and nonzero norm have been derived. Any quadratic form of the
zero vector of all means is zero, regardless of size-dependent coefficients.

7. A QUANTITATIVE SEARCH REDUCTION, NOT GLOBAL CLOSURE
For these finite corner sets define the actual bottleneck motion
 B_* = max_i min_(bijections beta:E_i->E_(i+1))
               max_(p in E_i) dist_l1(p,beta(p)).
All sets are finite of equal nonzero cardinality, so every minimum and maximum
exists and B_* is a nonnegative integer. Hence the theorem always applies
with the geometrically determined B_*. The necessary inequality becomes
 L < 2^18 q max(4,3+B_*)^2.                                (11)
For a family with q<=Q fixed and L>=2^22Q, (11) forces
 B_* > sqrt(L/(2^18Q))-3.                                 (12)
Indeed the alternative max=4 would contradict L>=2^22Q; then solve (11)
for3+B_*, using q<=Q. Thus any prospective O(n)-row recovery family in THIS
product-slice class must have increasingly rapid corner motion, at least the
quantitative lower bound(12). Bounded-motion families are excluded.

Independent deformation is genuinely allowed: if two or more transverse
endpoints move at different bounded increments while the slice partitions
remain nonempty, product endpoint matchings meet the hypothesis with the
sum of the two endpoint-motion bounds. A common transverse translation is
unnecessary. Arbitrary cyclic adjacent matchings, even with nontrivial
permutation on returning around x1, are also allowed.
These are weaker geometric restrictions than stationary cuts or translated
copies of one fixed grid. They remain restrictions: (11) can be inconclusive
for rapidly jumping cuts, and the proof says nothing about general nonproduct
canonical rectangle partitions. No unconditional hypothesis elimination or
global nlogn optimum is recorded. The finite parameter regime is nonvacuous:
stationary data B=0 recover257A and meet the threshold for arbitrarily large
4-divisible n at fixed q; genuinely varying bounded data can also have such n.

The classical capacity/minimum machinery is not claimed as original. The
