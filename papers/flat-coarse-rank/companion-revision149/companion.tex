This companion groups the finite critical rectangle estimate, the partition
construction for supplied disjoint lattice cubes, and an explicit obstruction
to a fixed coarse penalty in the actual flat physical cochain domain. In the
last construction kernel coercivity is proved on every actual cochain,
while an actual divergence-zero field defeats the same fixed penalty.
The complete fixed-penalty statement has been formally checked and reproduced
in two fresh Colab clones at one source commit. The earlier Sobolev and cube
arguments and the sharp free-penalty extension remain analytic; no common
verification label is applied to all parts. Suitable cubes are still inputs,
not consequences of a critical inverse moment. The unrestricted rectangular
question remains open. This is an unreviewed companion revision, not an AIRR
deposit, acceptance, peer review or an interacting or continuum mass gap.
\end{abstract}

be composed to discard those assumptions.

splitting method are credited in the respective parts. We claim no new
