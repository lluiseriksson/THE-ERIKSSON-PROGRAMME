\begin{abstract}
We report a Lean~4 / \texttt{mathlib} formalization of the transfer-matrix identification
and spectral-gap chain for the one-dimensional open-boundary $\Z_2$ Ising spin chain (one
$\Z_2$ spin per time slice, $2\times 2$ transfer operator): from the Boltzmann path weight
$W_\beta(\sigma)=\prod_{t=0}^{n-1} e^{\beta s(\sigma_t,\sigma_{t+1})}$ and an independently
defined normalized transfer operator $T_\beta$ on $\C^2$, through the computed partition
function $Z_n = 2(e^\beta+e^{-\beta})^n$ and the Hermitian identification theorem
$\Ex_n[\conj{A(\sigma_0)}\,B(\sigma_n)] = \inn{A\Om}{T_\beta^n (B\Om)}$ (using Mathlib's
inner product, conjugate-linear in the first slot), to the exact two-point correlation
$\Ex_n[f(\sigma_0)f(\sigma_n)] = (\tanh\beta)^n$ of the real-valued sign observable $f$.

We separate explicitly between \emph{machine-checked Lean~4 declarations} (quoted verbatim
from \texttt{Z2Transfer.lean}, \texttt{SharpBridge.lean}, and \texttt{Z2Identification.lean},
together with their presence in \texttt{oracle\_check.lean}) and \emph{elementary analytic
consequences proved in prose}: specifically, Lean proves the operator norm bound
$\|T_\beta - \ket{\Om}\bra{\Om}\| \le \tanh\beta$ for $\beta \ge 0$ and the scalar
positivity inequalities $0 < 1 - \tanh\beta$ ($\forall\beta$) and $0 < -\log(\tanh\beta)$
($\beta > 0$), while the exact all-$\beta$ eigenvalue spectrum $\{1, \tanh\beta\}$, the
distinction at $\beta < 0$ between the \emph{absolute spectral gap} $1 - |\tanh\beta|$ and
the \emph{algebraic eigenvalue gap} $1 - \tanh\beta$, and the rank-one nonexistence of a
finite Hermitian $H$ with $T_0 = e^{-H}$ at $\beta = 0$ are elementary analytic consequences
established in Section~\ref{sec:spectral}. We further clarify that
\lean{z2\_pairing\_nondegenerate} covers only time-zero single-slice observables, whereas
the full positive-time Osterwalder--Seiler GNS quotient is nontrivial (witnessed by
$F(\sigma_0,\sigma_1) = f(\sigma_1) - (\tanh\beta)f(\sigma_0)$) and is formalized separately
(\texttt{ARR-2026-2TBRDN2DE598KSXD}), and that companion reflection positivity
(\texttt{ARR-2026-10JJK0VCCS87VTQH}) is not a logical dependency of this module. Nothing
here is a claim about $\SU(N)$, spatial-volume uniformity, continuum limits, or the Clay
Yang--Mills problem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and exact scope}

Constructive lattice field theory specifies a Euclidean probability measure, whereas a
mass gap is a spectral property of a self-adjoint transfer operator. Connecting the two
requires an \emph{identification theorem} expressing Euclidean expectations in the measure
as matrix elements of powers of the transfer operator, proved without circularity (without
defining the measure from the operator).

For the open-boundary 1D $\Z_2$ Ising spin chain on $n+1$ time slices
$\sigma : \{0,\dots,n\} \to \{0,1\}$, we define solely from $\exp$ and the bond sign
$s(i,j) = +1$ ($i=j$), $-1$ ($i \neq j$):
\begin{align*}
  W_\beta(\sigma) &= \prod_{t=0}^{n-1} e^{\beta s(\sigma_t,\sigma_{t+1})},
  \qquad
  Z_n(\beta) = \sum_{\sigma} W_\beta(\sigma), \\
  \Ex_n\bigl[\conj{A(\sigma_0)}\,B(\sigma_n)\bigr]
  &= \frac{1}{Z_n(\beta)}\sum_{\sigma}\conj{A(\sigma_0)}\,B(\sigma_n)\,W_\beta(\sigma).
\end{align*}
(We write $\conj{\cdot}$ explicitly rather than an overline so plain-text PDF extraction
never drops complex conjugation.) Independently, on $\C^2$ with Mathlib's Euclidean inner
product $\inn{x}{y} = \sum_{i\in\{0,1\}} \conj{x_i}\, y_i$ (conjugate-linear in the first
slot, linear in the second), we define
\[
  T_\beta = a(\beta)\,\mathbf{1} + b(\beta)\,S,
  \qquad
  a(\beta) = \frac{e^{\beta}}{e^{\beta}+e^{-\beta}},\quad
  b(\beta) = \frac{e^{-\beta}}{e^{\beta}+e^{-\beta}},
\]
where $S$ swaps the two coordinates.

\paragraph{Scope boundaries.}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Open 1D $\Z_2$ Ising spin chain, not 1D pure-link gauge theory.} An open
  one-dimensional pure-link gauge theory has no plaquettes and every link configuration is
  gauge-equivalent; the model formalized here is the open 1D $\Z_2$ Ising spin chain.
  \item \textbf{Transfer-matrix identification, not full positive-time OS reconstruction.}
  The companion reflection-positivity module (\texttt{ARR-2026-10JJK0VCCS87VTQH}) is
  \emph{not} imported or consumed by \lean{Z2Identification.lean}.
  \item \textbf{Temporal vs.\ spatial uniformity.} The exact identity $(\tanh\beta)^n$
  holds for every temporal separation $n \in \mathbb{N}$; spatial-volume uniformity does not
  arise because each spatial slice is a single site ($\dim \C^2 = 2$).
  \item \textbf{Classical mathematics.} The 1D Ising transfer matrix is textbook
  \cite{Simon}; the candidate contribution is a replayable, non-circular Lean~4
  formalization connecting the Gibbs path sum to operator clustering. No exhaustive
  priority search over all external Lean/Isabelle/Coq Ising formalizations is claimed, and
  nothing here bears on $\SU(N)$, continuum limits, or the Clay problem \cite{Clay}.
\end{enumerate}

The measure-side dependency closure is
$\texttt{z2Expect} \to \{\texttt{z2PathSum}, \texttt{z2Partition}\} \to
\texttt{z2PathWeight} \to \texttt{z2Bond} \to \{\exp, \texttt{z2Sign}\}$,

meet at a single bridge lemma \osline{Z2Identification}{118}{z2Bond\_eq} expressing
$k_\beta(i,j) = (e^\beta+e^{-\beta})(a\delta_{ij} + b(1-\delta_{ij}))$.
\end{remark}

\section{Machine-checked spectral theorems vs.\ analytic consequences}\label{sec:spectral}

To avoid any ambiguity between kernel-checked declarations and standard mathematical
deductions in prose, we separate the two explicitly.

\begin{theorem}[Machine-checked spectral statements in \texttt{Z2Transfer.lean} and \texttt{Z2Identification.lean}]
\label{thm:lean-spectral}
The Lean~4 kernel verifies (with zero \texttt{sorry} and standard axioms only):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item $T_\beta\Om = \Om$, $T_\beta^* = T_\beta$, and $T_\beta f = (\tanh\beta)f$ for all
  $\beta \in \R$ (\lean{z2TransferOp\_fix}, \lean{z2TransferOp\_selfAdjoint},
  \lean{z2Vec\_signObs\_eigen}, \lean{z2A\_sub\_z2B\_eq\_tanh});
  \item the exact componentwise identity
  $(T_\beta - \ket{\Om}\bra{\Om})v = (\tanh\beta)(v - \inn{\Om}{v}\Om)$ for all $\beta \in \R$
  (\lean{z2Projected\_eq});
  \item the one-sided operator-norm bound $\|T_\beta - \ket{\Om}\bra{\Om}\| \le \tanh\beta < 1$
  for $\beta \ge 0$ (\lean{z2TransferOp\_gap}, \lean{z2A\_sub\_z2B\_lt\_one});
  \item the scalar inequalities $0 < 1 - \tanh\beta$ for all $\beta \in \R$
  (\lean{z2\_spectral\_gap\_pos}) and $0 < -\log(\tanh\beta)$ for $\beta > 0$
  (\lean{z2\_mass\_pos});
  \item the clustering corollary \lean{z2\_clustering\_of\_transfer} for $\beta \ge 0$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proposition}[Elementary analytic consequences across $\beta \in \R$, proved in prose]
\label{prop:analytic-spectral}
The following standard spectral statements follow immediately from the $2\times 2$
eigendecomposition $T_\beta = P + (\tanh\beta)(\mathbf{1}-P)$ (where $P = \ket{\Om}\bra{\Om}$,
$\mathbf{1}-P = \ket{e_-}\bra{e_-}$ with $e_- = 2^{-1/2}(1,-1)$), and are \emph{not}
separately formalized as operator-logarithm theorems in Lean:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Exact spectrum:} $\operatorname{spec}(T_\beta) = \{1, \tanh\beta\}$ for every
  $\beta \in \R$, and $\|T_\beta - P\| = |\tanh\beta|$ for every $\beta \in \R$.
  \item \textbf{Positive coupling ($\beta > 0$):} $0 < \tanh\beta < 1$, so $T_\beta$ is
  strictly positive definite and admits the unique Hermitian logarithm
  $H = -\log(T_\beta) = (-\log\tanh\beta)(\mathbf{1}-P)$ satisfying $T_\beta = e^{-H}$,
  with Hamiltonian mass gap $m(\beta) = -\log(\tanh\beta) > 0$.
  \item \textbf{Negative coupling ($\beta < 0$):} the non-vacuum eigenvalue $\tanh\beta$ is
  negative, so $T_\beta$ has no real self-adjoint logarithm $H$ with $T_\beta = e^{-H}$.
  Here one must distinguish the \emph{absolute spectral gap}
  $1 - |\tanh\beta| = 1 + \tanh\beta \in (0,1)$ (which governs the contraction rate
  $\|T_\beta - P\| = |\tanh\beta|$) from the \emph{algebraic eigenvalue gap}
  $1 - \tanh\beta \in (1,2)$ (for instance, at $\tanh\beta = -1/2$, the absolute spectral
  gap is $1/2$ while the algebraic eigenvalue gap is $3/2$). The Lean lemma
  \lean{z2\_spectral\_gap\_pos} states only the scalar inequality $0 < 1 - \tanh\beta$.
  \item \textbf{Zero coupling ($\beta = 0$):} $\tanh 0 = 0$, so $T_0 = P$ is rank-one and
  singular; no finite Hermitian operator $H$ on $\C^2$ satisfies $e^{-H} = T_0$ (as $e^{-H}$
  is always invertible). For every $n \ge 1$, the sign two-point correlation
  $\Ex_n[f(\sigma_0)f(\sigma_n)] = 0$ and all connected two-point correlations across
  distinct sites vanish identically (whereas uncentered expectations of constant observables
  remain $\Ex_n[1 \cdot 1] = 1$). Restricting \lean{z2\_mass\_pos} to $\beta > 0$ avoids
  Lean's junk value \lean{Real.log 0 = 0}.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\section{The identification theorem and exact correlation}\label{sec:ident}

\begin{theorem}[Hermitian transfer-matrix identification; \lean{z2\_identification}]\label{thm:ident}
For every $\beta \in \R$, every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, and all $A, B : \{0,1\} \to \C$,
\[
  \Ex_n\bigl[\conj{A(\sigma_0)}\,B(\sigma_n)\bigr]
  \;=\; \inn{A\Om}{\,T_\beta^{\,n}(B\Om)},
\]
where $\inn{x}{y} = \sum_{i\in\{0,1\}} \conj{x_i}\,y_i$ is Mathlib's Euclidean inner
product, conjugate-linear in the first argument (\osline{Z2Identification}{269}{z2\_identification}).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof, as formalized]
Split the sum over $\sigma : \mathrm{Fin}(n+1) \to \mathrm{Fin}\,2$ at the first spin
$\sigma = (s,\tau)$. Factoring out the first bond
$k_\beta(s,\tau_0) = (e^\beta+e^{-\beta})(T_\beta)_{s,\tau_0}$ and using self-adjointness of
$T_\beta$ yields by induction on $n$ (\osline{Z2Identification}{171}{z2PathSum\_succ}):
\[
  \sum_{\sigma} \conj{A(\sigma_0)}\,B(\sigma_n)\,W_\beta(\sigma)
  = (e^\beta + e^{-\beta})^n\,\inn{A}{T_\beta^n B}.
\]
Setting $A = B \equiv 1$ and using $T_\beta\mathbf{1} = \mathbf{1}$ gives
$Z_n(\beta) = 2(e^\beta + e^{-\beta})^n$ (\osline{Z2Identification}{221}{z2Partition\_eq}).
Dividing cancels $(e^\beta+e^{-\beta})^n$ and leaves
$\tfrac{1}{2}\inn{A}{T_\beta^n B} = \inn{2^{-1/2}A}{T_\beta^n(2^{-1/2}B)} = \inn{A\Om}{T_\beta^n(B\Om)}$.
(Without $\conj{\cdot}$ on $A(\sigma_0)$, the identity fails already at $n=0$ for
$A \equiv i$, $B \equiv 1$, where $\Ex_0[i\cdot 1] = i \neq -i = \inn{i\Om}{\Om}$.)
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Exact sign two-point function; \lean{z2\_two\_point\_tanh}]\label{thm:endpoint}
For the real-valued sign observable $f(0) = +1$, $f(1) = -1$ (so $\conj{f(\sigma)} = f(\sigma)$),
every $\beta \in \R$, and every $n \in \mathbb{N}$,
\[
  \Ex_n\bigl[f(\sigma_0)f(\sigma_n)\bigr] = (\tanh\beta)^n,
  \qquad
  \Ex_n\bigl[f(\sigma_0)\bigr] = 0
\]
(\osline{Z2Identification}{346}{z2\_two\_point\_tanh},
\osline{Z2Identification}{352}{z2\_one\_point\_sign\_eq\_zero}).
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[Portability to general finite kernels]
For a general finite symmetric kernel $K$, path induction yields $Z_n = \mathbf{1}^\top K^n \mathbf{1}$.
The power formula $Z_n = |S|\,\lambda^n$ and the uniform vacuum $\Om = |S|^{-1/2}\mathbf{1}$
require \emph{constant row sums} ($K\mathbf{1} = \lambda\mathbf{1}$), not symmetry alone.
\end{remark}

\section{Time-zero definiteness, the positive-time OS quotient, and clustering}

\begin{remark}[Time-zero definiteness vs.\ the full positive-time OS quotient]\label{rem:quotient}
The $n=0$ instance of Theorem~\ref{thm:ident} gives
$\Ex_0[\conj{A(\sigma_0)}A(\sigma_0)] = \|A\Om\|^2$, so
\osline{Z2Identification}{417}{z2\_pairing\_nondegenerate} proves that the pairing is
strictly positive definite on \emph{time-zero single-slice observables} $A(\sigma_0)$.
This does \emph{not} mean that the Osterwalder--Seiler reflection pairing on the full
positive-time algebra $\{\sigma_0, \sigma_1, \dots\}$ is definite: under site reflection
across $t=0$, the nonzero observable
$F(\sigma_0,\sigma_1) := f(\sigma_1) - (\tanh\beta)f(\sigma_0)$ satisfies
$\Ex[F \mid \sigma_0] = 0$ for every finite $\beta$, hence has zero reflected norm
$\Ex[\conj{\theta F}\,F] = 0$ by Markov conditional independence across $\sigma_0$.
Thus the full positive-time GNS quotient is nontrivial even for the 1D Ising chain and is
formalized separately (\texttt{ARR-2026-2TBRDN2DE598KSXD}).
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Algebraic structure of $T_\beta$ and role of the abstract clustering criterion]
With $P = \ket{\Om}\bra{\Om}$ and $r = \tanh\beta = a-b$, algebraically
$T_\beta = P + r(\mathbf{1}-P) = r\mathbf{1} + (1-r)P$. The distinction from earlier toy
witnesses is \emph{constructive}, not algebraic: $T_\beta$ is built from the Boltzmann bond
weights $e^{\pm\beta}$ rather than taking a target rate $r$ as input. Finally,
\lean{z2\_two\_point\_tanh} is proved directly from the eigenfunction
$T_\beta f = (\tanh\beta)f$; the separate corollary
\osline{Z2Transfer}{301}{z2\_clustering\_of\_transfer} feeds $T_\beta$ into the abstract
dense-family clustering criterion \lean{connCorrC\_le\_of\_gap}
(\texttt{ARR-2026-31ED4MTKC18JPSDE}) for arbitrary $v \in \C^2$ and $\beta \ge 0$, rather
than serving as a dependency of Corollary~\ref{thm:endpoint}.
\end{remark}
